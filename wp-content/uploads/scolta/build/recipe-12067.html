Cocoa-Coconut Bites

Originally posted to Foodista by Anne Pedersen. Foodista recipes are released under a CC-BY license. Ingredients ⅓ cup (45 g) coconut oil ¼ cup (25 g) unsweetened cocoa powder ⅛ cup + 1 tsp (30 ml + 1 tsp) maple syrup 1 pinch salt Preparation Melt the coconut oil in a small saucepan over low heat. Once melted, transfer to a bowl and whisk in unsweetened cacao powder, maple syrup, and salt until smooth and lump-free. Pour the chocolate mix into small chocolate molds. You can also use a regular baking tray. Place them in the freezer/fridge to harden for a few hours. If using a regular baking tray, cut the chocolate into pieces with a sharp knife. Notes, tips, and variations Because these confections are not made of tempered chocolate, they will not hold their shape at room temperature. They must be stored in the fridge or freezer.
