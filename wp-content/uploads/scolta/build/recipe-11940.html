Chile Lime Marinade

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Recipes Ingredients ½ cup (125 ml/4.2 US fl oz) freshly-squeezed lime juice ¼ cup (85 g/3.0 oz) honey 4 serrano chiles, minced (seeded if you want) 1 cup (250 ml/8.5 US fl oz) chicken broth 2 tbsp salt Procedure Combine all ingredients in a large mason jar and keep refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
