Heinz Burger Sauce

A classic recipe by the Heinz corporation, this sauce will go great with any burger and it’s easy to make to boot. Ingredients 1 cup Heinz mayonnaise 1 cup Heinz tomato ketchup ¼ cup Heinz sweet relish 1 Tbsp Heinz apple cider vinegar 2 tsp granulated sugar 1 ½ Tbsp jarred minced garlic 1 pinch salt 1 pinch pepper Procedure Combine all ingredients in a bowl. Let sit for 30 minutes to allow flavors to blend together.
