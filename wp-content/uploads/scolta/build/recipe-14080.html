Rocky Road Confectionery II

Rocky road is a type of confectionery commonly made in Australia. It is made from chunks of marshmallow in a matrix of chocolate. This is a similar recipe to Rocky Road Confectionery l. Ingredients 375 g milk chocolate 30 g coconut oil 350 g marshmallows Equipment Baking paper Pan Microwave Big bowl Procedure Put the chocolate in the bowl and microwave until melted. Then, once the chocolate is melted, stir in the coconut oil, which will get melted by the chocolate. Let cool slightly, then fold in the marshmallows. Spread mixture in a greased and parchment-lined pan. Let set. Unmold and cut into pieces.
