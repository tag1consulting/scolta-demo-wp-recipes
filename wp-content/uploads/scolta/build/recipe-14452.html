Split Peas (Cooked)

Ingredients 2 cups dry split peas, picked over 5–6 cups water Procedure Rinse the split peas well, and discard the water (potted plants love it!) Simmer the split peas in salted water for ¾–1 hour. Serve. Notes, tips, and variations If you have hard water in your area, adding ⅛ teaspoon baking soda will reduce the cooking time. Bacon, ham, kielbasa, salt pork, or smoked sausage may be added during cooking for added flavor.
