Carrot and Raisin Salad

Ingredients 2 ea. (250 g / 8.8 oz) carrots, shredded ½ cup (40 g / 1.4 oz) raisins 1 lemon, zested and juiced 1 orange, zested and juiced Sugar or honey to taste Procedure Mix carrots with raisins in a bowl. Add approximately 1 teaspoon of finely-grated lemon and orange zest, then add the juices. Add sugar or honey to taste, and toss everything together well. Refrigerate and serve.
