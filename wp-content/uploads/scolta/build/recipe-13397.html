Miso Soup

In traditional Japanese cuisine, miso soup is served for breakfast every day, and often served with meals as well. Ingredients ½ tsp sesame oil 3 cups water 2 tbsp miso 2 green onions (optional), finely minced ½ cake tofu (optional), cut into ½-inch cubes 2–3 fresh mushrooms (optional), thinly sliced 5–6 shrimp (optional), cubed or shredded Some pieces of seaweed (optional) Procedure Heat oil in a small pot. Add green onion. Cook for a few minutes while stirring frequently. Add 2 ½ cups of water. Bring to a boil. Dissolve miso in remaining ½ cup of water and add to pot. Lower heat and add mushrooms, seaweed, and tofu, if desired. References
