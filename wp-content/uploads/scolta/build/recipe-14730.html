Tomato Basil Sausage Pasta

This tomato basil sausage pasta recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty. Ingredients 225 g (8 oz) uncooked spaghetti 450 g (1 lb) sausage meat ½ onion, chopped 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon pepper 2 cups (500 g / 1.1 lb) marinara (tomato) sauce 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 oz) milk ½ cup (50 g / 2 oz) fresh basil leaves, chopped Procedure Boil the spaghetti in water until al dente. Meanwhile, cook the sausage meat in a pan over a medium heat for 6 minutes until well browned. Add the onion, salt, and pepper and cook for a further 2 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the marinara sauce, milk, and basil, and cook for 2 minutes. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the sauce. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve. References
