Charoset (Iraqi)

Iraqi charoset is a dried fruit and nut-based paste served during the Jewish celebration of Passover by Jews from Iraq. Ingredients 500 ml Iraqi dates, chopped and seeded Water Salt 125 ml chopped walnuts or pistachios Procedure Boil dates in lightly salted water stirring frequently until it is a thick paste. Mix in finely chopped nuts. Notes, tips, and variations A traditional variant is made by straining the pulp out of the paste producing a honey-like date syrup. This procedure uses considerably more dates and can take several days to produce sufficient syrup.
