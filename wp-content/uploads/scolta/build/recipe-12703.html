Grilled Pork Loin

Ingredients 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard 2 Tbsp cider vinegar 1 boneless pork loin roast 3 Tbsp Barbecue Rub Procedure Combine mustard and vinegar, and rub all over pork. Coat pork evenly with the Barbecue Rub. Grill on a preheated grill, turning often until internal temperature reaches 145°F (63˚C). Remove and let rest for 10 minutes. Serve.
