Southern Fried Chicken Sliders

Ingredients 4 ea. boneless skinless chicken breasts Poultry shake Cornmeal Melted butter 6 ea. 1-inch buns, split in half 2 ½ quarts peanut oil Salt Freshly-ground black pepper Procedure Brush cut sides of buns with melted butter, and season with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Toast cut side down in a hot pan. Set aside. Coat chicken with melted butter. Season liberally with poultry shake and dredge in cornmeal. Let rest 2–3 minutes. Heat oil to 350°F. Add chicken breasts and fry, 1 at a time, about 10 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack. Cut chicken into portions that fit the buns. Place 1 chicken piece on each bottom half of a bun and top with the other half. Serve warm.
