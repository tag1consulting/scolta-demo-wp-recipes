Cheese Stuffed Tomatoes

This is great as an appetizer for your next dinner party. Ingredients ¼ cup cream cheese 1 tbsp basil chiffonade 1 tbsp minced garlic 2 tbsp unsalted butter ½ tsp salt ½ tsp freshly-ground black pepper ¼ cup ricotta cheese 30 cherry tomatoes, with the very top removed Procedure Combine all ingredients except tomatoes to make the filling. Place filling into a gallon-size zip-top bag with a small hole snipped in one of the bottom corners. Fill tomatoes about ¾ full. Serve chilled as an appetizer.
