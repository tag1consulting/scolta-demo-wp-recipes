Chocolate Marmalade Layer Cake

This recipe for chocolate cake uses the 6-6-6-3 method. Ingredients 6 oz self-raising flour 1 tsp baking powder 2 tbsp cocoa powder 6 oz butter 6 oz superfine sugar 3 eggs Marmalade Powdered sugar (optional) Procedure Preheat oven to 170°C fan/convection. Grease 2 round cake tins and line with baking paper. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, cocoa powder, and baking powder. Beat the butter and sugar together until fluffy. Beat in the eggs until well-combined. Mix in the flour mixture just until combined. Divide batter evenly between the prepared tins. Bake for 20 minutes in the preheated oven until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. Leave to cool for 10 minutes, then turn the cake out onto a cooling rack. When completely cool, cover one cake layer with a generous, even layer of marmalade. Place the second cake on top. If desired, dust the top with a small amount of powdered sugar as decoration.
