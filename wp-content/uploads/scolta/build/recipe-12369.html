Eggnog Milkshake (Vegan)

Originally posted to Foodista by user Teresa Lindsey. Foodista recipes are released under a CC-BY license. Ingredients 2 cups of Silk Nog (vegan eggnog substitute) 1 pint of Soy Dream vanilla ice cream Nutmeg Preparation Blend nog and ice cream together in blender. Pour into a glass, and sprinkle with nutmeg.
