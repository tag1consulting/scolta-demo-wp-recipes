Passover Orange Walnut Cake

Ingredients 5 eggs 1 cup white granulated sugar 1 cup oil 1 cup matzah meal Orange extract 1 cup ground walnuts Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. Beat eggs and add sugar, mix. Mix in oil and add matzah meal. Add walnuts and mix. Add orange extract generously and mix. Pour into 9-inch cake pan. Bake for 30–40 minutes in the preheated oven.
