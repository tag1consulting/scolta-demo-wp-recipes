Durian Pancake

Ingredients 90 g grated fresh coconut flesh 140 g rice flour 1½ tablespoons arrowroot flour 2 ripe bananas, mashed 50 g durian 170 g palm sugar, shaved 1 egg 250 ml coconut cream Vegetable oil 120 ml palm sugar caramel Passion fruit ice cream Procedure Mix coconut flesh with the rice flour and arrowroot flour. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas and durian together. Add palm sugar, egg, and coconut cream, and mix thoroughly. Pour banana mixture into a blender and process until the mixture is smooth. Mix coconut mixture and banana purée. Cover this and let it chill in refrigerator for at least 1 hour. Heat a pan to medium hot. Brush the pan surface with oil and spoon on the mixture. Fry it until it is light brown color. Serve it hot, with the palm sugar caramel and passion fruit ice cream.
