Meringue Cookies II

Ingredients French meringue or Swiss meringue, as needed Procedure Using a pastry bag, pipe the meringue into the desired shapes on parchment paper or a silicone mat. Bake at 200–225°F (100°C) until crisp but not browned. This should take 1–3 hours depending on the size. Cool before peeling off the parchment paper.
