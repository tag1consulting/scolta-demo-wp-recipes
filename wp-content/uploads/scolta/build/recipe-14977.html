Zimtsterne (German Cinnamon Star Cookies)

Zimtsterne are German cinnamon cookies often eaten for the advent season. Ingredients 4 egg whites 300 g powdered sugar 1 tsp cinnamon 350 g ground almonds or hazelnuts A little grated lemon zest Procedure Whip the egg whites until creamy. Sift in powdered sugar, and mix into egg whites. Set aside almost a third of the resulting mass for casting. Stir in the remaining mass of cinnamon and almonds, or hazelnuts and lemon zest, and knead into a dough. Roll out the dough, cut out star shapes and place on the greased baking sheet. Mix the remaining stiff egg white with rum until a glaze mass forms. Brush the cinnamon stars with glaze. Bake in a preheated oven at 160°C for about 20 minutes.
