Chocolate Sandesh

Ingredients 1 cup freshly-made low-fat paneer 1 tbsp sugar substitute 2 tbsp fresh cream 1 tbsp cocoa powder ½ tsp vanilla essence Procedure Combine paneer and sugar substitute, and mash together well. Add cream, cocoa powder, and vanilla essence. Mix well. Divide into 8 equal parts and shape into balls. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour and serve.
