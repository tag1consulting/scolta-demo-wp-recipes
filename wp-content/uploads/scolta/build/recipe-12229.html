Curtido (Spicy Cabbage Slaw)

Curtido is a spicy cabbage slaw often served with pupusas. Ingredients Chopped cabbage 1 medium onion, sliced Carrot, finely shredded Cooked green beans, not too soft (optional) Beets (optional) Grated horseradish (optional) Red pepper, finely chopped (optional) 1 teaspoon oregano Vinegar or lime juice to taste Water, to taste Salt to taste Procedure Quickly swirl the cabbage in a pot of boiling water, then drain. Combine all the ingredients in a glass or plastic container, and mix with a wooden spoon (never metal). Put the container in the refrigerator, and let it rest for a week so that the ingredients pickle.
