Grenadine

is a pomegranate syrup used in various drinks, both alcoholic and nonalcoholic. This recipe originates from a Reddit thread. Ingredients ½ cup (120 ml) pomegranate juice ½ cup (120 ml) white sugar Procedure Mix the juice and sugar in a pot or saucepan. Boil the juice and sugar, stirring constantly, until it becomes a thick and viscous syrup resembling dark honey. Remove from the heat, and let it cool. Let it cool. Notes, tips, and variations Be careful while mixing the syrup; hot sugar can splash and cause serious burns. You can use grenadine to make a Shirley Temple or grenadine soda. References
