Spanish Rice

Ingredients 2 cups uncooked rice 2 tbsp unsalted butter ¼ cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped (optional) 1 green bell pepper, finely minced 2 serrano chiles, finely minced ½ cup shredded cooked chicken or pork 1 onion, finely minced 3 ½ cups chicken broth 3 bay leaves ¼ cup minced chorizo, casing removed 1 Tbsp minced garlic Procedure Melt butter in a large pot over medium high heat. Add onion, chiles, garlic, meats, and rice, and cook until chorizo and rice are golden brown. Add broth and bay leaves. Bring to a boil and cook until liquid has evaporated and rice is tender. Sprinkle with cilantro before serving, if desired.
