Crystallised Orange Peel

Ingredients 3 organic or unwaxed oranges (Sevilles are good) 90 g caster sugar (twice) 220 ml water (twice) Procedure Peel just the skin of the washed, dried orange with a potato peeler (include only skin, no white pith). Boil the peels in a syrup made from the sugar and water for 5 minutes. Remove peels from syrup. Dry peels on a wire rack for 4 hours. Boil peels again in a fresh syrup. Dry again on the wire rack until brittle (about 48 hours). Grind in a clean coffee grinder and store in a sealed container. Notes, tips, and variations Using a fan oven set on the lowest setting (100 °C) with the door slightly ajar to allow free passage of air will dry the peel in under 8 hours.
