Hot Wings

Hot wings are a dish of seasoned and fried chicken wings tossed in a spicy sauce. Ingredients 3 oz (85 g) butter, melted 3 oz (85 g) hot sauce 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 tsp soy sauce Oil for deep frying 12 whole chicken wings, tip removed and cut in half 1 tbsp seasoned salt 1 tbsp black pepper Procedure To make the sauce, combine melted butter, hot sauce, minced garlic, and soy sauce in a stainless steel bowl. Preheat oil to 350°F (180°C). Combine seasoning salt, pepper, and wings. Toss to coat. Fry wings in batches in oil for 11 minutes. Drain on a rack. Do not use paper towels—they will make the food greasy. Toss wings with sauce and serve warm.
