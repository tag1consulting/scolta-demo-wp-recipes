Poundo Yam

Poundo yam is a swallow made from yam flour. It is similar to pounded yam; however, this version is made with yam flour and does not require a mortar and pestle. It can be served with ẹfọ riro, ogbono soup, or ẹgusi soup. Ingredients Water Yam flour Procedure Boil water using a pot. While stirring slowly, pour yam flour until the mixture is lump-free and smooth. Add a small quantity of water and cook for few minutes. Serve with the soup of your choice.
