Grand Union Bacon

Ingredients 1 egg 3 drops Tabasco sauce Bacon strips (very thin and lean) Very fine cracker crumbs or dust Butter Procedure Beat the egg with the Tabasco sauce. Dip bacon in the egg mixture, and roll in cracker dust. Add butter to a frying pan over medium heat. When pan is well heated, add the bacon. Fry until golden brown. Flip bacon over, cook the other side, and serve on a napkin. References Adapted from Grand Union Cookbook, compiled by Margaret Compton (Grand Union Tea Co., 1902).
