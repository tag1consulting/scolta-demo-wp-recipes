Spicy Crab and Arugula Salad

Ingredients ¼ cup freshly-squeezed lemon juice ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil 1 tbsp cayenne pepper 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley 10 sprigs fresh dill 2 cups pasteurized lump crab meat 1 cup arugula Procedure Purée lemon, oil, cayenne, parsley, and dill in a blender or food processor until smooth. Add crab and arugula and toss to coat. Chill, if desired, before serving.
