Garlic and Tarragon Dressing

This is a simple salad dressing. Ingredients ¼ cup (60 ml) vinegar 2 Tbsp olive oil 2 tsp garlic salt 2 tsp sugar (optional) ½ tsp dried tarragon Procedure In a small bowl or jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine vinegar, oil, garlic salt, sugar and tarragon. Pour it on lettuce or cabbage salads. References This article contains information from Frosina.org and it is used with permission.
