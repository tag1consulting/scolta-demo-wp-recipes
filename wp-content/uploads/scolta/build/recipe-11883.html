Chicken and Ricotta Paninis

Ingredients 1 ¼ cups shredded cooked chicken Ricotta cheese Extra-virgin olive oil 8 ea. ½-inch slices white bread Hot pepper sauce (e.g. tabasco), to taste Procedure Spread slices of bread liberally with ricotta cheese. Toss chicken with hot sauce and spread on in a single layer evenly on each slice of bread. Top with another slice of bread. Heat a grill pan or a griddle over medium high heat. Meanwhile, brush sandwiches with extra-virgin olive oil. Place sandwiches on grill, working in batches if necessary, until browned on both sides and cheese is melted. Serve warm.
