Island Ceviche

Ingredients ½ cup freshly-squeezed lime juice ½ cup freshly-squeezed lemon juice ½ cup freshly-squeezed orange juice 1 tsp salt 1 tsp freshly-ground black pepper 1 ea. (8 ounces) pompano fillet, thinly sliced 1 ea. (7 ounces) mahi-mahi fillet, thinly sliced Procedure Combine juices, salt, and pepper. Pour into a gallon-size zip-top bag and add fish. Refrigerate overnight. Serve.
