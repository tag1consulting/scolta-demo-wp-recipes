Macaroni Salad

Ingredients ½ cup mayonnaise 1 teaspoon dry mustard 1 teaspoon sugar 2 Tablespoons cider vinegar Salt to taste Pepper to taste 1 lb elbow macaroni pasta, cooked, drained, and chilled ⅓ cup chopped red onion ⅓ cup chopped celery Procedure Whisk together mayonnaise, mustard, sugar, and vinegar in small bowl. Add salt and pepper to taste. Toss the pasta, onion, and celery in a large bowl. Pour the dressing over top, and toss to combine. Cool before serving.
