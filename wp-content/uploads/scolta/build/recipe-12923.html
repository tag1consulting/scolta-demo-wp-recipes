Indian Rice

This makes an excellent side dish, and goes well with a large variety of things, from spicy eggplant to tofu to potatoes in coconut sauce. Ingredients 2 teaspoons olive oil 1 teaspoon cumin ½ cup raisins ¼ cup cashews 2 cups basmati rice 4 cups water Procedure Heat the olive oil in a pot. Add the cumin, then the raisins and cashews. Sauté for two minutes. Add the rice, and sauté for another minute. Add water, and bring everything to a boil. Lower the heat, cover, and cook for 12 minutes. Notes, tips, and variations Basmati rice is recommended, as other types may turn out poorly. Do not use Italian risotto rice, or any sticky rice.
