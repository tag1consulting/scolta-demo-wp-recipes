Indian Omelet

The Indian omelet is called so since it has various Indian spices and vegetables added to it. Ingredients 4 large eggs 1 cup finely-chopped onion ½ cup chopped tomatoes ½ teaspoon salt ¼ teaspoon pepper ½ teaspoon cumin seeds Finely-chopped coriander leaves, to taste Finely-chopped hot green chiles, to taste Red chile powder, to taste Oil Procedure In a small bowl, whisk the eggs lightly. Add in all the other ingredients and mix properly. Set a skillet on stove, and smear its surface with a thin coat of oil. Pour the contents of the bowl into the skillet. Lightly brown on one side. Turn over and lightly brown other side.
