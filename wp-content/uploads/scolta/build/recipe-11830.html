Charoset (Ashkenazi)

Ashkenazi charoset is a variety of charoset that uses apples and walnuts as the base, with honey for sweetness. Ingredients 1½ cups (360 g) peeled, cored, and chopped apples (e.g. MacIntosh) ¾ cup (180 g) finely-chopped walnuts 2 tbsp sweet red wine 1 tsp cinnamon 2 tbsp honey Procedure Mix all ingredients together, and refrigerate overnight for best flavor. Adjust seasoning, if necessary, after it’s chilled. Serve with matzoh, or just eat with a spoon. Notes, tips, and variations For a family seder, triple this recipe.
