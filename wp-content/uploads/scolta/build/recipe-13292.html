Mascarpone Mac &#8216;n Cheese

Folding in unsweetened whipped cream makes this mac ‘n cheese casserole light and airy. Ingredients ¼ cup unsalted butter ¼ cup all-purpose flour 1 cup mascarpone cheese 3 tbsp sugar 1 pinch of salt 2 cups heavy cream 1 pound cooked macaroni pasta 1 cup heavy whipping cream, whipped to soft peaks Procedure Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook until light blond color is achieved. Add remaining ingredients except for macaroni and whipped cream. Bring to a boil until reduced by half. Remove from heat and gently fold in cream. Place macaroni in the bottom of a 9×13-inch square baking pan. Pour cheese mixture over and bake in a 350°F oven for 25 minutes, or until bubbly on top and puffed up. Remove and serve.
