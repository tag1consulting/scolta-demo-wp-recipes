Penne with Hearty Meat Sauce

Ingredients 1 package (20 ounces) Italian sausage 1 can (26½ ounces) meat-flavored spaghetti sauce 1 can (6 ounces) tomato paste ½ cup hot water 8 ounces dry penne pasta, cooked according to package directions 2 tablespoons shredded Parmesan cheese Procedure Remove casings from sausages. Cook sausage in large skillet over medium heat until browned and crumbled; drain. Stir in spaghetti sauce, tomato paste, and water. Add pasta and stir until well coated. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat, simmer over low heat 10 minutes or until heated through. Sprinkle with cheese.
