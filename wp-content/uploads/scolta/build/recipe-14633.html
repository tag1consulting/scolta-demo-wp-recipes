Tamina (Algerian Semolina Honey Dessert)

Tamina (الطمينة) is a simple Algerian dessert mainly made of semolina. It is usually served during celebrations such as engagement parties, weddings, baby showers, or Mawlid. Ingredients 500 g semolina (medium-grind) 250 g melted butter 250 g honey 1 tbsp orange blossom water (mazhar) Ground cinnamon (for decoration) Dragées or almonds (for final decoration) Procedure Toast the semolina in a frying pan or in a cast iron dish until it takes on a nice golden color. Remove from the heat, and let it completely cool down. Melt the butter and honey in a saucepan, and add orange blossom water. Gradually add the cooled semolina while mixing. Cook for 3–5 minutes while stirring to combine everything well. Pour the tamina on a plate without waiting too long, and even out the surface. Sprinkle with cinnamon and garnish with almonds or dragées. Let cool and serve.
