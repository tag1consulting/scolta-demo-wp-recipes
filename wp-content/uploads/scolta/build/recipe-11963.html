Chipotle Dip

This is great as a dip to spice up garlic bread, or a spread on burger buns. Ingredients 1 cup sour cream ¼ cup mayonnaise 4 chipotle chiles in adobo, stemmed 1 pinch of salt ¼ tsp freshly-ground black pepper ¼ cup adobo sauce Tortilla chips Procedure Pulse everything except chips in a blender until smooth. Serve with chips.
