Betty&#8217;s Salad

Betty’s Salad is a regional, Midwest salad created by Betty Timko and featured as a menu item at her restaurant, Timko’s Soup and Such, in Toledo, Ohio. The restaurant was located in a brick building at the northwest corner of Sylvania and Douglas Roads of the Devaux Village strip mall in West Toledo [1]. After Betty died, the restaurant closed, and her signature salad dressing is still produced and distributed at regional grocery stores. Ingredients Salad ½ package fresh spinach 6 hard boiled eggs, sliced 1 can bean sprouts, drained 1–2 cans water chestnuts, drained and sliced (optional, depending on the recipe you follow) 1 pound fried bacon, crumbled Salad dressing 1 cup salad oil ¾ cup white granulated sugar ¼ teaspoon salt, or to taste ⅓ cup catsup ¼ cup white vinegar 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce 1 small to medium-sized onion, minced Procedure Layer the salad ingredients in the order listed. Mix all the dressing ingredients in blender. Pour over salad. Store salad in fridge.
