Rava Dosa (Indian Semolina Pancake)

Ingredients 1 cup rice flour 1 cup maida (or all-purpose flour) ½ cup suji (semolina flour) Salt to taste 3 Indian green chiles ½ tsp jeera (cumin) ½ cup buttermilk Water Procedure Mix all the ingredients together to a watery batter. Heat a griddle or pan, and grease it with oil. Sprinkle the batter on the pan with your hand . Cook it on medium temperature for 3–4 minutes, or until it is completely cooked. Serve it with tomato or peanut chutney.
