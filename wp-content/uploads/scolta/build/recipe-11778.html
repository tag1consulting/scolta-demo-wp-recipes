Caramel Coffee Cake

Ingredients Cake 3 eggs 400 g (14 oz) white granulated sugar 355 ml (12 fl oz) vegetable oil 2 teaspoons vanilla extract 375 g (13.2 oz) all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon baking soda Topping 115 g (4.1 oz) butter or margarine 60 ml (2.0 US fl oz) milk 220 g (7.8 oz) packed brown sugar 1 pinch salt Procedure In a mixing bowl, beat eggs until foamy; gradually beat in sugar. Blend in oil and vanilla. Combine flour, salt, and baking soda; add to egg mixture, and fold in. Pour into a greased 10-inch tube pan; bake at 350°F (175°C) for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until the cake tests done. Cool in pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes. Remove cake to a serving platter. Topping Combine all ingredients in a saucepan; boil 3 minutes, stirring constantly. Slowly pour sauce over warm cake (some topping will run down onto the serving plate).
