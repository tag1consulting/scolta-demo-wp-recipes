Cheese Filling for Peynirli Börek

Cheese filling to make peynirli börek Ingredients 1 cup cottage cheese 1 cup feta, crumbled 1 egg, beaten 1 Tbsp parsley or dill, chopped ¼ tsp pepper Procedure Mix the feta into the cottage cheese by mashing together with a fork. Add the beaten egg, chopped herbs and pepper, and stir until just mixed. Place in a plastic covered container and refrigerate until ready to use. See Börek recipe for further instructions.
