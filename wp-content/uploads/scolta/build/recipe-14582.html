Sweet Onion Ranch Dressing

A slightly sweeter ranch. Ingredients 3 cloves garlic, minced ½ cup minced onions ¼ cup white granulated sugar ¼ cup red wine vinegar ½ cup olive oil 2 cups mayonnaise Salt Black pepper 2 tsp dry mustard 1 tbsp fresh oregano, minced 1 tbsp fresh basil, chopped Procedure Mix all the ingredients in a large bowl.
