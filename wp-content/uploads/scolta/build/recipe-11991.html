Chocolate Chip Cookies IV

Chocolate chip cookies are an all-round dessert. This recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty. Ingredients ½ cup (100 g / 3.5 oz) granulated sugar ¾ cup (140 g / 5 oz) light brown sugar, packed 1 teaspoon kosher salt ½ cup (1 stick / 125 g / 4.5 oz) unsalted butter, melted 1 standard egg 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1¼ cups (175 g / 6 oz) all-purpose flour ½ teaspoon baking soda 4 oz (115 g) milk or semi-sweet chocolate chunks 4 oz (115 g) dark chocolate chunks Procedure Cream the butter, sugars and salt in a mixing bowl until combined. Beat in the eggs and vanilla. Sift the flour and baking soda into the mixture and fold in. Fold in the chocolate. Cover the dough with clingfilm and chill for at least 2 hours. Preheat the oven to 180°C (356°F). Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper. Scoop the dough onto the baking sheets, making sure the cookies are at least 4 inches (10 cm) apart from each other. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the edges are light brown in color. Take the cookies out of the oven and leave them to cool. References
