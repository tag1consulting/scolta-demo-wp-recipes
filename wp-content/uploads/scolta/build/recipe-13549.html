No-Bake Chocolate Chip Cookie Pie

This recipe for no-bake chocolate chip cookie pie is quite easy, as it doesn’t require cooking. Ingredients 1 package (15–18 oz/450 g) chocolate chip cookies (recipe uses 26 cookies) 1 cup (240 ml) milk 1 ea. (9 oz/270 g) commercial graham cracker pie crust (“extra serving” or “deep dish” size) 1 container (8 oz/240 g) container frozen whipped topping, thawed Procedure Dip 8 cookies in milk, then place in a single layer in graham cracker crust. Top with one-third of the whipped topping. Repeat the above step so that you have a total of 3 layers of cookies and whipped topping. Crumble 2 chocolate chip cookies, and sprinkle over pie. Cover and chill for 8 hours before serving.
