Butterbeer

is a drink from the Harry Potter book series. Ingredients 1 pint vanilla ice cream, softened ¼ cup butter, softened ⅓ cup brown sugar 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon 1 teaspoon grated nutmeg 1 quart apple juice Procedure Mix all ingredients thoroughly in a bowl. Divide between mugs. Heat as desired, and serve. Notes, tips, and variations Add rum or brandy for an adult butterbeer.
