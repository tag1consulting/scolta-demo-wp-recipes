Grilled Summer Fruit

Ingredients Syrup ¼ cup unsalted butter, melted ¼ cup white granulated sugar 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract ¼ cup peach nectar 1 tbsp lemon zest, finely grated Fruit 3 ripe yellow peaches, halved and pitted 2 red apples, cored and halved 4 plums, halved Special equipment 8 wooden toothpicks, soaked in water 30 minutes Procedure Combine all syrup ingredients in a large saucepan, and bring to a boil over high heat until reduced by ⅓. Set aside until cool. Skewer plums through the side on the toothpicks. Brush cut side of all fruits liberally with syrup, and place syrup-side down on a medium high grill until marked. Remove from grill, and serve warm with remaining syrup.
