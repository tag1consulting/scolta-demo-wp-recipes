Seasoned Pepper

Use this in place of regular black pepper for a burst of flavor. It’s also great as a steak rub or blackening seasoning. Ingredients 1 cup black peppercorns ¼ cup whole coriander seeds ¼ cup rock salt 2 tbsp granulated garlic 2 tbsp dried minced onion 4 sugar cubes Procedure Combine everything, and put it in your pepper mill. Use like you would ordinary pepper.
