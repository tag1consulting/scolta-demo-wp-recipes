Orange Sherbet Smoothie

Ingredients 2 large scoops of orange sherbet 1 large scoop of vanilla ice cream 6 ounces (170 g) Dr. Pepper (or equivalent) soda 1 large squeeze of chocolate syrup Dash of lime juice 1 pinch of salt (optional) Procedure Combine all the ingredients in a blender. Blend until mixture is thin enough to pour out of blender into a glass. Enjoy with a spoon and/or soda straw.
