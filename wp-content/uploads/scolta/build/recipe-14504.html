Stracciatella II

Tuscan soup or stracciatella is a simple soup consisting of broth or stock and eggs. Ingredients Stock Eggs Procedure Whip up 3–4 eggs. Gradually add hot stock to them, and keep on whisking them up until they begin to curdle. Keep the soup hot in a bain-marie.
