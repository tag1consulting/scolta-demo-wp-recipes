Israeli Salad

Ingredients 1 cucumber, chopped in 1 cm cubes 1 tomato, chopped in 1 cm cubes 1 onion, chopped in 1 cm cubes ¼ cup chopped parsley 2 tbsp oil 2 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar ½ tsp salt ½ tsp freshly-ground black pepper 1 tsp za’atar (can substitute ¼ tsp thyme) Procedure Mix cucumber, tomato, onion, and parsley. Mix oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and za’atar to make dressing. Pour dressing over vegetables. Correct seasoning to taste.
