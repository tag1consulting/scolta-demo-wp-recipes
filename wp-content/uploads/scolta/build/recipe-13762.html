Pezzetti di Cavallo (Italian Horse and Tomato)

Pezzetti di cavallo (pieces of horse) is a dish from Italy. Ingredients 1 kg horse meat 1 L tomato sauce Onions, chopped Olive oil (extra virgin) Parsley Carrots Celery Chilli peppers 2 leaves laurel Procedure Cut the horse meat in cubes, and fry it in a pan with some onion until it turns brown. Add tomato sauce. Add carrots, parsley, celery, laurel, and chilli peppers to taste. Cook on low heat (preferably in a clay pot). Skim the foam on the top and let simmer for 4 hours. Serve with home-made bread.
