Yeasted Rolls I

Ingredients 3¼ cups bread flour 1 tsp salt 7–8 oz milk ¼ cup water 2 tsp active dry yeast 2 tbsp white sugar ¼ cup unsalted butter 1 egg Procedure Knead all the ingredients together, and let rise for 1 ½ hours. Alternatively, use the dough setting on a bread machine. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Divide dough into 12 rolls. Brush melted butter over the top. Bake for 20 minutes.
