Rum Balls

This recipe involves no cooking. Ingredients 1 cup ground vanilla wafer cookies 1 cup pecan crumbs 1 cup powdered sugar 2 tbsp Dutch-processed cocoa powder 1½ tbsp light corn syrup ¼ cup apple juice Powdered sugar and pecan crumbs, for coating Procedure Combine ingredients. Shape approximately 1 tbsp of dough into balls, approximately 1 inch in diameter. Coat in powdered sugar or pecan crumbs. For best results, let age for a week or so in a tin.
