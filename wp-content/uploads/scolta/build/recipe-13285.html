Marzipan Muffins

Muffins with oatmeal and marzipan filling. Ingredients 150 g marzipan 150 g butter 3 eggs 100 ml milk 100 g flour 100 g white granulated sugar 100 g fine oatmeal 2 teaspoons baking soda 2 bags of vanilla sugar Procedure Finely dice the marzipan or mash it with a fork. Beat it well with butter, milk, and eggs. Mix the remaining dry ingredients. Add them to the butter and egg mixture, and mix to make a smooth batter. If it is too dry, add a little more milk. Place muffin tins on a baking sheet. Spread the batter in the tins and bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes. If desired, decorate the muffins with chocolate icing and sprinkles.
