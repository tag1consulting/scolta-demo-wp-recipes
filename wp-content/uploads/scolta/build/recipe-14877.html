Wacky Cake

A common recipe for wacky cake follows. Ingredients 3 cups sifted flour 1½ cups white granulated sugar 1 cup cocoa powder 2 tsp baking soda 1 tsp salt 2 cups water ¾ cups vegetable oil 2 tbsp vinegar 2 tsp vanilla Instructions Combine flour, sugar, cocoa, and baking soda in a large bowl. Add water, oil, vinegar and vanilla. Blend thoroughly. Pour into a greased square cake pan. Bake at 180°C or 350°F for 35–40 minutes. Cool completely.
