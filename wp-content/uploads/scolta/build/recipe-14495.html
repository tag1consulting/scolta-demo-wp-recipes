Stir-Fried Shrimp with Broccoli

Ingredients 1 tbsp vegetable or peanut oil 1 tbsp minced garlic 200 g shrimp, peeled 300 g broccoli, cut into florets and rinsed 1 tsp salt 1 tbsp oyster sauce Procedure Heat a pan, add the oil and garlic, and fry until golden. Add the shrimp and broccoli. Season with salt and oyster sauce, and add a little water. Stir together. Put on a plate and serve.
