Chipotle Lime Hot Wings

Ingredients Oil for deep frying 24 chicken wingettes Kosher salt Black pepper ¼ cup Smoky Chipotle Hot Sauce 6 tbsp butter, melted 3 tbsp freshly squeezed lime juice 3 cloves garlic, minced Procedure Heat oil to 350°F. Meanwhile, sprinkle wingettes with kosher salt and black pepper. Fry wingettes in batches for 12 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack. Combine remaining ingredients in a bowl large enough to toss wingettes. Add wingettes and toss to coat. Serve.
