Barbecue Sauce (Sweet)

Ingredients 1 cup tomato catsup (ketchup) ¾ cup cider vinegar 2–3 tablespoons mustard 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce 1 cup brown sugar 1–2 teaspoons celery seed 3 tablespoons margarine ¼ teaspoon black pepper ¼ teaspoon red pepper 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon onion flakes Garlic powder or minced garlic to taste (optional) Procedure Combine all ingredients together and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, until it thickens slightly. Arrange chicken pieces or pork chops in a greased baking dish. Pour half of sauce over and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 to 40 minutes. Pour remaining sauce over before serving. Notes, tips, and variations You can also keep sauce in refrigerator and use it like bottled BBQ sauce.
