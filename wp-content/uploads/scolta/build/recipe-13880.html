Potato Spelt Oat Burger

Ingredients Mashed potatoes Spelt Oat flakes Soy sauce Tomatoes (optional), chopped Onions (optional), chopped Salt Pepper Savory Chili powder Gyro spice Procedure Combine the mashed potatoes, spelt flour, and oat flakes in about equal amounts (1:1:1). Mix in soy sauce and water. Fry the onions and tomatoes in a little oil, and add to the potato mixture. Mix in salt, pepper, savory, chili powder, and gyro spice to taste. Form the mixture into patties. Fry lightly in a pan with a thin layer of oil until browned on both sides. Notes, tips, and variations Gyro spice is a spice blend consisting of paprika, chili pepper, onions, oregano, parsley, rosemary, thyme, marjoram, allspice, bay leaves, garlic, salt, and/or monosodium glutamate.
