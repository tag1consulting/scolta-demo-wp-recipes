Orange Bread

> “This bread is a fond memory of my childhood, visiting Agnes Frances Primm, my Grandma-Ag. As a young adult, I tried to duplicate the recipe, and unable, I went back and begged her for the secrets of it. When I asked her how long it had taken to develop the tricky recipe, she flattened my ego by telling me she’d gotten it out of a magazine years before! I like to make it for Christmas.” Ingredients Procedure Cover grated orange peel with water and boil for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse, discarding water. Barely cover with cold water (about ¼ cup). Add ½ cup sugar and cook until peel is tender and water is cooked away; cool. Preheat the oven to 350°F (~175°C). Mix thoroughly egg, 1 cup sugar, melted butter, baking powder, all-purpose flour, milk, and salt. Add cooled peel to this mixture and mix well. Pour into greased and floured 9 x 5 x 3 inch (23 x 13 x 8 cm) pan. Bake in the preheated oven for about 55–65 minutes or until a toothpick comes out dry when inserted in the loaf. Notes, tips, and variations Good warm and plain, or toasted and buttered.
