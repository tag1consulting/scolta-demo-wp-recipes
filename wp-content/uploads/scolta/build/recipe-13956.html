Quick Guacamole I

This recipe is great if you have the ripe avocados because you can make it in about five minutes. Ingredients 3–4 ripe avocados Juice of ½ lime Mild store-bought salsa Procedure Peel, seed, and mash the avocados. Add the lime juice. Stir in salsa to taste. Serve immediately. Notes, tips, and variations For fancier version, make your own pico de gallo for use instead of store-bought salsa.
