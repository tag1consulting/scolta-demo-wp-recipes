Nutty Brownie Bars

Ingredients 1 regular package brownie mix 3 tablespoon vegetable oil 1 cup sweetened condensed milk ½ cup chopped almonds ¼ cup chopped walnuts 10 miniature chocolate coconut candy bars, chopped Procedure Prepare brownie mix according to package directions, reducing oil to 3 tablespoons. Pour into lightly greased 9-by-3-inch pan. Spread condensed milk over the brownie batter. Sprinkle with chopped nuts and candy bars. Bake at 350°F for 35–38 minutes or until done. Cool completely and cut into bars.
