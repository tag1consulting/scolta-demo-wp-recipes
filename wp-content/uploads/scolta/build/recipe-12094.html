Cola Cake

Ingredients Cake 2 cups (275 g / 9.7 oz) flour 2 cups (450 g / 16 oz) white granulated sugar 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 oz) cola 2 sticks (250 g / 8.8 oz / 1 cup) margarine 3 tablespoons cocoa 2 eggs ½ cup (125 ml / 4.2 oz) buttermilk ½ teaspoon baking soda 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1 ½ cup (125 g / 4.4 oz) miniature marshmallows Frosting 6 tablespoons cola 2 tablespoons cocoa 1 stick (125 g / 4.4 oz) margarine 1 teaspoon vanilla 4 cups (500 g / 1.1 lb) powdered sugar 1 cup (80 g / 2.8 oz) chopped nuts (optional) Procedure Cake Combine flour and sugar in a bowl. Mix cola, margarine, and cocoa in a saucepan, and bring to a boil. Pour over flour/sugar mixture and mix well. Beat the eggs and mix them in. Add milk, soda, vanilla and marshmallows to make thin batter. Marshmallows will float to the surface. Bake in a 9×13-inch (23×33 cm) pan at 350 °F (177 °C) for 35 minutes. Make frosting before it cools. Frosting Mix cola, cocoa, and margarine in a pan and bring to a boil. Add vanilla, sugar, and nuts. Mix until smooth. Spread the warm frosting on the cake.
