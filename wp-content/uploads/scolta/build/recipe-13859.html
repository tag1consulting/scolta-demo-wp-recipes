Potato Casserole (Rakott Krumpli)

Rakott krumpli is a popular Hungarian potato casserole. Ingredients 6 medium potatoes 4 hard boiled eggs Salt 1 kolbász (Hungarian sausage) or 1 cup diced ham (optional) 4 tablespoons olive oil 350 grams sour cream around 40% fat (called tejföl or smetana) Procedure Cook the potatoes for about 15–20 minutes. Peel the potatoes and the eggs. Slice the potatoes, eggs, and sausage. Put a little olive oil in a baking dish. Arrange alternating layers of potatoes, eggs, and salt or sausage in the dish. Drizzle olive oil over the filling, and top with sour cream. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C for about 35 minutes. Notes, tips, and variations Traditionally most Hungarian cooking used lard (preferably home-made) but olive oil works just as well as a substitution.
