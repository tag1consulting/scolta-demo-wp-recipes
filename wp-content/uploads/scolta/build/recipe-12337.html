Easy Nachos

Ingredients 12 oz (360 g) tortilla chips 1 lb (450 g) cheddar cheese, grated ⅓ cup (80 ml) chopped canned peeled green chiles (optional) ½ cup (about 120 ml) chopped onion (optional) Procedure Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Lay the tortilla chips on a cookie sheet. Toss together the cheese, chilies and onion; sprinkle over the tortilla chips. Bake for about 2 minutes, or until the cheese has melted. Serve hot.
