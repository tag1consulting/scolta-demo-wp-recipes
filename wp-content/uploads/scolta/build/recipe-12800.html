Hogao (Colombian Scallion Tomato Sauce)

Hogao is a criollo sauce used in many Colombian dishes. Ingredients 2 large red tomatoes, finely diced 2 scallions, finely chopped ¼ cup oil Allspice Chicken bouillon cube Salt Pepper Procedure: Heat oil in a medium frying pan. Add scallions, and fry 5 minutes over medium heat. Add tomato, allspice, bouillon, salt, and pepper. Let cook over low heat for 10 minutes.
