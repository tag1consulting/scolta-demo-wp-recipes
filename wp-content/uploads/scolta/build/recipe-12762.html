Hartshorne Cream Sauce for a Hare

This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne. Ingredients ½ pint cream Hare or venison 2 egg yolks Some onion Butter Flour Salt Procedure Run the cream over the hare or venison just before frothing it, and catch it in a dish Boil the cream up with the yolks, onion, and a piece of butter rolled in flour and salt.
