Banana Bread I

This is a basic, reliable banana bread recipe. The perfect thing to do with over-ripe bananas! An alternative version of this recipe can be found here. Ingredients Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease an 8 x 4-inch (20 x 10 cm) loaf pan. Combine all ingredients into a large mixing bowl. Beat well. Pour batter into pan. Bake on middle shelf of oven for 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean. Notes, tips, and variations ½ tsp baking powder and ⅓ cup milk can be substituted for the sour milk/buttermilk and baking soda. ½ cup nuts may also be added to the mixture Conversion notes
