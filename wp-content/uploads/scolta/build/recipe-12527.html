Fried Plantain and Egg

Fried plantain and egg is a Nigerian dish mostly eaten as breakfast. Ingredients Plantain Vegetable oil Egg Salt Equipment Frying pan Frying spoon Bowl Knife Fork Procedure Peel and slice the plantain. Season with salt and toss to mix. Heat a little oil in a frying pan. When hot, add the plantain. Cook, turning occasionally, until browned. Remove the plantain and set aside. Whisk the egg with a pinch of salt. Add the egg to the pan, and cook like scrambled eggs. Serve the eggs and plantain together.
