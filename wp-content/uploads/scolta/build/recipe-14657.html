Tea Eggs

Ingredients 6 chicken eggs 4 cups water 3 tbsp Jasmine green tea leaves ¼ tbsp dark soy sauce ½ tsp minced ginger ½ tsp minced garlic Procedure Bring water and spices to a boil. Add eggs, and boil for 3 minutes. Remove eggs from the liquid and roll them gently on a hard surface to crack the shell all over. Return the eggs to the pot of liquid, reduce to a simmer, and simmer for 1 hour. Reduce to low heat and allow eggs to sit in brine until served.
