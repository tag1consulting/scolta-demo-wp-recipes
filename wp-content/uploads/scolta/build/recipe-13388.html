Mini English Muffin Pizzas

Ingredients 4 English muffins 1 cup pizza sauce (store-bought) 1 package pepperoni 2 cups mozzarella cheese Procedure Slice English muffins in half. Spoon 1 tbsp pizza sauce on each English muffin half and spread evenly. Place 4 pepperoni slices on each English muffin half. Cover each mini pizza with ¼ cup mozzarella cheese. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10 minutes/ Let cool then eat.
