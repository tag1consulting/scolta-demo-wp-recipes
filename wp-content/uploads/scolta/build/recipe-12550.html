Fruit Salad with Orange Juice

This is a very simple and popular fruit salad. Any fruit can be used. Ingredients Apples Bananas Oranges Maraschino cherries Orange juice Procedure Peel the apples and bananas. Dice them into small pieces. Peel and separate the orange sections. Cut sections into smaller pieces if desired. Cut the cherries into halves or quarters, depending on the size. Pour a bit of the cherry juice in bowl. Mix everything together, and add orange juice as desired. If desired, refrigerate prior to serving. Notes, tips, and variations This recipe has been handed down for more than 5 generations and is very quenching and satisfying as a snack or dessert.
