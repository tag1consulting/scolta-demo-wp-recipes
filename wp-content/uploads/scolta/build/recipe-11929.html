Chicken Wings Asian Style

Ingredients 24 chicken wingettes Poultry shake, as needed Cornmeal, as needed 2 eggs, beaten 6 tbsp hoisin sauce 1 tbsp dark soy sauce 2 tbsp hot sauce ½ tbsp minced garlic ½ tbsp minced ginger Spicy garlic oil for deep frying Procedure Coat chicken with egg. Season chicken liberally with poultry shake. Dredge chicken in cornmeal. Fry at 350°F (180°C) in batches, if needed for 12 minutes. Drain on a cooling or oven rack. Combine remaining ingredients in a large bowl. Add wings and toss to coat.
