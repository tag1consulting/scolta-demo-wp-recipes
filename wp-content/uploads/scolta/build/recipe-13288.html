Masala Chai II

Ingredients 2 cups boiling water 2 tbsp heavy cream 1 bag black tea leaves 2 tsp vanilla sugar ½ tsp cinnamon 1 pinch cocoa powder 3–4 cardamom seeds 1 pinch caraway seeds 1 pinch anise seeds 1 pinch ground ginger (optional) 1 pinch ground cloves (optional) 1 pinch saffron (optional) Crushed almonds (optional) Procedure Combine all ingredients in a serving cup. Steep for about 5 minutes. Stir vigorously to incorporate the cocoa and cinnamon. Reheat in the microwave if you prefer it very hot.
