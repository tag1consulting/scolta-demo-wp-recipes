Polish Cauliflower with Breadcrumbs

Cauliflower with breadcrumbs is a popular dish and side-dish in Poland and many parts of Central Europe. It’s really simple to make. Ingredients 1 head of cauliflower 50 ml butter 2–3 tablespoons breadcrumbs ½ teaspoon fresh dill (optional) Procedure Boil the cauliflower for about 10 minutes. Melt some butter in the oven or a microwave. Mix the butter with the breadcrumbs, and add dill if using. Drain the cauliflower and add the mixture on top. Eat!
