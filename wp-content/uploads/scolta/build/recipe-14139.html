Salade Frisee

Ingredients 16 cups (loosely packed) frisee About ½ cup Champagne Vinaigrette 8 goat cheese cakes Crumbled crisp bacon pieces, for garnish Procedure In a large mixing bowl, toss frisee with the Champagne Vinaigrette. Divide salad among 8 salad plates. Top each salad with a goat cheese cake. Garnish with crumbled bacon.
