Lemon Italian Ice

Italian ice, also known as water ice, is a frozen dessert made from either concentrated syrup flavoring or fruit purees. Ingredients 2 cups water 1 cups light corn syrup ½ cup white sugar ⅔ cup lemon juice Procedure In heavy, medium-sized saucepan, stir together water, corn syrup, and sugar. Heat to boiling over medium-high heat. Reduce heat to low and simmer, uncovered, 5 minutes. Remove from heat; cool for 30 minutes. Stir in lemon juice. Pour mixture into a 9 inch square baking pan. Cover surface with plastic wrap. Freeze 3–4 hours, or until frozen but still soft in center. Spoon into blender container; cover. Blend on high speed until mixture becomes white and creamy. Turn back into the pan. Cover with plastic wrap. Freeze mixture about 3 hours or until firm.
