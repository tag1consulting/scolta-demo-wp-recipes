Deep Fried Wheat and Chickpea Flour Dough Balls (Muthiya)

Ingredients Oil for deep frying 1 cup chopped methi (fenugreek) leaves ⅓ cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta) 2 tbsp besan (Bengal gram flour) 1 pinch asafoetida (hing) 1 tsp sugar 1 tsp green chilli-ginger paste 1 tbsp hot oil Procedure Heat the oil for deep frying. Combine all remaining ingredients together in a bowl, and mix well. Divide dough into 15–20 equal portions, and shape each into a round ball. Fry the balls in hot oil in batches of 10–12 over a slow flame until they turn golden brown in colour. Drain on an absorbent paper and serve.
