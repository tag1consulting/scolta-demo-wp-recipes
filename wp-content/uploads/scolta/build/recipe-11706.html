Buckeyes

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Recipes | Confections Buckeyes are an easy snack named from their resemblance to the nut of the Buckeye tree. Ingredients 1 cup (320 g/11 oz) peanut butter 2 tablespoons melted butter 1 teaspoon vanilla 3 cups (375 g/13.2 oz) powdered sugar 1 small bag (100 g/3.5 oz) semi-sweet chocolate chips 25 toothpicks Procedure Mix peanut butter, melted butter, vanilla, and powdered sugar in a bowl. Form the mixture into 1-inch balls. Place a toothpick into each ball. Refrigerate or freeze for an hour. Melt the semi-sweet chocolate chips in a double boiler. Dip the balls in the melted chocolate, leaving a small exposed circle at the top. Place on wax paper and remove toothpick. Refrigerate or freeze. Related recipes Chocolate balls
