Haupia (Hawaiian Coconut Pudding)

Haupia is a Hawaiian pudding made of coconut milk and cornstarch (originally Polynesian arrowroot starch). Ingredients 3 cups coconut milk ½ cup white granulated sugar 1 dash salt ½ cup cornstarch Procedure Mix coconut milk with sugar and salt over a medium heat. Slowly add cornstarch and stir until thickened. Pour into a pan or square form. Cool for 1 hour or until fully set. Unmold and cut into squares.
