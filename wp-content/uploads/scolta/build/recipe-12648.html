Gochujang (imitation)

This recipe is a non-traditional chili paste meant to replace gochujang when none is available. Ingredients 1 teaspoon soy sauce ½–1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon sugar 2 teaspoons vinegar 1 teaspoon red chile powder 2 teaspoons chopped green onion 1 teaspoon sesame salt Procedure Mix ingredients together thoroughly. Refrigerate any leftover portions.
