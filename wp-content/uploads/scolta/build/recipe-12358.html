Egg Noodles II

Ingredients 3 egg yolks 1 whole egg 1 teaspoon salt 1 dash pepper 80 ml (¼ cup) water 580 ml (2 cups) flour 1–2 teaspoon olive oil Procedure In a bowl, beat together egg yolks, egg, salt, and pepper. Stir in water, then flour. Knead into ball. Roll out on a floured surface until 2–4 mm (1/16–⅛ inch) thick. Cut into noodles 6 mm (¼ inch) wide. Bring water to a boil in a pot, and add oil. Add noodles to boiling water, and cook until done.
