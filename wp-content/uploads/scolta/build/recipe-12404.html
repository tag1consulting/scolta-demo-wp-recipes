Ensalada Rusa (Uruguayan Potato Salad)

Ensalada Rusa, which translates into “Russian Salad” is a simple potato salad that is a staple in Uruguayan tables. It is served with “asado” in restaurants. Ingredients 2 large potatoes, diced 1 cup fresh peas 2 carrots, diced ½–¾ cup mayonnaise Salt to taste Pepper to taste Procedure Cook all vegetables separately until tender, but not too soft. Allow to cool completely. Dress with mayonnaise, salt, and pepper. Serve as a side dish. Notes, tips, and mayonnaise Please note that the mayonnaise used for this dish should not be sweet.
