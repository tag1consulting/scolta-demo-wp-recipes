Smoked Salmon Dip

Ingredients 4 oz (110 g) smoked salmon 8 oz (225 g) low-fat cream cheese 2 oz (55 g) low-fat sour cream 1 tbsp lemon juice 2 tbsp green onion, chopped 2 tbsp basil, chopped 2 tbsp parsley, chopped 1 tbsp black pepper Procedure In medium-sized bowl, crumble salmon; add cream cheese, sour cream, lemon juice, and pepper. Mix until well incorporated or creamy. Mix in basil and parsley. Serve and enjoy. Notes, tips, and variations Whipped cream cheese is much easier to mix. Add a pinch of paprika/cayenne for added heat.
