Savory Pumpkin Scones

Ingredients 2 cups self-raising flour, sifted ¼ cup skim milk powder 2 spring onions, chopped ¼ tsp ground nutmeg ¾ cup cold cooked pumpkin ½ cup buttermilk ½ cup natural mineral water Cottage cheese or butter, for serving (optional) Procedure Preheat oven to 220°C (430°F). Combine the flour, milk powder, onion and nutmeg. Fold in the pumpkin, buttermilk and mineral water to make the dough. Pat out the mixture onto a lightly floured board and roll out to a thickness of about ⅝ inch. Using a 2-inch scone cutter, press out 12 rounds. Place these on a lightly floured baking tray and bake at 220°C for 12–15 minutes or until golden. Serve with dollops of cottage cheese or butter.
