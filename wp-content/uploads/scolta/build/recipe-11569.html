Beer Marinated Shrimp

This is an excellent shrimp dish that must be marinated overnight, so if you need dinner half an hour from now this isn’t for you. Ingredients 2 pounds (900 g) peeled raw shrimp 1 onion, sliced 3 cloves garlic, minced 12 ounces (340 ml) of beer Tabasco sauce Salt Pepper Butter Procedure Combine onion, garlic, shrimp, and beer in a plastic or glass bowl. Add as much Tabasco as you’d like (start with 1 tbsp) and some salt and pepper to taste. Cover and marinate overnight. Remove shrimp from marinade, and discard marinade. Melt some butter in a pan and sauté shrimp until pink and done. Don’t overcook the shrimp or they’ll be rubbery. You can also grill the shrimp. {displaystyle }
