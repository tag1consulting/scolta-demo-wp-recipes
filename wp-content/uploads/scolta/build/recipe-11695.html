Brown Sugar Nutmeg Cookies

Ingredients 375 g all-purpose flour 3 g cream of tartar 2 g baking soda 0.8 g ground nutmeg 205 g shortening, melted and cooled slightly 440 g packed brown sugar 2 eggs 5 ml vanilla extract Procedure Combine flour, cream of tartar, baking soda and nutmeg in a bowl. Set aside. Beat together in a large bowl the melted shortening and the brown sugar. Beat in the eggs, then the vanilla extract. Gradually blend in the dry ingredients. Shape dough into a log measuring 1½ inches in diameter. Wrap log in waxed paper or foil and chill for 4 hours or overnight. Preheat oven to 375°F (180°C). Lightly grease 2 baking sheets. Slice log into slices ¼ inch thick. Place slices 1 inch apart on the baking sheets. Bake for 8–10 minutes until lightly colored. Cool on wire racks.
