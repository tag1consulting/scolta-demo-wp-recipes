Vegan Pate

Vegetarian pate is a hearty recipe that can be used as a dip or spread. Sweet peas are a good source of protein and Vitamin A, while the walnuts are jammed with omega-3 essential fatty acids. Ingredients 1 medium onion, chopped ¾ cup walnuts 1 can sweet peas ⅛ teaspoon (1 pinch) salt ⅛ teaspoon (1 pinch) black pepper ⅛ teaspoon (1 pinch) ground cumin Equipment Pot Hand-held blender or food processor Procedure Sauté onion with walnuts in a pot for about 5 minutes. In a different bowl, combine sweet peas, salt, black pepper and cumin. Mix the ingredients well. Add the sweet pea mixture to the onion and walnuts, and continue sautéing for about 3 more minutes. Turn off the heat and let cool. Purée with a hand-held blender or in food processor until you achieve a creamy, though slightly nutty texture similar to that of pate or dip. Serve with crackers, bread, baby carrots and other snack type vegetables.
