Mango Coconut Sticky Rice

Ingredients 210 g coconut milk 6–7 pandan leaves Salt 8 cups coconut water 2 cups uncooked sticky rice, rinsed well 250 g brown sugar Mango Young coconut meat Procedure Bring coconut milk to a boil with pandan leaves. Add a little salt to taste. Stir in the coconut water. Stir in the sticky rice, and simmer about 15 minutes over low heat. Stir in the mango, and simmer for 5 minutes. Add sugar and stir until the sugar has dissolved. Add the coconut meat. Serve.
