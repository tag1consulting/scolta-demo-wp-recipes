Banana Bread II

An alternative version of this recipe can be found here. Ingredients Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F (~175°C) Grease 9×5-inch (23×13 cm) loaf pan (optionally mix some sugar with a little cinnamon and spread over buttered surface of pan). Beat eggs. Mix melted butter and sugar in a large bowl (alternatively, cream the butter). Stir in eggs and vanilla. Combine flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon; add to mixture. Add sour cream, sliced bananas, and walnuts. Pour mixture into pan, optionally using a spatula to even out the top. Bake for about 1 hour, let cool, and enjoy! Conversion notes
