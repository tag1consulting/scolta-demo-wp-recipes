Jal-jeera (Cumin Mango Lemonade)

Jal-jeera, alternatively spelled jaljira, is an Indian beverage flavored with cumin. Ingredients 1 tsp cumin powder ⅛ tsp green mango powder 1 pinch Himalayan black salt ¼ tsp ground mint leaves (grind to paste) 1 tsp lemon juice 1 cup plain soda water Procedure Combine all the ingredients in a glass. Serve.
