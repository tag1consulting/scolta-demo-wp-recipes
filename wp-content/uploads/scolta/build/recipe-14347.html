Soppa ta&#8217; l-Armla (Maltese Widow&#8217;s Soup)

How to make widows soup (soppa ta L’ armla) Ingredients 100 g onions, sliced 100 g potatoes, sliced 100 g courgettes, sliced 150 g peeled tomatoes, sliced 125 g cauliflower, sliced 125 g peas 50 g tomato paste Salt Pepper 200 g ricotta 4 eggs 4 cheeselets Procedure Fry the onions in a pan with a small amount of oil until translucent. Combine the cooked onions, potatoes, courgettes, tomatoes, cauliflower, and peas in a pot. Season with salt and pepper, and bring to a boil. Simmer until the vegetables are cooked. In the last few minutes of cooking, add the eggs, ricotta, and cheeselets.
