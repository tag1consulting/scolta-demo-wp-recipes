Brown Sugar Mulling Spice Mix

Ingredients 1 cup (180 g/6.3 oz) brown sugar 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon 1 teaspoon ground cloves 1 teaspoon dried ground orange peel 1 teaspoon ground allspice ½ teaspoons ground nutmeg Procedure Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl. Stir with wire whisk until well blended. Store in an airtight container in a cool place.
