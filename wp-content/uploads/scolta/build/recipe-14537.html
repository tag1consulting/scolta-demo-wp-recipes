Stuffed Portabella Mushrooms

Ingredients 4 large Portobello mushrooms Balsamic vinegar Garlic, minced 1 egg ½ cup cottage cheese 4 oz mozzarella cheese (fresh or regular), diced or grated 10 Saltine crackers, crushed or ½ cup bread crumbs Salt Pepper Other seasonings to taste Procedure Generously sprinkle vinegar over the mushrooms, and top with garlic. Leave for 30 minutes or so. Combine the egg, cheeses, crackers and seasonings in a bowl. Mix well. Spoon the mixture onto the mushrooms. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes.
