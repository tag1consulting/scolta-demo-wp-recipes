Char Sui (Asian Barbecued Pork)

BBQ sam-chang-bak is a very simple barbecue seasoning recipe for pork meat. Ingredients 3 katis (1.8–2 kg / 4 lbs) of pork (sam-chang-bak) 5 tablespoons char-siu sauce 5 tablespoons sugar Pepper A few drops of tenderizer Procedure Sprinkle the tenderizer on the meat, then mix the rest of the ingredients together with the meat. Barbecue the meat until done.
