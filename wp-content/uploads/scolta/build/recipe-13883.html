Potatoes Colcannon

This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne. Ingredients Potatoes Greens (e.g. spinach) Butter Black pepper Salt Procedure Separately boil the potatoes and greens until tender. Drain both. Mash the potatoes. Squeeze as much liquid as possible out of the greens, then finely chop them. Combine the potatoes, chopped greens, butter, pepper, and salt. Mix well. Transfer the mixture to a greased mold, then bake in a hot oven for 10 minutes.
