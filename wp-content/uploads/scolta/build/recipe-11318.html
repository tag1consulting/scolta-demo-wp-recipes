Anchovy Herb Salsa Verde

This anchovy herb salsa verde is different from Mexican salsa verde. It may be a Spanish recipe. Ingredients ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil 9 cured anchovy fillets, drained, patted dry, and minced 6 tablespoons finely-chopped fresh flat-leaf curly parsley 3 tablespoons finely-chopped fresh rosemary 3 tablespoons finely-chopped fresh thyme 3 tablespoons finely-chopped fresh mint ⅛ teaspoon freshly-ground black pepper Procedure In a small bowl, mix together all ingredients. Serve with chips or grilled meat.
