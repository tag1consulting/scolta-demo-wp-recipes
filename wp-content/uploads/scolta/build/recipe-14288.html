Sindhi Fried Potatoes

Ingredients Large potatoes, peeled and cubed Vegetable oil 2 pinches salt 2 pinches red chile powder 2 pinches coriander powder 2 pinches ground pepper Procedure Heat enough oil in a pot for deep frying. Add the potatoes and deep fry until golden brown. Remove potatoes from the oil, and place on paper towels to drain. Transfer potatoes to a serving plate. Sprinkle with salt, chili powder, coriander, and pepper. Serve hot.
