Tropical Sausage Combo

Ingredients Ripe plantains (gonja) Butter or margarine Smoked sausages (farmers choice smokies) Vegetable confetti (chopped carrots, tomatoes, onions, bell peppers, etc. As many colours as you can get) Olive oil Vinegar Black pepper Procedure Preheat oven to 175°C. Peel the plantains and smear them with butter. Place on oven tray. Place sausages on oven rack. Put tray and rack in oven. Bake at 170°C for 30–35 minutes. Chop the vegetables, and douse with olive oil and vinegar. Serve and sprinkle with black pepper if you so wish.
