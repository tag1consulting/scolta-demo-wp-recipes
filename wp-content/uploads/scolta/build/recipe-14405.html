Spelt Burger

Ingredients Spelt Leek Oat flakes Onions Cumin Chillies Pepper Procedure Grind the spelt coarsely (if you have entire corns). Soak the spelt with oat flakes in some water, in order to get a viscous substance. The burgers will be more compact if you add more oat flakes. Cut onions and leek in small pieces. Fry onions and leek with some oil. Mix the substance with onions, leek and spices of your choice (cumin is a good choice). Form flat burgers in a hot, oiled pan. Notes, tips, and variations The burgers are also quite tasty when they are cold and on the next day. See also Bean Spelt Oat Spread Hamburger
