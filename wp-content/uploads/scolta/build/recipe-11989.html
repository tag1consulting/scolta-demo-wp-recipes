Chocolate Chip Cookies II

Ingredients 1 ½ cup (145 g) all-purpose flour ½ tsp salt ½ tsp baking soda ½ cup (115 g) butter ¼ cup (50 g) brown sugar ½ cup (100 g) white granulated sugar 1 egg 1 tsp vanilla essence 1 pack (6 oz / 170 g) semi-sweet chocolate chips Procedure Combine the flour, salt, and baking powder. Preheat the oven until it reaches 375°F/190°C. In another bowl, cream the butter and sugars until light and fluffy. Add egg and vanilla, and beat until smooth. Mix in the flour mixture until well-blended. Add the chocolate chips, and stir with a wooden spoon. Drop the cookies onto a lightly-greased or parchment-lined cookie sheet by teaspoonfuls in 2-inch (5 cm) ball shapes. Bake for 15 minutes or until the edges are light brown. Remove the cookies from the cookie sheet with a metal spatula, then let them cool on a wire rack.
