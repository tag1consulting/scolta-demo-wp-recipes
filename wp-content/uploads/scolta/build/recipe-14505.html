Strawberry And Banana Smoothie

Ingredients 1½ cups (125 g / 4.4 oz) strawberries 2 bananas ¾ cup (200 ml / 6.8 oz) apple juice ⅝ cup (150 g / 5.3 oz) natural (plain) yogurt ⅔ cup (50 g / 1.8 oz) ice cubes 1 tbsp honey Procedure Cut off the tops of the strawberries. Peel the bananas. Blend all the ingredients in a blender until smooth. Add honey to taste. Pour the smoothie into 2 glasses. Enjoy!
