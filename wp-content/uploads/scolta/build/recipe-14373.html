Southwestern Black Beans and Rice

Ingredients 2 cups cooked rice 15 ounce can black beans, drained 10 ounce can tomatoes and green chiles ½ tsp cumin Procedure In medium saucepan, combine beans, tomatoes and green chiles mix, and cumin. Heat through. Serve over hot rice. Notes, tips, and variations Ro-Tel makes canned tomatoes and green chiles.
