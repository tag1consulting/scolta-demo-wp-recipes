Simmering Tofu Stir-fry

Ingredients 1 block firm tofu Mixture of preferred vegetables (suggested combination: onion, green pepper, tomato) ½ ea. apple Soy sauce Olive oil Preferred seasonings (suggested seasonings: pepper, hot sauce, salt) Procedure Slice tofu into manageable slices (no more than ¼ inch thick). Slice all vegetables and apple. Simmer tofu and vegetable in a light mixture of olive oil and soy sauce on medium high heat. Cook until tofu is golden brown and vegetables have thoroughly soaked up juices. Serve with a side of rice or couscous and season.
