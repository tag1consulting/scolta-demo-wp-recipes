Drawn Butter

Drawn butter is a butter-based sauce typically served with lobster or crab. Ingredients 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 oz) water 2 teaspoons flour 2 ounces (57 g) unsalted butter Procedure Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan. Mix the flour and butter together until they are perfectly smooth. Stir this into the boiling water, and add salt to taste.
