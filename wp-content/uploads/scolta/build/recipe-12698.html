Grilled Lamb Chops

Ingredients 4 thick-cut bone-in lamb loin chops 1 tsp rosemary, finely chopped 2 tsp thyme, finely chopped Salt Freshly-ground black pepper 1 Tbsp smoked paprika ½ head garlic 4 tsp cayenne pepper Olive oil Procedure Rub the cut side of the halved garlic head all over lamb chops. Brush with olive oil and rub seasonings into meat. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Grill chops over high heat for 1 ½ minutes. Rotate 90° and cook for another 1 ½ minutes. Flip and repeat these steps on the other side of the chops. Move meat to medium heat and cook, turning often, until internal temperature reaches 140°F for medium rare. Remove and let rest 7 minutes. Serve warm.
