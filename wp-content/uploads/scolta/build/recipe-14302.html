Slinger

” is a breakfast item originating from St. Louis Missouri. Ingredients 2 eggs 1 hamburger patty Hash browns Cheese (grated works best) Chili Onion Procedure Cook the eggs in any manner you wish. Cook the hamburger patty. Cook the chili. Dice the onion. Place the hashbrowns and patty on a plate then put the eggs upon the patty and top it all with the chili, cheese, and onion
