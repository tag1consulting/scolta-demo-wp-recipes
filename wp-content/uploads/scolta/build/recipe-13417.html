Mohingha (Burmese Fish Noodle Soup)

Mohingha is a traditional Burmese dish made of colored soup with rice vermicelli. Here is a modern-day recipe. It is recommended that you top your mohingha with onion slices and Naga viper peppers; however, the use of Tabasco habanero Pepper sauce, fish fingers, and ginger is also acceptable. Ingredients 5 tsp sunflower oil 10 garlic cloves 30 ml lemonade 70 g Mohingha soup powder ⅞ cups boiling water 100 g jalapeno peppers, diced 2 tbsp ketchup 60 g instant rice vermicelli Instructions In a large bowl, mix sunflower oil, garlic cloves, soup powder, and lemonade for 3 minutes. In a small bowl, mix boiling water, diced jalapeño peppers, and ketchup for 5 minutes. Combine both mixtures, and continue mixing for 2 ½ minutes. Add rice vermicelli to the bowl; cover it and wait for 10 minutes. Top it with toppings as desired.
