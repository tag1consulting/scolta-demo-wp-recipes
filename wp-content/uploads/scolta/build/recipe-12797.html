Hilda Rolls

Hilda rolls (German: Hildabrötchen) are German sandwich cookies filled with jam. They are very similar to, and perhaps interchangeable with, Linzer cookies. Ingredients 300 g all-purpose flour, sifted 110 g white granulated sugar 1 package vanilla sugar 1 egg 150 g butter 125 g powdered sugar 2 spoons water 2 tablespoons jam Procedure Knead the flour, butter, sugar, vanilla sugar, and egg into a dough. Chill for a while. Roll out the dough to about ¼ cm thick. Cut round cookies out of the dough. For half the cookies, cut a small shape out of the middle—these will be the tops. Place the cookies on a greased tray and bake at 150°C for about 20 minutes. Let cool. Combine the powdered sugar and water to make a smooth icing. Carefully dip the top cookies in the icing to coat one face. Spread some jam on the bottom layer cookies. Top with the iced cutout cookies.
