Firecracker Rub

This rub kicks it up a notch from oversalted flavorless commercial rubs by packing quite some heat. Ingredients 1 cup smoked paprika ¼ cup salt ¼ cup cayenne pepper ¼ cup black pepper ¾ cup dark brown sugar ¼ cup ground coriander 3 tbsp granulated garlic ½ cup dried rosemary Procedure Combine all ingredients in an airtight container.
