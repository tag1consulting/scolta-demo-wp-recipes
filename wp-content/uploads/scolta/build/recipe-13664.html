Pan-Seared Pork Chops

Ingredients 4 thick-cut bone-in pork rib chops ½ cup Chop Rub Cider vinegar, as needed 1 tbsp olive oil Procedure Brush pork chops with cider vinegar. Season each pork chop with the chop rub, pressing lightly. Heat oil in a very large oven-proof cast iron or stainless steel skillet over medium high heat. Add pork chops and sear for 3 minutes per side. Insert an oven-safe probe thermometer into one pork chop and place pan in a 350°F oven. Bake until internal temperature reaches 145°F. Remove and let rest for 5 minutes; serve.
