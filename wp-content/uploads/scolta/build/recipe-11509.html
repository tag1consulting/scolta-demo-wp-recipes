Basic Mulling Spice

Ingredients 6 cinnamon sticks 1 small whole nutmeg ½ cup (60 g/2.1 oz) whole cloves ½ cup (60 g/2.1 oz) whole allspice Grated peel of 1 whole orange Procedure Place whole cinnamon sticks and nutmeg in a large ziplock bag. Use a hammer to crush the spices into rough chips. Combine the chips with cloves and allspice. Store in an airtight container. Use To make spiced wine or cider: Add 2 tablespoons of the spice mixture to 4 cups (1 L/1.1 US qt) of wine or cider. Add peel of 1 fresh orange. Simmer slowly over medium heat for 5–10 minutes. Strain liquid; serve hot wine or cider in mugs.
