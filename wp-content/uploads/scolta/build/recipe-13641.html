Ozark Pudding

Ozark pudding is a baked cake-type pudding that apparently originated in the Ozark region of the USA. Ingredients 2 eggs, well-beaten 4 tablespoons flour 1½ cups brown sugar 3 teaspoons baking powder ½ teaspoon salt 2 teaspoons vanilla 2 cups chopped apples 1 cup chopped nuts Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. Blend all ingredients together, starting with eggs and ending with nuts. Grease casserole dish, pour in casserole. Bake for about 35 minutes. Top with whipped cream or ice cream.
