Hazelnut and Black Raspberry Panna Cotta

Ingredients 4 cups whole milk 2 cups white granulated sugar 6 sheets gelatin 2 tbsp vanilla extract 1 vanilla bean, seeded 1 oz (28 g) hazelnut liqueur 1 oz (28 g) black raspberry liqueur Procedure Heat all ingredients in a saucepan over medium-low heat until gelatin is melted. Pour into ramekins, and chill until set (usually 6 hours, but they are better left overnight). Serve with fresh berries or a berry sauce.
