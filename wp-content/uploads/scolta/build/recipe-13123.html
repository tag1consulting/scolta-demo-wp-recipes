Layered Pecan Pie

Ingredients Filling 1 package (8 ounces) cream cheese ⅓ cup white granulated sugar ¼ teaspoon salt 1 egg 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1 ea. 9-inch unbaked pie shell, or single homemade pie crust 1¼ cups chopped pecans Topping 3 eggs 1 cup light corn syrup ¼ cup white granulated sugar 1 teaspoon vanilla extract Procedure Preheat oven to 375°F. Combine the cream cheese, sugar, and salt; beat until smooth. Add egg and vanilla, beating well after each. Spoon mixture into unbaked pie shell. Sprinkle with pecans. Combine 3 eggs, corn syrup, remaining sugar, and vanilla; beat well. Pour this mixture over pecans. Bake for 35–40 minutes or until set.
