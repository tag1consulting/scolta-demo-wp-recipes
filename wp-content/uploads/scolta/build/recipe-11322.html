Ants on a Log

Ants on a log is a snack consisting of celery, peanut butter, and raisins. It is so-named since the raisins on the celery look similar to bugs. It is often a snack for children. Ingredients Celery stalks (enough to have 1 or 2 for each person) Peanut butter Raisins Procedure Cut the celery up into pieces, maybe 4 or 5 inches (about 10-13 cm). This is the “log”. Spread peanut butter on top of the celery. Put raisins on top. These are the “ants”. Notes, tips, and variations Peanuts are a common allergen. If serving to children, check for peanut allergies.
