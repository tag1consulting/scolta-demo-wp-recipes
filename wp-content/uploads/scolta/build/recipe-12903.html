Igbouh Ahi (Nigerian Roasted Bambara Nuts)

Roasted bambara nuts, also known as igbouh ahi, is a crunchy snack commonly consumed by the Tiv people of Nigeria. Ingredients Bambara nuts Pepper, blended Salt, to taste Procedure Rinse bambara nuts and add salt to taste. Combine blended pepper with bambara nuts. Roast the peppered bambara nuts until tender. Serve with plantain or roasted maize.
