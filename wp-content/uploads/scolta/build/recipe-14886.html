Watermelon Mojito Smoothie

This watermelon mojito smoothie recipe incorporates the flavors of a mojito, although it contains no actual alcohol. Originally posted to Foodista by Peas of Cake. Foodista recipes are released under a CC-BY license. Ingredients 1.5 cup cubed frozen watermelon 1 tbsp or less of honey (optional) 1 lime, juiced 3–4 oz water (depending how on thick you want it) 4–5 mint sprigs Preparation Blend all ingredients together in a blender. Adjust the amount of liquid as desired. Garnish with mint if you like.
