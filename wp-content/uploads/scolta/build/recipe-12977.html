Jamaican Banana Fritters

Jamaican banana fritters are a traditional dessert and snack dish in the Caribbean. They can be served by themselves, in a platter along with other fritters, or with different toppings, like cinnamon, coconut, or even ice cream. Ingredients 3 ripe bananas 200 grams all-purpose flour 60 ml room-temperature milk 2 teaspoons baking powder 3 tablespoons brown or white sugar ½ teaspoon vanilla extract 1 pinch salt Oil for frying Procedure Peel the bananas and mash them in a bowl until runny. Heat oil in a deep frying pan at medium heat. Add the sugar, milk, vanilla, salt, and flour to the bananas and mix until approximately the consistency of pancake batter or a bit thinner. Drop tablespoons of the mixture into the heated oil, and fry until edges are firm, then flip to cook other side until golden brown. Drain on a towel and serve hot. Notes, tips, and variations Sprinkling sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon or coconut on the fritters after frying. Serving with a scoop of ice cream.
