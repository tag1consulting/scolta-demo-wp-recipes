Pineapple Side Dish

Hot pineapple side dish is a casserole of pineapple with cheese. Ingredients 1 can (20 ounces / 600 ml) pineapple chunks ½ cup white granulated sugar 3 tablespoons flour ¼ cup butter, melted 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided ⅔ cup coarsely crushed saltines (14 crackers) Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F (175˚C). Grease a 1½ quart (1½ liter) baking dish. Drain the pineapple, reserving ¼ cup juice; set aside. In a small bowl, combine sugar and flour. Add the butter and 1 cup cheese; mix well. Gently stir in pineapple. Pour into the prepared baking dish. Sprinkle crushed crackers on top; drizzle with reserved pineapple juice. Bake, uncovered, for 30–35 minutes or until bubbly around the edges. Remove from oven and sprinkle with remaining cheese.
