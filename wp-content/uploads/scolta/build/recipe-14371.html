Southern Style Greens

Ingredients 2 pounds assorted dark leafy greens, such as mustard, collard, and rainbow chard 8 rashers bacon, cut into about ½-inch pieces 1 tbsp bacon fat (optional) 1 ½ tsp salt (optional) 1 ½ tsp freshly-ground black pepper ½ cup chicken broth Procedure Cook bacon to slightly less than done in a large stainless steel pot. Drain. If desired, pour 1 tbsp bacon fat back into pot. Add greens, and sauté over medium-high heat for 7 minutes. Add remaining ingredients and lower heat to medium low. Cook for 45 minutes. Defat if needed. Serve warm, with a vinegar-based, clear hot sauce to accompany.
