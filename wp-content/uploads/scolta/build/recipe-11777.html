Caprese Salad

Caprese salad is a delicious Italian dish made with tomato and mozzarella cheese. It is quite simple but tastes great and looks attractive. Ingredients 1 medium tomato A few basil leaves about ¾ the weight of Mozzarella cheese as the tomato, sliced Olive oil Balsamic vinegar Italian seasoning or oregano and pepper (optional) Procedure Cut the tomato into about 5 slices. Put a slice of mozzarella cheese on top of each tomato slice. Cut a horizontal slit halfway through each slice. Put a basil leaf on top of each one. Sprinkle herbs over each slice. Drizzle some vinegar and olive oil on top. Do not mix the vinegar and olive oil. Eat fresh.
