Nuremberg Gingerbread

Recipe for gingerbread from Germany. Ingredients 500 g honey 250 g granulated sugar 150 g flour 1 tsp cloves, ground 1 tsp nutmeg 1 tsp cardamom 1 tbsp cinnamon 1 pinch of ground ginger Grated zest of 1 orange Grated zest of 1 lemon 200 g chopped hazelnuts 50 g lemon flavoring 50 g orange flavoring Gingerbread wafers 1 packet of baking powder Procedure Bring the honey and sugar to the boil in a saucepan while stirring. Remove from the heat and let cool. Sift the flour into a bowl. Stir in the remaining ingredients. Pour the honey mixture, then the baking powder into the flour. Stir and knead the dough until it cools down. Roll out the dough. Cut out the dough, and place on the gingerbread wafers. Decorate with an almond, and transfer to a baking sheet. Let cool for about a day Bake at 180 °C for 15–20 minutes
