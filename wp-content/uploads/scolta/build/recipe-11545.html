Beef Burgers with Onion

These burgers are filled with onion and topped with melted cheese. Ingredients Vegetable oil Onion, chopped Ground beef Egg Sliced cheese Lettuce Tomato, sliced thin Ketchup Equipment Frying pan Bowl Knife Grill Procedure Heat a thin layer of vegetable oil in a frying pan. Add the onion, and fry for about 5 minutes until softened. Remove from the heat and cool. Combine the ground beef, egg, and cooked onion. Shape the beef mixture into patties. Cook the burgers on a grill for about 6 minutes, flipping halfway until browned on each side and cooked through. In the last minute of cooking, lay a slice of cheese on top of each patty. Cover and let the cheese melt slightly. Lightly toast the hamburger buns. Sandwich the patties in the buns, topping with lettuce, tomato, and ketchup as desired.
