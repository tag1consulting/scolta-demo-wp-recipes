Sweet Mango Lassi

Ingredients 1 L (4 cups) mango nectar (strained puréed mango) 500 ml (2 cups) full-fat yoghurt 500 ml (2 cups) chilled milk 250 ml (1 cup) sugar Procedure Mix sugar with the mango nectar until dissolved. Gradually add the milk to the yoghurt. Mix together the mango and yoghurt mixtures.
