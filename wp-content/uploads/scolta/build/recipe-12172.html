Creamy Aioli Salsa

This is a cross between an aioli (a mayonnaise-like sauce which uses garlic instead of egg yolk as an emulsifier) and a pico de gallo-style salsa. Ingredients 1 lime Several tomatoes, chopped Olive oil 1 pinch salt 5 cloves garlic 1 or more jalapeño chiles or serrano chiles, chopped 1 bunch cilantro (optional), chopped Procedure Squeeze the lime juice into a blender-safe bowl. Add 2 teaspoons of olive oil and all other ingredients. Use an immersion blender to blend everything to a purée. Gradually blend in more olive oil, tasting, until the mixture reaches your desired creaminess. See also Spicy Citrus Carrot Aioli
