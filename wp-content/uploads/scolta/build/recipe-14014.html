Rhubarb Marmalade

Ingredients 1 lemon, juiced 2 oranges, juiced 4 pounds rhubarb, cut into small pieces 1 pound raisins 3 pounds sugar Procedure Combine the lemon juice, orange juice, and rhubarb. Chop lemon and orange rind along with raisins; add to rhubarb mixture. Mix all and let stand for 30 minutes. Add sugar and bring to a boil, then simmer for 1 hour, stirring often. When the mixture gets thick, pour into glasses or crock. When cool, seal.
