Apple Fritters

Ingredients 1 cup peeled and chopped apple ½ cup granulated sugar 2 eggs, beaten Flour ¼ cup unsalted butter, divided ⅛ tsp kosher salt 2½ quarts (2.5 liters) peanut or canola oil Powdered sugar Procedure Run the apple, sugar, eggs, butter, and salt in the food processor until a thick paste forms. Roll paste into 3-inch balls. Dredge balls in flour. Set aside for 2–3 minutes. Heat oil to 375°F (190°C). Add paste balls and fry until golden brown and cooked through. Drain fritters on a cooling rack. Dust with powdered sugar before serving.
