Tuwo Alkama (Wheat Swallow)

Tuwo alkama is a swallow made from wheat flour and water. It is commonly eaten in the northern part of Nigeria Ingredients Whole wheat flour Water Equipment Saucepan Sieve Turning stick Procedure Sift the flour to remove any debris. Bring a saucepan of water to a boil. Gradually and continuously stir in the flour until it forms a thick mixture. Cook for 5 minutes, then serve.
