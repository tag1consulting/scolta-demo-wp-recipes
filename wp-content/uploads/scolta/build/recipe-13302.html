Matzah Lasagne

Ingredients 4 sheets of matzah 1 lb (450 g) cottage cheese 1 ¼ cups grated mozzarella cheese ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese ½ teaspoon black pepper (optional) 30–34 ounces marinara sauce or tomato sauce Procedure Grease a baking pan that will fit the matzah sheets. Spread about ½ cup sauce in the pan to cover the bottom. Place 1 matzoh sheet on top. Spread more tomato sauce over the matzah. Spread a layer of cottage cheese on top of the matzah. Sprinkle with mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses. Repeat these layers until all 4 matzah sheets are used. Spread tomato sauce and sprinkle mozzarella and Parmesan cheese on top. Add ½ cup water Cover with foil, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 35–40 minutes.
