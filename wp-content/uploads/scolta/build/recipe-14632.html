Tamil Spice Mix (Milagai Podi)

Milagai podi is a mixture of crushed spices used as a condiment in Tamil cuisine. The mixture can be used dry or mixed with ghee or oil. It’s great served with hot idlis or dosais. Ingredients 2 spoons red chile powder 1 pinch salt 1 pinch ground turmeric 3 garlic cloves, crushed 3 spoons ghee, melted Procedure Mix all ingredients Let sit 3 minutes.
