Proja (Serbian Cornbread)

Proja (Serbian Cyrillic: Пpoja) is a Serbian national dish of cornbread. Ingredients 5 cups cornmeal 3 cups flour 3 eggs 3 cups oil 1 baking powder 1 cup yogurt 1 glass mineral water 1 big slice cheese, crushed Salt Procedure Mix everything together to make a batter. Bake the batter in a 5 cm deep greased pan until golden. Serve with kiseli kupus and pavlaka.
