Nigerian Dodo (Fried Ripe Plantain)

Dodo is a Nigerian dish of fried ripe plantain. It is often served with jollof rice, rice and beans, and beans porridge. Ingredients 4 ripe plantains ¼ teaspoon salt ½ cup puréed chile pepper mixed with tomatoes ½ cup vegetable oil Procedure Cut each ripe plantain into 6 small pieces. Toss with salt and chile-tomato purée to taste. Heat ½ cup of oil in a frying pan. Add the 6 plantain pieces, and deep fry until all sides are golden brown. Serve with jollof rice or bean porridge.
