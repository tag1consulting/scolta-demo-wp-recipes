Mocha Delight

Ingredients Cake 4 tablespoon butter, melted 1 cup graham cracker crumbs 1 box (22.5 ounces) brownie mix Frosting 3 egg whites ½ cup white granulated sugar 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened 2 tablespoons instant espresso powder 2 tablespoons warm water Decoration 1 bag Dove Rich Dark Chocolate Hearts 1 cup whipped cream or topping Procedure Base Preheat oven to 350°F. Line bottom of 9-inch springform pan with waxed paper; grease. Add melted butter to graham cracker crumbs and press into bottom of pan. Prepare brownie mix according to package instructions; spread over crumbs. Bake until firm, approximately 35 to 50 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Remove from oven to a wire rack; cool completely and remove from pan. Frosting Whisk egg whites and sugar together in a bowl over simmering water until sugar is dissolved. Remove from heat; using electric mixer, whip until fluffy and cooled. Add butter one nugget at a time while continuing to beat. Dissolve espresso powder in warm water. Beat espresso into frosting. Frost top and sides of cake. Finishing Snip small corner of a resealable plastic bag; place whipped cream inside. Pipe 3 rosettes around outside edges of cake. Place a chocolate heart atop each rosette. Place 8 chocolate hearts in a small resealable plastic bag. Microwave until melted, about 15 seconds. Cut a corner of the bag; randomly drizzle chocolate on dessert plates. Place a cake wedge on each plate.
