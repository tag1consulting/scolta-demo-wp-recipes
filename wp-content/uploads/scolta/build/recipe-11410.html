Backyard BBQ Chicken

BBQ chicken is always a barbecue favorite. Ingredients 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts and drumsticks 1 tbsp cayenne pepper 1 tbsp kosher salt 1 tbsp black pepper 2 tbsp lemon pepper 1 ½ tsp garlic powder 2 tbsp smoked paprika 2 tbsp dark brown sugar 1 ½ cups tomato paste ¼ cup honey ¼ cup apple cider vinegar Procedure Combine dry ingredients and rub onto chicken. Refrigerate 1 hour. Combine liquid ingredients, bring to a boil, and simmer until reduced by ⅓. Place on medium grill, and turn often, brushing with sauce when turned until interior temperature reaches 165 °F (74 °C). Let rest for 10 minutes; serve.
