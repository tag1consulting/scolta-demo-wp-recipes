Blackberry Mush

Ingredients 100 g (3.5 oz / 1 cup) blackberries 80 g (2.8 oz / ⅓ cup) white granulated sugar 240 ml (8.1 oz / 1 cup) water 20 g (0.71 oz / ⅙ cup) cornstarch Procedure Put the fruit in a saucepan. Add sugar, cornstarch, and water, and mash to a pulp. Simmer or boil, uncovered, to reduce moisture. Serve hot over ice cream, or cold as jam. Notes, tips, and variations Raspberries, plums, blueberries, and peaches may be cooked in the same way.
