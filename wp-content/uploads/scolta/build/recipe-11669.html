Breaded Potato Wedges

Ingredients 2 pounds Russet potatoes, cut into 10 wedges 2 tbsp salt 2 tbsp black pepper 1 ½ tbsp cayenne pepper 1 tbsp smoked paprika ½ cup crushed cornflakes 3 eggs, beaten 2 tbsp minced garlic Oil for deep-frying Procedure Combine eggs with seasoning. Dip potatoes into egg mixture then dredge in cornflakes. Let rest 2–3 minutes. Heat oil to 350°F. Fry wedges, in batches if needed, 3–4 minutes or until limp. Drain on a cooling rack set above a baking pan. Heat oil to 375°F. Fry wedges again, until golden brown and crispy. Drain again on same rack. Serve warm.
