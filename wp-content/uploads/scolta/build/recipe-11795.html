Carrot Jam

As carrots are fairly sweet, they can often be used in sweet dishes with good results. One such recipe is carrot jam; this is a simple version from Denmark. Ingredients 1 kg carrots, peeled and finely grated ¼ liter water Juice of 4 lemons ½ kg sugar Procedure Boil the water and carrots until tender. Add juice and sugar, and continue to boil for a further 15–20 minutes.
