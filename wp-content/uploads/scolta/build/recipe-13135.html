Lemon Cream Sauce

Ingredients ½ cup vermouth ½ cup chicken stock 2 tbsp lemon juice, strained 4 oz (1 stick) chilled butter, cut into 8 pieces ¾ cup heavy cream, whipped 3 tbsp chopped chives or parsley Procedure Boil the vermouth, chicken stock and lemon juice in a small non-reactive saucepan until reduced to about 2 tablespoons. Remove from the heat and beat in two pieces of chilled butter. Set over a very low heat and beat in the rest of the butter, a piece at a time. Remove from the heat and stir in the whipped cream and chopped herbs.
