Chipotle BBQ Chicken Nuggets

Ingredients 4 boneless skinless chicken breast halves, cut into 1 ½ in pieces Cornmeal as needed Freshly-squeezed lemon juice as needed 3 tbsp Chipotle BBQ Rub Vegetable oil for deep-frying Black pepper Procedure Place cornmeal in a pie pan and lemon juice in another pie pan. Dip chicken pieces in lemon juice then season with BBQ rub. Dip again in lemon juice. Dredge pieces, three at a time, in the cornmeal, then dip in lemon juice again then dredge again in cornmeal. Heat oil to 350°F and fry pieces in batches for 6 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack but not paper towels. Sprinkle with black pepper and serve.
