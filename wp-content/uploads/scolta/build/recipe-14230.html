Sesame Noodle Salad

From the original recipe contributor: > “The salad recipe reminds me of those quiet and peaceful Sunday brunches at the local corner bakery and is an attempt to duplicate the bakery’s original, secret recipe.” Ingredients 16 oz (1 lb) angel hair pasta ⅛ cup sesame oil Olive oil, if needed 1 tbsp table salt 1 cup or more finely-chopped fresh cilantro ½ cup chopped fresh scallions 1 ½ cups thinly-sliced fresh carrots Procedure Cook angel hair pasta according to package instructions, then drain. Add sesame oil, olive oil, and salt (to taste) to drained pasta Toss in cilantro, scallions, and carrots. Chill before serving.
