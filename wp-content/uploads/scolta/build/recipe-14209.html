Seafood Seasoning

This seafood seasoning also works great on chicken or steak, especially filet mignons. Ingredients 6 tbsp butter-flavored salt ½ cup sweet paprika 4 bay leaves, torn 3 tbsp celery seed 3 tbsp dehydrated lemon peel 1 tbsp dehydrated lime peel 2 tbsp dried rosemary 3 tbsp dried thyme 3 tbsp dried oregano ¼ cup smoked paprika 1 tbsp freshly ground cinnamon Procedure Pulse everything in a spice grinder until relatively smooth. If your spice grinder doubles as your coffee grinder, make sure you pulse some rice after. Keep in an airtight container with a shaker lid for up to 1 year.
