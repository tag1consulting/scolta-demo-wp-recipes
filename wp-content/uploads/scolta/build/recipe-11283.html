Albanian Stewed Fig Pudding

Stewed dry figs (hoshaf me fiq të thatë) is an Albanian pudding. Ingredients 1 cup (240 g) white granulated sugar 4 cups (900 ml) milk 1 cup (240 g) finely-grated dried figs Ground cinnamon Procedure Stir sugar into milk, bring to a boil, and remove from heat. Gradually add figs, stirring continuously. Pour into earthenware serving bowls and bake in moderate oven (350°F or 180°C) for 10 minutes. Chill and sprinkle with cinnamon. Serve cold.
