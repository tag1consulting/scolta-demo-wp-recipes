Seviyan Ji Khirni (Sindhi Vermicelli Pudding)

Ingredients 1 tbsp ghee 3 tbsp South Asian vermicelli (short, fine wheat noodles) 2 litres milk 10 cardamom pods 20 strands saffron 2 cups white granulated sugar 10 almonds Procedure Heat ghee in a skillet. Add vermicelli, and fry over low heat until golden brown. Remove from the heat. Bring the milk to a boil. Add vermicelli, cardamom, and saffron. Simmer until the vermicelli are tender and the milk is reduced to a little more than half the original volume. Mix in the sugar, and boil for 5–7 minutes. Serve, garnished with almonds.
