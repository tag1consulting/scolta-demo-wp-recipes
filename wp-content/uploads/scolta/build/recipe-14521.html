Strong Candied Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients 5 large sweet potatoes 2¼ cups brown sugar 5 cloves Zest of 1 orange 2 cups water ½ cup brandy 1 tsp nutmeg 1 stick cinnamon Procedure Slice the sweet potatoes and lay in a deep baking pan with the cinnamon stick. Mix the liquids, zest, cloves, brown sugar and nutmeg together in a pot; bring to a boil and cook for 5 minutes. Pour liquid mixture over the dish’s contents. Cover with foil and bake at 350°F for 30 minutes or until sweet potatoes appear to be tender and glazed. Chill for 3 hours, and serve cold.
