Karak Chai II

Karak chai is a type of chai tea popular in Southwest and South Asia. This recipe originates from the Youtube channel MATBAKH UK. Recipes 2 cups (480 ml) milk or non-dairy milk 7 cardamom pods 2 black tea bags or 2 teaspoons (10 ml) black tea 2½ tbsp (37.5 ml) sweetened condensed milk or cream of coconut Procedure Combine the milk, cardamom, and tea in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes. Stir in the condensed milk. Strain the mixture, discarding the solids.
