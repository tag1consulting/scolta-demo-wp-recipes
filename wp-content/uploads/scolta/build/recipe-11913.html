Chicken Pot Pie II

Ingredients 1 package pie crusts ⅓ cup margarine or butter ⅓ cup chopped onion ⅓ cup all-purpose flour ½ teaspoon salt ¼ teaspoon pepper 1 ½ cups chicken broth ⅔ cup milk 3 cups chicken, cooked 1 cup frozen corn, thawed 1 cup frozen peas, thawed Procedure Heat oven to 425°F. Prepare pie crusts as directed on package for a two-crust pie in a 9-inch pie pan. In a medium saucepan, melt margarine over medium heat. Add onion; cook 2 minutes or until tender. Stir in flour, salt, and pepper until well-blended. Gradually stir in broth and milk; cook, stirring constantly, until bubbly and thickened. Add chicken, corn, and peas; remove from heat. Spoon chicken mixture into crust-lined pan. Top with second crust and flute; cut slits in several places. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes or until crust is golden-brown. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
