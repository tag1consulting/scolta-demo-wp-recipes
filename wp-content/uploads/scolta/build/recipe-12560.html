Fudge Frosting

This is a rich non-greasy frosting that goes on hot and stiffens as it cools. Ingredients 5 cups (625 g / 1.38 lb) powdered sugar 3 oz (85 g) unsweetened baking chocolate 2 tablespoons margarine 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1 teaspoon corn syrup Procedure Melt the chocolate and margarine together in a double-boiler or, carefully, in a saucepan on low heat. Mix in the vanilla extract and corn syrup, keeping the mixture somewhat hot. Mix in the sugar, still keeping the mixture somewhat hot. You might not use the exact amount listed. Just keep adding sugar until the frosting is as thick as desired. While still hot, spread the mixture on cake or brownies. Mixture will stiffen. Notes, tips, and variations A very small amount of milk may be added if the mixture is unspreadable even while hot. Don’t add more than you truly need. Warnings Steaming hot cake will release moisture which may collect under a frosting. Cold cake may cause a frosting to stiffen prematurely. Thus, you should use a warm cake.
