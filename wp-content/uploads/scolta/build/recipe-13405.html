Miyan Shuwaka (Hausa Bitter Leaf Soup)

Miyan shuwaka is a bitterleaf soup eaten in the northern part of Nigeria. Ingredients 100 g bitterleaf (fresh or dried) 2 large chunks meat 1 onion, chopped 2 pieces fresh red chile pepper, puréed 2 tomatoes, puréed 1 teaspoon palm oil 4 cup fermented locust beans (daddawa) 1 teaspoon salt Procedure If using fresh bitter leaf, wash with salt and squeeze to reduce the bitterness. Boil the meat in a pot of water for a few minutes until cooked through. Remove the meat and set aside. Heat a skillet over medium heat, and add the palm oil. Add the onion, pepper, and tomatoes, and simmer for 5 minutes. Combine the cooked purée, meat, and bitterleaf in a pot. Add enough water to cover everything. Add the daddawa, stir the soup well, and cook for 3 more minutes over low heat. Season with salt to taste and serve.
