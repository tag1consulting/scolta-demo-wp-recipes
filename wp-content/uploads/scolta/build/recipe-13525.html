Nigerian Palm Oil Boiled Yam (Ji Mmanu)

Palm oil boiled yam, also known as ji mmanu, is a typical Igbo food. Ingredient Yam, peeled and diced Fresh palm oil Salt to taste Fresh chile pepper, blended to paste Utazi leaves, shredded Onion (optional) Procedure Cook the yam pieces in salted boiling water for a few minutes. Drain the yam pieces. Toss the yam with palm oil, salt, fresh pepper, and utazi leaves. Serve.
