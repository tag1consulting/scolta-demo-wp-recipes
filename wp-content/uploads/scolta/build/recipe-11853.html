Cheese Tart

Ingredients ½ cup Brie cheese, rind removed ¾ cup mascarpone cheese ½ cup white granulated sugar ½ cup unsalted butter, melted 1 pinch of salt ½ dough from Classic American Apple Pie, made with mascarpone cheese instead of applejack Procedure Mash together all ingredients except dough. Set aside. Roll out dough into a rough circle. Place dough in a 10-inch tart pan to make a tart shell, and remove the excess dough from the edges. Transfer the cheese mixture into tart shell and bake at 425°F on the bottom of the oven for 15 minutes. Move to the bottom rack and bake for 10 minutes more. Remove and let rest 10 minutes. Serve.
