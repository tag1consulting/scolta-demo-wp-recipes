Hot Sauce (Homemade)

This is somewhat an interpretive recipe, so you have to get a little creative. But, creativity is what spawned it, and no two people come up with exactly the same sauce. Regardless, what results will be very hot, and very delicious provided you like hot sauce. Ingredients 1–5 cloves of garlic, depending on size and taste Vinegar (any kind) Lime juice 1–3 squirts of honey About 10 habanero peppers, de-stemmed Salt Procedure Mix all ingredients except peppers and honey in blender until you think you’ve got enough liquid. It doesn’t matter how much of anything you use—just throw it all in. As a general rule, though, your total liquid volume should be about the size of 3–4 peppers. Make sure it covers up to the middle of the blender blades. Pulse everything until smooth. Add habaneros and pulse more, until they’re fairly chopped up but still chunky. Add honey, and blend for about 30 seconds. Your resulting sauce should be fairly thick, but contain only very small particles of peppers and garlic. If there are chunks bigger than grains of salt, keep blending until they are tiny.
