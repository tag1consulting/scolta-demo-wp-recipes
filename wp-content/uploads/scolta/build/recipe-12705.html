Grilled Portobello Mushrooms

Portobello mushrooms are large mushrooms that can be prepared as a vegetarian entrée. They can substitute for hamburger patties, or be eaten alone. Ingredients 1 large portobello mushroom per person Olive oil Salt (as desired) Procedure Remove stems from the mushrooms, then wash and dry them. Brush with olive oil. Grill for about 10 minutes, alternating sides, until both sides are grill-marked. Notes, tips, and variations When cooking on a barbecue, mixing the oil with a water based liquid (e.g. balsamic vinegar) will cause the fire to flare more and give the food more flavour; baste them as they cook.
