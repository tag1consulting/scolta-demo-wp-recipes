Apple Brown Betty

A brown betty is an American dessert of fruit and bread crumbs. This version uses apples. Ingredients 2 cups soft bread crumbs ½ cup white granulated sugar ¼ cup butter or margarine 1 teaspoon cinnamon 3 cups apples, sliced (they don’t need to be peeled) ¾ cup water Procedure Preheat oven to 375°F. Sauté bread crumbs in butter. Add apples, cinnamon, sugar, and water. If the skillet is oven-proof, cover and place in the oven. If the skillet is not oven-proof, transfer to a greased casserole dish. Cover and bake for 30 to 40 minutes, until apples are tender. If mixture becomes too dry during baking, add another ¼ cup of water. (Apples vary as to moisture content.) Notes, tips, and variations Serve with milk or whipped cream. If cooking in a Dutch oven, preheat with 15 charcoal briquettes, ⅓ on the bottom, and ⅔ on the top.
