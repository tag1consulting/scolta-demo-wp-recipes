Feijão Tropeiro (Brazilian Beans and Sausage)

Feijao tropeiro is a traditional side dish in many parts of Brazil. Ingredients 2 lbs pinto beans 2 lbs sausage of your choice (mild Italian is good in this dish) 2 cloves garlic 2 small yellow onions ½ pound slab bacon 4 hard boiled eggs 1 bunch green onions, chopped 1 bunch collard greens 2 tbsp olive oil Procedure Prepare beans according to their package. Drain and set aside to cool. In large pan, cook bacon and sausage. Remove sausage and bacon. Slice sausage into 1 inch-thick pieces. In the pan with bacon grease, sauté white onion and garlic. Slice collard green into small (⅛-inch) strips. Mix all ingredients except hard-boiled eggs in large bowl using a wooden spoon. Drizzle with olive oil and stir gently. Garnish with sliced boiled eggs. Serve hot or cold.
