Sindhi Chickpea Confection

Ingredients 500 g gram flour 250 g ghee 150 g sugar 10–15 cashews, chopped 10–15 almonds, chopped 5–10 pistachios, chopped Procedure Heat the ghee in a pan. Add the gram flour, and stir until toasted. Remove from the heat, and stir in the sugar. Return to the heat, and stir until the sugar melts. Transfer mixture to a bowl, and garnish with nuts. Serve hot or cold.
