Brown Sauce (Hartshorne)

Brown Sauce is a tangy, slightly sweet sauce accompanying cooked breakfasts and chips (fries). This recipe is an interpreted method from the original recipe in Henry Hartshorne’s the Household Cyclopedia. Ingredients 1 tbsp sugar 2 tbsp French vinegar 3 tbsp olive oil 1 tbsp mustard 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp pepper Procedure Mix together the sugar, vinegar, olive oil, mustard, salt, and pepper. Serve or store in a bottle. References This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne.
