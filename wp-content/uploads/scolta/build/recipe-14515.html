Strawberry Pie

Ingredients Pillsbury pie crust 1 cup sliced strawberries ¼ cup water ⅔ cup white granulated sugar 1 tbsp lemon juice Whipped cream Procedure Place your strawberries in a saucepan and add water, sugar, and lemon juice. Bring to a boil and stir occasionally until thick and syrupy. Arrange the pie crust in a pie pan (unless the pie crust comes with a pan). Pour filling into the pie crust. Bake in the oven at 350°F for 60 minutes or until golden brown. Remove pie, and let it cool. Serve with whipped cream on top.
