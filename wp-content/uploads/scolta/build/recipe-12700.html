Grilled Peaches with Vanilla Maple Sauce

Ingredients 4 ripe yellow peaches, halved and pitted Granulated sugar ½ cup maple syrup 1 Tbsp pure vanilla extract ¼ cup unsalted butter, melted 1 pinch of salt ¼ cup peach nectar Procedure Combine all ingredients except peaches and sugar. Bring to a boil over high heat in a large saucepan, and simmer until reduced by half. Dip cut side of peaches into hot mixture. Sprinkle cut side liberally with sugar. Place peaches cut-side down on a high-heat grill until marked and slightly charred. Serve warm with remaining sauce.
