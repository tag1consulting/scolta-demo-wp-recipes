Rock Cookies

Ingredients 4 cups flour ½ tsp salt 1 tsp baking powder ¾ cup butter 1 cup granulated sugar ¼ pound dried currants ¼ tsp mace Zest of half a lemon 3 eggs, well beaten Candied cherries Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. Sift flour, salt, and baking powder; rub in butter. Add sugar, currants, mace, and lemon zest. Stir in eggs. Form into rough balls about 1 inch in diameter. If balls aren’t holding together well, sprinkle with water. Garnish with ⅛ of a candied cherry. Bake for 9 minutes at 350°F. Cool.
