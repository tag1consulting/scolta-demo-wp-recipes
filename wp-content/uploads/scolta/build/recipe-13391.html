Mint Tea

There are two ways to make this mint infusion. You can either use dried up mint leaves or fresh mint leaves. Ingredients Dried or fresh mint leaves Water Equipment Kettle Teapot Procedure Boil the water in a kettle. Pour the boiling water into the teapot. Put the mint leaves in the teapot. Let steep for several minutes. Pour some hot tea in a cup and dilute with additional water. Even if it starts to look transparent, the taste is still there.
