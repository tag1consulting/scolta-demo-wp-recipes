Rice with Coconut (Arroz con Coco)

Ingredients 1 small jar (4.3 oz / 125 g) concentrado de coco or 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 fl oz) fresh coconut milk 4 cups (1 L / 1.1 qt) water 2 cups (200 g / 7.1 oz) rice 2 tsp salt 2–3 tbsp sugar Raisins to taste Procedure Combine everything in a pot. Cover, bring to a simmer, and reduce to very low heat. Cook until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender. Served with a half of avocado and grilled meats of all varieties. Notes, tips, and variations Some recipes add coconut browned in oil to the mix.
