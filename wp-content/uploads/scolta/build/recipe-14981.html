Zucchini Pasta Bake

Ingredients 400 g any small pasta 250 g cheese, grated 2 eggs 200 ml milk 50 g butter 3 medium zucchini, grated Vegetable stock base Salt Pepper Procedure Cook the pasta as directed on the packaging. Drain. Combine the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper in a mixing bowl. Mix in the grated cheese. Melt the butter and add to the mixture. Boil a small amount of water and add the stock, then stir it into the mixture. Stir in the grated zucchini, then the drained pasta. Grease a baking or casserole dish, and spread the ingredients from the bowl evenly in it. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for about 30 minutes, or until the pasta bake is firm all the way through.
