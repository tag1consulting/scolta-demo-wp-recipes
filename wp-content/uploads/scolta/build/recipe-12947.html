Italian Chicken Rolls

Ingredients 2 ounces ricotta cheese 2 ounces cream cheese 1 cup grated mozzarella cheese, divided 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut in half Salt Freshly-ground black pepper 1 cup Italian Tomato Sauce Procedure Combine ricotta, cream cheese, and ½ cup mozzarella cheese. Set aside. Place chicken on a sheet of plastic wrap and cover with another. Pound out to ¼ inch thickness. Sprinkle one side of chicken liberally with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Spread unseasoned side with cheese mixture and roll up. Place seam-side down in a greased 13×9-inch (or smaller) baking dish. Pour sauce over and bake in a 425°F oven 20 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Heat broiler. Sprinkle chicken with cheese and place under broiler until cheese is melted. Remove and serve warm.
