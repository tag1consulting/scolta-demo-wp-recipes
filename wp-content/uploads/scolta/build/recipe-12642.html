Glazed Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients ½ cup (120 ml) maple-flavored syrup 1 tablespoon butter or margarine 1 can (40 oz / 1100 g) sweet potatoes, drained Pecan halves (optional) Procedure Bring syrup and butter just to boil in large skillet on medium heat. Reduce heat to low, and simmer about 2 minutes. Add sweet potatoes. Cook on low heat 10 minutes, gently turning sweet potatoes frequently to glaze. Garnish with pecan halves, if desired.
