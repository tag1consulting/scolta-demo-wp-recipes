Meat Masala

Meat masala is a spice mix used in Keralan cuisine, typically as a marinade for various types of meat, especially mutton. Meat masala can either be rubbed onto pieces of meat as a dry powder mix, or combined with oil to make a paste that more easily coats the meat. Once coated, the meat should be left to marinate for at least 20 minutes, but preferably up to 2 hours, before cooking. Ingredients 50 g whole dried chillies 100 g coriander seed 2 tbsp black peppercorn 1 tsp cumin seed 1 tsp mustard seed 1 tsp turmeric powder 12 cloves ¼ tsp cardamom seed 2 short pieces of cinnamon Procedure Toast the chillies, coriander, peppercorn, cumin, and mustard in a frying pan. Grind all ingredients to a powder. Store in a glass jar with a tight-fitting lid.
