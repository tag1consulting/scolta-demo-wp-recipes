Chocolate Chocolate Chip Muffins

A deliciously sweet muffin recipe that makes an excellent breakfast. Ingredients 1 ¾ cup (250 g / 8.8 oz) all-purpose flour 1 cup (80 g / 2.8 oz) chocolate chips ¾ cup (175 g / 6.2 oz) granulated sugar ⅓ cup (40 g / 1.4 oz) cocoa, sifted if lumpy 1 tablespoon baking powder ½ teaspoon salt 1 egg 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 oz) milk ⅓ cup (80 ml / 2.7 fl oz) cooking oil 1 teaspoon vanilla Procedure Combine all-purpose flour, chocolate chips, sugar, cocoa, salt, and baking powder in a mixing bowl. Create a well in the center. Beat egg in a small bowl. Add milk, cooking oil, and vanilla. Stir and pour into well. Stir until just moistened. Grease 12 muffin cups with cooking spray. Fill cups ¾ full. Bake at 400°F (205°C) for 20 minutes until a skewer inserted into the center tests clean. Notes, tips, and variations These taste best when still warm. If you don’t have cooking spray, use paper liners. Try adding white chocolate shavings on top, or even white chocolate chips.
