Buffalo Wings

Ingredients 48 chicken wingettes Oil for deep frying Poultry Shake, as needed ½ cup unsalted butter, melted ½ cup Smoky Chipotle Hot Sauce ¼ cup tomato paste 1 tbsp soy sauce Chili leaves, optional Procedure Sprinkle chicken with the Poultry Shake. Heat the oil to 350 °F (180°C). Add the chicken pieces to the hot oil, and fry for 11 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack. Meanwhile, combine remaining ingredients. Add wings and toss to coat and serve. Garnish with the chili leaves, if desired.
