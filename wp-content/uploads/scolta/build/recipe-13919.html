Pulse Chutney

Pulse chutney is an vegan Indian recipe. Use fewer chili peppers if you do not want it so hot. Ingredients 2 oz (50 g) green mung beans 1 ½ inch (3 ½ cm) piece of ginger root 4 green chile peppers Salt Lemon juice Procedure Soak the green mung beans for 3–4 hours. Allow to sprout. Mince the green chilies, fresh ginger, and salt. Grind to a paste in a mortar and pestle or food processor. Season with lemon juice to taste.
