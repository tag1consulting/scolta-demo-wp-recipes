Chunky Guacamole

Ingredients 3 ripe avocados 2 tablespoons lime juice 1 cup of salsa fresca Procedure Peel and dice avocados. Sprinkle the avocados with lime juice. Add salsa fresca, and toss to mix. Notes, tips, and variations If you don’t plan on serving immediately, place an avocado pit in the bowl to prevent browning, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and store in refrigerator.
