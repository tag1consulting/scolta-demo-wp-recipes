Honey BBQ Hot Wings

Like BBQ and like hot wings? This combines both that in one dish! Ingredients 24 chicken wingettes Salt Black pepper ½ cup tomato paste 2 Tbsp honey 2 Tbsp cider vinegar 2 Tbsp hot sauce Oil for deep frying Procedure Sprinkle wings with salt and black pepper. Heat oil to 350°F (175°C). Deep fry wings in batches for 11–12 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack. Combine liquid ingredients except for oil. Bring to a boil over medium high heat until reduced to 10 Tbsp. Remove from heat and let rest for 5 minutes. Toss wings with sauce; serve warm.
