Firecracker Grilled Shrimp

Ingredients 36 peeled and deveined shrimp (21/25 count) ½ recipe Firecracker Marinade Special equipment 9 bamboo skewers, soaked in water for 30 minutes Procedure Pour marinade into a gallon size zip-top bag. Add shrimp and toss to coat. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. Drain shrimp and discard remaining marinade. Thread 4 shrimp onto each skewer. Preheat your charcoal grill using a chimney starter, not lighter fluid to medium high. Reapply the grate and add shrimp. Cook, turning often, until shrimp is pink and slightly charred. Remove shrimp from skewers and discard skewers. Serve warm.
