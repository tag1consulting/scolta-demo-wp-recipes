Yam and Egg Sauce

Yam and egg sauce is a dish eaten mostly as breakfast Ingredients Yam Tomatoes Peppers Egg Salt Groundnut oil Equipment Frying pan Pot Cooking spoon Bowl Knife Blender Procedure Peel and slice the yam. Boil yam in water until soft, then drain. Blend the tomatoes and peppers together. Whisk together with the egg and a pinch of salt. Cook the egg mixture in a hot pan with a little oil. Serve the eggs with the yam.
