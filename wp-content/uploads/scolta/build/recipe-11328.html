Apple BBQ Sauce

This barbecue sauce is great on ribs and chicken on the grill. Ingredients 4 cups ketchup ½ cup blackstrap molasses ¾ cup apple jelly ¾ cup apple cider vinegar 2 tbsp chipotle-based hot sauce Procedure Combine all ingredients in a large saucepan and bring to a boil over medium high heat. Cook until reduced to 4 cups. Keep refrigerated for up to 3 weeks in a large mason jar or a squirt bottle.
