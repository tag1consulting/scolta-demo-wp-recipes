Zucchini Salad

Ingredients Salad 1 bowl of zucchini, washed and sliced 1 bowl of lettuce, washed 1 cup of cherry tomatoes, washed and sliced Dressing 2 lemons, juiced 2 blood oranges, juiced 1 orange, juiced Procedure Combine the lemon juice, blood orange juice, and orange juice to make the dressing. Combine everything in a large bowl, and toss well.
