Pudina Hilsa (Bengali Fish with Mint)

Pudina hilsa is a wonderful Bengali preparation of hilsa fish. It is very easy and can be served hot with boiled rice. Ingredients 1 kg hilsa fish (kelee shad), cut into 10 pieces 1 tsp pudina leaves (mint) 1 tsp ginger paste 1 tsp nutmeg 50 g butter, chopped 3 tbsp capsicum Salt according to taste 1 pinch of sugar 1 tsp green chili paste Procedure Wash the fish thoroughly in water. Put the salt, sugar, 25 g of butter and all the other ingredients into a baking dish. Add the fish pieces and sprinkle the rest of the butter on the top. Bake at 200°C until the fish turns golden in color or otherwise appears to be done.
