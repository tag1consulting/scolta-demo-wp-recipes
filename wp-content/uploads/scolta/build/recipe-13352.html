Mexican Rice II

Ingredients 1 cup uncooked white rice 1 fresh red chile, seeded and finely diced (or 1 dehydrated chile that has been rehydrated) 5 tbsp Ancho Enchilada Sauce, strained through a medium mesh 1 ⅔ cups chicken broth 4 cloves of garlic, peeled and crushed ½ small onion, peeled and finely diced 1 tablespoon oil or lard Procedure Sauté garlic, onions, and red chile in oil over medium heat for about 1 minute. Add in the rice and stir frequently until rice begins to brown. Mix chicken stock and chile sauce in another saucepan and bring to a boil. When mixed, make sure liquid mixture equals out to 2 cups of liquid. Add chicken stock and chile sauce mixture to rice and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and bring rice to slow simmer. Cook for 25–30 minutes with the lid on. Remove lid and fluff rice with a fork.
