Maffé (West African Peanut Stew) III

Maafe is a stew made from groundnuts. It originated from the Madinkwa and Bambara people of Mali. Ingredients 2 lbs stew beef ½ cup peanut butter 1 cup water 1 teaspoon black pepper 1 teaspoon salt 2 tablespoons tomato paste 1 onion Garlic (optional) Equipment Blender Pots Bowl Frying pan Procedure Boil meat in a pot with salt and onion. Fry the meat in a pan of oil. Blend together the tomato, pepper, and garlic until smooth. Pour the blended mixture into a pot and add the peanut paste. Stir until thick. Notes, tips, and variations Maafe is usually eaten with white rice, tuwo, fonio, fufu and sweet potatoes It is usually made with vegetables in Ivory Coast, and the stew is cooked with boiled egg in Ghana. When using beef to make maafe, use chuck, short ribs, or oxtail.
