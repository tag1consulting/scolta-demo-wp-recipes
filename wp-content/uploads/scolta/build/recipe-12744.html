Halva (Wheat-based)

Ingredients 1 cup all-purpose flour 1 cup butter or margarine 1¼ cup granulated sugar 5 cups water Procedure Heat the margarine very gently in a saucepan. Add the flour, and cook for 40 minutes over low to moderate heat until golden brown. When the flour turns light golden, combine the sugar and water in a separate saucepan. Boil gently to make a syrup. Add the syrup to the saucepan with flour, and simmer for 20 minutes. Scoop out small portions of halva with a wooden spoon, form into balls, and place on a serving plate. Serve warm.
