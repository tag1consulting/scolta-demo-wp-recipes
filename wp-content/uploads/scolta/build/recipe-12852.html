Honey Mustard Salad Dressing

There are many variations of this salad dressing. This should serve as a basic template. Ingredients 1 tablespoon (15 ml) Dijon-style mustard 2 tablespoons (30 ml) honey 3 tablespoons (45 ml) lemon juice ½ teaspoon (7.5 ml) salt ½ teaspoon (7.5 ml) freshly-ground black pepper ¾ cup 250 ml safflower oil Procedure Combine the mustard, honey, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Gradually whisk the safflower oil into the mixture to form a creamy dressing. Notes, tips, and variations When whisking in a bowl on the counter, put a towel under it to keep it from spinning around.
