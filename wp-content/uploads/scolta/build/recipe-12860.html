Horchata

is a Mexican almond rice-water beverage. Ingredients 1 lb uncooked rice 1 lb almonds, blanched and peeled 5 ea. cinnamon sticks 12 cups hot water 20 cups cold water 15 slivers lime peel, 2 inches in length 5 cups white granulated sugar Procedure Soaking Thoroughly pulverize the rice in a blender. Transfer to a medium size bowl and add the almonds, cinnamon stick, and lime peel. Stir in 12 cups of hot water. Cover and let stand at least 6 hours or overnight. Blending Place the mixture in the blender and blend for 3–4 minutes, until it no longer feels gritty. Add 10 cups cold water and blend until combined. Strain a little at a time using a chinois or strainer lined with cheesecloth. Finishing Add 10 cups of cold water and stir in enough sugar to sweeten the drink to taste. If the consistency is too thick, add additional water. Cover and refrigerate. Stir before pouring.
