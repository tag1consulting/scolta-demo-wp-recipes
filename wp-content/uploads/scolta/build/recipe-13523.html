Nigerian Noodles

Nigeria has a variety of packaged noodle brands and numerous ways to prepare them. Brands include Indomie, Golden penny, Minimie, Mimee, Tummy Tummy, etc. Ingredients Base Noodles Seasoning cubes Pepper Water Vegetable Optional additions Egg Roast chicken Sauce Beef Onion bulb Green bell peppers Tomatoes Oil Equipment Pot Stove Knife (optional) Spoon Colander or strainer Procedure Method I Toast the noodles in a dry pan. Add some boiling water, along with seasoning and pepper. Cook, stirring, until the water is fully absorbed and the noodles are tender. Serve with the optional additions. Use a timer to avoid overcooking Don’t overcrowd the pot Method II Bring water to a boil in a pot with a dash of oil. Add the noodles, and cook until the noodles are tender. Drain the noodles, and serve with the optional additions.
