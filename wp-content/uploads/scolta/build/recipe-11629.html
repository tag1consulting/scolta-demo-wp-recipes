Boiled Chicken with Broth

Ingredients 1 whole tender chicken, cleaned 3 quarts water 2 tbsp rice or pearl barley Salt to taste Fresh parsley Clarified butter, melted Procedure Cut the chicken into quarters, and place in a large pot with the water, rice, and salt. Bring pot to a slow boil, removing any scum that rises to the surface. Cook until the chicken is thoroughly done. Remove the chicken and place in a serving dish. Garnish with parsley. Strain the broth. Serve the chicken with clarified butter and the hot broth on the side. — The information in this module was taken from the public domain 1881 Household Cyclopedia and modernized by Wikibooks editors.
