Nigerian Corned Beef Sauce

This corned beef sauce is also known as spaghetti sauce in Nigeria, but you can also use it for boiled rice, boiled yam, and boiled potatoes. Ingredients Vegetable oil 2 medium onions, diced 6–10 firm tomatoes, diced 8–10 fresh habanero peppers, diced 3 bell peppers (optional), diced 1 tablespoon minced garlic 1 tablespoon minced ginger 740 g canned corned beef, minced Salt to taste Seasoning/stock cube Procedure Heat the vegetable oil in a saucepan. Add the onions, tomatoes, peppers, garlic, and ginger. Stir, and fry for about 10 minutes. Mix in the corned beef, and fry for about 10 minutes. Add salt and seasoning cube to taste. Stir, then cover and cook for a few minutes. Adjust seasoning as needed, and serve.
