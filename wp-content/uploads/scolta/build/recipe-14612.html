Tabbouleh VI

Ingredients 1 cup (240 ml) bulgur wheat 2 cups (480 ml) fresh parsley, finely chopped 1 large tomato, diced with seeds discarded 1 cup (240 ml) green onions, chopped ⅓ cup (80 ml) extra virgin olive oil ½ cup (120 ml) fresh lemon juice 1 pinch of salt Procedure Boil 2–3 cups of water Once water comes to a boil, add bulgur wheat and remove from heat. Let cool. Drain the bulgur wheat, and mix wheat with all other ingredients. Refrigerate.
