Scribbled Eggs

This recipe was found in Githima, a small town outside the city of Nakuru in Kenya. If you ask for fried eggs in the West of Kenya, you’ll probably have the eggs scribbled. Ingredients Oil Tomatoes (optional), chopped Scallion (optional), sliced 2 eggs Procedure Heat a small amount of oil in a skillet over medium heat. If using tomatoes, add them to the pan. Fry for a minute. If using scallion, add to tomatoes. Cook for another minute. Beat the eggs well, and pour into the pan. Start stirring the eggs—Kenyans call this scribbling. Cook like this until the egg is done.
