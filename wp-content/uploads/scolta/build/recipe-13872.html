Potato Pie

Ingredients 1 lb potatoes ¾ ounce chopped onion Salt Pepper 2 ounces butter, chopped Puff pastry, rolled out ⅛ inch thick Procedure Peel and slice your potatoes very thin. Layer the potatoes in a pie dish, adding some onion, salt, and pepper between each layer. Add a dash of water to the potatoes, and scatter the chopped butter over top. Cover the filling with the rolled-out puff pastry. Bake at 375°F until golden and cooked through (about 1½ hours).
