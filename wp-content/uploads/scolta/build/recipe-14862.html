Very Simple Cookies

Here are very simple and very delicious cookies! Ingredients 1 cup white granulated sugar 2 sticks (1 cup, 8 ounces, or 225 g) butter, melted 3 cups flour Chocolate chips (optional) Oats (optional) Procedure Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Combine the sugar and melted butter. Mix in the flour until you have a thick dough. Mix in the chocolate chips and oats, if using. Shape the dough into your desired cookie shapes. Transfer to a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet. Put the cookies in the oven. Bake until the cookies are a golden colour and are hard (probably 8–12 minutes, depending on size), and then take them out.
