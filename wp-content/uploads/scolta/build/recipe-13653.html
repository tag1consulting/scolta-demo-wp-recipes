Palatschinken with Sweetened Fruit

Ingredients Crepes ½ cup (120 ml) flour 1 egg 1 tsp granulated sugar 3 tbsp melted butter ⅔ cup (160 ml) milk 1 pinch of salt Filling 2 cups (480 ml) chopped fruit or whole berries of your choice 2 tsp vanilla extract ½ cup (120 ml) granulated sugar 1 tbsp lemon juice 4 tbsp orange juice Garnish Powdered sugar Cinnamon Procedure Crepes Mix all ingredients together in a large mixing bowl to make a batter. Strain the batter to get rid of any lumps, then chill in the refrigerator for 2 hours. Brush a 12 inch (30 cm) frying pan with butter. Ladle batter onto pan as if making crepes, leaving space for spreading. Cook for 1 minute on each side. Filling Combine orange juice, lemon juice, sugar, and vanilla extract in medium saucepan. Bring to boil and add fruit. Put 2 tbsp fruit mixture on each pancake, and fold over to enclose the filling. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and cinnamon.
