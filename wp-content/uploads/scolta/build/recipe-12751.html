Hamburger Skillet Dinner

The original contributor of this recipe provided the following anecdote: > “This was the first thing I could cook. I learned to make it staying with a friend as I was leaving the Navy. For years I have called it ‘Keith’s Whats-it.'” Ingredients 1 cup uncooked pasta, such as macaroni, elbows, or wagon wheels 1 pound (about 500 g) ground beef, crumbled a sprinkle of garlic salt a sprinkle of minced onion 1–2 cans condensed tomato soup Procedure Boil pasta in 4–6 cups salted, oiled water until al dente. Drain. While pasta is cooking, sprinkle a 10-inch (25 cm) skillet with garlic salt and minced onion, and brown beef. Add tomato soup and cook a minute or two. Stir in drained pasta. Notes, tips and variations May add beans, vary spices, add onions; anything you like! Makes a decent chili base with chili powder added. Good in leftovers, if that is possible.
