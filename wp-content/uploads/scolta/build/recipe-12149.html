Country Roast Chicken

Ingredients 1 ea. (3 lbs) broiler/fryer chicken, giblets removed ¼ cup Country Roast Chicken Seasoning 3 sprigs rosemary 8 sprigs thyme 1 large lemon, sliced thinly 1 large lemon, cut in wedges 2 tbsp butter Procedure Loosen skin from chicken. Rub flesh with chicken seasoning. Place lemon slices in the bottom of a roasting pan and place chicken on top. Stuff chicken cavity with thyme, rosemary, and lemon wedges. Place 1 tbsp butter under the skin of each breast. Insert an oven-safe probe thermometer into the breast but not touching the butter. Bake in a 325°F oven until breast internal temperature reaches 165°F and thigh meat reaches 170°F. Cover with foil and let rest 10 minutes; serve.
