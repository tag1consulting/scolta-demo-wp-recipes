Pork Pot Pie

Ingredients 2 Pillsbury pie crusts ⅓ cup margarine (or butter) ⅓ cup onion, chopped ⅓ cup all-purpose flour ½ teaspoon salt ¼ teaspoon pepper 1½ cups pork broth ⅔ cup milk 3 cups pork, cooked 1 cup frozen corn, thawed 1 cup frozen peas, thawed Procedure Heat oven to 425°F. Prepare pie crusts as directed on package for two-crust pie using 9-inch pie pan. In a medium saucepan, melt margarine over medium heat. Add onion; cook 2 minutes or until tender. Stir in flour, salt, and pepper until well-blended. Gradually stir in broth and milk; cook, stirring constantly, until bubbly and thickened. Add pork, corn, and peas; remove from heat. Spoon pork mixture into crust-lined pan. Top with second crust and flute; cut three slits on top. Bake for 30–40 minutes or until crust is golden-brown. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
