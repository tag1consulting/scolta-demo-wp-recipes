Parson&#8217;s Sauce (Hartshorne)

Parson’s sauce is a light sauce that can be used for a number of chicken and vegetable dishes. This recipe is an interpreted method from the original recipe in Henry Hartshorne’s the Household Cyclopedia. Ingredients 1 lemon, zest removed 2–3 pickled cucumbers 2 tbsp butter 1 tsp salt 1 tsp pepper 20 g plain flour 2 tsp catsup (ketchup) Procedure Chop lemon-peel and pickled cucumbers very fine. Mix in all remaining ingredients. Transfer to a saucepan, and simmer for 10–15 minutes. Use or serve. References
