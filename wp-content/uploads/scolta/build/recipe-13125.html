Leaf Masala Curry

Ingredients 200 g onions, chopped Oil 20 g tomatoes, chopped 1 tsp minced garlic 1 tsp minced ginger Meat (chicken or beef), cut into pieces Vegetables 2 tbsp leaf masala 2–3 cups warm water Procedure Fry onions in a dash of oil until golden brown. Add the tomatoes, and cook. Stir in garlic, ginger, meat, and vegetables. Cook until nearly done. Stir in leaf masala and warm water. Bring to a boil, then simmer until done. Serve.
