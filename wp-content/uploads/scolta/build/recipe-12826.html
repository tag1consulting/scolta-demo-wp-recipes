Homemade Orange Juice

Ingredients Oranges Sugar Water Special equipment Blender Strainer Procedures With a knife, remove the peel and skin from the oranges. Using a tiny knife, slice the orange in half and remove all of the seeds. Place it in the blender after further cutting it into smaller pieces. To the blender, add 1 tablespoon of sugar and either ice or water. Pulse several times very smooth. Strain the mixture. Once ready, pour the filtrated orange juice into a glass cup. Serve cold.
