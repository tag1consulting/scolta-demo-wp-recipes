Khus Khus Halwa

Ingredients 1 medium-sized mango, peeled and fleshed removed 1 tablespoon sugar 1½ cups milk 2 drops vanilla extract (optional) 1 pinch of cinnamon powder 1 tablespoon maida (all-purpose flour) 2 bread slices Procedure Blend together the mango flesh, sugar, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon powder until smooth. Add a bit of maida to the batter and whisk it well. Dip the bread slices in the batter and fry them in a oiled pan until brown on both sides. Serve hot.
