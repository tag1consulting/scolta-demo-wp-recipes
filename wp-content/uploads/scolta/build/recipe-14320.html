Smoky Chipotle Glazed Hot Wings

Ingredients 24 chicken wingettes 2 tsp soy sauce 6 tbsp Smoky Chipotle Hot Sauce 6 tbsp butter, melted 3 cloves garlic, minced Oil for deep frying Procedure Heat oil to 350°F. Fry wings in batches for 12 minutes each batch. Drain on a rack. Combine remaining ingredients, add wings, and toss to coat. Serve.
