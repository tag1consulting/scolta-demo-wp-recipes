Beef Stew II

Ingredients 60 g (2 oz) flour Salt to taste Pepper to taste Garlic powder to taste 50 ml (3 Tablespoons) olive oil 1 medium onion, peeled and chopped 500 g (1 lb) beef suitable for soup/stew, cut into cubes and trimmed of fat and gristle 1 L (1 qt) beef broth 200–250 g (approx. ½ lb) potatoes, diced 100–125 g (approx. ¼ lb) frozen carrots, sliced 100–125 g (approx. ¼ lb) frozen mushrooms, sliced 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) diced tomatoes Marjoram to taste 1 large or 2 small bay leaves Procedure Mix the flour, salt, pepper, and garlic powder together in a small bowl. Heat a large saucepan on the stovetop, then add olive oil and chopped onion. Dredge the beef cubes in the flour mixture and drop them into the saucepan; reserve the remaining flour mixture. Brown the beef on all sides. Pour in the broth and add the remaining flour mixture, the potatoes, carrots, mushrooms, tomatoes, marjoram and bay leaf/leaves. Once it has come to a boil, reduce the heat to the lowest setting, cover, and simmer for an hour or more, until the beef and potatoes are tender. See also Cookbook:Beef Stew
