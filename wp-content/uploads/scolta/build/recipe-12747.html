Ham Sauce (Modern)

Ham sauce (in Italian, Salsa di Prosciutto) is a relatively quick and simple sauce used to coat pasta or served over rice. It should not be confused for a glaze for baked ham—rather, it is a sauce on its own that incorporates ham. Ingredients 3–4 oz ham or prosciutto, cut into strips 1 tsp tomato purée 1 oz butter 2 cups vegetable or meat stock Procedure Melt butter in small saucepan, and gently sauté the strips of meat. Stir in tomato purée. Add stock and simmer for about 5 minutes. Serve over rice or toss with pasta.
