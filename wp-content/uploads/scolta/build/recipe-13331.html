Medu Vada (South Indian Savory Lentil Donut)

A vada is a savoury South Indian doughnut made from lentils (or sometimes potatoes). This is a recipe for medu vada. It is commonly accompanied by idli. Ingredients 1 cup (240 g) skinless black gram Salt to taste ¼ tsp asafoetida 8–10 curry leaves 1 tsp cumin powder 1 tsp crushed black peppercorns Oil to fry Procedure Wash and soak urad dal for 6 hours. Grind into a fine paste. Add salt, asafoetida, curry leaves, cumin powder, and crushed peppercorns to the batter and mix well. Heat oil in a kadai. Wet your palms and take batter into the palms. Shape into balls and make a hole with the thumb in the centre like a doughnut. Deep-fry this in medium-hot oil until golden brown and crisp. Serve hot with sambhar and coconut chutney.
