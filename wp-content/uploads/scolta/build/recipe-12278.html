Dill Cucumber Salad

Ingredients ½ cup reduced-fat sour cream 2 Tbsp cider vinegar 1 tsp sugar ¾ tsp garlic powder ¾ tsp dill weed ½ tsp salt 3 medium cucumber, sliced ½ cup sliced onion Procedure Combine sour cream, vinegar, sugar, garlic powder, dill, and salt. Add cucumbers and onion, and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Serve with slotted spoon.
