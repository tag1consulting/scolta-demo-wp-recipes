Mexican Tequila Marinade

Ingredients ½ cup tequila 3 tbsp mesquite honey ¼ tsp liquid mesquite smoke ¼ cup freshly-squeezed lime juice 1 habanero chile, puréed until smooth 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp freshly-ground black pepper Procedure Combine all ingredients in an 8-ounce mason jar. Keep refrigerated for up to 2 weeks. Shake well before using.
