Creamy Mascarpone Dessert

In Italy they will just mix creamy light cheeses like mascarpone with just a bare bit of sugar, egg yolk custard, sweet spices, citrus zest, and chocolate. If you have ever tried one of these cheese desserts, you’ll know that they’re very good. Ingredients 4 egg yolks, beaten ¼ cup granulated sugar 2 cups mascarpone cheese 2 tbsp heavy cream 2 tsp vanilla Chocolate syrup Crushed vanilla cookies and semi-sweet chocolate curls for topping Barely-sweetened whipped cream Cocoa powder Procedure Cream mixture Whisk yolks and sugar in a stainless steel bowl until light and creamy. Place the bowl on top of a pot of 1 inch simmering water to create a double boiler. Whisk yolks non-stop, until warmed, thickened, and falling off the whisk in ribbons. Set aside until needed. Fold together egg yolks, mascarpone, cream, and vanilla. Serving Pour a small amount of chocolate syrup into the bottom of a glass dish. Fill with cheese mixture. Top with crushed cookies and chocolate curls. Refrigerate before serving. Place a serving portion into each plate. Smooth out into a small rectangle. Place a dollop of whipped cream on top, and dust the whole thing with cocoa powder.
