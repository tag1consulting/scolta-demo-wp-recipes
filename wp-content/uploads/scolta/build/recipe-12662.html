Granola

Ingredients ½ cup chopped Castilla walnuts ½ cup chopped Indian walnuts ½ cup of chopped almonds ½ cup brown sugar ½ cup wheat germ ¾ cup olive oil 1½ cup honey 1 teaspoon salt 4 cups rolled oats ½ cup raisins ½ cup blueberries Procedure Combine all ingredients except the raisins and blueberries. Spread the mixture on a tray and bake for 1 hour at 250°F, stirring every 10 minutes. Let cool, then mix in the raisins and blueberries.
