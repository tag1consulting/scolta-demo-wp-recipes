Macaroni and Cheese II

Ingredients 8 oz elbow macaroni Salt to taste ½ cup milk ½ cup cheese (of the chef’s preference), grated ¼ cup butter 1 egg dash of dried parsley and oregano Procedure Fill a pot with water, and add macaroni and salt. Bring to boil, stirring occasionally. While waiting for the pasta to cook, heat the milk in a pot. Stir the grated cheese into milk. This should create a basic cheese sauce. If too thin, add more grated cheese; if too thick, add more milk. When pasta becomes tender (chef’s discretion), drain. Melt the butter in a pot, and stir in the drained macaroni until pasta is completely coated. Crack the raw egg into the mixture, and stir until completely incorporated. Add cheese sauce and herbs. Stir well, and serve hot.
