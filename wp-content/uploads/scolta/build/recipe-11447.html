Balsamic Marinade

Balsamic vinegar adds a tangy sweetness to balance the buttery flavor of extra-virgin olive oil and the heat of cayenne pepper. Ingredients 1 cup (250 ml/8.5 US fl oz) balsamic vinegar 1 tbsp salt 2 tbsp cayenne pepper 1 cup (250 ml/8.5 US fl oz) extra-virgin olive oil 1 tbsp freshly ground black pepper Procedure Combine all ingredients in a gallon size zip-top bag. Add whatever you’re marinating and marinate like you would normally.
