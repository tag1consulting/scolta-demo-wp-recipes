Thai Green Curry Paste

Ingredients 16 green piri-piri chilli-peppers, chopped 2 branches lemongrass, smashed 3 cloves garlic, pressed 3 scallions, chopped 1 tablespoon grated ginger root 1 tablespoon coriander seed 1 tablespoon caraway seed 4 whole cloves 1 tablespoon nutmeg 1 tablespoon trassi 3 tablespoons oil Procedure Grind the green chilli peppers, garlic, lemongrass, and scallions in a mortar. Add the remaining ingredients except for the oil, and keep grinding to a fluid paste. Mix in the oil. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 month. Notes, tips, and variations Alternatively, instead of using a mortar and pestle, you can add all ingredients to a food processor or blender and process until smooth. Thin with water or oil as necessary.
