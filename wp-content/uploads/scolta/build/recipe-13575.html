Oat Milk (Homemade)

Ingredients ½ cup oats 2 liters water Procedure Put oats in a blender. Blend the oats to the finest powder you can get Add as much of the water as possible to the blender. Blend some more. Strain the mixture into a jug, and add the rest of the water. Notes, tips, and variations Stir the milk before consuming it. This will keep in the refrigerator at least a week. The blended oat solids can be used to make porridge.
