Quaker Oat Swallow

Quaker oat swallow is a Nigerian swallow made from Quaker brand rolled oats. The oats are ground into powder form and cooked. Ingredients Water Rolled oats, ground to a powder Procedure Bring the water to a boil in a pot. Stir in the powdered oats until the mixture thickens. Continue stirring to prevent lumps. Add a very small amount of water, and cook for 5 minutes on low heat. Adding too much water will make the mixture too soft. Stir again to make sure the mixture is smooth. Remove from the heat and put in a wrapper. Serve with any soup of your choice.
