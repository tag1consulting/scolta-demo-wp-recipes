Ashwe Soup

Ashwe soup is a Nigerian soup prepared from ashwe (roselle) leaves. It is a quick-to-prepare Tiv soup. Ingredients 3 fresh chiles 6 medium tomatoes 1 medium onion, chopped 1 cup fresh Ashwe leaves 1 tablespoon Palm oil 0.3 kg Beef 1 teaspoon salt or to taste Procedure Blend pepper, tomatoes and onions together. Pluck and chop the ashwe leaves. Heat a small amount of palm oil in a pot. Add the blended mixture along with the ashwe leaves and beef. Add salt to taste and stir slowly. Cook for 5-6 minutes, then serve.
