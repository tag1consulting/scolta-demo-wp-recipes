Kansas City Barbecue Sauce

Ingredients 4 cups ketchup ¾ cup molasses ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce ¼ cup soy sauce ¼ cup cider vinegar 2 tbsp cayenne pepper Procedure Combine all ingredients in a large saucepan, and bring to a boil over high heat. Cook, stirring often for 15 minutes. Cool, and keep refrigerated in a nonreactive container for up to 2 months.
