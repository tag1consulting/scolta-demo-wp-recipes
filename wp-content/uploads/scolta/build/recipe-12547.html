Fruit Crumble

This is a generic recipe for fruit crumble. You may use a variety of fruits. Ingredients 85 g (3 oz) margarine or butter 170 g (6 oz) flour Fruit Spoonful sugar Procedure Rub together margarine and flour until crumbly. Add the fruit to an oven-safe bowl. Sprinkle sugar onto the fruit. Sprinkle a thick layer of the crumble over the fruit. Bake in the oven at 160°C (320°F) for 30 minutes. Notes, tips, and variations You can use a variety of fruits in this recipe, including but not limited to: Apples, peeled, cored, and cut in pieces Blackberries Raspberries Blueberries Alter the time and temperature to suit the oven. The time and temperature may also need adjusting for different sizes of fruit crumble. This recipe can be adapted for people with an allergy by substituting the ingredients for equivalents. For example, to make this suitable for people with milk or dairy allergy use a dairy-free margarine.
