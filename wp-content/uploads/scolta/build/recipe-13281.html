Marshmallow Rice Squares I

This recipe for quick marshmallow rice squares is super-simple and requires little knowledge of cooking! Ingredients 1 cup mini marshmallows 1 cup puffed cereal (e.g. Rice Krispies) Procedure Combine marshmallows and cereal in microwaveable bowl. Microwave for 1–2 minutes, depending on your machine’s power. Stir to coat the cereal with marshmallow. Press into a buttered baking pan or dish. Cut it into squares and allow it to cool and set.
