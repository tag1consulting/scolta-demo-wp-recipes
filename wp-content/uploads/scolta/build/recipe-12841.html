Hominy Bread

Ingredients 2 cups cooked hominy grits 1 cup cornmeal 1 tablespoon baking powder 1 scant teaspoon salt 2 tablespoons butter 2 standard eggs 1 ½ cups milk 3 tablespoons sugar (optional) Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). In a large bowl combine grits, cornmeal, baking powder, salt, butter, and sugar. In another bowl beat 2 eggs thoroughly, then add milk and mix together completely. Add egg and milk mixture to dry ingredients. Grease a large baking dish or 2 loaf pans. Pour mixture into pan(s). Bake in preheated oven for 35 minutes, until golden brown and a knife inserted in the bread comes out clean. Notes, tips, and variations This bread is best eaten warm for breakfast with butter or honey.
