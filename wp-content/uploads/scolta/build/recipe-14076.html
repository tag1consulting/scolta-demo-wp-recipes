Roasted Spiced Chicken Drumsticks

Ingredients 1½ teaspoon paprika 1 teaspoon onion powder ½ teaspoon chilli powder 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon olive oil 6 chicken drumsticks Equipment Oven Paper towels Baking dish Procedure Combine the paprika, onion powder, chili powder, and salt together in a bowl. Preheat oven to 400°F. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Coat the chicken with oil and the prepared spice blend, ensuring all sides are well-coated. Transfer to a baking dish. Bake the chicken drumsticks until the skin is brown and crispy (about 25 minutes). Serve hot.
