Queens Soup

Queen’s Soup, or Zuppa Regina di Riso, is a rice soup. One recipe is as follows. Ingredients 1 tablespoon ground rice ½ pint (285 ml) milk 1 oz (30 g) butter Fowl (poultry) stock Equipment Saucepan Bain-marie Sieve Procedure Put the ground rice into a saucepan. Gradually add the milk. Cook gently for 12 minutes in a bain-marie, stirring the whole time, until very smooth. Add the butter, and pass through a sieve. Mix the fowl stock, and serve immediately.
