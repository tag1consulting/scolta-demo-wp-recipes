Chicken Bog (Grand Strand)

Ingredients 2 ½ lb chicken pieces 1 cup chopped onion 1 ½ lb smoked sausage 1 tsp black pepper 2 cups long-grain rice Procedure Boil chicken and onions in 4 cups of water until tender. If desired, debone chicken. Slice sausage into ½-inch pieces. Add sausage, pepper and rice to chicken. Simmer until all broth is absorbed and rice is cooked.
