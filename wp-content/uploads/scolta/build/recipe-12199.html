Crispy Salmon

Ingredients 4 ea. (24 ounces) skinless wild salmon fillets, pin bones removed 1 cup panko breadcrumbs Olive oil 1 cup all-purpose flour 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp freshly-ground black pepper Freshly-squeezed lemon juice Procedure Combine flour, salt, and pepper in a pie pan, place panko in another pan, and place lemon juice in a large bowl. Using wet-hand dry-hand, dredge salmon into flour then into lemon, then into panko. Coat with olive oil and place on a sheet pan. Bake in the center of a 375°F oven for 27 minutes or until slightly underdone. Remove from oven, and heat up the broiler. Place salmon under broiler and cook until deeply browned. Serve immediately.
