Portable Eggs Benedict

This is a neat to-go version of our favorite poached egg dish. Ingredients 1 dash white vinegar 2 large eggs 1 pita 2 tbsp cream cheese Smoked salmon Procedure Boil enough water to fill a deep pan. Add vinegar to the water. Crack eggs into the boiling water/vinegar mixture. Leave them alone for 5–7 minutes. Cut open the top of a pita. Line the inside of the pita with cream cheese and smoked salmon. Take eggs out of the pan using a slotted spoon and drop them into the pita. Eat immediately.
