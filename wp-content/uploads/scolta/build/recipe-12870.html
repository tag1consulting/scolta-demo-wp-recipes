Hot Peach Drink

Ingredients 1 bottle (40 ounces) pure peach drink ¼ cup packed brown sugar 2 cinnamon sticks ½ cup peach schnapps (optional) Cinnamon sticks for garnish (optional) Procedure Combine juice, brown sugar, cinnamon sticks, and margarine in a Dutch Oven. Heat to boil. Remove from heat. Discard cinnamon sticks. Add schnapps, if desired. Garnish with additional cinnamon sticks, if desired.
