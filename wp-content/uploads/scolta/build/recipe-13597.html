Olive Ascolane (Stuffed Fried Olives)

Olive ascolane is an Italian starter from Ascoli Piceno made of fried green olives, stuffed with minced meat (beef, pork and chicken liver) and spices. This recipe was contributed to ItaliansRUs.com by Phil and Jackie Cicconi. Ingredients Filling Tomato sauce or gravy 1 ½ pounds pork 1 ½ pounds chicken breast ½ cup chopped green olives ⅔ cup Parmesan cheese 1 lemon rind, grated 1 teaspoon freshly-grated nutmeg 1 teaspoon cinnamon ½ teaspoon allspice Salt to taste Base About 50 large pitted green olives, halved Breading and frying 2 eggs, beaten Flour Plain bread crumbs Canola oil Procedure Cook the pork and chicken breast in your favorite tomato gravy recipe. Remove cooked, cooled meats from the sauce and grind them using a meat grinder or food processor. Add the remaining filling ingredients to the ground meat mix (do not over-process). Mix the ingredients into the meat and put into the refrigerator to cool for at least one hour. Remove the mixture from refrigerator and roll meat into balls about the size of a small walnut. Put half of an olive on each side of the rolled ball. Flour each ball, dip in beaten egg, then dip in bread crumbs. Arrange olive balls on a tray. Fry them quickly in about ½ inch of canola oil until golden brown. Drain and serve. Notes, tips, and variations
