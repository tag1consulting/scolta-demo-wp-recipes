Eba (Cassava Flake Swallow)

Ẹba is a popular Nigerian swallow made from cassava flakes (garri) and is eaten with various soups, such as ẹgusi, ẹfọ riro, and ọgbọnọ soup. It is starchy and considered a heavy food. Ingredients 1 liter water 2 milk tins of processed cassava flakes (garri) Equipment Wooden spatula Pot Procedure Boil water in a pot. Gradually stir in the cassava flour, and cook until it forms a smooth dough. Serve with your soup of choice. References
