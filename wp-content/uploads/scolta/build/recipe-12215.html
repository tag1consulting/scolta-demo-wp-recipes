Cucumber and Buttermilk Soup

A cold tangy soup, refreshing and unique. Ingredients 5 hot house cucumbers 4 cups scallions 6 cups buttermilk 1 cup cream 2 tbsp tarragon 2 tbsp lemon juice 1 tbsp sugar Salt to taste Pepper to taste Procedure Peel cucumbers. Dice into ¼-inch dice. Finely chop scallions. Mix everything together. Notes, tips, and variations Dill can be substituted for tarragon.
