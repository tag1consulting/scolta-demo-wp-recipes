Wine Glazed Pork Chops

Ingredients 4 thick cut bone-in pork rib chops ½ cup Chop Rub Cider vinegar, as needed 1 cup red wine ¼ cup butter, divided Procedure Brush pork chops generously with cider vinegar. Season pork with chop rub, patting down slightly. Bring wine to a boil over high heat in a large saucepan, and ignite it. Cook until reduced by half. Add butter, 1 tbsp at a time while whisking, until all butter has melted. Grill pork chops over high heat 2 minutes, brushing with wine mixture, then rotate 90° and cook for 2 minutes, brushing. Flip to the other side and repeat 1 more time. Remove and let rest 5 minutes. Serve with remaining wine mixture.
