Ginger Biscuits

This recipe is adapted from the public domain cookbook Mrs. Beeton’s Book of Household Management. Ingredients 3½ cups (475 g/1.05 lb) all-purpose flour 2 sticks (250 g/8.8 oz) unsalted butter 1 cup (225 g/7.9 oz) caster sugar 2 tablespoons ground ginger 2 eggs Milk as needed Procedure Preheat the oven to 160 °C (320 °F). Grease a baking sheet. Rub the butter and ginger into the flour in a bowl until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs. Add the eggs and mix to a soft dough, adding a little milk if necessary. Roll out the dough to 5 mm (0.20 in) and cut out biscuits with a plain round cutter. Place them on the prepared baking sheet and bake for 5 minutes until golden. Take the biscuits out of the oven and leave them to cool. References
