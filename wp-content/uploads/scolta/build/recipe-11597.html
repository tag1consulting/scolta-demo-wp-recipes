Biscuits II

Ingredients 1 cup (140 g / 4.9 oz) all-purpose flour, plus extra for dusting ½ teaspoon salt 2 teaspoons baking powder 1 tablespoon cold butter ½ cup (125 ml / 4.2 oz) milk Procedure Preheat the oven to 200 °C (392 °F). Sift flour, salt and baking powder twice in a bowl. Cut butter in with a knife until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs. Add milk to the mixture and mix just until you get a soft dough—don’t overmix. Place dough on a floured board. Knead the dough gently until smooth. Roll out to ½ inch (1.3 cm) thick. Cut out biscuits with a small cutter and place biscuits onto a greased pan. If you have scraps of dough, knead into a ball, re-roll, and cut out more biscuits. Bake for 15 minutes until nicely browned. Transfer the biscuits to a wire rack and leave it to cool. References
