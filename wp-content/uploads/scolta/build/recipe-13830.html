Pork Braised with Garlic and Onions of Indonesia (Babi Kecap)

This is a traditional Indonesian dish. Ingredients 1 kg pork 5 large white onions, chopped 5 cloves garlic, minced 4 tsp soy sauce Fresh ginger Black pepper Chile pepper (optional) Procedure Cut the meat into 1-inch (2.5 cm) cubes. Add the onions, garlic and ginger to a pan and cook at around 200°C. Add the pork and mix with the soy sauce and black pepper (and chili pepper if desired). After about 3–4 minutes, reduce the heat to around 120°C and cook, covered, for one hour. Serve with nice-tasting rice.
