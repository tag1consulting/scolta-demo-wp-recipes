Herrencreme (German Custard Dessert)

Herrencreme is a German dessert based on custard and cream. Ingredients 1 L milk 6 tbsp granulated sugar 2 packets (88 g) German custard powder (e.g. Dr. Oetker) 1 pinch salt ¼ L whipped cream Grated chocolate or chocolate sprinkles Rum Procedure Combine milk, sugar, salt, and custard powder in a saucepan. Cook, stirring, over medium heat until it has thickened. Place a sheet of cling film on the surface of the pudding to prevent a skin from forming, and let cool completely. Transfer the pudding to a bowl. Stir in rum and chocolate to taste. Fold in the whipped cream. Chill in fridge for about 2 hours. Serve.
