Roman Sauce II

Ingredients 1 onion, chopped Butter Flour 1 lemon, zested and juiced 1 bouquet of herbs 1 pinch nutmeg A few stoned raisins Pine nuts or almonds, shredded 1 tbsp burnt sugar Espagnole sauce Procedure Fry the onion slightly in butter and a little flour. Add the lemon juice and a little of the zest, herbs, nutmeg, raisins, almonds or pinocchi, and burnt sugar. Add this to a good Espagnole sauce, and warm it up in a bain-marie.
