Quick Tomato Noodle Soup

Ingredients 2 packages instant ramen or udon noodles with flavoring mix 2 tomatoes, chopped 2 sausages ½ medium onion, chopped Procedure Bring 1.5 liters of water to a boil. Add noodles to to boiling water with one of flavoring packets. Add chopped tomatoes and sausages. Boil for 5 minutes. Add chopped onion and serve.
