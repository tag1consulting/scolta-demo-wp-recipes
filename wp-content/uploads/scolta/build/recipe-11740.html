Cabbage Salad

This simple cabbage salad goes wonderfully with pizza. Ingredients ½ head (500 grams) white cabbage 100 ml rapeseed oil 2 tablespoons white vinegar 1 pinch of salt 1 pinch of coarsely-ground pepper Procedure Slice the cabbage finely (about 1–2 mm thick). Make a dressing of oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Toss the cabbage with the dressing. Put the salad in the fridge overnight.
