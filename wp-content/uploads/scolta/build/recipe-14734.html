Tomato Curry

This recipe was adapted from Grand Union Cookbook, compiled by Margaret Compton (Grand Union Tea Co., 1902). Ingredients 1 apple, grated 1 onion, chopped 2 tomatoes, sliced 2 oz (55 g) butter 1 tsp (5 ml) curry powder Milk Salt Black pepper Boiled rice Procedure Fry apple and onion in the butter until blended. Mix curry powder in a little milk, and stir in. Season with salt and pepper. Fry tomatoes in a separate pan. Cover them with the curry mixture, and serve within a wall of boiled rice.
