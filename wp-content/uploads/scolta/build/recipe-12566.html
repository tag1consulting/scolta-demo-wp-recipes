Fusion Spice Cookie (Gluten-Free)

Originally posted to Foodista by Julie Cecchini. Foodista recipes are released under a CC-BY license. Ingredients 1 cup white sugar 1 egg ¾ cup shortening 1 package gluten-free all-purpose baking mix 3 tsp Chinese five-spice powder Preparation Preheat oven to 350°F. Cream sugar, egg, and shortening together. Add all-purpose baking mix and five-spice. Mix well to make a dough. Roll teaspoonfuls of dough into balls, and roll the balls in sugar. Bake at 350°F for 5 to 6 minutes.
