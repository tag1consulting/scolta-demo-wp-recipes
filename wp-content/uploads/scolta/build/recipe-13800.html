Pizza Rollups

Ingredients 1 package (8 ounces) croissant roll dough 24 slices of pepperoni 4 slices of provolone cheese Pizza spices as desired (anise, basil, fennel, garlic, oregano, etc.) Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. Unroll 2 croissant rolls and place together on ungreased cookie sheet, pointing in opposite directions, forming a rectangle by pinching sides together. Lay 6 slices of pepperoni on top of each dough rectangle. Lay a slice of provolone cheese on top of pepperoni slices. Roll each into a tube. Bake 12–15 minutes.
