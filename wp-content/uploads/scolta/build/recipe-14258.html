Shortcrust Pastry

Shortcrust pastry or shortcrust dough is a buttery, crumbly pastry dough which is often used as a base for tarts. This is an eggless version. Ingredients 150 g (½ cup) unsalted butter, softened 100 g (½ cup) caster sugar 1 tsp grated lemon rind 120 g (1 cup) plain flour, plus extra for dusting 120 g (¾ cup) potato flour Procedure Cream together butter, sugar, and lemon rind in a bowl. Sift together the plain and potato flours into a mound. Make a well in the center of the mound and add the creamed mixture. Knead lightly until the dough is consistent. Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and chill for 1 hour before use.
