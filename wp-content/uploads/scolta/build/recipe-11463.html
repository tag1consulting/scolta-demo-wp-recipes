Banana Cream II

Banana cream is a milk-based dessert. It is based on Mrs Beeton’s Book of Household Management, which is public domain. Ingredients 2 oz (57 g / ½ cup) cornstarch 1 quart (950 ml / 4 cups) milk 2 oz (57 g / ¼ cup) caster sugar 2 egg yolks, beaten ½ teaspoon vanilla extract 2 bananas, thinly sliced Procedure Mix the cornstarch smoothly with a little milk to form a paste. Put the remaining milk into a pan with the sugar. When boiling add the cornstarch paste, and boil for about 10 minutes. Let the mixture cool a little, then add the beaten egg yolks, and stir until they thicken. Add the vanilla, the bananas. When cool, pour into the prepared mould. Chill for a few hours to set.
