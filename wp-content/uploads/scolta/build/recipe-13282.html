Marshmallow Rice Squares II

Ingredients 1 cup marshmallows (either regular white, cream soda flavour, or bubblegum flavour) 100–150 ml milk ¼ cup powdered sugar 1 cup puffed cereal (e.g. Rice Krispies) Procedure Melt marshmallows in the microwave in 30 second intervals until it melts into a creamy mixture. Remove marshmallow fluff from microwave, pour in the milk, and mix for 1½ minutes. Add the powdered sugar and mix for 2 more minutes. Add the puffed cereal, and mix for 1 more minute. Put back in microwave and heat for 13 seconds. Remove from the microwave and mix a final time. Press into a greased dish or pan. Let set, then cut into pieces.
