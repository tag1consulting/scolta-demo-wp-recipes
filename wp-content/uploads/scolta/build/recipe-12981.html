Jamaican Jerk Spice I

Jamaican jerk spice is a pungent, hot mixture of spices used to flavor meats before grilling or roasting. Ingredients 2 tablespoons cayenne pepper 1 teaspoon cinnamon, ground 1 teaspoon nutmeg, ground 1 teaspoon allspice, ground 1 teaspoon dried onion powder 2 teaspoons dried thyme 1 tablespoon sugar 1 tablespoon dried minced onion 1 tablespoon salt Procedure Combine all ingredients together. Store in an airtight container.
