Chicken Alfredo Pizza

Ingredients 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts Pizza dough (e.g. Basic Pizza Crust or a pre-packaged dough for convenience) 1 large jar of Alfredo sauce 1 large package of shredded cheese (e.g. cheddar, mozzarella) 1 package of pepperoni Preparation Cut up the chicken breast, and sear/bake/grill it until it is cooked through. Prepare the pizza crust and spread it on a medium-sized pizza pan. Spread some Alfredo sauce on the crust, and top with cooked chicken. Repeat to make a few layers, then sprinkle shredded cheese on top. Bake in oven at 350°F (175°C) for about 20 minutes or until the crust is done. Notes, tips, and variations You can add whatever you like if you find this recipe a bit bland. Add more of any of the ingredients if you would like.
