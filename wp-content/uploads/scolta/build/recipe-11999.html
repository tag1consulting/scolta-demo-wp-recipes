Chocolate-coated Marshmallow Treats (Flødeboller)

Flødeboller are a Danish chocolate-coated marshmallow treats. You can use flat, round shortbread (or other) biscuits instead, or even sponge circles instead of the marzipan biscuits. Ingredients Marzipan biscuits 100 g icing sugar 2 ea. (60 g) pasteurized egg whites 300 g marzipan (pure) Vanilla filling 250 g white granulated sugar 75 g water 110 g glucose syrup 4 ea. (140 g) pasteurized egg whites 15 g sugar ½ vanilla pod/bean 1 teaspoon icing sugar Concentrated or dry flavoring (optional) Chocolate coating 500 g dark (58–64%) chocolate Procedure Marzipan biscuits Mix icing sugar and egg whites in a bowl Mix the egg-sugar mixture into the marzipan in several stages until the dough is uniform. Use a pastry bag to make flat, round biscuits, approximately 5 cm in diameter. Bake in oven at 190°C until golden (approximately 10 min). Cool to ambient temperature. Filling Mix 250 g of sugar, the water and glucose syrup in a saucepan, and heat to exactly 117°C. Meanwhile, beat the egg whites lightly with 15 g of sugar. Gradually pour the hot sugar mixture directly into the egg whites, while still beating. Whip the egg white mixture until very stiff. Split open vanilla bean and scrape out the seeds, mix these with the icing sugar. Add this to the egg white mixture along with any flavoring, and stir slowly until uniform in appearance. Avoid deflating the meringue. Use a pastry bag to pipe 5 cm tall meringue tops on the marzipan biscuits. Make sure the foam adheres to the biscuit, otherwise it will fall off while coating. Leave at room temperature or put in refrigerator while preparing chocolate coating. Chocolate coating Finely chop the chocolate. Melt half the chocolate carefully in a bain-marie (or a pot floating in water in a bigger pot). Aim for 48–50°C. Put the other half of the chocolate into a bowl (make sure it is big enough for the entire cake can be submerged in chocolate). Add ⅔ of the melted chocolate, and let the two melt together WITHOUT HEATING! Only add more melted chocolate if the lumps do not melt away. The temperature must not exceed 34°C. This will keep the chocolate shiny and crunchy. Dip the meringue tops into the chocolate. Make sure all the meringue is covered. The biscuit is up to you. Sprinkle or roll in desiccated coconut, then leave to set. Do not reheat the chocolate if it starts to set, use instead some of the warm melted chocolate.
