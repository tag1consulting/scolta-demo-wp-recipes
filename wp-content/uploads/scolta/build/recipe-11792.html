Carrot Cake II

Ingredients Cake 450 ml (14 fl oz) vegetable oil 400 g (14 oz) plain flour 2 tsp bicarbonate soda 570 g (1 lb 4 oz) granulated sugar 290 g (10 oz) eggs 1 pinch of salt 12 g (½ oz) ground cinnamon 530 g (1 lb 3 oz) carrots, peeled and grated 150 g (5 oz) walnuts, chopped Topping 200 g (7 oz) cream cheese 150 g (5 oz) granulated sugar 100 g (3½ oz) butter Procedure Mix all the ingredients together in a food processor for 5 minutes. Pour batter into a cake tin 26 cm (10½ inches) in diameter. Bake at 160°C (325°F/Gas 3) for 45 minutes. Check to see if the cake is cooked, then remove from the oven and allow to cool for 10 minutes in the tin. Unmold cake and place it on a cooling rack. For the topping, place the cream cheese, sugar and butter into a mixing bowl and cream together until smooth and soft. Spread frosting over the cake using a palette knife.
