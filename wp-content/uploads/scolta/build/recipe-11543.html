Beef and Vegetable Kabobs

Ingredients 1 ½ pounds (about 700 g) beef sirloin, cut into 1 ½ inch (4 cm) cubes 2 tbsp smoked paprika 1 tbsp kosher salt 1 tbsp freshly-ground black pepper 1 ½ tbsp cayenne pepper ½ cup olive oil ¼ cup balsamic vinegar 1 large onion, wedged Several assorted bell peppers, cut into 3-inch (7.5 cm) strips 1-inch (2.5 cm) slices zucchini 6 bamboo skewers, soaked in water 30 minutes 1 ½ tsp rosemary, finely chopped 1 tbsp thyme, finely chopped Procedure Combine paprika, salt, pepper, cayenne pepper, olive oil, rosemary, thyme and balsamic vinegar in a gallon (4 liter) size zip-top bag. Add beef and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Sort beef cubes into equal size rows. Skewer 1 onion wedge, 1 strip bell pepper, 1 slice zucchini from the side, and 3 cubes of meat across the grain in a pattern per skewer. Grill over medium high heat, turning often, 11 minutes for medium. Serve warm.
