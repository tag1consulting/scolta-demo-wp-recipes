Crab and Corn Salad

This salad is very easy to make. Ingredients 8 oz imitation crab meat 16 oz canned whole kernel sweet corn ½ cup mayonnaise 1–2 green onions Shredded cheese (optional) Procedure Chop imitation crab meat and green onions. Combine the above, corn and mayonnaise in an appropriately-sized bowl. Mix well. Add shredded cheese to taste and mix again.
