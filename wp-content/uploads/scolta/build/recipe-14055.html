Roast Chicken on Salt

This is an old family recipe for chicken on salt from Russia. Ingredients 1 chicken, plucked and dressed 1 lemon, quartered 4 peeled cloves of garlic Several sprigs of fresh rosemary 1 kg salt Procedure Stuff the chicken with the quartered lemon, garlic and rosemary. Bind the chicken ready for roasting. Fill a baking tray with the salt and place the chicken on top. Roast for 90 minutes at 190°C. The flesh should be moist and the skin crisp.
