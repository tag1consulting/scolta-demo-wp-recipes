Succotash

Ingredients 2 quarts shelled lima beans 24 ears of corn cut off the cob 1 pound pickled pork (optional) 2 tbsp sugar (optional) 2 tbsp butter or olive oil (optional) Pepper Procedure If using pork, cover pork with water, and parboil it. Add beans, then corn, sugar, butter, and pepper to taste. After corn is added, watch carefully to keep from scorching. Serve. Notes, tips, and variations A chopped red bell pepper can be added. Note that lima beans that have not been cooked should be thoroughly boiled for at least 10 minutes to rid the beans of phytohemagglutinin, a toxin found in uncooked lima beans. Canned beans are safe to eat without cooking. References Mrs. S. A. Powers, “New England Succotash” in Recipes Tried and True at Project Gutenberg
