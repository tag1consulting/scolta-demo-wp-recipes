Egg in a Mist

Ingredients a little butter 4 eggs, separated Salt to taste Pepper to taste 1 tablespoon milk 4 oz finely-grated cheese Procedure Grease a shallow baking dish with butter. Add the egg yolks. Mix in the salt, pepper, milk, and a little of the grated cheese. Whip the egg whites to a stiff froth, and lightly fold in the remaining cheese. Pile the egg white mixture on top of the egg yolks. Bake the whole dish in a moderate oven.
