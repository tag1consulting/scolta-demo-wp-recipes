South Side Fizz

A south side fizz is the same as a traditional south side, but with soda water. Ingredients 1¼ oz gin ½ oz lime juice ½ oz simple syrup 2 sprigs mint Club soda Procedure In a shaker, muddle the mint, lime juice and simple syrup. Add the gin and fill with ice. Shake, and strain into a highball glass filled with crushed ice. Stir until frost appears on the outside of the glass. Fill with club soda and garnish with another mint sprig.
