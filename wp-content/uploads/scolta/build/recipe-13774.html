Pickled Oysters

This recipe for pickled oysters was adapted and modernized from the 1881 Household Cyclopedia. Ingredients 100 oysters 1 tbsp salt 1 teacup vinegar 1 tbsp black peppercorns 1 tsp mace 1 tsp cloves Procedure Drain off the liquor from the oysters, and wash them. Combine the oysters with the salt and vinegar; simmer them for about 10 minutes, discarding any scum that rises to the surface. Take out the oysters, leaving the liquid. Place oysters in a jar. Add the peppercorns, mace, and cloves to the oyster liquid, and boil for 5 minutes. Skim the liquid, and pour it over the oysters in the jar.
