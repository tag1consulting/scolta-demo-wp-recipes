Hot Chocolate

Ingredients 1 cup (240 ml / 8.1 oz) milk or water 1 teaspoon cocoa powder 1 teaspoon sugar Procedure Heat the milk. While heating the milk/water put the other ingredients in a mug and mix them very well. It’s best if you try to grind it a bit using a spoon. Fill half the mug with hot milk and stir very well.
