Spinach Mushroom Pesto Spaghetti

This spinach mushroom pesto spaghetti recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty. Ingredients 225 g (8 oz) spaghetti 2 tablespoon olive oil 140 g (5 oz) spinach 200 g (7 oz) mushrooms, sliced 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon pepper 1 cup (275 g / 9.5 oz) pesto ½ cup (60 g / 2 oz) grated parmesan cheese, plus extra for sprinkling Procedure Boil the spaghetti in water until al dente. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan over a medium heat. Cook the spinach for 5 minutes until it is wilted. Add the mushrooms, salt, and pepper and cook for a further 3 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the pesto and cheese and cook for 2 minutes. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the sauce. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve. To serve, sprinkle with some extra cheese.
