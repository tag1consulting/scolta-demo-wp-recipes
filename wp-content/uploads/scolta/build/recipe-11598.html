Biscuits III

This recipe is adapted from the public domain cookbook Mrs. Beeton’s Book of Household Management. Ingredients 1¼ cups (175 g/6.2 oz) all-purpose flour ¼ cup (55 g/1.9 oz) caster sugar 2 tablespoons butter, softened 1 egg yolk 2 tablespoons milk Procedure Preheat the oven to 220 °C (428 °F). Grease a baking pan. Beat the egg and milk together in a bowl. Mix the other ingredients into the egg mixture to form a soft dough. Roll out the dough to 5 mm (0.20 in) and cut out into very thin biscuits with a round cookie cutter. Place the biscuits on the baking pan. Bake for 6 minutes until golden. Take the biscuits out of the oven and leave them to cool. References
