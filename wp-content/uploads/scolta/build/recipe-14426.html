Spicy Corn Bread

Ingredients 1 cup corn meal 1 cup all-purpose flour 4 tsp baking powder ½ tsp salt 1 cup milk 1 egg ¼ cup shortening 1 tsp–¼ cup chopped jalapeños, to taste ¼ cup sugar (optional) ½ of a 15 ounce can whole corn, drained (optional) Procedure Preheat oven to 425°F. Grease an 8-inch baking pan. Combine corn meal, flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar in a large mixing bowl. Add egg, sugar, and shortening. Beat until fairly smooth (about 1 minute). Bake for 20–25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. If corn is added to batter, increase time by about 50% Serve hot!
