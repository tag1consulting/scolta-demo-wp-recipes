Coconut Biscuits

This recipe is adapted from the public domain cookbook Mrs. Beeton’s Book of Household Management. Ingredients 1¼ cups (280 g/9.9 oz) caster sugar 2 egg whites 2½ cups (170 g/6.0 oz) desiccated coconut Procedure Preheat the oven to 150 °C (302 °F). Whip the egg whites in a mixing bowl until soft peaks form. Add sugar gradually, whipping until stiff peaks form. Fold in the coconut until combined. Form the dough into pyramids. Cover each pyramid with baking parchment and place them on a baking tray. Bake for 15 minutes until they are colored light brown. Uncover the biscuits and leave them to cool. References
