Indian Beans

This is excellent with Indian potatoes and Indian rice. Ingredients 1 can lentils 1 can kidney beans 1 onion ½ can tomatoes ½ teaspoon paprika ½ teaspoon salt ½ teaspoon sugar ¼ teaspoon cumin 1 pinch turmeric Procedure Chop and sauté the onion. Add the tomatoes. Add all the spices. Add the lentils and kidney beans. Cook everything for 20 minutes over medium heat.
