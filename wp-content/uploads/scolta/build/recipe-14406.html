Spelt Yellow Cake

A recipe for a spelt cake, an ancient-wheat alternative to yellow cake. Ingredients 1 teaspoon vanilla 1 cup (240 g) butter 1 cup (240 ml) milk 2 cups (450 g) white granulated sugar 3 cups (650 g) spelt flour 3 teaspoons baking powder 3 pinches salt 4 eggs Procedure Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Cream the butter and sugar together until fluffy. Beat in the eggs. Mix in vanilla. Gradually sift in the dry ingredients, alternating with small additions of the milk. Transfer to a greased and floured baking pan. Bake at 175°C (350°F), until a skewer inserted shows no moist crumbs; approximately 30 minutes.
