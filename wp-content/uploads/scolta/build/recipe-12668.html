Greek Chicken Wrap

Ingredients 2 cups shredded cooked chicken 2 ½ cups crumbled feta cheese ½ cup Greek Yogurt Sauce ½ cup fresh oregano, finely chopped 4 flour tortillas Olive oil Procedure Combine feta, chicken, yogurt sauce, and oregano. Set aside. Brush one side of each tortilla with olive oil. Add ¼ of chicken mixture and roll the longer side up. Repeat until all tortillas have been rolled. Sprinkle with additional chopped oregano, and serve cold as a snack. Notes, tips, and variations You could also do this with pitas for a more traditional version.
