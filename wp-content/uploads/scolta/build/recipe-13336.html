Meringata di Frutta in Coppa (Meringue with Fruit and Cream)

A quick and easy meringata is made as follows: Ingredients Vanilla pastry cream, home-made or store-bought, as needed Any fruit of choice or a mixture, as needed Crème chantilly, as needed Crumbled meringue cookies, as needed Fresh mint or basil Raspberry coulis or other topping of choice Procedure Spoon a few tablespoons of vanilla cream into the bottom of a martini or parfait glass. Cover the custard with your fruit of choice, leaving enough room for the topping. Cover the fruit with crème chantilly, and sprinkle crumbled meringue chips on top. Serve with a sprig of mint or basil and drizzle with raspberry coulis.
