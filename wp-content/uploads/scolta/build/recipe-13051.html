Kid-Friendly Pasta

This easy recipe for pasta is designed for children to make and eat. Make sure to supervise children when cooking. Ingredients 1 lb whole-wheat spaghetti 2 carrots, cut into thin strips ½ lb green beans ¼ cup tomato pasta sauce Procedure Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add spaghetti and carrots to the boiling water, and cook for 4 minutes. Add green beans, and cook for another 4 minutes. Meanwhile, heat the tomato sauce. Drain the spaghetti and the vegetables, then mix it with the heated sauce. Add a pinch of salt if needed. Serve.
