Creamy Mustard Vinaigrette

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Recipes | Salad Recipes This variation on the basic vinaigrette is much thicker and goes great with contrasting flavors in salad such as dried cranberries and bell peppers. Ingredients 6 tablespoons Dijon mustard 5 tablespoons olive oil (light or virgin) 2 teaspoons white wine vinegar or cider vinegar 1 tablespoon honey Salt to taste Freshly-ground pepper to taste Procedure Mix all ingredients except oil until well blended. Slowly mix in oil until fully incorporated. Add salt and pepper to taste.
