Wheat-Free Baking Mix

This wheat-free baking mix is a mixture of several other flours that, along with the addition of xanthan gum or guar gum, approximates wheat flour. Ingredients 2 parts (28.5%) by weight white rice flour 2 parts (28.5%) by weight brown rice flour 2 parts (28.5%) by weight tapioca starch 1 part (14%) by weight amaranth flour 2–4% by weight xanthan gum (optional) Procedure Combine all ingredients. Mix until very thoroughly blended. Use the blend as a substitute for wheat flour, using the same weight of the blend as of the wheat flour; for example, if a recipe calls for 100 g wheat flour, use 100 g of this blend. Notes, tips and variations Cakes, cookies, and other tender baked goods will need less xanthan gum. Breads and chewier doughs will need more xanthan gum.
