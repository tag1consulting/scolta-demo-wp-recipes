Mustard and Curry Leaf Lassi

Ingredients Curd (thick plain “natural” yogurt) Cold water Salt to taste Oil Mustard seeds Curry leaves Procedure Whip the curd in a bowl until it becomes soft and loosened. Mix in half as much cold water as curd, along with salt to taste. Heat a small amount of oil in a pan, and add mustard seeds. When hot, add curry leaves. Heat through for a few more seconds. Mix the mustard and curry leaf into the curd mixture. Chill the mixture well, and serve cold.
