Fried Potato Wedges

Ingredients 4 medium/fair sized potatoes (Russets are preferable) 4 tablespoons of olive oil or (canola oil) 5 grams sea salt (paprika or onion salt works too) 4 grams of parsley or oregano (many seasonings can work) Procedure Slice potatoes evenly (over-sized wedges can result in uneven cooking). Sprinkle seasoning and salt over each potato. Deep fry potato wedges until brown and crispy (cook longer for more crispiness.) Put in a plastic container and put in fridge (or just put on a cooling area if eating immediately).
