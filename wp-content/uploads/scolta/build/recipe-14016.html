Rhubarb Pudding

Ingredients Stale cake or bread 2 cups rhubarb 4 tbsp white granulated sugar 2 egg whites ½ cup sugar Procedure Line a buttered baking dish with slices of plain stale cake or bread. Combine the rhubarb and 4 tbsp sugar, and add to the dish. Cover and bake in a moderately slow 325°F (160°C) oven for about 30 minutes. Make a meringue of the egg whites by whipping until very stiff, gradually beating in the ½ cup sugar. Remove pudding from oven and cover with the meringue. Return to a slow oven to brown the meringue.
