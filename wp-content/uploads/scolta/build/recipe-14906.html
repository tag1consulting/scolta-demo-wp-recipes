White Russian Cocktail

A White Russian is a creamy, coffee-flavored cocktail made by combining vodka, coffee liqueur, and cream or milk. Ingredients 2 oz vodka 1 oz coffee liqueur 1 oz heavy cream or milk Procedure Fill a glass with ice. Pour in the vodka, followed by the coffee liqueur. Top with heavy cream or milk. Gently stir to mix. Notes, tips, and variations For a blind Russian, replace the cream with Irish cream liqueur. For a dairy-free option, replace the milk with either almond or oat milk.
