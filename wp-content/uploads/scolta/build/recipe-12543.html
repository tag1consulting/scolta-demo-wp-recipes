Froga (Maltese Omelet)

Ingredients 2 medium potatoes, peeled 1 bunch of spring onions, chopped 100 g cooked lean beef or 200 g corned beef (optional), chopped very small and fine Cheddar cheese, grated 1 handful of parsley, chopped fine Salt Pepper 6 eggs Procedure Boil potatoes, then mash them. Place the mashed potatoes in a bowl and add the spring onions, beef, cheese, the parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix together, then add the eggs and mix everything together. Heat some oil in a frying pan. Add 3 large spoonfuls of the mixture to the frying pan, and flatten them out to around 1 cm thick. Cook until golden brown. Serve hot with salad and fresh buttered bread.
