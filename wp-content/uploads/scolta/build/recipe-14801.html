Tuscan Roast Potatoes

This is great served with grilled steak, roast chicken, or vegetable sauté. Ingredients 1 pound (450 g) each Russet potatoes and redskin potatoes, cut into 2-inch (5 cm) cubes 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp freshly ground black pepper ½ cup olive oil 1 ½ tsp each dried parsley, rosemary, thyme, basil and oregano Procedure Heat a large pot of water to a boil. Add potatoes and cook for 10 minutes. Shock in a bowl of ice water. Drain and refrigerate for 1 hour. Combine remaining ingredients. Add potatoes and toss to coat. Spread out in one even layer on your largest baking pan. Roast at 450°F (230°C) for 20 minutes, stirring halfway. Serve warm.
