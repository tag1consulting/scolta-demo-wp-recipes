Chicken and Broccoli Casserole

Ingredients 1 lb chicken breast, cubed 14 oz frozen broccoli 10.5 oz canned cream of celery soup 4 oz mayonnaise 6 oz skim milk 1 teaspoon curry powder Procedure Sear the cubed chicken in saucepan. Place cooked chicken and remaining ingredients into a casserole dish. Cover with foil and bake in oven at 375°F for 30 minutes. Serve with rice.
