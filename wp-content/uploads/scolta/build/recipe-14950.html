Yeung Chow Fried Rice

Ingredients 3 tbsp corn oil 2 tbsp minced or finely-chopped garlic ½ cup (100 g) bacon, diced ½ cup (100 g) prawn, diced ¼ cup (50 g) Char siu (barbecued pork), diced ⅛ cup (25 g) scallion, diced ¼ cup (50 g) peas ½ cup (100 g) beaten egg 3 cups (600 g) cooked jasmine rice 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp pepper 2 tbsp jalapeno, diced (optional) Procedure Heat the oil in a non-stick skillet, and fry the garlic until golden brown. Add the bacon, prawn, and char siu, then cook slightly for 5 minutes. Add the scallion, peas, and egg, and cook until all cooked. Stir in the rice, salt, pepper and jalapeno.
