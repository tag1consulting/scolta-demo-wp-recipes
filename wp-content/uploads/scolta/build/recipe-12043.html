Churri (Indian Yogurt Herb Sauce)

Ingredients 1 teaspoon cumin seed 10 grams fresh mint, chopped 15 grams fresh coriander leaves, chopped 2 cm fresh ginger root, chopped 2 fresh green chile peppers, chopped 3 deciliters natural (plain) yoghurt 3 deciliters cultured buttermilk 1 pinch of salt 1 onion, chopped Procedure Add cumin seed to a skillet over medium heat, and toast until fragrant. Don’t let burn. Grind the toasted cumin seeds with a mortar. Grind the mint, coriander leaves, ginger, and chili peppers into a paste. Add the yoghurt, buttermilk and salt and make sure everything gets mixed up well. Add the onion and the ground cumin.
