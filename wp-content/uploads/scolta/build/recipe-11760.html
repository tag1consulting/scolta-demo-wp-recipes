Campfire S&#8217;mores

Campfire s’mores are a classic snack and a favorite with children. Ingredients 2 graham crackers 1 marshmallow 4 squares milk chocolate Procedure Toast marshmallow until golden brown over campfire on long green stick with bark peeled off. Place on graham cracker, then top with chocolate and remaining cracker. Warnings Be careful; when the marshmallows are ready they are very hot and can catch fire. Don’t burn yourself!
