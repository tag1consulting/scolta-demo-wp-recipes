Spicy Chickpea Soup

Ingredients 2 cans (32 ounces) chickpeas, drained 1 can (14 ounces) light coconut milk 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth ½ cup prepared salsa 1½ teaspoons garam masala 1 teaspoon ground ginger 2 tablespoons frozen apple juice concentrate ¼ cup packed cilantro leaves Procedure Purée all ingredients in a blender until smooth. Heat in a large saucepan or Dutch oven, and bring to a simmer. Simmer, stirring often, 4–5 minutes, to blend flavors. Notes, tips, and variations Garnish with ¼ cup plain yogurt and ¼ cup thinly sliced green onions. Replace the chicken broth with vegetable stock for a vegan dish.
