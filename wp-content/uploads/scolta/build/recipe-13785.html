Pineapple Bread

A basic and quickly-prepared recipe for pineapple bread with coconut flakes. Can be served as a side dish or dessert. Ingredients Procedure Add the banana to a medium bowl, and mash it with a fork. Cut the pineapple into small pieces and mix them into the banana. In a separate large bowl, combine all dry ingredients except coconut flakes. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Put the butter, eggs, milk and fruits into the second bowl and beat well. As the actual water content of fruits can vary, add an additional tablespoon or two of flour if the mixture appears too wet. Lightly grease or line an 8×4 in (20×10 cm) loaf pan. Pour batter into pan. Sprinkle a thin layer of coconut flakes on top. Bake on middle shelf of oven for 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean. Conversion notes
