Garum (Fermented)

Garum is a traditional Roman fermented fish sauce or condiment. It was used often in now-ancient Roman cuisine, as a condiment or sauce on its own, an additive to sauces, or as a seasoning. Ingredients Intestines of fish Whole small fish Salt Procedure Salt the fish and fish intestines in a barrel. Place said barrel in a warm, bright, place, turning regularly for at least 48 hours. Place a basket, cloth-covered colander, or similar into the barrel, and let the liquids seep up into the container. Notes, tips, and variations Unit conversions to modern measures based on Wikipedia’s page on Roman measures. Boiled version from Apicius, fermented from Geoponika. Warnings Whether boiling or fermenting the fish sauce, the smell can be quite intense. Especially if fermenting. Be respectful of your neighbors.
