Ginger Peach Glazed BBQ Ribs

Ingredients 2 slabs St. Louis style/cut pork spareribs 6 tbsp Barbecue Rub 2 tbsp minced fresh ginger 1 cup peach preserves 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 2 tbsp cider vinegar Dry applewood chunks Procedure Season ribs with the Barbecue Rub. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Place applewood in the firebox of a 250°F smoker or in the coal area of a 250°F charcoal grill set for indirect heat. Combine remaining ingredients and bring to a boil over high heat until reduced by ⅓. Cover tightly and set aside. Place ribs in smoker/on top of grill grate and cook, brushing often with preserve mixture, for 5–6 hours. Brush ribs generously with preserve mixture and place on a medium high grill and cook, repeatedly brushing side facing you, until well browned. Repeat one more time. Remove and let rest 10 minutes; slice into 1-rib portions and serve.
