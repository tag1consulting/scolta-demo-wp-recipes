Onion Salt

Onion salt is a flavorful seasoning made by combining dried onion powder with salt. It provides a convenient way to add onion flavor to dishes without the need for fresh onions. Onion salt is commonly used in various cuisines and can enhance the taste of soups, stews, meat dishes, and more. Ingredients 1 cup dried onion flakes ½ cup salt Instructions In a blender or food processor, add the dried onion flakes. Pulse or blend the onion flakes until they turn into a fine powder-like consistency. In a bowl, combine the powdered onion with the salt. Mix the onion powder and salt thoroughly until well incorporated. Store the onion salt in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Label the container with the date of preparation. Use Use onion salt as a seasoning in various savory dishes. Sprinkle it on roasted or grilled meats, such as chicken, beef, or pork, before cooking. Add it to soups, stews, or sauces to enhance their flavor. Incorporate it into salad dressings or marinades for an extra kick. Substitute onion salt for regular salt in recipes that require both salt and onion flavor. Notes, tips, and variations Adjust the ratio of onion flakes to salt according to your taste preferences. You can experiment with different types of salt, such as sea salt or kosher salt, for variations in flavor. Ensure that the onion salt is well blended to avoid any clumps of onion powder.
