Chocolate and Nut Covered Strawberries

Ingredients 4 ounces semisweet chocolate, melted using a double boiler ¼ cup hazelnut-flavored syrup ¼ cup white granulated sugar 2 tbsp pure vanilla extract 1 pinch of salt 50 strawberries, hulled 50 bamboo or wooden toothpicks Chopped hazelnuts, pecans, walnuts, or almonds (optional) Crushed gingersnap cookies (optional) Procedure Combine melted chocolate, syrup, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. If using cookies or nuts, place them in a pie plate. Add strawberries to chocolate mixture and toss to coat. If using nuts or cookies, roll strawberries in them. Insert a toothpick into the side of each fruit and refrigerate until coating sets. Serve chilled.
