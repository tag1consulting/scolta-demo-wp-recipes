Cheese Spread on French Bread

Ingredients ½ tbsp Dijon mustard 1 tbsp butter, room temperature 2 tsp dried sage ¼ cup apple juice 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese ½ tsp pepper 8 oz package cream cheese, softened 1 loaf French bread, cut in small slices Procedure In a large bowl, mix together mustard, butter, and sage. Add the apple juice, cheddar cheese, and pepper, then stir well. Add the cream cheese, blending well. Cover and refrigerate for 3 hours. When ready to serve, remove from refrigerator and allow to stand for ½ hour. Spread on bread and serve with milk.
