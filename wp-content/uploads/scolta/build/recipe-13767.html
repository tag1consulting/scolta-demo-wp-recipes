Phulourie (Split Pea Fritters)

This is a side dish that may be eaten with spicy dhaal, or with spicy “sour” chutney (similar to mango chutney). Ingredients ½ pt split peas, or 1 lb ground split pea flour ½ cup flour 1 teaspoon baking powder 2–3 cloves garlic, ground 1 beaten egg Salt to taste ¼ teaspoon turmeric ½ teaspoon ground gheera (cumin) Oil for frying Procedure If using whole split peas, soak overnight in lightly salted cold water, lightly salted. Drain and grind finely using a mill or food processor. Mix all the ingredients, adding enough water to make a dropping consistency. Drop dollops of batter into hot oil, squishing through thumb and forefinger to make balls about ¾ inch in diameter. Fry until golden brown, remove from oil, and drain on brown paper or kitchen towel.
