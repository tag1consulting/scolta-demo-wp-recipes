Tisane ma Louisa (Algerian Lemon Herb Tea)

Tisane ma Louisa (شاي ماء لويزة, Louisa’s Water Remedy Tea) is an Algerian herbal remedy tea. Ingredients 2 g (around 3 tsp) dried verbena leaves 250 ml water ½ ea. lemon, juiced Sugar to taste (optional) Procedure Heat the water in a saucepan and bring it to a boil. Add the verbena leaves to the boiling water. Let the verbena leaves steep in water for about 10 minutes. Stir the leaves in the water (attempt to crush them as small as possible), then strain out the leaves. Add the lemon juice and sugar.
