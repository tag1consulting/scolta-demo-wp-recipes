Müsli

or muesli is a German cereal mix that is often oat-based with dried fruits. Although it can be bought in several flavors, this is the recipe to make it yourself. Ingredients 3 cups (720 g) rolled oats 2 cups (480 g) coarse oat bran ¾ cup (180 g) unsweetened dried coconut ⅓ cup (80 g) currants ⅓ cup (80 g) golden raisins ¼ cup (60 g) chopped dried mangoes ⅓ cup (80 g) chopped dried apples ⅓ cup (80 g) chopped dried apricots ½ cup (120 g) chopped almonds ¼ cup (60 g) chopped raw cashew nuts ⅓ cup (80 g) pumpkin seeds ⅓ cup (80 g) sunflower seeds (shelled) Procedure Combine all the ingredients in a large bowl, then transfer to an airtight container to store. Serve with milk, yogurt, honey or berries. The dried müsli will keep for up to a month in airtight container.
