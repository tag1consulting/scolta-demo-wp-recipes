Crème Fraîche Chocolate Sauce

This is a simple delicious sauce for éclairs and other desserts. Ingredients 1½ oz low-fat butter 1½ oz caster sugar 1½ fluid oz (70 ml) crème fraîche 1½ oz plain chocolate Procedure Melt all the ingredients together in a saucepan and allow to bubble for a while until the mixture darkens in colour slightly. Be careful not to let the chocolate burn. Remove from the heat and allow to cool until it thickens.
