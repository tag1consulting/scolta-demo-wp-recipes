Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies III

These are traditional chewy chocolate chip cookies with oatmeal added to provide a bit of extra body to the cookie. Ingredients 1 stick butter, softened ⅓ cup white granulated sugar ½ cup brown sugar 1 egg ½ tsp vanilla extract ½ tsp salt ½ tsp baking soda 1 cup plus 2 tbsp flour ¼ cup rolled oats (oatmeal) ½ cup chocolate chips Procedure Preheat oven to 375°F. Cream together butter and sugars. Add egg and vanilla and mix. Add salt, baking soda, and flour; mix. Mix in oatmeal. Mix in chocolate chips. Scoop cookies onto a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake 9–11 minutes at 375°F.
