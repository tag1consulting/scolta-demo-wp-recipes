Hartshorne Sweet Sauce

This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne. Ingredients 2 glasses red wine 1 glass vinegar 3 tsp cullis (strong meat broth) A bit of sugar 1 onion, sliced A little cinnamon 1 bay leaf Procedure Combine all ingredients in a saucepan. Simmer for 15 minutes.
