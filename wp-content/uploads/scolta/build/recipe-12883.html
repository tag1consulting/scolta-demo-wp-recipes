Hutspot (Dutch Mashed Vegetables)

Ingredients 5 lbs potatoes, quartered 3½ lbs carrots, cut into pieces 2 lbs onions, diced 2 cups ham, cubed Add cheese, salt, and pepper to your liking Procedure Cook potatoes in pan of water, adding salt to taste, until done. Drain. Cook carrots, onions and ham, adding a small amount of salt, until carrots are done. Drain, reserving water from carrot, onion, and ham pot. Mash all ingredients, adding enough of reserved juices from carrots to reach a mashed potato consistency. Add 2 to 3 chunks of butter while mashing, to taste.
