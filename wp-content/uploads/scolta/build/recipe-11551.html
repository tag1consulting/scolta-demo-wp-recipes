Beef Gravy Soup

This recipe for beef gravy soup is derived from the Household Cyclopedia of 1881. Ingredients 1 pound lean beef, sliced 2 medium onions, sliced 1 pound sliced root vegetables, such as carrots, turnips, or radishes 2 tablespoons beef broth 2 cups vegetable broth Salt, to taste Procedure Place the onions and root vegetables in a large stock pot. Add the beef and the beef stock on top. Bring to a simmer over low heat for 30 minutes, stirring well. When the beef and vegetables look cooked, add the vegetable broth and salt. Bring back to a simmer and serve.
