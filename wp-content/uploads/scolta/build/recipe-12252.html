Date Squares

A wonderful square recipe with the flavor of dates. Also see the Date Bar recipe. Ingredients Crumb mixture 1 ½ cup (360 g) rolled oats 1 cup (240 g) brown sugar (packed) 1 ½ cup (260 g) all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon baking soda ¼ teaspoon salt 1 cup of melted butter Filling 2 cups (480 g) chopped dates ½ cup (120 g) brown sugar 1 cup (240 ml) water 1 tablespoon lemon juice Procedure In a mixing bowl, combine rolled oats, 1 cup brown sugar, flour, baking soda, salt and melted butter. Mix until crumbly. Press half the crumb mixture into a parchment-lined and greased square baking pan. In a saucepan over low heat, cook dates, sugar and water until thickened and smooth (about 10 minutes). Add lemon juice and mix well. Spread date mixture over crumble layer. Sprinkle remaining crumb mixture over date filling. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for about 35 minutes, until lightly browned.
