Soft Boiled Meringues

Ingredients Meringue 5 weights (e.g. 100 g) egg white 4 weights (e.g. 80 g) white granulated sugar 1 pinch salt Boiling 3 volumes (750 ml) water 2 volumes (500 ml) milk Toppings (as desired) Custard Whipped cream Chocolate sauce Chopped hazelnuts Procedure Whip all the meringue ingredients together to soft peaks. Boil the water with the milk, then reduce to a simmer. Drop spoonfuls of the meringue mixture into the simmering liquid. Simmer for about 4 minutes then scoop out and dry on kitchen paper rolls. Serve with toppings.
