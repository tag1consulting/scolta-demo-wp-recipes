Malpua (South Asian Sweet Pancake)

Ingredients 1 litre milk ¼ cup all-purpose flour ¼ cup semolina flour Ghee Water Procedure Boil the milk until it reduces to half of its original volume. Roast the semolina until light brown. Mix the all-purpose flour and milk into the toasted semolina to make a batter. It should be smooth and lump-free. Heat the ghee in a skillet. Use a circular pouring motion to pour batter in the pan and make a small pancake. Keep turning over. Take care that it does not become crisp or harsh. Make a sugar syrup of good consistency. Dip the malpua in the syrup. Serve.
