Daniel Webster&#8217;s Chowder

From the 1881 Household Cyclopedia Ingredients 4 tbsp chopped onion Fatty pork 1 quart mashed potatoes 1 ½ pounds sea biscuit, broken 1 tsp thyme 1 tsp summer savory ½ bottle mushroom catsup 1 bottle port or claret ¼ nutmeg pod, grated A few cloves Mace Allspice 6 pounds sea bass or cod, cut into slices 25 oysters Black pepper A few slices lemon Procedure Fry the onion with the fatty pork. Transfer to a large pot. Add all remaining ingredients to pot. Add enough water to cover everything by 1 inch. Bring to a boil. Simmer for 1 hour, stirring gently every so often. Serve.
