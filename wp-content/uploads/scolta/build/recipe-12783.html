Herbed Corn

Ingredients ½ cup butter, softened 2 tablespoon minced fresh parsley 2 tablespoons minced fresh chives 1 teaspoon dried thyme ½ teaspoon salt ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper 7 ears sweet corn, husks and silks carefully removed Procedure Combine all ingredients except corn in small bowl. Mix well. Measure about 1 tablespoon of the mixture and spread over each ear of corn. Wrap each ear of corn in heavy foil. Grill over medium heat 10–15 minutes, or until tender.
