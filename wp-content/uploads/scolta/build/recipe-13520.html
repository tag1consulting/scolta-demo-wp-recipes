Nigerian Fried Yam

Nigerian fried yam is usually eaten with a pepper sauce. Ingredients Yam, peeled and sliced into sticks Salt Vegetable oil Equipment Stovetop Frying pan Sieve Bowl Procedure Rinse the cut yam and place in a bowl. Season with salt. Heat oil in a pan or pot. Add the cut yam little by little to avoid overcrowding. Stir, and cook until the yam is golden. Flip and cook until golden on all sides. Remove fried yam from pan, and drain away any excess oil.
