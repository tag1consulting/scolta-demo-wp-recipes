Simple Nachos

Ingredients 1 pound (450 g) lean ground beef (92% lean or greater) 1 can diced tomatoes with green chiles 2 teaspoons or less taco seasoning mix (mostly cumin) 2 tablespoons sour cream 1 dash Mexican seasoning Corn tortilla chips Procedure In a skillet, brown ground beef with Mexican seasoning and Taco Mix. When beef is browned add the can of diced tomatoes with green chiles, cover and allow to simmer an additional 5 minutes. Put tortilla chips on a large plate. Place 2 tablespoons of sour cream on plate. Sprinkle beef and diced tomato mixture over top of the chips. Serve and enjoy.
