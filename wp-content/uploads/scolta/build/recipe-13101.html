Lady Arundel&#8217;s Manchet

Lady Arundel’s Manchet is a variety of British scone made with yeast rather than baking powder. Ingredients 2 lb fine wheat flour ½ oz salt 2 oz butter 1 egg 1 pint warm milk 1 oz yeast 1 teaspoon caster sugar Procedure Mix the salt into the flour, then rub in the butter Combine the yeast with the sugar. Add it to 1½ cups of the warm milk. Beat the egg, and mix with the yeast and flour. Make a well in the flour, pour in the yeast mixture, and mix into a dough. Shape the dough into small, flat, round cakes about ¾ inch thick and 3 ½ inches across. Mark manchets with lines to form a diamond pattern. Leave to rise for 30 minutes in a warm place. Bake in a moderate oven (350-375 °F / 180-190 °C) until cooked. References White, Florence (1932). Good Things in England. p. 72.
