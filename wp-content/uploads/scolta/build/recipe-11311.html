Ambrosia Fruit Salad

is a sweet mixture of mandarin oranges, fruit cocktail, coconut, and marshmallows coated in whipped cream. It originated in the 1900s and had a period of popularity in the United States. It is widely considered to be a traditional dish, suitable for family gatherings. Below is one interpretation. Ingredients 4 cups of mandarin oranges, drained 2 cans of pineapple, drained 2 cups of grapes 3 cups whipped cream 1 cup sour cream 6–8 cups miniature marshmallows Procedure Mix the mandarin oranges, pineapple, and grapes in a large bowl. Fold in the whipped cream, sour cream, and marshmallows. Chill for at least an hour. Stir again before serving.
