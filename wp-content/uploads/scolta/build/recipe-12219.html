Cucumber Sprout Sandwich

These cucumber sprout sandwiches are stuffed with cucumbers, alfalfa sprouts, tomatoes, and avocados. Ingredients 2 slices whole wheat bread 2 tbsp cream cheese, softened 6 slices peeled cucumber 2 tbsp alfalfa sprouts 1 tsp olive oil 1 tsp red wine vinegar 1 tomato, sliced 1 romaine (cos) lettuce leaf 2 tbsp pepperoncini, sliced ½ avocado, peeled and mashed Salt to taste Pepper to taste Procedure Spread 1 tablespoon cream cheese on each slice of bread. Arrange cucumber slices in a single layer on one slice of bread. Cover same slice of bread with alfalfa sprouts, sprinkle with oil and vinegar. Layer same slice of bread with tomato slices, lettuce, and pepperoncini. Spread mashed avocado on other slice of bread. Add salt and pepper to taste Assemble sandwich and serve immediately.
