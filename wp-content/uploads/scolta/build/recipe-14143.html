Salmon Seasoning

Ingredients ¾ cup paprika ¼ cup dehydrated lemon peel ¼ cup granulated garlic 3 tbsp granulated sugar ¼ cup black peppercorns, cracked ⅓ cup salt 3 tbsp red pepper flakes 1 tbsp cayenne pepper Procedure Combine all ingredients, mixing well. Transfer to an airtight container with a shaker lid, and store in a cool dark place.
