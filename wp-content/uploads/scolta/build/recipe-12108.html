Communion Wafers

Ingredients 1 cup (240 ml) water 2 cups (480 ml) all-purpose flour Procedure Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Knead together the water and flour in a bowl to get a smooth dough. Divide the dough into 4 equally sized portions. Divide each of the 4 equally sized portions into four smaller portions for a total of 16 portions. Roll each of the 16 small portions into small balls. Flatten each of the small portions into small, thin, flat disks. If desired, use a butter knife to cut a small cross onto each wafer. Bake the wafers for 3 minutes. Take the wafers out of the oven and flip them over using a spoon or chopstick. Bake the wafers for another 3 minutes. Take the wafers out of the oven and let them cool. Notes, tips, and variations Use a spoon or chopstick to flip the wafers over to avoid burns. Do not use your bare hands, even if wearing gloves.
