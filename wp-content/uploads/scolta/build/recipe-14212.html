Seasoned Bread and Butter Balls

Ingredients Slices of bread (3 per person) 3 tablespoons butter 3 shakes garlic powder 3 shakes ground oregano leaves Procedure Remove crusts from bread, and add other ingredients onto a microwave safe plate. Make each slice of bread into ball, and lay on same plate. Microwave plate for 25 seconds, wait 30 seconds, and serve.
