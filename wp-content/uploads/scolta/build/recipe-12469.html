Flan de Queso (Cream Cheese Flan)

This recipe for flan de queso was originally contributed by Brenda Ortiz. Ingredients Flan 8 oz cream cheese 14 oz leche condensada (sweetened condensed milk) 12 oz evaporated milk 4 large eggs 1 tsp vanilla extract Caramel 1 cup of white granulated sugar Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) Add cream cheese, leche condensada, evaporated milk, eggs, and vanilla extract into a mixing bowl. Mix ingredients thoroughly at medium speed until smooth. Place the sugar in the bottom of a small circular baking pan. Add the flan mixture on top without mixing the two layers. Fill a large rimmed baking sheet with about 1.5 inch of water. This is called baño de Maria (bain-marie). Carefully place baking sheet with flan mixture in the baño de Maria. Bake flan for 45–50 minutes in the preheated oven. The flan is ready when you insert a toothpick in the center and it comes out clean. Let flan cool until safe to handle. Tip the flan pan upside-down onto a serving plate, and refrigerate for 5 hours. Serve with fresh mint leaves for garnish.
