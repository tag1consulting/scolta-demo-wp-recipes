Philly Cheesesteak

Ingredients 1 pound filet mignon, pounded flat Olive oil Salt Freshly ground black pepper 1 small onion, julienned Hoagie rolls, almost halved American cheese slices, torn Procedure Drizzle filet with olive oil and season with salt and black pepper. Sear in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat for 6 minutes per side. Remove and let rest 7 minutes before slicing into bite-size pieces. Keep warm. Toss onion with a little olive oil and sauté in same pan until lightly browned around edges. To serve, place a big scoop of steak into a hoagie roll. Top with onion and torn cheese. Serve.
