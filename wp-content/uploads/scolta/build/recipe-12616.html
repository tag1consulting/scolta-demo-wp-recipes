Ghavoot (Iranian Nut and Seed Powder)

Ghavoot (قاووت) or ghovvatoo (قووتو) is a nutritionally-dense recipe made of powdered seeds, nuts, and sugar. There are several versions, depending on the components chosen, and their ratios. It is a traditional souvenir of Kerman, Iran. Ingredients Coffee beans Cinnamon Sesame seeds Cottonseeds Nigella seeds Poppy seeds Chickpeas Walnuts Almonds Pistachio nuts Procedure Grind and roast each component. Add an equal weight of sugar; grind everything together. Serve as powder, or blend it with milk or hot water. Notes, tips, and variations Ghavoot is a traditional energizer. You can consume it as a pre-workout snack.
