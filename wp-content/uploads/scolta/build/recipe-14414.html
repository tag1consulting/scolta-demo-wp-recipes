Spiced Oysters

This recipe for spiced oysters has been adapted and modernized from the 1881 Household Cyclopedia. Ingredients 100 oysters 12 whole cloves 24 whole allspice Mace, to taste Cayenne pepper, to taste Salt to taste 1 cup cider vinegar Procedure Strain the oyster liquor through a sieve, and put it in a saucepan. Add the oysters, spices, salt, and cider vinegar. Place them over a slow fire, and as soon as they boil remove them from the heat. Pour the oysters into a large bowl, and let cool. When cooled, cover and store the oysters.
