Nigerian Barbecued Chicken Wings

Nigerian barbecued chicken wings are usually eaten as a side dish or served alongside grains like jollof rice and fried rice. They are especially common at parties, gatherings and festivals. Ingredients 1 cup flour 2 tablespoons cornmeal 1 teaspoon black pepper 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon ginger powder 1 teaspoon paprika powder 10 chicken wingettes Equipment Oven or grill Bowl Baking tray Procedure Combine the flour, cornmeal, pepper, salt, sugar, ginger, and paprika in a bowl. Add the chicken pieces, and rub the mixture into them well so they are completely coated. Set in the fridge, and allow the chicken to rest for 1 hour. Arrange the chicken pieces on a baking tray or on a grill. Grill or bake until cooked through (about 45 minutes baking at 375°F).
