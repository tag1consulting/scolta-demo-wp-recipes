Peach Curry Chutney

This chutney is great served with pork chops or curry. Ingredients 4 peaches, peeled and sliced into 10ths ½ apple cider 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar 1 pinch of salt ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper 1 tbsp curry powder 2 tbsp tikka masala paste Procedure Blend all ingredients on high speed just until mostly liquid. There should still be chunks. Bring to a boil over medium high heat until reduced by ⅓. Serve warm.
