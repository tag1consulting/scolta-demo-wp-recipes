Percolator Punch

Ingredients 2¼ cups pineapple juice 2 cups cranberry juice 1¾ cups water ¼ teaspoon salt ½ cup brown sugar 1 tablespoon cloves 1 tablespoon allspice 3 sticks cinnamon Special equipment Percolator-style coffee pot Procedure Place the pineapple juice, cranberry juice, water, and salt in the bottom reservoir of the percolator. Place the brown sugar, cloves, allspice, and cinnamon in the top compartment. Heat for 10 minutes. Serve hot.
