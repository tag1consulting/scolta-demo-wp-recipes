Ham and Cheese Crepes

Ingredients 12 prepared crêpes 1 ½ cups shredded cheddar cheese 12 slices cooked, thinly sliced ham 2 Tbsp finely-chopped green onion ½ teaspoon salt ⅛ teaspoon pepper ½ cup sour cream ½ teaspoon prepared mustard ⅓ cup bread crumbs 3 Tbsp melted butter Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. In small bowl, combine cheese, onion, salt, pepper, sour cream, and mustard. Lay one slice of ham on each crêpe. Spread about 1 tbsp of the mixture over each ham slice. Roll up crêpes, and arrange in 13×9-inch baking pan. Combine melted butter and bread crumbs, and sprinkle over crepes. Cover pan loosely with foil. Bake for 15–20 minutes at 350°F.
