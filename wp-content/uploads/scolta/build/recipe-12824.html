Homemade Ketchup

Ingredients 8 quarts (950 ml) ripe tomatoes ½ cup (120 g) white granulated sugar 2 tablespoons dry mustard 1 tablespoon ground allspice 2 cups cider vinegar 3 tablespoons salt 1 tablespoon black pepper 1 teaspoon ground cloves Procedure Scald tomatoes, and press through a coarse sieve to remove seeds and skins. Let cool. Measure out 1 gallon (3.8 litres) of tomato pulp, and combine with the remaining ingredients. Let simmer slowly for 3 hours. Pour hot ketchup into hot sterilized 1-pint jars and seal. Process for 15 minutes in boiling water bath. Notes, tips, and variations For the canning process, add 5 minutes boiling time for altitudes from 1,001–6,000 feet (300–1800 metres), and another 5 minutes if over 6,000 feet (1800 metres).
