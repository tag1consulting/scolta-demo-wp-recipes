Tvorog (Russian Farmer Cheese)

Tvorog is a Russian cheese. Ingredients 1 part cultured buttermilk 2 parts milk Procedure Mix milk and buttermilk in a pot, and cover with a lid. Let stand for 1–2 days, until the mixture thickens to a consistency of thin yogurt. Heat mixture gently, without letting it boil, until the whey separates from the curds. Pour out through cheese cloth. For a drier tvorog, hang it up for 1–12 hours to let it drain. See also Sirniki
