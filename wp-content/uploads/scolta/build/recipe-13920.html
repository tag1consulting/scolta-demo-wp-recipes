Pulut Durian (Durian with Sticky Rice)

Ingredients 1 kg glutinous rice 1 pinch of salt 1 durian 500 ml coconut milk 300 g sugar 2 screwpine leaves (or pandan paste) Procedure Steam the glutinous rice with a pinch of salt until it is cooked. Combine the flesh of durian with coconut milk, sugar, salt and screwpine leaves. Boil them together until the sauce thickens. Serve it hot with the rice.
