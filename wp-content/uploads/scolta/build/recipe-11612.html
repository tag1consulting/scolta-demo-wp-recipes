Black Pepper Syrup

Ingredients 1 tsp ground black pepper ¼ cup (60 ml) water ¼ cup (60 ml) granulated white sugar 1 tsp lemon juice (optional) Procedure Combine pepper, water, sugar and lemon in a saucepan, and heat slowly until the sugar dissolves and the mixture is a light-brown colour. Remove from heat and let cool for 1 hour. Notes, tips, and variations The lemon juice can be replaced by lemon rind, or lime. Makes a good sauce for fresh fruit or fruit with soured cream.
