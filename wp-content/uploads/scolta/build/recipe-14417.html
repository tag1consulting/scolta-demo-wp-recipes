Spicy Black Beans

Ingredients 1½ tbsp chile oil ½ tbsp vegetable oil 2 cups canned black beans, rinsed and drained 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp freshly-ground black pepper 2 tsp rice vinegar 1½ tsp soy sauce 1 Thai bird chile, minced Procedure Heat both oils in a large skillet over medium high heat. Add all remaining ingredients and toss; cook just until heated through. Serve immediately as a side dish, or cool slightly and serve as a salad.
