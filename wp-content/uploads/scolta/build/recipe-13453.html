Mushroom Fried Rice

Ingredients Rice 5½ tbsp canola oil 5 cloves garlic, minced 1 big onion, sliced 2 green chiles, sliced 4 chicken fillet, minced 150 g prawn, skin removed ½ can button mushrooms ½ pack enoki mushrooms ½ pack shiitake mushrooms, washed and sliced 1 cup (700 g) cooked rice 2 tbsp oyster sauce 2 tbsp chicken powder 1½ tbsp salt 1½ tbsp hoisin sauce Garnish Lettuce Pickles Tomato Cucumber Shrimp crackers Chilli sauce Procedure Heat the oil in a non-stick pan, and fry the garlic and big onion until fragrant. Add the prawn and minced chicken fillet. Stir fry until the prawn and the chicken change color. Add the button mushroom, enoki mushroom, and shitake mushroom. Then, add the rice together with oyster sauce, chicken powder, salt, and hoisin sauce. Serve hot with garnishes.
