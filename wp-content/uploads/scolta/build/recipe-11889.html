Chicken Bog II

From Come and Get It! by Jr. Welfare League of Talladega Ingredients 6 cups water 1 tbsp salt 1 medium onion, finely chopped 1 ea. (3 lb) whole chicken 1 cup long-grain rice ½ lb smoked sausage, sliced 2 tbsp herb seasoning 1 packet “chicken and herb” seasoning Procedure Put water, salt, onion, and chicken in pot. Boil until tender (about 1 hour). Remove chicken, let cool, and remove bones. Chop meat into bite-sized pieces. Skim off fat from juices. Measure 3 ½ cups of this broth into a 6-quart saucepan. Add rice, chicken pieces and smoked sausage, herb seasoning and “chicken and herb” seasoning. Cook these ingredients for 30 minutes. Bring to a boil and reduce heat and simmer, keeping covered the entire time. If rice mixture is too wet, cook uncovered until desired consistency.
