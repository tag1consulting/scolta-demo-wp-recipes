German Style Sweet Mustard (Senf)

Senf is German for “mustard”. This particular senf, known as ‘Süßer Senf’, has a sweet, yet sharp taste that makes it great on sandwiches and crackers. While this recipe is from a 3rd or 4th generation German American, such recipes are said to originate from the southern parts of Germany (Bavaria). Ingredients 1.7 oz dry ground mustard ½ tsp sugar ½ cup vinegar ½ tsp salt pepper Procedure Mix sugar, mustard, salt, and pepper together. Mix in water. Mix in vinegar last, as the mustard will lose some of its flavour if vinegar is introduced too early. Stir using a wire whisk to prevent lumps. Add some water if too thick. Notes, tips, and variations It gets better as it cools and ages awhile.
