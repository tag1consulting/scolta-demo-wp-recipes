Ove (Nigerian Yam Moi Moi)

Ove is a typical Nigerian pudding prepared from yam. Ingredients 2 tsp chile pepper paste 1 medium-sized yam, peeled and grated ¼ cup shredded fish 2 tbsp groundnut oil ¼ ea. onion, chopped 1 pinch salt 1 wrapping leaf (e.g. banana leaf) Procedure Remove unwanted substances from the fish. Then, rinse and cook for few minutes. Combine the groundnut oil, onion, pepper paste, and fish to make a sauce. Stir the sauce into the grated yam along with the salt. Wrap this mixture up in the leaf, then boil for a few minutes to cook it.
