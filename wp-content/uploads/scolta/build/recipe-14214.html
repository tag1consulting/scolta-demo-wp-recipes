Seasoned Salt I

Ingredients 1 cup (300 g/11 oz) salt ⅓ cup (50 g/1.8 oz) garlic powder ⅓ cup (50 g/1.8 oz) paprika ¼ cup (30 g/1.1 oz) celery seed ¼ cup (30 g/1.1 oz) dehydrated lemon peel 3 tbsp dried rosemary 3 tbsp dried parsley Procedure Combine all ingredients in an airtight container. Stored in a cool dark place, this will keep indefinitely.
