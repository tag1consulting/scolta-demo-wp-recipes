Monkey Bread I

Monkey bread is a type of sweet pull-apart bread, typically flavored with cinnamon sugar. Ingredients Bread 4 cans of biscuit dough, each biscuit cut into quarters ¾ cup white granulated sugar 1 tsp cinnamon Glaze ¾ cup butter or margarine ¾ cup brown sugar ¼ cup white granulated sugar 1½ tsp cinnamon Procedure Combine cinnamon and sugar for rolling. Roll biscuit sections in sugar mixture, placing in greased bundt pan or high-sided cake pan. Do not pack tightly. Bring glaze ingredients to a boil, then pour over the dough in the pan. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 40–50 minutes. Invert onto plate, and serve warm.
