Tuna Curry

Ingredients 3 tablespoons (45 g / 45 ml) butter or olive oil 1 medium onion, diced 1 tablespoon (8 g) flour 1 tablespoon (6 g) curry powder 250 ml (1 cup + 1 tablespoon) milk 100 g (3½ oz) frozen vegetables, such as carrot slices, green beans, or peas 1 can (150 g/5¼ oz) tuna in water, drained Salt to taste Pepper to taste Procedure Heat the oil or melt the butter in a saucepan. Sauté the onion in the butter or olive oil until transparent. Add the flour and stir until the flour is completely incorporated. Add the curry powder and stir until it is completely incorporated. Add the milk and frozen veggies and stir until the mixture gets hot again. Stir in the tuna. Salt and pepper to taste. Tuna is naturally salty, so add the tuna before tasting. Heat thoroughly and serve over rice.
