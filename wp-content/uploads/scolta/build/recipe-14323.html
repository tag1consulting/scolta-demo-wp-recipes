Smoky Tuna Melts

Ingredients 1 container (4.5 oz) hickory smoke-flavored albacore tuna 2 tbsp melted butter Juice of 1 lemon wedge 4 slices white bread 2 slices smoked mozzarella cheese Procedure Combine tuna, melted butter, and lemon juice. Spread some of the tuna mixture on each slice of bread. Top with cheese. Broil until the cheese is melted.
