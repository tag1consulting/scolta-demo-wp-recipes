No-Bake Matsa Cake

Ingredients 7 ounces brown sugar 2 ounces cocoa powder 2 ounces raisins, soaked in 1 tablespoon sweet red wine ½ lb butter, melted and cooled slightly 7 sheets of matzah, crumbled Procedure Combine brown sugar, cocoa, soaked raisins, and melted butter. Add this mixture to the crumbled matzah and mix well. Divide the mixture into two 9-inch springform or other cake pans. Knead the batter to create a flat and smooth surface. Refrigerate for several hours.
