Thai Lemonade

This is the Thai version of lemonade. Ingredients 125 ml water 100 g sugar 2 stalks lemongrass 2 cm piece of ginger, peeled and cut into pieces 1 liter carbonated water Lime, sliced, for garnish Procedure Bring the water to a boil with the sugar, lemon grass, and ginger. Boil for 20 minutes. Allow the syrup to cool, then remove lemongrass and ginger. Pour the syrup in 4 large glasses, and add the carbonated water. Garnish glasses with lime discs.
