Italian Tricolor Soup

Soup alla Nazionale is a patriotic Italian soup. This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook The Cook’s Decameron: A Study In Taste, Containing Over Two Hundred Recipes For Italian Dishes by Emily Waters. Ingredients Savoury custard Spinach Tomatoes Broth Procedure Make a savoury custard and divide it into three parts. Leave one white, color one red with tomato, and color the last one green with spinach. Put a layer of each colored custard in a buttered saucepan, and cook for about 10 minutes until solidified. Cut the solidified custards into dice. Put the colored custard pieces into a soup tureen and pour broth over top.
