Crock Pot Potato Soup

Ingredients 5 large potatoes, diced 3 medium onions, diced 5 cups water 1 tablespoon parsley 1 stalk celery, diced 1 teaspoon salt Black pepper, to taste 4 cups chicken broth 4 to 5 tablespoons butter 1 (13-ounce) can evaporated milk Procedure In a slow cooker, combine potatoes, onions, water, parsley, celery, salt, pepper, chicken broth and butter. Cook until done — on low heat, 9 to 11 hours, or on high heat, 3 to 4 hours. When done, add milk and reheat. Serve plain or use an immersion blender/put mixture in blender for a smoother soup. Reheat if necessary. Tips, Notes, and Variations Garnish each serving with a dollop of sour cream and chives, if desired. If you don’t have evaporated milk on hand, it’s possible to mix 1.5 cups of cold milk with flour to form a thick slurry to mix it in with good results.
