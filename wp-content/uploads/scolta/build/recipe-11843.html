Cheese Pierogi Filling

Ingredients 8 oz farmer’s cheese (a soft, NOT a hard cheese) 4 oz cream cheese 1 egg 2 tbsp flour Procedure Mash together all of the ingredients until well mixed. Notes, tips, and variations Farmer’s cheese is similar to a dry-curd cottage cheese and is unavailable outside predominately Eastern European and Jewish areas. Dry-curd cottage cheese is a sub-par substitution, and must be drained of all excess liquid if it is to be used. If the farmer’s cheese is unsalted, add 1 tsp salt.
