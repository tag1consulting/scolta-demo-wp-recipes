Barbecued Spare Ribs

are a classic American barbecue meal made from pork. This version is cooked in the oven, not on a barbecue, and uses a barbecue sauce with Asian-associated flavors like soy sauce, rice vinegar, and garlic. Ingredients 2 pounds (910 g) pork spare ribs ¼ cup (60 ml / 2 oz) soy sauce 2 tablespoons honey 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce 2 tablespoons white vinegar 1 tablespoon sake 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 teaspoon sugar ¼ teaspoon of freshly ground white pepper 2 tablespoons pork stock Freshly-ground black pepper and salt to taste 2 teaspoons cumin (optional) Procedure Place spare ribs in a large baking dish in one layer. In a small bowl, combine the remaining ingredients. Pour over spare ribs and coat on both sides. Marinate overnight, basting occasionally. Preheat the oven to 375 °F (191 °C). Place a large, shallow roasting pan on the oven bottom. Fill with 0.5 inches (1.3 cm) water. Place spare ribs on a roasting rack. Roast ribs for 45 minutes. Raise oven temperature to 450 °F (232 °C), and roast for 15 more minutes. Serve hot or cold. Notes, tips, and variations Alternatively, bake at 375 °F (191 °C) for 20 mins then lower the temp to 325 °F (163 °C) for 1 hour; this renders most of the fat and produces very tender meat. To actually barbecue the ribs on a grill, get grill temperature to 225 °F (107 °C) and cook for 5-6 hours.
