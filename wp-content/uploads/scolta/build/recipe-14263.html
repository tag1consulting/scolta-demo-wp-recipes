Shrimp Fritters (Accra)

Ingredients ¼ cup (60 ml) green pepper, chopped 2 spring onions, chopped 1 small onion, chopped 1 Scotch bonnet or other hot pepper, minced 2 cups (480 ml) flour 2 tsp (10 ml) baking powder 1 tsp (5 ml) sugar ½ lb (~225 g) chopped shrimp 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tsp (5 ml) thyme 1 tsp (5 ml) parsley Black pepper to taste Salt to taste Water Vegetable oil Procedure Combine green pepper, spring onions, onions, and scotch bonnet in a bowl. Add remaining ingredients except water, and mix thoroughly. Stir in enough water to make a soft mixture using a spoon. Beat mixture until is smooth. Using a pan or deep fryer, drop spoonfuls in to hot oil, and fry until all sides are brown.
