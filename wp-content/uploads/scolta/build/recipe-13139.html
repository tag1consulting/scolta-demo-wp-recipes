Lemon Icing

Ingredients 1 egg yolk 2 tablespoons lemon juice 1 pinch of salt 1½ cups confectioner’s sugar Procedure Break the egg yolk into a small bowl. Add the lemon juice and salt; beat lightly. Gradually beat in confectioner’s sugar. Notes, tips, and variations If you want extra-stiff icing, add a little more powdered sugar.
