Apple Pudding

Ingredients ¾ cup white granulated sugar 1 egg ¼ cup flour 1¼ teaspoons baking powder 1 pinch of salt 1 teaspoon vanilla 1 cup chopped walnuts or hickory nuts ½ cup chopped apples Procedure Beat sugar and egg until smooth. Beat in flour, baking powder, salt, and vanilla. Stir walnuts or hickory nuts and apples into the mixture. Pour into a greased Dutch oven or 9 x 9-inch pan. Bake at 325°F (160°C) for 30 minutes. Notes, tips, and variations If you like topping, use whipped cream or ice cream.
