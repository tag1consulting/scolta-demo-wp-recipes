Cinnamon Rolls

Cinnamon buns are another name for cinnamon rolls. These sweet rolls are created with flour and sugar. Ingredients Dough Milk, warm Active dry yeast 1 pinch sugar 1 egg, room temperature 2½ tbsp sugar Butter, softened 3 cups flour 1 pinch salt Assembly 16 tbsp (8 oz) butter, softened 1 cup sugar 2 tbsp ground cinnamon Procedure Dough Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk. Stir in the sugar, and let it sit for 5 minutes to activate the yeast. Combine the egg, 2½ tbsp sugar, and softened butter until smooth. Mix in the milk mixture. Combine the flour and salt. Gradually stir this dry mixture into the wet mixture until it forms a dough. Knead the dough for at least 5 minutes until it is smooth, stretchy, and no longer sticky. If it is still sticky, add a little more flour. If it is too dry, add a little bit of warm water. Cover the dough in the bowl, and let rest for 5 minutes to make it more controllable because it becomes too soft and flexible to roll into the proper cylindrical shape. Assembly Place the dough on a lightly floured surface, and roll it out into a flat rectangle. Lightly dust the dough if needed to prevent sticking. Spread the softened butter in a thin layer over the dough. Combine the sugar and cinnamon, and sprinkle it evenly over the butter. Roll up the dough rectangle like a mat so you get a cylindrical log. Cut the log into slices a few inches thick. Arrange the slices cut-side up in a baking pan. Bake in the oven at 350°F until it turns golden brown. Cool slightly and serve warm.
