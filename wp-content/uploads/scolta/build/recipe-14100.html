Rosemary Garlic Oil

Infused oils like this rosemary garlic oil can be easily made in your own home. Ingredients ½ gallon any flavorless high-smoke point oil, such as canola, grapeseed, peanut, or safflower 6 sprigs rosemary 20 cloves garlic ½ tsp salt Procedure Heat the oil in your tallest largest pot over low heat. Add remaining ingredients and warm for 1 hour. Strain and keep in an airtight container. Some may want to keep this in the refrigerator, others may not.
