Garden Bulgur

Ingredients 2 Tbsp margarine ½ cup chopped celery 1 medium onion, chopped (or a little less) 1 cup fresh sliced mushrooms 1 cup bulgur or cracked wheat ¼ tsp dried dillweed ¼ tsp dried whole oregano ½ tsp salt ¼ tsp pepper 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth Procedure Melt margarine in a large skillet; add celery, onion and mushrooms. Stir until vegetables are tender. Add bulgur and cook until golden. Add seasonings and broth. Cover and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes. Serve. Notes, tips, and variations If you are cooking on a gas stove, make sure to stir once or twice during the 15 minute simmering. If available, try sprinkling in a little fresh parsley at the end of the cook time.
