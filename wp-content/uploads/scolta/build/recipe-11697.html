Browned Rice

Ingredients ½ cup (120 g) uncooked rice 1 can beef consommé ½ stick margarine 1 small onion, chopped Procedure Sauté onion in margarine over medium heat. Mix rice and consommé in a baking dish. Pour onion and margarine over rice and consommé and mix. Bake covered for 30 minutes at 350 °F (180 °C). Remove from oven, and add ½ can water over rice mixture. Cover and cook an additional 30 minutes.
