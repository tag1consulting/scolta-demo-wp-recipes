Bubble and Squeak

Bubble and squeak is a traditional English vegetable dish made from the leftover vegetables from a Sunday roast. The name is derived from the sound the dish makes while cooking. Ingredients 1 medium onion (optional), chopped 1 tablespoon fat or oil Various cooked vegetables (e.g. potatoes, cabbage, carrots, peas, and/or Brussels sprouts) Procedure Fry the chopped onion in fat or oil. Chop the vegetables, and add to the pan. Cook over a medium heat till the underside is golden brown and crispy, then turn it, scraping the crispy bits into the mix. Repeat the turning and scraping a few times until browned and cooked through.
