Tofu and Couscous

Ingredients ½ cup fresh red bell peppers, diced ½ cup raisins 1½ cups cubed tofu 2 cups water 2 cups instant couscous 1 tsp olive oil Procedure Sauté tofu, red peppers, and raisins together until soft. Bring the water to a boil, stir in the couscous, cover, remove from the heat, and let rest for 5 minutes until the water is absorbed. Fluff the couscous with a fork. Combine the tofu mixture with couscous and olive oil. Flavor to taste with curry or other mixed spices.
