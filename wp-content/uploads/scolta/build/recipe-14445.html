Spinach Calzones

Ingredients 426 g ricotta cheese 2 eggs 9 g dried Italian seasoning 340 g shredded mozzarella cheese 100 g freshly grated Parmesan cheese 284 g frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry Salt to taste Pepper to taste 908 g frozen white bread dough, thawed Procedure Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). In a large bowl, mix together the ricotta cheese, eggs, Italian seasoning, mozzarella cheese, Parmesan cheese and spinach. Set aside. Divide the bread dough into 8 pieces and roll each piece out to about an 8-inch circle. Spoon about ½ cup of the ricotta filling onto each circle, fold over and seal the edges. Place onto a greased cookie sheet. Bake for 30 minutes in the preheated oven, or until nicely browned on the tops and bottoms.
