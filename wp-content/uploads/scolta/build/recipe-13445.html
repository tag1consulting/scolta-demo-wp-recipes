Mulling Spice with Anise

Ingredients 2 cups (320 g/11 oz) dried orange peel 2 cups (250 g/8.8 oz) broken cinnamon sticks 1 cup (125 g/4.4 oz) whole allspice berries 1 cup (125 g/4.4 oz) whole cloves 4 broken star anise Procedure Mix ingredients together well. Store in closed jar. Use Mix ¼ cup (150 g/5.3 oz) of spice mixture per 16 cups (4 L/1.1 US gal) of wine, cider, or apple juice. Simmer for 30 minutes. Serve hot in mugs with cinnamon stick stirrers or fill a punch bowl and garnish with orange slices.
