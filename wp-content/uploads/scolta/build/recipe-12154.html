Crab Fried Rice

Ingredients 5 tbsp olive oil 2 cloves garlic, minced 2 cloves red onion, sliced 150 g bean sprouts, soaked in hot water 2 big red chilli, cut into small pieces 2 cans (200 g) crab, drained 3 scallion, cut into 1 cm pieces 1 plate (500 g) cooked rice 2 tbsp fish sauce 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp black pepper Procedure Stir fry the crab in the 5 tbsp oil for 1 minute. Remove from the heat, and reserve. Heat the remaining oil, and use to stir fry the garlic, red onion, scallion, and red chillies for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the fried crab and bean sprouts. Stir until the crab changes color. Add the rice, fish sauce, salt, and black pepper. Stir until well-mixed. Serve hot.
