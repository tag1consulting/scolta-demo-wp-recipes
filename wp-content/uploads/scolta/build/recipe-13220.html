Macaroni and Cheese (Microwaved)

Ingredients Uncooked macaroni pasta Cheese, grated ½ cup milk Salt Pepper Chives, chopped Water Procedure Add macaroni in a bowl and add water for the macaroni to absorb. Cook in the microwave for 3 minutes. Remove from the microwave. Add cheese and milk then stir. Insert into the microwave again for 30 seconds. Add salt and pepper Add chives for garnish
