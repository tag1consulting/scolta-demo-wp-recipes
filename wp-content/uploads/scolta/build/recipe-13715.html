Peach Cobbler

Ingredients Crust 1 cup all-purpose flour ½ cup butter ¼ cup milk Filling 2 cans (15 oz / 425 g) peaches ½ cup white granulated sugar Procedure Combine flour, butter, and milk. Cut together to make dough and set aside. Pour peaches in 9×13-inch baking pan. Sprinkle sugar on top. Roll out crust and place on top of peaches. Bake at 350 °F or until top is browned lightly (approximately one hour). See also Easy Peach Cobbler Sweet Peach Cobbler
