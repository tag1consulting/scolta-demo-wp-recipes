Persian Apple Dessert

Ingredients 3 medium apples, peeled and cut up 2–3 tablespoons white granulated sugar 2 tablespoons lemon juice 1 tablespoon rose or orange flower water 1 dash of salt Procedure Place half the apples and the remaining ingredients in blender. Cover and blend until coarsely chopped, 20–30 seconds. Add remaining apples; blend until coarsely chopped. Notes, tips, and variations Rose water and orange flower water can be found in gourmet and specialty shops.
