Dry Rub

A dry rub useful for ribs, pork roast or whatever you like. After rubbing on the meat, let it sit in the refrigerator for about an hour. Ingredients ¼ cup paprika ¼ cup light brown sugar 3 tbsp salt 1 tsp dry mustard 1 tsp ground black pepper ½ tsp cayenne pepper ½ tsp ground cumin Procedure Combine ingredients in a Zip-Lock bag, shaking thoroughly. Store in airtight container.
