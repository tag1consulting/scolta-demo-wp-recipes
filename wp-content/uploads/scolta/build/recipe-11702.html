Bubble Tea

Bubble tea is a tea beverage with black tapioca pearls mixed in. Ingredients ½ cup (125 ml) cooked boba tapioca pearls 1 cup (250 ml) crushed ice 1 cup (250 ml) very strong chilled black tea 1 cup (250 ml) milk, to taste Honey or granulated sugar, to taste Procedure Place pearls in a large glass. Combine all remaining ingredients in a cocktail shaker. Shake vigorously until mixture is frothy. Pour into glass and serve with large-diameter straws. Notes, tips, and variations If you don’t want the drink to be frothy, simply stir the ingredients together instead of shaking.
