Rosemary Grilled Salmon Steaks

Ingredients 5 ea. 3-ounce salmon steaks 1 tbsp canola oil 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar 1 rosemary sprig 2 tsp black pepper Procedure Combine the oil, vinegar, rosemary, and pepper. Add salmon steaks, and marinate for 15 minutes. Place the steaks on a hot grill, cooking on each side for 3 minutes until flaky. Notes, tips, and variations Use cedar/alder planks or charcoal for added flavor.
