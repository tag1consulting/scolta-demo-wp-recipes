Seven Minute Icing

Ingredients 1 egg white 1 cup granulated sugar 3 tbsp cold water ½ tsp baking powder ½ tsp flavoring Procedure Put the egg white, sugar, and water in the top of a double boiler. Place over boiling water and beat with a rotary egg beater seven minutes. Then add baking powder and flavoring. Remove from heat and beat until thick. Spread in swirls over cake.
