Sour Cream Dip

Ingredients 2 cups (480 ml) sour cream ¼ cup (60 ml) mayonnaise 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill, or 1 tbsp dried and crumbled dill 2 tbsp grated onion 2 tbsp chopped chives 1 tbsp chopped parsley Salt Ground pepper Procedure Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, dill, onion, chives, and parsley. Season to taste with salt and pepper, then chill.
