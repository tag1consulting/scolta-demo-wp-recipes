Common Sauce (Hartshorne)

Common Sauce is a multi-purpose sauce that can be used for a number of meat-based dishes and side vegetables. This recipe is an interpreted method from the original recipe in Henry Hartshorne’s the Household Cyclopedia. Ingredients Veal slices Ham slices 2 onions 2 parsnips 2 cloves of garlic 2 cloves 500 ml broth 175 ml white wine 2 slices of lemon Procedure Combine veal, ham, onions, parsnips, garlic, and cloves in a large saucepan. Add broth, wine, and lemon slices. Simmer over low heat for 15 to 20 minutes, or until reduced. Sieve the sauce and serve. References This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne.
