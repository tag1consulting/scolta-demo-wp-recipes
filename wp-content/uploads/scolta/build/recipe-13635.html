Oven Pancakes (Vegan)

Ingredients 1 cup (236 ml) flour 2 teaspoons baking powder ½ teaspoon baking soda ½ teaspoon salt 2 tablespoon white granulated sugar 1 cup (236 ml) soy milk 2 tablespoons vegetable oil 2 teaspoons vanilla 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar Procedure Preheat oven to 230 °C (446 °F) and oil an 8-inch oven-safe skillet. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar. Add the soy milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla; stir well. Stir in the apple cider vinegar, mix well, and pour immediately into the skillet. Bake at 230 °C (446 °F) for 18 to 20 minutes, and serve. Notes, tips, and variations When a toothpick is inserted into the completed product, it should come out clean.
