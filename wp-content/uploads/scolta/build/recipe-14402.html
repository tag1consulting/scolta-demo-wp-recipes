Sparkling Lemonade with Fresh Herbs

Ingredients 4 lemons 4 tablespoons sugar 1 liter carbonated water A few leaves of fresh mint and/or lemon balm (Melissa officinalis) Procedure Juice the lemons, and dissolve the sugar in the juice. Add carbonated water and the fresh herb leaves. Serve cold. Notes, tips, and variations Lemon juice may be substituted by orange juice or other citrus juice, if desired.
