Portobello Eggs

Ingredients 4–5 large portobello mushrooms 3 eggs Salt Pepper Olive oil Shredded Parmesan cheese Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. Wipe mushrooms clean with a damp cloth, then remove stems and discard. Brush the outsides with olive oil. Beat eggs with salt and pepper. Set mushrooms on a cookie sheet and fill each with egg. Top with Parmesan cheese and bake until eggs are fully cooked (about 10 minutes).
