Poundo Potato

Potato poundo is a Nigerian swallow made from potato flour. Ingredients Potato flour Water Procedure Bring water to boil in a small pot. Stir in potato flour until you get a thick mixture. Continue stirring to prevent lumps from forming. Add some water, and cook for 5 minutes over low heat. Stir again to ensure that it’s smooth. Remove from the fire and put in a wrapper. Serve with any soup of your choice.
