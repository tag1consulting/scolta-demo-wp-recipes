Curry Fried Rice

Ingredients 3 tbsp cooking oil 2 tbsp sesame oil 2 cloves garlic, crushed and minced 2 scallions, cut into 2 cm pieces 200 g sukiyaki beef 100 g bean sprouts, soaked in hot water 200 g chicken, diced 1 cup (600 g) Basmati rice, cooked 10 g chilli powder 20 g curry powder 2 tbsp light soy sauce 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp pepper Procedure Heat the oils in a non-stick frying pan. Add the garlic and scallion, and fry until fragrant. Add sukiyaki beef and chicken. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute until golden brown. Add bean sprouts, basmati rice, chilli powder and curry powder. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Stir until well-mixed.
