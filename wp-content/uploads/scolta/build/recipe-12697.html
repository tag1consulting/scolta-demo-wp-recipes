Grilled Fruit Pouches

Ingredients 4 peaches, peeled and sliced into eighths 2 cups crystallized ginger, crushed 4 plums, quartered 6 tbsp white granulated sugar ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, divided 6 oz brandy 1 pinch of salt 1 tbsp finely-grated lemon zest Procedure In a large bowl, toss together fruit, sugar, salt, lemon, and brandy. Set aside. In four large sheets of aluminum foil, scatter a foundation of crystallized ginger. Place the fruit mixture on top of the ginger. Top with 1 tablespoon of the butter. Fold into a pouch. Place pouches in the hot coal area of a kettle grill. Cook 7 minutes. Serve warm.
