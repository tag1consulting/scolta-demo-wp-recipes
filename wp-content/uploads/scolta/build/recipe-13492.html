New World Affogato

Ingredients 1 scoop vanilla ice cream 1 tsp coffee powder 1 tsp brandy 1½ tsp sugar 1½ tsp cinnamon powder, for garnishing (optional) Procedure Place the ice cream in a coffee cup. Boil water in a pan, and then add coffee and sugar to it, and then mix well. Add brandy to coffee mixture. Pour coffee mixture over the ice-cream. Serve immediately, garnished with cinnamon powder.
