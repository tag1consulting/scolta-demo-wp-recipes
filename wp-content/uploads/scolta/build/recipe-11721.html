Burple Nurples

This recipe is loosely derived from the animated television show Chowder. It is unclear whether it is intended for consumption. Ingredients 1 egg 2 sticks butter, melted 1 cup granulated sugar 1 pinch ground coriander 1 cup flour Procedure Preheat oven to 320°F. In a bowl, gently crack egg. Mix in sugar, then butter, flour, and coriander to make a dough. Shape dough into small balls. Place dough balls on a cookie sheet. Bake for 1 hour. References
