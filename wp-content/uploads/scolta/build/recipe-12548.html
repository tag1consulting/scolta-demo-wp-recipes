Fruit for the Day Smoothie

Ingredients 1 can (397 g / 14 oz) Mandarin oranges (including syrup) 2 frozen bananas, peeled and sliced 2 cups (160 g / 5.6 oz) frozen strawberries 1 ½ cups (375 ml / 12.7 oz) milk Procedure Add all ingredients to a blender in the order listed above. Hold down the lid, and blend on low speed for 30 seconds. Blend on high speed for 1 minute. Pour into a glass and enjoy.
