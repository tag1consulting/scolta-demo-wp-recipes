Homemade Fines Herbes

Fines herbes is a mixture of herbs used in French cuisine. It normally contains parsley, chives, tarragon and chervil. Ingredients 1 tablespoon chopped tarragon 1 tablespoon chopped chervil 1 tablespoon chopped chives 1 tablespoon chopped parsley Procedure Combine all ingredients together. Use as directed in your recipe.
