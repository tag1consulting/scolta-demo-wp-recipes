Pumpkin Substitute

Pumpkins are unavailable most of the time in some countries. An excellent substitute for all uses is made from the following: Ingredients 1 kg (about 2 lbs) mixed carrot and swede 500 g (about 1 lb) plain yogurt Procedure Purée the vegetable mix until smooth. Add the yogurt. Use in place of pumpkin purée. Notes, tips, and variations Frozen carrot and swede mix makes this a very easy substitution. Purée thoroughly to obtain a smooth mixture. The ingredients can be added whole to foods that will be puréed later. Other winter squashes may also be used as substitutes for pumpkin
