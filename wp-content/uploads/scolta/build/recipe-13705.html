Paste for Tarts

This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne. Ingredients 1 ounce white granulated sugar 1 pound fine flour 3 ounces butter 1 gill cream, boiling Procedure Combine the sugar and flour. Make it into a stiff paste with the boiling cream and butter. Work the dough well, and roll it very thinly.
