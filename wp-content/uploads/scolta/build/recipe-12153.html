Crab Dip

Ingredients 1 cup (7½ ounces / 200 g) crab meat 1 cup mayonnaise ½ cup sour cream 1 teaspoon lemon juice 3 teaspoons dry sherry Salt to taste Freshly-ground pepper to taste Procedure Drain, rinse, and pick over crab meat to remove any bits of shell and cartilage. Combine all ingredients, blending well. Refrigerate until ready to serve. Notes, tips, and variations Serve before dinner with chips and crackers. For color and variety, mixed nuts and colorful vegetables can be laid out.
