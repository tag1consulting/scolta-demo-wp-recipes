Stuffed Courgette

Ingredients 2 large courgettes (zucchini) 1 large onion Garlic Olive oil 1 large green bell pepper 2 large tomatoes Herbs 4 oz cooked brown rice Mustard Seasoning Cheddar cheese Preparation Slice courgettes in half lengthways, and scoop or cut out middles; reserve middles. Chop onion and garlic, and sauté for a few minutes in olive oil. Chop pepper, tomatoes, and herbs. Add to pan together with courgette middles. Add rice, mustard, and seasoning to taste. Cook for 5 minutes and drain off any extra liquid. Stuff the 4 courgette halves with the cooked mixture, and sprinkle grated cheese on top. Bake in medium-hot oven for 20 minutes. Serve with green salad.
