Soy Sauce Chicken

Soy sauce chicken (醬油雞) is a northern Chinese dish. Ingredients Chicken parts (legs or wings) Green onions Cooking oil 2–3 tbsp (30–45 ml) soy sauce 2–3 tbsp (30–45 ml) cooking wine Chopped ginger Up to 1 tbsp sugar Water Procedure Fry the chicken parts in a pan with green onions and oil until the outside of the chicken parts are brown. Add soy sauce, cooking wine, chopped ginger, and sugar. Add enough water to cover the chicken. Simmer, uncovered, over low heat until most of the liquid has evaporated.
