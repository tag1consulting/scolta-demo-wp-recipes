Tzatziki

is a yogurt sauce that’s great over gyros, pitas, salads, lamb, as a dip, or for anything you’d like a cool tasty sauce on. Ingredients 1½ cups yogurt 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tablespoon dill (sub. ¼ tablespoon of tarragon if no dill is available) 2 tablespoons olive oil 4 tablespoons lemon juice (optional) 2 small cucumbers, diced Salt White pepper, to taste (optional) Procedure Drain yogurt in a sieve or a cheesecloth for about 3 hours prior to using it. Drain the cucumber and salt it to further remove moisture. Combine yogurt, garlic, olive oil, and lemon juice in a bowl and cover and refrigerate for 1 hour or more. After refrigerating, add dill and cucumber into bowl and mix with a whisk until smooth. Add pepper and salt to taste.
