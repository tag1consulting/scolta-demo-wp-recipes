Hasude (Albanian Cornstarch Pudding)

Hasude is an Albanian dessert. Ingredients 1¼ cup (300 g) cornstarch 6–7 tablespoons butter 1 cup (240 g) white granulated sugar 2 cups (480 ml) water Procedure Melt butter in pot over medium-high heat. Gradually mix in the cornstarch. Once all the cornstarch is added, cook until the mass becomes yellow. Sweeten with some sugar. Separately prepare a syrup using the remaining sugar and water. After it is done cooking, shape the mass using a spoon or plastic shapes, and pour the syrup on top. References
