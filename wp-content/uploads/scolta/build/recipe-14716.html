Toasted Coconut Flakes

These toasted coconut flakes are a crunchy, caramelized snack made from fresh coconut. They are a popular snack in Nigeria. Ingredients Ripe fresh coconut Granulated sugar Salt Equipment Oven Grater Bowl Baking sheet/tray Procedure Crack open the coconut, and separate out the coconut meat. Grate the coconut meat into flakes. Toss the coconut flakes with sugar and a pinch of salt. Spread the coconut mixture on a baking sheet, and place on the lowest rack of the oven. Bake at 300˚F, stirring every few minutes until golden brown and crisp. Remove the coconut flakes and cool completely. Notes, tips, and variations Ensure you are fully concentrated while toasting the coconut, as it will burn quite fast. If you layer the coconut too thick on the pan, they will take very long to crisp up.
