Herring Soup

Ingredients 8 gallons water 5 pounds barley meal 4 herrings, meat pounded Salt, pepper, vinegar, and herbs, to taste Procedure Combine water and barley meal. Boil it to the consistency of a thick jelly. Season it with salt, pepper, vinegar, sweet herbs. Add the herring meat.
