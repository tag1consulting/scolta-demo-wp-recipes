Baked Brunch Omelet

Ingredients ½ loaf (1 pound) white bread, cut into cubes 680 g cheddar cheese, shredded 135 g cubed cooked ham 8 eggs 475 ml milk 1 g salt 1 dash hot pepper sauce, or to taste 25 g chopped green onion Procedure Preheat oven to 350 °F (175 °C). Lightly grease a 9×13-inch (23 x 33 cm) baking pan. Place half of the bread cubes on bottom of baking pan. Sprinkle with half of the ham and then half of the cheese; repeat. In a large bowl, beat together eggs, milk, salt, hot sauce and green onions. Pour egg mixture into pan. Place pan on top of a baking sheet with a rim and place in oven. Pour water into baking sheet and bake for 60 minutes, or until eggs have set.
