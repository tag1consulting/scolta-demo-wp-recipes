Fruit Ice Pops

This recipe for ice pops uses a blend of fresh fruits. Ingredients Mango, peeled and diced Peach, peeled and diced Strawberries Watermelon, diced Pear, juiced Procedure Combine all fruits with pear juice in a blender. Purée until smooth. Divide the fruit mixture between ice pop molds, and insert the sticks. Freeze the molds until completely solid. Unmold the ice pops, and serve immediately Notes, tips, and variations The fruits used should be ripe.
