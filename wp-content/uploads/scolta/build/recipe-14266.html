Silog

is a heavy, easy-to-make breakfast on every Filipino’s table. Ingredients 1 serving of fried rice 1 fried chicken egg (sunny side-up or beaten) Add-ons of your choice, such as: 2 hot dogs 1 can of corned beef 1 longganisa sausage Sliced cucumber and tomato Procedure Place the fried rice and the egg on your plate. Arrange your desired add-ons around the rice. Serve with any desired condiments, such as spicy vinegar. References
