Watalappam (Sri Lankan Coconut Custard)

Watalappam is a Sri Lankan steamed coconut custard. Ingredients 2 cups thick coconut milk ½ lb brown sugar (or jaggery) 4 eggs 1 pinch of ground cardamom (optional) 3 cloves (optional) 12 cashews Procedure Beat the eggs in a mixer. Add the milk, brown sugar, cardamom, and cloves to the beaten eggs. Mix well. Transfer the mixture to a pot and steam for 20 minutes. Add the cashews. Notes, tips, and variations One can also vary or enhance the aroma and flavour by adding thick pandan juice or vanilla pod gratings. Alternatively, the mixture can be poured into multiple small aluminum baking foils and then steamed for about 5–10 minutes.
