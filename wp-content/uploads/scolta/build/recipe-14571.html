Sweet Carrot Juice

Carrot juice is a refreshing beverage that is best enjoyed cold. Ingredients Carrots Sugar Water Equipment Grater Blender Strainer Knife Procedure Chop or grate the carrots. Place carrots and sugar in a blender, and add a small amount of water as needed. Blend this mixture until well-puréed. Strain the carrot purée to obtain a pure juice. Chill and serve.
