Frito Pie (No-bake)

This recipe is based on the recipe for home-made Frito Pie Chili. Ingredients 1 recipe of home-made Frito Pie Chili, hot 1 bag (10 oz / 280 g) Fritos corn chips 1½ cups (350 ml) grated Monterrey Jack cheese 6 Tbsp (90 ml) sour cream 1 small jar of jalapeños Procedure Place a handful of the chips in each of 8 shallow bowls. Spoon the Frito Pie Chili over the chips. Garnish with the grated cheese, sour cream, and jalapeños.
