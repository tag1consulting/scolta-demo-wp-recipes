Spicy Crab Cakes

These spicy crab cakes get an extra kick from cayenne pepper. Ingredients Chipotle remoulade ¼ cup mayonnaise ¼ cup Dijon mustard 2 tbsp dill, finely chopped 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo sauce, puréed Salt to taste Freshly-ground black pepper to taste Crab cakes 1 pound lump blue crab meat 1 tbsp Old Bay seasoning 1 tbsp cayenne pepper 1½ tsp kosher salt 1½ tsp black pepper ½ cup Dijon mustard 2 tbsp mayonnaise Juice of 2 lemons Vegetable oil for deep frying Panko breadcrumbs as needed 1 egg, beaten Procedure Chipotle remoulade Combine all ingredients in a small bowl. Crab cakes Combine all ingredients except for oil, panko, and egg. Shape into 4 equal patties. Dip into egg then dredge in panko. Fry at 375°F just until heated through and golden brown. Serve with chipotle remoulade sauce.
