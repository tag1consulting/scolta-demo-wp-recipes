Cinnamon Bun

A cinnamon bun (or a cinnamon roll) is a type of pastry made with cinnamon, sugar and butter. Ingredients Procedure Pastry Combine flour, baking powder and salt. Cut in the shortening. Add the milk, stirring with a knife until the dough is soft and slightly sticky. Knead the dough until it “springs back” when you push your finger into it. Roll the dough into a 9 x 13-inch (23 x 30 cm) rectangle. Use a 9 x 13-inch (23 x 30 cm) baking pan as a mould for the size. Filling Brush butter over the dough, leaving about ¼ inch (0.75 cm) around the edges clear. Combine brown sugar, white sugar, cinnamon and salt. Sprinkle over the buttered dough, leaving about ¼ inch around the edges clear. Press the sugar mixture down. Roll the dough up lengthwise into a log, as tight as you can. Trim the edges, then cut into 8 pieces. Arrange the buns in a small pan, so that all the buns are touching. Bake at 425°F (210°C) for 15–20 minutes. Let cool. Topping Combine icing sugar and liquid. Whisk together to make an icing. Spread topping on buns after they have cooled. Conversion notes
