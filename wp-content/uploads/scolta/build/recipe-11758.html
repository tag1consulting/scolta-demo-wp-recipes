Campfire Banana Boat

Campfire banana boat is a very easy and popular camping dessert. It requires no dishes—you eat it straight from the peel! Ingredients 1 banana Mini marshmallows Chocolate chips Procedure Peel back a 1 ½ inch (4 cm) wide strip of peel from the inside curve of the banana, leaving one end attached to the banana. Cut out and remove a wedge-shaped cavity from the length of the banana. Fill cavity with mini-marshmallows and chocolate chips Replace peel and wrap well in heavy duty foil. Place on coals for about 5 minutes. Open carefully and eat with a spoon right from the banana peel.
