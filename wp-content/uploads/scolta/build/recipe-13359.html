Microwave Applesauce

For all of you who would like to make your own applesauce, but without having to do much work, this recipe is for you. Ingredients 5 apples, peeled, cored, and diced 1½ cups apple jelly ¼ cup Grade B Amber maple syrup (Grade A Dark Amber would be okay too) ¼ cup freshly-squeezed orange juice 1 cinnamon stick Procedure Combine all ingredients in a microwave-safe container. Cover container, leaving 1 corner open, and microwave on high 7–8 minutes or until apples are easy to mash. Remove cinnamon stick and discard. For a chunky sauce, mash the apples with a potato masher. For a slightly smoother sauce, use a hand mixer. For a smooth pureé, use an immersion blender.
