Chipotle Lime Grilled Chicken

Ingredients 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil 3 tbsp Smoky Chipotle Hot Sauce ¼ cup freshly squeezed lime juice 2 tbsp honey ½ cup Chipotle BBQ Rub Procedure Combine all ingredients except for BBQ Rub in a gallon zip-top plastic freezer bag. Add chicken breasts and refrigerate for 45 minutes. Remove chicken from bag. Season each breast with BBQ Rub. Place on a preheated charcoal grill and cook, turning often, until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Remove and let rest for 5 minutes. Serve.
