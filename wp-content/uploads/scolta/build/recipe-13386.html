Minestrone alla Capucina

Minestra alla Capucina is an Italian recipe of soup in which Cappuccina lettuce is used. Ingredients Anchovy, scaled Butter 1 small onion, cut across 4 oz boiled rice Stock Procedure Pound anchovy. Fry it in butter with the onion and rice until the rice is golden brown. Remove the onion and season with salt. Gradually add some stock, cooking until the dish is of the consistency of rice pudding.
