North Carolina-Style BBQ Ribs

You want some tomato in your sauce, go over to the west side of N.C. Ingredients 1 slab pork spareribs 6 tbsp Rib Rub 1 cup apple cider vinegar ⅓ cup honey 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce Procedure Season ribs with Rib Rub and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Combine remaining ingredients and place in a spray bottle. Place ribs in a 250°F smoker or a charcoal grill set for indirect heat at 250°F Cook for 5–6 hours, spraying often with vinegar mixture. Spray heavily with vinegar mixture and place on a high grill, turning and spraying constantly until well browned. Remove and let rest 15 minutes; serve. Notes, tips, and variations There will be extra vinegar mixture. You can use it for almost anything including beef briskets.
