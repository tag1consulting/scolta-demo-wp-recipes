Vegetable Party Sandwiches

Ingredients 1 envelope (¼ ounce) unflavored gelatin ¼ cup cold water 2 large carrots, grated very fine 2 ribs celery, chopped very fine 2 cucumbers, peeled, seeded and chopped fine 1 small onion, minced 1 tablespoon lemon juice 1 pint mayonnaise, such as Hellmann’s 2 long square loaves white bread Procedure Dissolve gelatin in water according to directions. In a large bowl, mix dissolved gelatin with carrots, celery, cucumber, onion, lemon juice, and mayonnaise. Refrigerate for 25 hours. If the mixture becomes too jelled to spread well, add ⅓ to ½ cup more mayonnaise. Using a knife, trim crusts from bread edges. Spread vegetable mixture on bread and make 28 sandwiches. Cut into quarters. Layer cut sandwiches in a large covered plastic container with damp paper towels between layers until serving time.
