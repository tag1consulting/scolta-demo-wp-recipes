Cranberry Walnut Fudge

Ingredients 2 cups white granulated sugar ½ cup butter ¾ cup sour cream 2 cups white chocolate chips 7 ounces (198 g) marshmallow creme 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 2 cups chopped walnuts 1 cup dried cranberries Procedure Place butter, sour cream, and sugar into a saucepan. Put the saucepan on a stove and cook for 15 minutes over medium heat. Remove from heat and add vanilla extract, marshmallow creme, and white chocolate chips. Using a mixing spoon, stir until the mixture is smooth. Fold in the walnuts and cranberries. Pour the mixture into an 8×8-inch baking pan. Put the pan into the refrigerator to cool for 1 hour. Unmold and cut into 1-inch pieces.
