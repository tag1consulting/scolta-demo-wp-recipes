Alfajores (Latin American Sandwich Cookies)

Alfajores are a Latin-American sandwich cookie filled with dulce de leche. Ingredients ½ cup (120 ml) butter 1 cup (240 ml) white granulated sugar 1 egg 2 egg yolks 1 tsp vanilla 2 tsp lemon rind, grated 1½ cup (360 ml) cornstarch ½ cup (120 ml) flour 1 tsp baking powder ¼ tsp salt Dulce de leche Procedure Cream butter; add sugar and beat until fluffy. Add egg and egg yolks one at a time, beating well. Beat in vanilla and lemon rind. Sift together cornstarch, flour, baking powder, and salt. Add to batter and mix well. Drop batter by small spoonfuls onto well-buttered baking sheet. Bake at 350°F for 15 minutes. Remove immediately and cool. Sandwich the cookies together with dulce de leche.
