Deluxe Burgers

Ingredients 1½ pound ground beef ½ teaspoon garlic salt 3 tablespoon finely chopped onion ½ teaspoon pepper 6 strips of bacon, cooked well done and crumbled 4 ounce (1 cup) shredded cheddar cheese ¼ cup mayonnaise ⅓ cup canned sliced mushrooms 6 split hamburger buns Procedure Mix beef, garlic salt, onion, and pepper thoroughly in a bowl. Shape hamburger mixture into 6 patties, about ¾-inch thick. Mix bacon, cheese, mayonnaise, and mushrooms together and store in the refrigerator or cooler. Cook burgers on the grill, covered, for 5 or 7 minutes on each side. During the last 3 minutes, put ¼ cup cheese mixture on top of each burger. Notes, tips, and variations Serve on buns, if desired. And of course, garnish with lettuce and tomato slices. Have available the usual: ketchup, mustard, and pickle relish.
