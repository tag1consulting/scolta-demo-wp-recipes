Russian Tea

Ingredients 1 cup (250 ml) powdered orange flavor drink mix (e.g. Tang brand) 1 cup (200 g) white granulated sugar ½ cup (125 ml) instant tea 1 tsp ground cinnamon 1 tsp ground cloves 1 tsp mixed spices 1 pinch nutmeg Procedure Mix together thoroughly all ingredients. Store in an airtight container. Add 2–3 teaspoons to 1 cup of hot water.
