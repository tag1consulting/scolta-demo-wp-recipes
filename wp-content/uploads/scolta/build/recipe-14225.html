Semolina Minestrone

This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook The Cook’s Decameron: A Study In Taste, Containing Over Two Hundred Recipes For Italian Dishes by Emily Waters. Ingredients 1 qt stock 3 oz semolina Parmesan, grated Procedure Bring the stock to a boil in a saucepan. Add semolina very gradually, stirring to keep it from getting lumpy. Cook until the semolina is soft. Serve with grated Parmesan.
