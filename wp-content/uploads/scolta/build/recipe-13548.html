No-Bake Chocolate Cake

This is a simple-to-make cake and delicious too! Ingredients 1 egg 3 sticks (1½ cups) margarine 2 cups (450 g) fine sugar 5 tsp vanilla extract ½ cup cocoa powder 2½ packs graham crackers, plain digestive biscuits, or whole wheat biscuits; crumbled Procedure Melt the margarine. Mix the sugar and the egg. Add the melted butter to the egg/sugar mixture. Add the cocoa and the vanilla extract. Add crumbled graham crackers. Mix well until all crackers are covered. Line a cake pan with aluminum foil and pour in mixture. Leave in refrigerator for 1 hour (preferably longer). Unmold and serve.
