Sour Mix (With Egg)

This recipe for homemade sour mix uses lemon juice with an egg white for texture. Ingredients 1 cup superfine sugar 1 egg white 2 cups water 2 cups lemon juice, preferably freshly squeezed Procedure Whisk egg white in a medium-sized bowl until frothy. Stir in the sugar and water or simple syrup. Add the lemon juice, and stir all ingredients well. Store the mix refrigerated, and use within 3 days of making it.
