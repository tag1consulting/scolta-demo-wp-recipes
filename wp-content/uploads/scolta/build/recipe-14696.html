Three Bean Confetti Salad

Ingredients 1 can (15 oz) pinto beans, rinsed and drained 1 can (15 oz) red kidney beans, rinsed and drained 1 can (12 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained 1 can (8¾ oz) whole kernel corn, drained 1 can (14½ oz) diced tomatoes with roasted garlic, undrained ½ cup fat-free Italian salad dressing 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning ¼ teaspoon pepper Procedure In a bowl, mix together beans, corn, tomatoes, Italian salad dressing, Italian seasoning and pepper. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
