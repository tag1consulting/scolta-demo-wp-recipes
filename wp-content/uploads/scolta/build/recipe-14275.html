Simple Chocolate Fudge

Chocolate fudge is a simple, sugary treat intended to be picked up and eaten at room temperature or refrigerated. Ingredients 3 cups (18 oz) chocolate chips 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk 1 tsp vanilla (optional) Special equipment Double boiler 2 feet of wax paper or aluminum foil (optional) Procedure Use a double boiler to melt the chocolate chips. Failing to use a double boiler will result in burned chocolate. Add the sweetened condensed milk. Stir until mixed. Pour into a lined baking dish, bowl, or other mold. Expect it to stick if you don’t use wax paper or foil. Let cool for several hours.
