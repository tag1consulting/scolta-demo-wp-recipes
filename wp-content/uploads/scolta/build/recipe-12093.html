Cola BBQ Sauce

Great on ribs or chicken! Ingredients 1 bottle ketchup ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce 1 ½ cans good cola (Pepsi is recommended) ½ cup soy sauce ¼ cup cider vinegar Hot sauce, to taste Procedure Combine all ingredients and bring to a boil. Cook over high heat for 10 minutes. Store refrigerated in a nonreactive container for up to 1 week.
