Herbal Iced Tea

Ingredients 0.75 L rosehip tea 3 tbsp honey 2 oranges, juiced 5 drops orange flower extract 1 lime, cut into pieces 1 lemon, cut into pieces Procedure Sweeten the rosehip tea with the honey and allow to chill. Add orange juice and orange flower extract. Divide the lime and lemon between 4 large glasses, and add the tea. Serve ice-cold. Notes, tips, and variations Note that mint tea, berry tea, or other herbal teas may replace the rosehip tea. However discard orange flower extract in these cases; add mint or fresh fruit instead. An artificial sweetener may be used instead of honey.
