Huancayo Potatoes in Cream Sauce (Papas a la Huancaína)

Ingredients 10 medium potatoes (new or red a good choice) 1 pound cheese (Romano, Mexican or feta work well) 4 aji amarillo (Peruvian yellow chili peppers), seeded and deveined 1 cup evaporated milk ½ cup vegetable oil 2 cloves garlic 8 saltine crackers 1 tablespoon prepared mustard Salt Pepper Lettuce 3 hard boiled eggs Black olives Procedure Peel and boil potatoes as you normally would. Drain water, and allow them to cool. In a blender, purée the cheese, peppers, milk, oil, garlic, crackers, mustard, salt and pepper. The sauce should be fairly thick; add more crackers if not, or add milk if too thick Lay a bed of lettuce in a serving dish and place the potatoes on top. Cover with the sauce. Cut the hard boiled eggs in half and place on top of the potatoes. Add black olives if desired. Notes, tips, and variations This dish can be served slightly cold. Some recipes suggest the use of huacatay, but this is a confusion. Huacatay is a key ingredient of ocopa, another Peruvian sauce.
