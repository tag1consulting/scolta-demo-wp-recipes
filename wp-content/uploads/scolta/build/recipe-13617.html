Orange French Toast

Orange french toast puts a twist on cinnamon bread. Ingredients 6 eggs, lightly beaten ¾ cup orange juice ½ cup half-and-half cream 2 tablespoons sugar 1 teaspoon vanilla extract ½ teaspoon grated orange peel 8 thick slices cinnamon bread ¼ cup butter, melted Procedure In a shallow bowl, combine the eggs, orange juice, half-and-half, sugar, vanilla, and orange peel. Dip both sides of bread into egg mixture; let soak for 5 minutes. Place in a greased 15 × 10 × 1 in (38 × 25 × 2.5 cm) baking pan. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Uncover, and drizzle with butter. Bake at 325 °F (163 °C) for 35–40 minutes or until browned.
