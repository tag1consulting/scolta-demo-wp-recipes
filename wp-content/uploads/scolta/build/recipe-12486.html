Four in One Pizza

One pizza, divided into four flavor quadrants. Ingredients Base Cooking spray Corn meal 1 can refrigerated pizza crust 2 teaspoons olive oil ½ cup (2 ounces) shredded cheddar cheese 1½ cups (6 ounces) shredded mozzarella cheese Barbecue chicken pizza corner 1 tablespoon chopped red onion ½ cup BBQ sauce mixed with shredded chicken Pizza Margherita corner 1 tablespoon shredded (fresh) Parmesan cheese 4–5 ¼-inch slices Roma (aka plum) tomatoes 1 tablespoon thin strips of fresh basil Hawaiian pizza corner 2 tablespoons pizza sauce 2 slices Canadian bacon, cut in quarters ¼ cup drained chopped pineapple Pepperoni pizza corner 2 tablespoons pizza sauce 8–10 slices pepperoni Procedure Preheat oven to 425°F. Spray cookie sheet with cooking spray. Sprinkle lightly with cornmeal, if desired. Unroll pizza dough and press out on cookie sheet into a 12 x 14-inch shape. Brush dough with olive oil. Bake crust for 6–8 minutes. Sprinkle shredded cheddar cheese over the crust in a cross shape to divide crust in fourths. In each corner, spread one of the above toppings (or one of your own!). Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the top of all. Bake another 10–14 minutes, until cheese is bubbly but not burned. Cut each corner into 4 squares.
