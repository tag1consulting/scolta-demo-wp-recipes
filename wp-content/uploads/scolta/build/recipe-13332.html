Memphis-Style BBQ Ribs

This is a classic U.S. style of BBQ pork ribs. Ingredients 1 slab pork spareribs 6 tbsp Rib Rub 1 cup tomato paste ½ cup molasses ¼ cup apple cider vinegar 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 2 tbsp soy sauce Hickory chunks Procedure Season spareribs with rib rub. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Place chunks in smoker or charcoal grill set for indirect heat at 250°F (120°C). Combine liquid ingredients and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce liquid by ⅓. Place spareribs into smoker or grill for 4–5 hours, basting occasionally with sauce. Brush remaining sauce on top of ribs and place on a medium-high grill until browned. The internal temperature of the ribs before removing from the grill needs to be at least 145°F (63°C). Let rest for 10 minutes, covered, and carve.
