Honey Apple Glazed Pork

Ingredients ¼ teaspoon black pepper 1½ tablespoons flour 1 teaspoon ground mustard ½ teaspoon dried sage leaves 1 teaspoon salt 3 pound (about 1.5 kg) pork butt roast 1 cup applesauce ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon ⅓ cup honey ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg Procedure Preheat oven to 325°F (about 165°C). Mix together pepper, flour, mustard, sage, and salt to get a rub. Apply rub to pork roast and set aside. Line a roasting pan with foil and spray with cooking spray. Place roast in pan and bake for 30 minutes. Mix together applesauce, cinnamon, honey, and nutmeg as a glaze. Remove roast from oven and spread with glaze. Return glazed roast to oven and bake until you get an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C), about 50–60 minutes. Notes, tips, and variations Good with sweet potatoes and green beans.
