Date Smoothie

A date milkshake is a delicious and refreshing beverage. Ingredients Dates Cinnamon powder Sugar Ice cubes Special equipment Blender Procedures Soak the dates in water for a few minutes to soften the tough texture. Drain. Place the soaked dates in the blender, and add a small amount of water. Add cinnamon, sugar to taste, and ice. Turn on the blender until smooth.
