Coconut Chutney (North Indian)

Coconut chutney is served as an accompaniment to many South Indian dishes, such as dosa and idli. This recipe is a North Indian variant. Ingredients 2 cups shredded fresh coconut 1–2 split green chillies 5 cloves garlic ½ cup chopped cilantro ½ teaspoon salt ½ cup water Procedure Grind all ingredients well in a blender. Serve within 2 days. See also Coconut Chutney
