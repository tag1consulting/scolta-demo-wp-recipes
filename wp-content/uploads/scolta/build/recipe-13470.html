Nachos

are a dish of tortilla chips served hot with cheese and various toppings. Ingredients 12 ounces (~350 g) tortilla chips 1 pound (450 g) cheddar cheese, grated ⅓ cup chopped canned peeled green chiles (optional) ½ cup chopped onion (optional) Procedure Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Lay the tortilla chips on a cookie sheet. Toss together the cheese, chilies, and onion, and sprinkle over the tortilla chips. Bake for about 2 minutes until the cheese has melted. Serve hot.
