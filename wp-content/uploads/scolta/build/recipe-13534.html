Nigerian Tea (Hot Chocolate)

Nigerian tea refers to a hot chocolate beverage usually served at breakfast and with bread, meat pie, and many other snacks. Despite the name, there is no actual tea in the beverage. Ingredients Evaporated milk (preferably Peak Milk brand) Milo chocolate drink mix Hot water Procedure Pour the evaporated milk into a mug, and stir in the chocolate drink mix. Stir in hot water. Serve with bread, Nigerian meat pie, or other Nigerian snacks. References
