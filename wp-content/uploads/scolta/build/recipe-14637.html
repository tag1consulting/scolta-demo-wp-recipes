Tangy Fried Shrimp

Ingredients 3 pounds peeled deveined jumbo shrimp Flour Juice of 4 lemons 3 quarts frying oil 3 tbsp salt 3 tbsp freshly-ground black pepper 2 tbsp cayenne pepper Procedure Dredge shrimp in the flour, then dip in lemon juice, then dredge in flour again. Set aside. Heat oil to 375°F. Fry shrimp, 6 at a time, for 2 minutes or until deeply browned and cooked through. Drain on a wire rack and repeat with remaining shrimp. Once all shrimp have been fried, sprinkle with seasonings and serve.
