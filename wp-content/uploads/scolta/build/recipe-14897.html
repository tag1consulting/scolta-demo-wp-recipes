Whiskey Barbecue Sauce

Ingredients 2 cups whiskey 1 cup ketchup ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce ¼ cup soy sauce ¼ cup cider vinegar ¼ cup chicken broth Procedure Combine all ingredients in a large saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat and cook, stirring, for 10 minutes. Refrigerate in a nonreactive container for up to 2 months.
