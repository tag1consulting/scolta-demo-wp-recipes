Mushroom Ketchup

Mushroom ketchup is a seasoning. Ingredients 2 lbs edible mushrooms, chopped 3 tbsp salt 1 onion, chopped 2 tbsp finely ground fresh horseradish 1 tbsp lemon zest ¼ tsp cloves ½ tsp allspice ¼ cup cider vinegar 1 pinch of ground cayenne Procedure Combine the mushrooms and salt. Stir, smash well, and let rest 10 minutes. Transfer mushrooms to a non-metal container, such as a ceramic crock. Cover and let sit overnight. Combine the mushrooms, onion, horseradish, lemon zest, cloves, allspice, vinegar, and cayenne. Bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes. Drape a clean cloth over a bowl. Pour the mixture over the cloth, and squeeze to extract all the liquid. The liquid is the mushroom ketchup. Notes, tips, and variations One may sprinkle leftover mushroom solids over food as a spice.
