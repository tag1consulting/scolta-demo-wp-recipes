Apple Crusted Ham

Ingredients 1 bone-in cooked city ham, skin removed ½ cup dried apples, minced finely ½ cup Chop Rub ¾ cup cola Procedure Place ham in a large roasting pan large side down. Bake at 250°F for 1 hour. Take ham out from oven and brush generously with cola. Pat on the chop rub, and then pat on apples. Bake for another 1 ½ hours at 250°F Remove and let rest 10 minutes. Slice thinly and serve.
