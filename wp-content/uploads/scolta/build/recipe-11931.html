Chicken with Paprika and Cream

Ingredients 2 boneless chicken breasts 1 tablespoon paprika 4 tablespoons half and half 1 tablespoon lemon juice 3 slices of Provel (a brand of processed cheese product) or Muenster Procedure Combine all ingredients in a baking dish. Bake at 400°F (205°C) until internal temperature of the chicken breasts reaches 165°F (74°C).
