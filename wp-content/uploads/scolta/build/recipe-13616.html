Orange Chocolate Chip Muffins

Ingredients 400 g (2 cups) self-raising flour 60 g butter 120 g (½ cup) granulated sugar 120 ml (½ cup) milk chocolate chips 120 ml (½ cup) white chocolate chips 2 eggs, beaten lightly 120 ml (½ cup) milk 120 ml (½ cup) orange juice 15 ml (1 Tbsp) orange rind Procedure Preheat oven to 220°C (350°F). Sift flour into bowl, then rub in butter. Add sugar, chocolate bits, eggs, milk, orange juice, and orange rind. Mix well. Spoon heaped tablespoons of mixture into greased muffin pans. Bake for 15–20 minutes until cooked through.
