Mussels with Potatoes

Ingredients 450 g (1 lb) mussels, rinsed and scrubbed 225 g (8 oz) potatoes, cooked and diced 175 g (6 oz) carrots, cooked and diced 1 tbsp olive oil 3 tbsp wine vinegar Salt Freshly-ground black pepper Procedure Place the mussels in a large pot or steamer with about 1–2 inches of boiling liquid (e.g. water, wine, or seasoned broth) Cover and steam over high heat for approximately 5 minutes or until the shells open Discard any mussels that do not open. Remove mussel meal from the shells Mix together the mussels, potatoes, and carrots and arrange on a suitable serving dish. Thoroughly blend the oil, vinegar, and seasoning and pour over the salad. Chill well before serving.
