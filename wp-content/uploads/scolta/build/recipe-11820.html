Champorado (Chocolate Rice Porridge)

Champorado is a chocolate porridge and favorite Filipino comfort food, especially during cold weather. Ingredients ½ kg glutinous rice Water (about 4–6 times the rice volume) ½ kg white granulated sugar 2 spoons cocoa powder 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk Procedure Wash the rice twice. Combine the rice and water in a pot. Place the pot over medium heat, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover the pot and stir in the sugar and cocoa powder until well mixed. Simmer over low heat, stirring regularly, until the rice is cooked and the porridge is thickened as desired. Stir in the evaporated milk and serve. Notes, tips, and variations Sometimes champorado tastes good when eaten with salted fish.
