Brioche Stuffing

Brioche bread is a slightly sweetened yeast bread, often used in stuffing applications. Ingredients 2 cups brioche bread, coarsely chopped ½ cup shelled walnuts, finely chopped ¼ cup dried apples, coarsely chopped Salt, to taste Pepper, to taste ¼ cup honey ¼ cup unsalted butter, cut into small cubes 2 tbsp brown sugar ¼ cup diced red onion 2 cloves garlic, smashed and minced ½ tsp dried rubbed sage ¼ cup diced celery Procedure Combine all ingredients in a deep roasting pan. Press into a loose, flat layer. Bake in a 375 °F (190 °C) oven for 25 minutes. Raise temperature to 425 °F (220 °C), and bake for 10 minutes. Serve immediately.
