Spice Rub for Chicken

Ingredients 2 teaspoons black pepper 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper 2 teaspoons ground celery seed 2 tablespoons garlic powder ½ cup paprika 2 tablespoons kosher salt 1 tablespoon dried thyme Procedure Mix together spices in a bowl. Rub on chicken, inside and outside. Let stand for a few minutes before cooking per your usual directions.
