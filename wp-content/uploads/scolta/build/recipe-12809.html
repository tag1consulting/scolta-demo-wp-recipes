Home Fries

Recipe Home fries are a classic American breakfast side dish made with cubed or sliced potatoes, pan-fried until golden and crispy. Unlike hash browns, which are typically grated, home fries keep their chunky texture and are often seasoned with onions, peppers, or herbs. Ingredients 3 medium potatoes (e.g., russet or Yukon gold), peeled and diced 1 tablespoon olive oil or butter ½ teaspoon salt ¼ teaspoon black pepper ¼ teaspoon paprika (optional) ½ small onion, finely chopped ¼ cup green bell pepper (optional) Instructions Boil diced potatoes in salted water for 5 minutes to soften slightly. Drain and let them dry. Heat oil or butter in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add onions (and peppers if using), sauté until translucent. Add the potatoes in a single layer. Let them brown undisturbed for 5–6 minutes. Flip and cook the other sides until golden and crispy. Season with salt, pepper, and paprika. Serve hot. Tips For extra crispiness, use pre-cooked or leftover potatoes. You can add garlic, thyme, or bacon bits for variation.
