Rhubarb Juice

Ingredients 2 lb (about 1 kg) tender rhubarb, chopped 5 cups water ¼ cup lemon juice Procedure Combine the rhubarb and water in a non-aluminum pot. Cook until mushy. Strain the mixture well, reserving only the juice. Mix in lemon juice. Notes, tips, and variations It’s important to use a non-aluminum pot, because when combined with an acidic food like rhubarb, aluminum can impart an unpleasant flavor. Use For each cup of concentrate, add ⅓ cup sugar and ½ cup grapefruit juice, and chill well. Just before serving, add 1 quart chilled ginger ale and stir well.
