Spicy Plantain Chips

Ingredients 2 green plantains, mashed 1 tsp salt 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper 1 serrano chile, seeded and minced 1 tbsp minced garlic 2 quarts peanut or canola oil for deep-frying Procedure Combine plantains, salt, black pepper, the chile, and garlic. Shape into rounds about the size of a thin slice of potato. Heat oil to 375°F. Add rounds, 6–8 at a time, and fry until golden brown and crispy on the outside. Drain on a cooling rack set above a sheet pan, and season immediately with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Serve warm or room temperature.
