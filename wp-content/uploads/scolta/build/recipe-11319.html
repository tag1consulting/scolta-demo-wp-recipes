Ancient Roman Pancakes (Ova Sfongia Ex Lacte)

Ova sfongia ex lacte are an ancient Roman cake similar to a modern pancake. Ingredients 3 eggs 3 cups milk ¾ cup olive oil ½ teaspoon salt 4 cups flour (preferably whole wheat for authenticity) 2 teaspoons honey 1 dash ground pepper Dates Procedure Beat together eggs, milk, oil, salt, and flour until you have a runny pancake dough. Add more flour if necessary. Cook on a hot skillet. Serve topped with honey, a little pepper, and dates (if you want to). Enjoy!
