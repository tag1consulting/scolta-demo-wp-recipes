Pizza-Flavored Omelet

Ingredients ¼ cup tomato sauce Salt, pepper, and other seasonings to taste (see tips below) Cooking spray 2 eggs 2 tablespoons chopped green bell peppers 2 tablespoons chopped mushrooms ¼ cup shredded mozzarella cheese Procedure Mix spices and tomato sauce and allow to meld flavors while preparing the rest of the recipe. Beat eggs well. Heat a small skillet over low heat, sprayed with cooking spray. Pour beaten eggs into skillet, then cover skillet. Cook for 3 minutes. Eggs should be almost set. Carefully flip omelet over. Cook another minute. Combine mushrooms and peppers and cook 30 seconds in microwave oven. Spread tomato-spice mix over omelet. Sprinkle cheese over tomato sauce. Top cheese with hot mushrooms and peppers. Cover skillet. Cook another 2 minutes. Cheese should be melted. Notes, tips, and variations Basil, crushed red pepper, and oregano work well.
