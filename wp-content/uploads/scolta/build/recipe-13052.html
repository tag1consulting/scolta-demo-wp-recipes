Kid-Friendly Pizza

Pizza is a tasty snack that many people all over the world enjoy. It can be fun to eat and even more fun to make! This recipe is specially designed for children to make and eat. Ingredients: 1 pre-baked whole-grain pizza shell ¼ cup tomato pasta sauce 1 cup shredded pizza cheese ½ cup cubed cooked chicken breast (optional) 1 red bell pepper, diced Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F. Spread the tomato sauce on the pizza shell. Top it with half of the shredded cheese. Add the chicken and red pepper on top of the pizza crust in whatever design you want. Have fun with it! Sprinkle the rest of the cheese on top. Bake it for 10–12 minutes.
