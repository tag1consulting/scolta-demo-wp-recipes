Sindhi Spiced Fish

Ingredients Oil 2 fish fillets 5 tbsp yogurt 2 medium-size onions, chopped 1 tbsp chopped coriander leaves 1 tsp minced green chile 1 tsp coriander powder 1 tsp cumin powder Salt to taste 1 tsp tamarind paste Procedure Shallow fry the fish on one side, then transfer it to a pan fried-side down. Combine yogurt, onions, coriander leaves, green chile, coriander powder, cumin powder, salt, and tamarind paste. Spread the yogurt mixture over the fish. Bake until golden. Serve with chapatis.
