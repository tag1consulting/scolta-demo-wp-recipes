Yemenite Charoset I

Ingredients 450 g date spread 150 g walnuts 1 pomegranate 25 g cinnamon 5g black pepper ½ cup wine vinegar 20 g dried marjoram or oregano, partially crushed Procedure Place date spread in bowl and add crushed walnuts. Peel the pomegranate and pound its kernels in mortar and pestle. Add to date spread. Mix the ground cinnamon and the ground black pepper into date spread. Add wine vinegar and dried herbs.
