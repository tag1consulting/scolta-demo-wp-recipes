Moroccan Salad

This Moroccan salad is something that is easy to throw together, refreshing to eat, and good-looking. Ingredients 3 large, ripe tomatoes 1 green bell pepper 1 smallish onion 1 handful of fresh coriander, finely chopped 1 tsp ground cumin Salt to taste Black pepper to taste 2 tablespoons vinegar or lemon juice Procedure Cut the tomatoes, pepper and onion into small dice. Add the fresh coriander, cumin, salt, pepper, and vinegar. Put it all into a container, put the lid on tightly, and give it a good shake to combine everything. Let rest for 30 minutes to allow the flavours to mingle before serving. Notes, tips, and variations You can peel the tomatoes if you wish.
