Greek Salad

Ingredients Red tomatoes, chopped Cucumber, sliced into rounds Red or white onions, sliced into half-moons Green bell peppers, cut into small pieces Greek olives Extra-virgin or virgin olive oil Vinegar or lemon juice Salt Feta cheese Oregano Procedure Combine tomatoes, cucumbers, onions, peppers, and olives in a deep bowl. Add enough olive oil, vinegar or lemon juice, and salt to taste. Toss the ingredients for half a minute. Place a feta cheese slice on top and season with a splash of olive oil. Top with a sprig of oregano if desired.
