Boiled Cocoyam

Boiled cocoyam, also known as lambo (Yoruba), gwaza (Hausa), and akasi (Igbo) is a typical Nigerian food. It is believed to have a richer taste and higher nutritional value than yam. Ingredients Cocoyam Water Salt Procedure Wash the cocoyam to remove dirt from the surface, and cut into smaller pieces without peeling. Transfer cocoyam to a pot, and cover with water. Add a pinch of salt, and simmer for several minutes until tender. Drain the cocoyam, and remove the peel. Serve with garden egg stew, egg sauce, or fried pepper.
