Bourbon Apples

Ingredients 1 Honeycrisp apple, cored, peeled, and sliced into 12ths ¾ cup bourbon 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted 1 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice ¼ cup white granulated sugar ½ tsp freshly ground allspice 2 cloves, freshly ground ½ tsp freshly ground cinnamon Procedure Coat apples with butter and lemon. Combine sugar, allspice, cloves, and cinnamon. Sprinkle over apples and toss to coat. Preheat a small stainless steel skillet over medium high heat. Add apples and sauté until lightly browned. Add bourbon to pan and deglaze. Bring to a boil, and cook, stirring occasionally, until apples are fork tender. Serve immediately.
