Ponche de Crème

Ponche de crème is a seasonal beverage usually enjoyed as a cocktail at Christmastime. It is similar to eggnog. Ingredients 6 eggs peel of 1 lime 3 cans (15 oz / 400 g) evaporated milk 1½ cans (14 oz / 380 g) sweetened condensed milk, or to taste 2 tsp aromatic bitters ½ tsp grated nutmeg ½ cup (120 ml) rum or brandy, or to taste Procedure Beat eggs and lime peel until light and fluffy. Add evaporated milk. Sweeten to taste with condensed milk. Add bitters, grated nutmeg and rum according to taste. Remove lime peel and serve over crushed ice. Notes, tips, and variations Instead of beating the raw eggs, you can gently stir them together with the other ingredients over a low heat until the mixture thickens (about 20–25 minutes).
