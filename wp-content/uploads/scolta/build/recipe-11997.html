Chocolate Chip Rolls

This is a recipe grown out of an experiment on a traditional biscuit recipe; they’re not extremely sweet, but fall closer to dessert than just rolls or biscuits. Ingredients Procedure Preheat oven to 425°F (210°C) Mix dry ingredients together. Mix liquid ingredients into dry. Turn dough out onto floured baking cloth and knead slightly. Roll dough into log, then cut into 8–12 pieces Press pieces flat, sprinkle with chocolate chips, and roll up to enclose the chocolate. Place rolls on greased baking sheet, and bake 13–15 minutes. Serve fresh. Notes, tips, and variations The recipe should work fine with all white flour as well (probably a little less milk). Conversion notes
