Svelekake (Norwegian Yogurt Pancakes)

Ingredients 4 eggs ½ cup white granulated sugar 2½ cups flour 1 litre kefir or yogurt 1 teaspoon baking powder 1 teaspoon baking soda ⅛ teaspoon horn salt (ammonium bicarbonate) Vanilla Procedure Mix all ingredients gently (do not use mixer). Let batter stand ½ hour. Fry on hot griddle. Serve with butter, brunost (brown/goat cheese), or Nugatti Crisp (caramel/hazelnut dessert spread).
