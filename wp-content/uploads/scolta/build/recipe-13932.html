Pumpkin Soup

Ingredients 2 onions, chopped Carrots, diced Potatoes, diced Zucchinis (courgettes), diced Oil for frying 200 g chopped pumpkin Chickpeas, soaked or canned and drained Water Procedure Fry onions, carrots, potatoes, and zucchinis in a spoonful of oil. Add pumpkin and chickpeas. Cover with hot water and simmer. The soup is ready when the carrots are soft. See also Pumpkin Soup with Curry Spiced Pumpkin Soup
