Fried Pickle

Fried pickle is a snack item found in the American South. It is made by deep-frying a sliced, battered, dill pickle. Ingredients Milk mixture 1 cup milk 1 egg, beaten 1 tbsp flour 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 6 drops hot sauce Flour mixture 3½ cup flour ¾ tsp salt ¾ tsp pepper Other ingredients 1 qt sliced dill pickles Dried dill (optional) Vegetable oil for deep frying Procedure Combine the ingredients for the milk mixture, stirring well. Set aside. Combine ingredients for the flour mixture, stirring well. Dip pickles in milk mixture and dredge in flour mixture. Repeat process. Deep fry in oil heated to 350 °F until pickles float to surface and are golden brown. Drain on paper towels. Sprinkle with dried dill to your liking.
