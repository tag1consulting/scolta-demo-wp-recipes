Hanjin Ligidi (Nigerian Lollipops)

Hanjin ligidi, also called ekana gowon, is a cone-shaped Nigerian confection mostly enjoyed by children. It is similar to a lollipop. Ingredients Tsamiya (tamarind) Water Sugar Flavoring of choice Procedure Shape food-safe paper or aluminum foil into cones. Clean any dirt or dust from the tsamiya. Wash the tsamiya in a clean bowl of water and drain it thoroughly. Soak the tsamiya in hot water for about 5 minutes. Transfer the tsamiya soaking liquid to a pot over medium heat. Gradually stir in sugar, making sure it dissolves, until it becomes very thick. Stir in your desired flavoring and/or coloring. Divide the syrup between the prepared cones. Let cool slightly, then insert a toothpick or other stick. Let set and harden completely. Unmold and serve.
