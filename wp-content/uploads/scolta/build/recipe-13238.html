Maitorieska (Finnish Barley Flatbread)

Maitorieska (milk rieska, pronounced “RE-ehskuh”) is a barley-based flatbread that is a local specialty and a very traditional food in the Ylivieska area of Finland. Ingredients 250 ml (1 cup) cold milk ⅓ teaspoon salt 250–330 ml (1–1½ cups) barley flour Procedure Preheat oven to 300°C (570°F), or as high as it will go. Combine milk and salt, and add flour. Mix the ingredients well to make a dough. Knead into a flat, round bread (about 0.5 cm / ¼ inch thick). Bake on an oven tray for about 10 minutes or until the surface gets some darker spots. Alternatively, fry it in a pan on the stove or on open fire. Eat fresh with butter and cold milk.
