Ayam Goreng Mentega (Fried Chicken in Sweet Butter Sauce)

Ayam Goreng is an Indonesian fried chicken dish. This version features an accompanying butter sauce. Ingredients Chicken 1 medium-sized chicken, cut into small pieces (12–16 pieces) Juice of 2 lemons 2 tsp salt 2 cups frying oil Sauce 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced 1 small onion, thinly sliced 4 tbsp butter 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 2 tbsp regular soy sauce (tamari) 2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine 8 tbsp Indonesian sweet soy sauce (kecap manis) Garnish Slices of 2 limes Procedure Season the chicken pieces with salt. Coat with lemon juice, and let marinate for 15–20 minutes. Heat the oil to about 350°F (180°C), and fry chicken until it is golden. Remove chicken from oil, and set aside to drain. Sauté the garlic and onion for a minute or two. Add the remaining sauce ingredients. Stir fry for a minute and then add chicken pieces. Toss to coat. Serve hot with lime wedges. See also Ayam Goreng
