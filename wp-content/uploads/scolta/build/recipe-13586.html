Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Ingredients ¾ cup butter, applesauce, equal parts butter and vegetable oil, or shortening 1 ¼ cup brown sugar, firmly packed 1 egg ⅓ cup water or milk 1 ½ tsp vanilla 3 cup raw oats (rolled or quick/old fashioned) 1 cup all-purpose flour ½ tsp baking soda ½ tsp salt ¼ tsp cinnamon 1 cup raisins ¾ cup walnuts, chopped Procedure Heat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease cookie sheet with oil, shortening, or butter. Combine your choice of fat (butter, apple sauce, butter/vegetable oil mix, or shortening), brown sugar, egg, milk, and vanilla; beat with mixer on high speed to blend well. Combine oats, flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon; add to shortening mixture and combine until just blended. Stir in raisins and nuts. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls onto greased cookie sheet, 2 inches apart. Bake for 10–13 minutes, or until lightly browned.
