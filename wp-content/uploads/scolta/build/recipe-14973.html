Zhug II

Ingredients 2–3 red chile peppers, stems removed 4–5 green chile peppers, stems removed 1 bunch of fresh coriander (cilantro) 2–3 heads of fresh garlic, peeled 1 cup water 1 tbsp salt 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp black pepper Olive oil (dash) Procedure Grind and blend together the chiles, coriander, and garlic. Add water, salt, cumin, and pepper. Blend all together again. Store in a sealed jar.
