Nigerian Oatmeal

Nigerian oatmeal is a nutritious and well-balanced dish that can be enjoyed straight or as porridge with milk. Nothing is more filling than oatmeal, making it ideal for a chilly morning or a day following a workout. Ingredients 1 cup oats Milk Water Sugar Salt Banana, sliced Procedure Soak the oats in enough milk or water overnight in the fridge. Bring a pot of water to a boil with a pinch of salt. Stir in the soaked oats and a pinch of sugar. Cook the oats, stirring, until they have absorbed the liquid and become soft. Remove from the heat. Serve warm with banana, milk, and/or yogurt.
