Spicy Grilled Duck Breasts

Ingredients 4 ea. (28 oz) boneless duck breasts Olive oil Salt Freshly-ground black pepper 1 tbsp red pepper flakes Procedure Brush duck with olive oil. Season liberally with salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes. Set aside. Heat a grill to medium-high heat. Add duck breasts and cook, covered, 4–5 minutes. Flip and cook 3–4 more minutes for medium rare. Remove to a plate and let rest 5 minutes. Serve warm, sliced on the bias.
