Peach Pie

Ingredients Crust For 1–2 crusts: 4 cups flour 1 tablespoon sugar 2 teaspoons salt 1¾ cups shortening ½ cup ice water 1 teaspoon vinegar 1 egg Pie 5 pounds peaches 1 teaspoon lemon juice ¼ teaspoon almond extract 2 tablespoons flour ½ teaspoon cinnamon ¼ teaspoon salt 4 Tablespoons butter Procedure Crust Mix flour, sugar, and salt together. Add shortening and mix until crumbly. Beat together ice water, vinegar, and egg and cut into flour mix. Divide into 4 balls. Wrap and refrigerate for ½ hour before rolling out. Line a pie dish with the rolled-out crust, and crimp the edges. Pie Preheat oven to 450°F. Peel and slice peaches. Transfer to a bowl and mix in lemon juice and almond extract. In a separate bowl, mix dry ingredients, and add to peach mixture. Pour into crust and dot with butter. Bake at 450°F for 15 minutes, then 350°F for 35–40 minutes.
