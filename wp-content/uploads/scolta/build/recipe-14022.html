Rice and Peas (Risi e Bisi)

Risi e bisi (or rixi e bixi) is a Northern Italian dish, a signature meal of the city of Venezia (Venice) in particular, in the Veneto region. Its name literally translates to “rice and peas”. Ingredients Rice (Vialone Nano is prefered) Peas in their pods Procedure Cook the rice per the cooking instructions on the packet. Boil the peas with their pods. Mash them. Add the mashed peas to the rice. Notes, tips, and variations Bacon can be added to make it even more delicious.
