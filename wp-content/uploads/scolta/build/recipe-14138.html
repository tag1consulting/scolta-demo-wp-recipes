Salad Shirazi

Salad shirazi is considered a fairly simple salad. It is popular, easy to make, and usually served in place of the green salad when there are health concerns. Ingredients Cucumber Onions Tomatoes Lemon juice or vinegar Salt Pepper Dried mint Procedure Chop the cucumbers, tomatoes, and onions finely. Marinate them in lemon juice or vinegar. Add salt and pepper. Sprinkle with the dried mint.
