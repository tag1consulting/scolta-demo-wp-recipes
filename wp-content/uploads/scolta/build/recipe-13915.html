Pufftaloons

In Australia these pufftaloons are usually made with a yeast-based bread dough, but this recipe uses baking powder instead. Ingredients 2 cups flour 2 tsp baking powder 1 cup milk 1 dessert spoon butter Procedure Combine flour and baking powder. Rub in the butter. Add enough milk to make a soft dough. Deep fry in hot vegetable oil. Serve hot with jam or golden syrup.
