Corny Soufflé

Ingredients 1 package (16 ounces) frozen corn 1 cup milk 3 eggs 2 tablespoons white granulated sugar ⅛ teaspoon salt 1 tablespoon butter, cut into 6 pieces Procedure Preheat oven to 325°F. Put frozen corn, milk, eggs, sugar, and salt into a blender. Blend on medium until ingredients are mixed thoroughly. Pour into a medium-sized baking dish coated with cooking spray. Dot the top with butter pieces. Bake for 70 minutes.
