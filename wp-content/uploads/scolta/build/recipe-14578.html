Sweet Maple Barbecue Chicken

Ingredients 4 whole chicken leg quarters ¼ cup Hickory BBQ Rub 1½ cups Maple Barbecue Sauce, divided Procedure Press rub into chicken. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Preheat grill to medium high. Add chicken pieces and cook, turning often, 15 minutes. Brush chicken liberally with 1 cup barbecue sauce and cook, turning often, until internal temperature reaches 165–170°F. Remove to a platter and cover with aluminum foil and let rest 10 minutes. Serve warm with remaining ½ cup barbecue sauce.
