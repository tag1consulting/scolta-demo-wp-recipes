Chapman (Nigerian Party Drink)

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Recipes | Cuisine of Nigeria Chapman is a sweet non-alcoholic drink widely served at parties and events all across the Nigeria. Ingredients 2 bottles (6 liters) Boom blackcurrant beverage Water 5 pineapples, peeled, cored, and diced 12 apples, cored and diced 10 watermelons, removed from the rind and diced Equipment Large mixing bowl or punch bowl Mixing spoon Ladle Cutting board Large knife Procedure Pour the blackcurrant beverage into the bowl. While stirring, dilute with water until it reaches your desired flavor. If not sufficiently chilled, transfer to the fridge and rest until cold. Divide the diced fruits between serving cups, then fill the cups with the beverage and serve.
