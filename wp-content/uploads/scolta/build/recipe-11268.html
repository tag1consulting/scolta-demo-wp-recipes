Agbalumo Smoothie

Agbalumo smoothie is a delicious Nigerian smoothie made from African star apples. Ingredients African star apple, washed Water Honey or sugar Equipment Blender Cup Procedure Peel the skin off the star apples and remove the seeds. Transfer the peeled fruit to the blender. Begin blending the fruit, adding water to get your desired consistency. Sweeten to taste with honey or sugar. Strain the smoothie through a sieve, and serve.
