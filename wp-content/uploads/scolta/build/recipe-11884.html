Chicken Bacon Broccoli Alfredo Spaghetti

This chicken bacon broccoli alfredo spaghetti recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty. Ingredients 225 g (8 oz) spaghetti 1 tablespoon oil 2 chicken breasts (400 g / 14 oz), chopped ½ teaspoon salt ½ teaspoons pepper 2 garlic cloves 2 cups (200 g / 7 oz) broccoli florets 2 cups (500 ml / 1.1 pints) milk 1 cup (125 g / 4.5 oz) grated parmesan cheese ¼ cup (25 g / 1 oz) chopped parsley 4 strips of streaky bacon Procedure Boil the spaghetti in water until al dente. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan over a medium heat. Cook the chicken in oil for 4 minutes until cooked. Add in salt, pepper, and garlic, and stir. Stir in broccoli, then add milk and cook for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in cheese, parsley and bacon. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the chicken sauce. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve. References
