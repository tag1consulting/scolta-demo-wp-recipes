Calzone

is a popular Italian form of pizza. The word means sock because the resulting pizza looks similar to a sock. Ingredients 1 cup (240 ml) ricotta cheese 1 cup (240 ml) shredded mozzarella cheese 4 ounces (115 g) any topping (such as ham, bacon, salami, pepperoni, and/or sausage) (optional) 2 teaspoons (10 ml) oregano 1 package store-bought pizza dough Procedure Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Combine ricotta, mozzarella, toppings, and oregano. Roll the pizza dough between 2 pieces of waxed paper. Spoon the cheese mixture into the center of the dough. Moisten edges of dough slightly. Fold the dough over, and seal edges by pressing with a fork. Bake until golden brown, about 10 or 12 minutes. Serve with marinara sauce.
