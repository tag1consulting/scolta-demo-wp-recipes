Balsamic Vinaigrette Salad Dressing

This is a basic recipe for Balsamic vinaigrette. Ingredients ¼ cup (60 ml/2.0 US fl oz) balsamic vinegar (preferably aged and/or Italian) 1 cup (250 ml/8.5 US fl oz) Italian olive oil 1 pinch of salt A few grinds black pepper ¼ teaspoon minced garlic (optional) Procedure Mix everything together with a wire whisk until well combined.
