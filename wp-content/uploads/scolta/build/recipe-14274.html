Simple Chocolate Cheese Pie

Chocolate cheese pie is a quick and tasty dessert that’s easy to make using simple ingredients. Ingredients 8 ounces (225 g) cream cheese ¼ cup (60 ml) unsweetened cocoa powder ¼ cup (60 g) powdered sugar 8 ounces (225 g) nondairy whipped topping 1 ready-made graham cracker crumb crust Procedure Melt the cream cheese, and cool slightly. Transfer to a mixing bowl. Mix in the cocoa powder and powdered sugar. Fold in the whipped topping. Pour mixture into the into ready-made crust Cover and place in a refrigerator/freezer. Remove when solid. Serve. Notes, tips, and variations Fresh fruit, such as blueberries and cherries, make a nice addition. Melted caramel makes a nice touch when drizzled on top. Use fudge or melted chocolate bars in place of the cocoa.
