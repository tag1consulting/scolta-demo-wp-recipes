Quick Sparkling Lemonade

Ingredients 7 cups (1.7 liters/1.8 quarts) cold carbonated water 1 cup (225 grams/7.9 ounces) granulated sugar 1 package of lemonade-flavored Kool-Aid, or similar powdered drink mix Procedure Pour the carbonated water into a 2-quart pitcher. Add sugar and Kool-Aid or drink mix. Stir thoroughly. Serve chilled over ice, if desired.
