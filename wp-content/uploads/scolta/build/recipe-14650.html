Tartar Sauce

This is a basic tartar sauce, suitable for fish and chips. The recipe is largely based on a trial and error recreation of one particular restaurant’s tartar sauce and is not typical of most tartar sauces. Ingredients 10 ounces (280 g) mayonnaise 2 ¼ ounces (65 ml) ketchup 2 ounces (55 ml) mustard 2 ounces (55 ml) sweet pickle relish or finely chopped sweet pickles ¼ teaspoon garlic salt ½ teaspoon celery salt Procedure Place the mayonnaise in mixing bowl, and whip it until smooth. Mix in the other ingredients Notes, tips, and variations Pickle juice may be substituted for the relish if a completely smooth texture is desired (for example, if using sauce in a squeeze bottle).
