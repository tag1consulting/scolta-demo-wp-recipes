Oven Pancakes

Ingredients 1 cup (150 g) all-purpose flour 2 teaspoons baking powder ½ teaspoon baking soda ½ teaspoon salt 2 tablespoons sugar 1 cup (250 ml) milk 2 tablespoons vegetable oil 2 teaspoons vanilla 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar Procedure Preheat oven to 232°C (450°F), and oil an 8-inch oven-safe skillet. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar. Add the milk, oil, and vanilla; stir well. Stir in the apple cider vinegar, mix well, and pour immediately into the skillet. Bake at 232°C (450°F) for 18 to 20 minutes, and serve. Notes, tips, and variations When a toothpick is inserted into the completed product, it should come out clean.
