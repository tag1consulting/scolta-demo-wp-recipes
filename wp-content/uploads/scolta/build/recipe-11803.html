Cassava Pone

Cassava pone is a dessert originating in the Caribbean. This recipe is modified from one by Alica’s Pepperpot. Ingredients 3 lbs (1.36 kg) raw cassava, peeled and grated ½ tsp (2.5 ml) ground cinnamon ½ tsp (2.5 ml) ground black pepper ½ tsp (2.5 ml) ground nutmeg 2 tsp (10 ml) vanilla extract 4 tbsp (60 ml) melted butter or coconut oil ¾ cup (180 ml) brown sugar 3 cups (720 ml) unsweetened evaporated milk or coconut milk Procedure Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Combine all the ingredients together, mixing well to incorporate. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish. Bake the cassava pone for 60 minutes until firm and golden.
