Garlic Croutons II

Ingredients 1 cup ½-inches bread cubes from good-quality French or Italian loaf 2 tablespoons olive oil 1 pinch of salt ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder Procedure Warm an 8-inch skillet over low heat while preparing bread. A minute before you are ready to cook, turn heat up to medium. Toss bread in a bowl with oil, salt, and garlic powder. Cook, stirring often, until crisp and golden brown (about 7 minutes). Use as a garnish for soups and salads.
