Welsh Fruit Bread (Bara Brith) II

Ingredients 1 lb dried assorted fruit (e.g. raisins, cherries, etc.) 1½ cups brewed tea 1 lb all-purpose flour 6 tbsp brown sugar 1 egg, beaten 1 tbsp mixed spice 2 tbsp jam Procedure Soak the fruit in the tea for 24 hours Mix flour, sugar, egg, spice, fruit, and jam in bowl to make a batter. Spoon batter into a loaf tin and bake for 45 minutes at 325°F. Let cool, remove from tin, and glaze with honey.
