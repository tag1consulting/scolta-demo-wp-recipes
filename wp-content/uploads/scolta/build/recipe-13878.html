Potato Snow

Ingredients White potatoes Procedure Put the potatoes in a pot, and cover them with cold water. Put the pot on the stove, and bring to a boil. Cook the potatoes until they begin to crack. Drain the potatoes, and place them in a pan by a fire or in the oven until they are quite dry and fall to pieces. Rub the potatoes through a wire sieve onto a serving dish. Serve immediately.
