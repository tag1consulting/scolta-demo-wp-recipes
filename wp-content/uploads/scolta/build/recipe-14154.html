Salty (Namkin) Lassi

Lassi are yogurt-based beverages in either sweet or salted flavors. The most common flavors include mango, rose water, and plain sugar-sweetened lassi. Ingredients 4 cups (1 L) yogurt 3 cups (750 ml) cold water Fresh green chile peppers (quantity according to taste), chopped 1 tsp cumin seeds, roasted and ground 3 tbsp lime or lemon juice 2 tsp salt 1 cup (250 ml) crushed ice Procedure Combine all ingredients except ice in blender and blend until smooth. Add ice and continue blending until frothy.
