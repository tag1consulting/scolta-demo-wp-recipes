Spinach and Ricotta Ravioli Filling

Ingredients 15 oz cooked spinach (fresh, frozen, or canned) 1 lb ricotta or cottage cheese ¾ cup fresh bread crumbs ¼ cup Parmesan cheese 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped ¼ tsp salt ⅛ tsp black pepper ½ tsp grated or minced garlic 1 egg Procedure Squeeze the spinach to remove excess water. Place in a mixing bowl. Add the other ingredients and mix well with a fork.
