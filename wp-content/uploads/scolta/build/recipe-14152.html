Salted Fish Fried Rice

Ingredients 2 tbsp cooking oil 20 g minced garlic 10 g red chopped chilli 20 g chopped spring onion 100 g salted fish 100 g shredded chicken 75 g bean sprouts 1 cup (500 g) cooked jasmine rice 1 tbsp salt 1 tbsp fish sauce 2 tbsp pepper Procedure Heat the oil in non-stick pan. Add the garlic, chilli, and spring onion, and stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the salted fish and chicken. Stir until the chicken and salted fish are changing color. Add the rice and bean sprouts, then add the salt, pepper, and fish sauce.
