Orange Soda I

This is a quick and easy homemade orange soda recipe. Ingredients 2¼ cups (560 ml / 19 oz) water, warm or room temperature 2¾ cups (650 ml / 625 g / 1.38 lb) white granulated sugar 1 oz (30 ml / 28 g) orange juice concentrate or orange syrup Carbonated water, as needed Procedure Combine the water and sugar in a 2-quart or larger saucepan. Stir over medium heat until the sugar dissolves into a syrup, then remove from the heat. Stir the orange juice concentrate into the syrup. Using a funnel, transfer the syrup to an empty bottle and let it cool. To make the finished syrup, mix one volume of syrup with one volume of carbonated water.
