Moroccan Soup

Ingredients 1 onion, diced 350 g lamb meat or 4 chicken breasts, chopped 1 L broth 3 tsp olive oil 400 g diced tomatoes (with garlic if possible) 200 g dried apricots divided into 8 portions 1 tsp paprika powder 1 tsp ground coriander 1 tsp cinnamon Salt Ground black pepper Cayenne Carrots (optional) Sweet potatoes (optional) Procedure Fry the onion lightly in oil. Add spices and the rest of the ingredients, and cook until ready. The cooking time depends on the meat used and on whether carrots and sweet potatoes are added.
