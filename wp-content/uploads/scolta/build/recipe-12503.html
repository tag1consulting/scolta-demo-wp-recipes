Fresh Herb and Cheese Ravioli Filling

Ingredients 4 oz (½ cup) ricotta or cottage cheese 2 oz (¼ cup) blue cheese 2 oz (¼ cup) mozzarella 2 eggs 4 tbsp fresh finely-chopped herbs (basil, chives, parsley, thyme) Salt Pepper Procedure Beat eggs and place in a bowl. Beat in the cheeses with a fork until well blended. Mix in the herbs and seasoning. Refrigerate until the pasta dough is rolled out. Notes, tips, and variations Serve ravioli with this filling accompanied by tomato sauce and spring vegetables.
