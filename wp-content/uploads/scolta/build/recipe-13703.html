Pasta with Sausage

Ingredients ¼ tsp salt ⅓ pound uncooked dried spaghetti 2–4 sausages 1 tbsp butter or oil 3–5 tbsp pasta sauce or ketchup Procedure Bring 2 liters water to a boil, and add salt. Add the spaghetti to the boiling water, and cook for 3 minutes. Add sausages to pasta, and boil for 5 minutes. Drain water. Add butter to the pasta, then mix in sauce or ketchup. Serve.
