Neapolitan Soup

This is a tasty and easy-to-make soup recipe. This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook The Cook’s Decameron: A Study In Taste, Containing Over Two Hundred Recipes For Italian Dishes by Emily Waters. Ingredients ¼ pound (115 g) forcemeat of fowl 1 tablespoon potato flour 1 tablespoon Bechamel sauce 1 egg yolk Peas Asparagus Spinach Clear soup Procedure Mix the fowl with potato flour, Bechamel sauce, and the yolk. Put this into a tube about the size round of an ordinary macaroni. Twenty minutes before serving, squirt the forcemeat into a saucepan of boiling stock, and nip off the forcemeat as it comes through the pipe into pieces about an inch and a half long. Let it simmer, and add boiled peas and asparagus tips. If you like to have the fowl macaroni white and green, you can color half the forcemeat with a spoonful of spinach colouring. Serve in a good clear soup.
