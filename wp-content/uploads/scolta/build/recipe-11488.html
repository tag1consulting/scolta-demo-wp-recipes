Barbecue Fried Rice

Ingredients Rice 2½ tbsp olive oil 1½ tbsp sesame oil 3 cloves red onion, minced 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 red chillies, cut into 1 cm pieces 200 g minced pork 1 carrot, diced ½ horseradish, cut into matchsticks 3 scallions, cut into 1 cm pieces 500 g cooked rice 25 ml barbeque sauce 2 tbsp black soy sauce 1½ tbsp salt 1½ tbsp pepper Toppings (optional) Fried egg Pickles Tomato Cucumber Fried onion Salt Shrimp crackers Beef steak Vegetables like corn, carrot, and peas Equipment Frying pan Knife Stove Procedure Heat the olive oil and sesame oil in a non-stick pan. Fry the garlic and red onion until fragrant. Stir until the garlic and red onion are golden brown. Add the red chilli and pork. Stir until the pork changes color. Add horseradish, scallion, rice, barbecue sauce, black soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Stir until well-mixed. Serve with toppings as desired.
