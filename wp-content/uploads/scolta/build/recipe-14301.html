Śląsko Modro Kapusta (Red Cabbage with Onion and Bacon)

Śląsko modro kapusta is a Silesian version of a side dish made with red cabbage. There also exists a version from the Greater Poland region. Serve as a side dish, normally with Silesian dumplings and roulade. Ingredients 1 kg red cabbage, finely sliced 150 g bacon 1 medium onion, chopped Salt Pepper Sugar Vinegar Procedure Cook sliced cabbage in salted water. Dice bacon and fry it in a pan. Add onion to the bacon, and sauté until translucent. Drain the cabbage, and toss with the bacon and onion. Season to your liking with salt, pepper, sugar, and vinegar.
