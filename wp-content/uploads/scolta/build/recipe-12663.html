Grape Lassi

Ingredients 2 cups black grapes 1 cup fresh curd (thick plain Indian yogurt) 1 cup milk 4 tbsp sugar 6 ice cubes 1 tbsp salt Procedure Blend black grapes, sugar, curd, milk, and salt in a blender until smooth. Crush ice-cubes, divide it into 4 portions, and divide between 4 glasses. Pour the lassi over the ice cubes. Serve chilled.
