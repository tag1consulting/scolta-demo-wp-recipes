Gullisuwa (Nigerian Milk Balls)

Gullisuwa is a type of fritter commonly eaten in the northern part of Nigeria during festive periods and weddings, and as street food. Ingredients ½ cup powdered milk ¼ cup white granulated sugar 2 tablespoons cornstarch Vanilla powder Water Vegetable oil Equipment Bowl Wooden spoon Frying pan Sieve Procedure Combine powdered milk, sugar, cornstarch, and vanilla powder in a bowl. Mix thoroughly. Mix in enough water to form a dough. Grease your hands with vegetable oil, and roll the dough into small balls. Deep fry the dough in a pan of hot vegetable oil until browned all over. Use spoon to remove the fritters from the oil and drain in a sieve. Serve fresh. Notes, tips, and variations Different variety of gullisuwa can be made by adding chilli pepper, cashew nuts, and coconut.
