Oatmeal Banana Waffle

Ingredients 2 cups oatmeal 2 cups water ½ banana 2 tablespoons powdered milk 2 tablespoons olive oil ½ teaspoon salt Procedure Turn waffle iron on high. Put all ingredients in a blender and blend on high until the contents are smooth. Let the mixture sit for 10–15 minutes. Spray the waffle iron with non-stick cooking spray. Pour some of the batter onto the waffle iron. Cook for about 10 minutes. Repeat until you are out of batter.
