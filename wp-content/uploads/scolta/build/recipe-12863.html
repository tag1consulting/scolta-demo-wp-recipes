Hot Chocolate (Orange)

Orange hot chocolate is a twist on the old childhood favorite. Note that this hot chocolate is not sweet. Those who like it sweeter should use a chocolate with a higher sugar content, or add 1 tablespoon sugar with the chocolate. Ingredients 4 cups (1 liter / 1.1 quart) milk 1 stick cinnamon 4 oz (110 g) bittersweet chocolate, chopped (can substitute semisweet) ½ tsp orange extract ¼ tsp vanilla extract Procedure Place the milk and cinnamon in a saucepan, and gently heat to just below a simmer, about 180 °F (82 °C). Add the chocolate, and stir while allowing to melt. Do not allow to come to a boil. When the chocolate has melted, strain the chocolate milk into another container, add the extracts, and whisk or blend until frothy. Transfer to mugs or heatproof glasses and serve.
