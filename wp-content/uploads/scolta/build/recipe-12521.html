Fried Green Tomatoes

Ingredients 4–6 green tomatoes Salt Pepper Yellow cornmeal Vegetable or olive oil Procedure Slice the tomatoes ¼–½ inch (0.75–1.25 cm) thick. Salt and pepper tomato slices to taste. Dip slices in cornmeal and shallow fry in hot grease or oil over medium heat for about 3 minutes or until golden on bottom. Gently turn over, and fry the other side. Serve by themselves or, for a elegant addition, add a little goat cheese.
