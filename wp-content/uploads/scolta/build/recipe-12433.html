Fast Food French Fries

Ingredients 2 pounds russet potatoes, cut slightly larger than julienne Salt Freshly-ground black pepper Neutral oil for deep frying Procedure Place potatoes in a large container of clean water. Rinse repeatedly until water runs clear. Spin potatoes in a salad spinner until dry. Deep fry at 350°F until tender and limp but not browned. Drain on a cooling rack. Heat oil to 375°F, and fry potatoes for 4–5 minutes. Drain fries on a cooling rack, and sprinkle heavily with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Serve hot.
