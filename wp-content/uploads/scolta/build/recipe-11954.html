Chinese Chews (Nut and Date Bars)

Chinese chews are a bar cookie containing nuts and dried fruit. They have a chewy consistency. Ingredients 2 eggs ⅔ cup sugar ⅔ cup self-raising flour ⅓ tsp salt 1 tsp vanilla ½ cup chopped dates ½ cup seedless raisins ¾ cup chopped walnuts Icing sugar to taste Procedure Beat eggs until foamy. Add sugar and mix. Add flour and salt. Add vanilla, then the fruit and nuts. Mix well. Spread in greased 8-inch cake pan. Bake for about 30–35 minutes at 350°F (175°C) until golden brown. Cut into squares while still warm. Roll in icing sugar. Notes, tips, and variations Can be enjoyed on its own or with ice cream. Cools to a firm consistency.
