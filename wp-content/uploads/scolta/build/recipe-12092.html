Cola BBQ Chicken

Sweet and spicy. It’s a nice twist on a barbecue favorite. Ingredients 4 each boneless skinless chicken breasts and chicken drumsticks ¼ cup Barbecue Chicken Rub 1 cup Cola BBQ Sauce Procedure Season chicken with Rub. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Preheat grill for medium heat. Place chicken on grill and turn often, basting with sauce every time the chicken is turned until internal temperature reaches 165°F inside the breast and 170°F in the drumstick. Rest 15 minutes; serve.
