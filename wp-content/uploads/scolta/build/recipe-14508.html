Strawberry Daiquiri

The strawberry daiquiri is a cocktail composed mainly of rum, schnapps, and strawberries. It can be shaken or puréed in a blender for a smooth consistency. Ingredients Strawberry juice, for rim Sugar, for rim 1 oz light rum ½ oz strawberry schnapps 1 oz lime juice 2 tsp powdered sugar 2 oz fresh or frozen strawberries Crushed ice Whipped cream, for garnish Fresh strawberry, for garnish Procedure Rim a glass in strawberry juice and sugar. Shake rum, schnapps, lime juice, powdered sugar, strawberries, and crushed ice in a cocktail shaker. Pour into the rimmed glass. Garnish with whipped cream and a whole fresh strawberry. Serve.
