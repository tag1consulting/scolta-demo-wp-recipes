Stewed Oysters I

There are several ways to stew oysters. Two recipes are given below. Ingredients 2 dozen (24 ea.) raw, whole oysters 2 oz (55 g) butter ¼ cup cream ¼ tsp mace 2 bay leaves Salt, black pepper, and nutmeg, to taste Procedure Shuck the oysters, saving any liquid in the oyster. Put your oysters with all their liquid into a saucepan; add the butter, salt, black pepper, mace, and bay leaves. Bring to a simmer for 5 minutes. Add the cream and shake all well together while heating through.
