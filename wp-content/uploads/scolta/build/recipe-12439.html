Fermented Soda

This recipe uses premade extracts and fermentation to make a carbonated beverage. It is adapted from an online blog. Ingredients ⅛ tsp (0.625 ml) champagne yeast or 2 tsp (10 ml) kefir grains ½ tbsp (7.5 ml) soda extract 1 cup (240 ml) white sugar 2 L water Procedure Sterilize all your equipment before beginning. Combine all of the ingredients in a container suitable for fermentation. Note that gas will be produced during the fermentation. Put the container in a warm place, and allow it to ferment for 3 days. If desired, strain the finished product. Store in the fridge. Notes, tips, and variations When fermenting in sealed containers from which gas cannot escape, there is a risk of explosion, which can range from messy to dangerous. Use caution and select an appropriate container. If you allow the fermentation to proceed for too long (i.e. longer than a few days, depending on the conditions), it will develop a significant alcohol content and will decrease significantly in sweetness.
