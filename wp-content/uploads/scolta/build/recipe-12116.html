Coquitos (Coconut Macaroons)

Ingredients 4 large eggs 250 g desiccated coconut 200 g white sugar 1 tsp vanilla extract 2 tbsp water (optional) Procedure Preheat your oven to 180°C. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl. If your paste is too thick to pipe with a large piping tip, then add the water, and keep adding one tablespoon of water at a time until you get a consistency you can pipe. If your eggs are extra large, you may not need the extra water. Pipe dollops on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake in a medium to hot oven for 30 minutes or until done.
