Egidi (Pounded Corn)

Egidi, also known as pounded corn, is a typical Yoruba dish common among the people of Ondo state. It is served with various soups, such as as okro, ogbono, and ẹgusi soups. Ingredients Fresh corn kernels Leaves, for wrapping Water Equipment Mortar and pestle Stove Pot Bowl/plate Procedure Grind the corn kernels to make a paste. Wrap the paste using wrapping leaves. Cook for few minutes. Pound using mortar and pestle until the mixture is lump-free. Serve with soup.
