Sweet Pumpkin Scones

Ingredients ¼ cup butter or margarine, softened slightly ¼ cup white sugar 1 egg 2 tbsp corn syrup 1 cup cooked, mashed butternut squash, pumpkin, or other squash 2 ⅔ cups all-purpose flour 2 tsp cream of tartar 1 tsp baking soda 1 pinch salt ½ tsp ground nutmeg 1 pinch ground ginger ⅓ cup milk (or less) Procedure Stir together the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, salt, ground nutmeg and ground ginger. In a separate bowl, beat butter and sugar together with an electric mixer. Add in the egg and corn syrup, and mix with a spoon to prevent over-mixing. Stir in the squash and half of the sifted dry ingredients. Add the rest of the dry ingredients with just enough of the milk to make a soft dough. If it is too sticky to work, add a sprinkle (or two) of flour. Knead only a little, gently, until you can pat it out into a big flat rectangle about ¾ inch thick. Cut into 12 pieces with a floured knife. Transfer to a lightly greased oven tray. Bake at 230°C (450°F) for 18 minutes.
