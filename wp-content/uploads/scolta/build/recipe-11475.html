Banana Split I

The Banana Split is a dessert of banana and ice cream, often with additional toppings. Ingredients 1 banana 1 scoop vanilla ice cream 1 scoop strawberry ice cream 1 scoop chocolate ice cream Chocolate sauce Whipped cream (optional) Procedure Split the banana in 2 parts and put them side by side. Place vanilla ice-cream in middle and strawberry and chocolate ice-creams on the side. Pour some chocolate sauce over them. Top with whipped cream if desired. Serve immediately.
