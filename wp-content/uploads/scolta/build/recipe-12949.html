Italian Dressing I

Ingredients ¼ cup drained capers 1 clove garlic, pressed 1 shallot, minced ½ cup olive oil ½ teaspoon black pepper 1 small dried red chile, sliced ½ teaspoon crushed fennel seed 1 tablespoon white Chianti or dry white wine 2 tablespoons lemon juice Procedure Combine all ingredients in a jar with tight-fitting top. Shake well. Notes, tips, and variations Best if used fresh.
