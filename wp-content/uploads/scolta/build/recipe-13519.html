Nigerian Fried Noodles

Nigerian fried noodles are a famous dish in Nigeria, consumed by all. Ingredients Instant noodles Vegetable oil Onion, chopped Bell peppers, diced Carrot, sliced thin Leafy green vegetables Stock cube Procedure Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add the instant noodles, and cook until softened but still slightly firm. Drain the noodles. Heat a thin layer of vegetable oil in a frying pan. Add the onion, peppers, carrot, and leafy greens, and stir-fry for 2 minutes. Season the vegetables with stock cube or the instant noodle seasoning powder. Add the cooked noodles to the vegetables, and stir-fry them until frothy. Lower the heat so the noodles can fry more slowly for a few minutes. Serve hot.
