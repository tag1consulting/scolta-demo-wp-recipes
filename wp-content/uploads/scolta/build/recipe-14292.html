Sindhi Spiced Moong Dal

Ingredients 200 g yellow moong dal 4 green chiles 8–10 g fresh whole ginger Salt to taste 1 pinch coriander powder 1 tbsp ground pepper 1 pinch red chile powder Oil Procedure Soak the dal in water for 1 hour. Drain. Combine the dal, chiles, and ginger in a pot. Do not add salt. Cover the dal with water. Boil in a pressure cooker for at least 3–4 minutes. Transfer the dal to a bowl. Season with salt, coriander, and pepper. Sprinkle with chili powder, and drizzle with some oil.
