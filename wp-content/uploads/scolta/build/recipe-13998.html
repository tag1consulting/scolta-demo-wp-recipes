Red Bell Peppers Stuffed with Cheese and Egg (Chushki Byurek)

Ingredients 1 kg red bell peppers 250 g brined sheep’s cheese 6 eggs Sunflower oil Salt Parsley, finely chopped Procedure Roast, peel, and seed the peppers. Mix the crumbled cheese with 4 of the eggs. Stuff the peppers with this mixture. Beat the remaining two eggs. Roll the stuffed peppers in breadcrumbs and beaten eggs. Fry the breaded peppers in hot oil. Serve sprinkled with finely-chopped parsley.
