Chocolate Sauce

A simple recipe that is a delicious sauce for Éclairs and other desserts. Ingredients 1½ oz butter 1½ oz caster sugar 1½ fluid oz (70 ml) double cream 1½ oz plain chocolate Procedure Melt all the ingredients in a saucepan and allow to bubble for a while until the mixture darkens in colour slightly. Be careful not to let the chocolate burn. Remove from the heat and allow to cool until it thickens.
