French Martini

The French martini is a cocktail served in two forms, sometimes referred to as “classic” and “modern.” The modern form is presented here. Ingredients 4 parts vodka 1 part pineapple juice 1 part Chambord (or other raspberry/blackberry liqueur) 2 maraschino cherries Procedure Shake ingredients with ice. Serve in a cocktail glass with maraschino cherries.
