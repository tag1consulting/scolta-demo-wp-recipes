Sweet Charoset

Ingredients 2 apples peeled, cored, and grated 3 bananas chopped and mashed Juice and zest of ½ lemon Juice and zest of ½ orange 15 dates, pitted and chopped finely ½ cup ground walnuts 1 tsp cinnamon ¼ cup red wine 3 tsp matzo meal Honey to taste Procedure Mix apples, bananas, lemon juice, lemon zest, orange juice, orange zest, dates, and nuts in a bowl. Add cinnamon, wine, and matzo meal, and blend thoroughly. Mix in honey to taste.
