Roasted Brown Rice Mochi

Ingredients 2 cups uncooked brown rice 2–2 ½ cups water 2 pinches sea salt Procedure Wash the rice and soak for a couple of hours or overnight. Cook the rice in a pressure cooker until the liquid is absorbed. Grind the cooked rice in a mortar until the grain is completely fluid and sticky. This will take at least 30 minutes of grinding when done by hand. Shape the mash into 2 cm squares. Place the rice squares on an oiled plate and allow to dry for 1–2 days. Keep covered in a cold dry room or in the refrigerator. After this, roast the squares for 5 minutes in a pan.
