Banana Curry

Ingredients 750 g banana (slightly unripe) ½ teaspoon cumin seeds ½ teaspoon turmeric powder ½ teaspoon garam masala or good curry powder 2 teaspoons lemon juice ½ glass of milk 4 tablespoons double cream ½ teaspoon chile pepper powder 30 g butter some salt Procedure Cut banana in 2 cm slices. Add milk, cream, garam masala (or curry) powder, chili, and lemon (add some salt) to the bananas. Melt butter, add cumin seeds and turmeric, and cook at medium low for a couple of minutes until a fragrant smell develops. Add bananas and their sauce. Cook on a gentle flame until ready (depends on banana’s ripeness)—do not overcook.
