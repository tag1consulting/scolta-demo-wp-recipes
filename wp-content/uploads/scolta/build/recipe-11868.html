Chess Pie

Ingredients 2.5 cups (600 g) white sugar 3 eggs 1 tablespoon vinegar 1 teaspoon vanilla 1 stick (4 oz / 113 g) butter 9-inch (22 cm) pre-made pie crust Procedure Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Mix sugar, butter, and vinegar in saucepan. Bring to a boil. Beat eggs in bowl. Gradually whisk the saucepan contents into the eggs, then add vanilla. Pour mixture into pie crust. Bake pie in 375°F (190°C) oven for 35 minutes. Let cool.
