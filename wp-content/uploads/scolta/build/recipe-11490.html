Barbecue Potato Chips

These chips are just as good as store-bought. Ingredients 1 pound Russet potatoes, sliced thinly on a mandoline Barbecue Rub Oil for deep frying Procedure Heat oil to 375°F. Add potatoes to hot oil one slice at a time, working in batches as needed. Fry potatoes for 4–5 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack. Once all potatoes have been fried, sprinkle liberally with the barbecue rub. Serve. Notes, tips, and variations Don’t fry too many potato slices at once—doing so will cause the oil temperature to drop.
