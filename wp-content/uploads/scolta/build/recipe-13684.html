Passover Apple Cake

This is like a coffee cake and can be served as dessert or a snack. It uses no flour and is therefore kosher for Passover. Ingredients Cake 3 eggs ¾ cup white granulated sugar ⅓ cup oil ¾ cup matzo cake meal 5 apples, peeled and sliced (Granny Smiths are best) Topping ⅓ cup chopped walnuts ½ cup sugar 2 tsp ground cinnamon Procedure In a medium-sized mixing bowl, beat the eggs with the sugar and oil until the mixture is light. Add the cake meal and mix well. Pour half of the mixture into a lightly-greased 8–9-inch square baking pan. Distribute half the apples over the batter. Pour the remaining batter over the apples, and cover with the remaining apples. Combine topping ingredients in a small bowl; sprinkle over the apples. Bake in a preheated 350°F oven for approximately 1 ½ hours.
