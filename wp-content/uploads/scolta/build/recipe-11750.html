Calamansi Juice Drink

Calamansi juice drink is a Filipino version of lemonade because calamansi is readily available in the marketplace. Ingredients 3 or 4 calamansi 1 glass cold water ½ tablespoon granulated sugar Ice cubes (optional) Procedure Halve the calamansi with a sharp knife. Use your hands to squeeze the juice from the calamansi. Combine the juice with the cold water in a glass. Stir in sugar until dissolved. Add ice cubes. Notes, tips, and variations This drink can also be served hot. It is a good source of Vitamin C.
