Kwado (Nigerian Cassava Flour Salad)

Kwado, also called coldmeal, is a Nigerian meal consumed primarily by students in boarding houses. It is prepared without cooking, hence the name ‘coldmeal’. Ingredients Nigerian smoked dried fish Onions, sliced and rinsed 2 cups garri (cassava flour) 1 kuli kuli, ground Salt to taste Dry pepper to taste Procedure Mash the smoked fish in a bowl. Add the garri, ground kuli kuli, sliced onions, salt, and dry pepper. Stir the ingredients together and serve. Notes, tips, and variations You can add some water to moisten the garri if desired.
