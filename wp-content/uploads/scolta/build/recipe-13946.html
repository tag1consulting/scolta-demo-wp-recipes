Queen&#8217;s Sauce (Hartshorne)

Queen’s Sauce is a thick, creamy sauce for heart meals. This recipe is an interpreted method from the original recipe in Henry Hartshorne’s the Household Cyclopedia. Ingredients 200 ml gravy White breadcrumbs 50 g almonds, pounded 2 egg yolks Fowl breast, roasted 200 ml cream 3 tbsp salt 3 tbsp pepper Procedure Place the gravy into a saucepan and simmer, stirring the breadcrumbs into it until thickened. Add the almonds, egg yolks, fowl breast, and cream. Mix well until combined. Place onto a simmer and add salt and pepper to serve. References
