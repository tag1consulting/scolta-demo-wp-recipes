Pizza Hot Dogs

Ingredients 4 hot dog buns 4 hot dogs 12 slices mozzarella cheese 1 jar pizza sauce Grated Parmesan cheese Procedure Split hot dogs and hot dog buns lengthwise. Place buns on a cookie sheet. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over buns. Place split hot dog on each bun. Spread pizza sauce over the hotdogs, sprinkle with Parmesan cheese, and broil until cheese bubbles and the hot dogs are hot.
