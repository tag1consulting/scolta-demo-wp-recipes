Peanut Chicken Stir-Fry

Ingredients 2 tablespoons butter ¾ cup celery ¼ cup onion 2 cups cooked chicken or turkey 1 small can (8–10 ounce) mushrooms, drained 1 tablespoon cornstarch 3 tablespoons soy sauce 1 cup clear chicken broth 1 cup peanuts Cooked rice Procedure Melt butter in wok or skillet. Stir-fry celery and onions. Add chicken and mushrooms and stir over lowered heat 5 minutes. Combine (in separate bowl) cornstarch, soy sauce, and broth; slowly add to hot mixture in wok and cook 5 minutes. Stir in peanuts. Serve over hot rice. Notes, tips, and variations You could use cashews.
