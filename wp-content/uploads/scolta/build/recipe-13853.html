Potato and Garlic Ravioli Filling

Ingredients 3 large russet potatoes 4 tbsp olive oil 6 cloves garlic, grated with a micro plane or finely chopped 1 tbsp chives, finely sliced 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice ½ tsp salt ½ tsp black pepper Procedure Boil the potatoes in their skins until tender when pierced with a fork. Peel them as soon as they are cool enough to handle and mash in a bowl. Sauté the garlic in the oil for 1 minute. Add the potatoes and mix well. Add the chives, lemon juice and seasoning and mix well. Cover and allow to cool while you prepare the pasta dough.
