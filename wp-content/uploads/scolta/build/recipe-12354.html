Egg Casserole

Egg casserole is great at any time of the day and is good to serve when family gets together or in other large groups. Ingredients 8 slices of bread 7–8 eggs 2 cups milk 1 stick butter, melted 2 cups diced ham ½ tsp salt 1 tablespoon prepared mustard 2 cups grated Velveeta cheese 1 dash each of red pepper, paprika, and garlic powder Procedure Put bread in a greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Mix melted butter, milk, seasonings, and beaten eggs. Pour mixture over bread. Sprinkle with diced ham and top with grated cheese. Refrigerate overnight. Bake for 1 hour at 350°F.
