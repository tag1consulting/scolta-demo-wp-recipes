Fisherman&#8217;s Casserole

Ingredients 1 package (8 ounces) macaroni ½ pound grated cheddar cheese ⅔ cup milk ½ teaspoon dry mustard ½ cup chopped onion 1 can (10¾ ounces) cream of celery soup 1 teaspoon salt 1 can (14¾ ounces) salmon 1 bag (8 ounces) frozen peas 1 cup bread crumbs 2 tablespoons butter, melted Procedure Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Cook macaroni according to package directions; drain. In a large bowl, combine cheese, macaroni, milk, dry mustard, onion, soup, and salt. Add salmon and frozen peas. Place in a greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Cover mixture with bread crumbs; drizzle with melted butter. Bake for 45 minutes.
