Chuck Wagon Beans

Ingredients ½ pound bacon, cut into bite-sized pieces 1 pound ground chuck 1 large onion, chopped 2 cloves garlic, minced ⅓ cup brown sugar ⅓ cup white sugar 2 tablespoons molasses ¼ cup barbecue sauce 2 tablespoons mustard 1 teaspoon pepper 2 teaspoons salt ½ teaspoon chili powder 32 ounces canned pork and beans Procedure Brown bacon, ground chuck, and onion. Stir in remaining ingredients in order; mix well in between each addition. Bake at 350°F for about 40 minutes, or until thickened. Notes, tips, and variations Good with corn bread
