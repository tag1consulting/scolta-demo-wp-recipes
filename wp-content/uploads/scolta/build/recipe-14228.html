Sesame Ginger Marinade

You can use this as a sauce or a marinade for either pork or shortribs. Ingredients ½ cup soy sauce ¼ cup dark brown sugar 2 tsp sesame oil 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds 3 tbsp minced fresh ginger 2 tbsp minced garlic Procedure Give everything a big shake in an 8-ounce mason jar until smooth. Refrigerate for up to two weeks.
