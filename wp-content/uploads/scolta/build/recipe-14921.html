Wine-Roasted Pork Roast

Ingredients 2 tablespoons olive oil 2 tablespoons Montreal steak seasoning 4 to 5-pound boneless pork loin roast 1 large red onion, chopped 1½ cups red wine Procedure Heat olive oil in 10-inch Dutch oven over campfire. Rub seasoning on roast. Add roast to Dutch oven, turning to brown on all sides. Add onion, sauté 5 minutes. Pour wine over roast. Cover and cook slowly for three hours over fire.
