Fried Bananas

Fried bananas is a delightful dessert or snack enjoyed in many cuisines around the world. These sweet, caramelized banana slices offer a perfect balance of crispy and tender textures. Whether served on their own or as a topping for ice cream or pancakes, fried bananas are a simple yet satisfying treat. Ingredients 4 ripe bananas, peeled and sliced 2 tablespoons butter 2 tablespoons brown sugar 1 teaspoon cinnamon (optional) Vanilla ice cream (optional, for serving) Procedure Heat a skillet or frying pan over medium heat and melt the butter. Add the banana slices to the pan and sprinkle them with brown sugar. Cook the bananas for 2–3 minutes on each side or until they turn golden brown and caramelized. If desired, sprinkle cinnamon over the bananas during cooking. Remove the fried bananas from the pan and serve them hot. Optionally, serve with a scoop of vanilla ice cream for an extra treat. Notes, tips, and variations Experiment with different spices like nutmeg for unique flavour variations. Drizzle honey or chocolate syrup over the fried bananas for added sweetness. You can also add a splash of rum or liqueur for an adult twist on this dessert. Fried bananas can be served as a topping for pancakes, waffles, or French toast.
