Baked Pork Chops

A relatively simple recipe for baked pork chops. Ingredients 3–4 bone-in pork chops, ¼ inch (6 mm) thin 1 medium apple, cored and sliced thin 1 medium onion, sliced thin 1 tablespoon (15 ml) honey 1 tablespoon (15 ml) caraway seed (can be salvaged from the bag your rye bread comes in) Garlic salt, black pepper, and Dijon mustard, to taste Procedure Heat oven to 350 °F (180 °C) Layer sliced onion and apple in a baking dish that can be covered; drizzle honey and half the caraway over apple and onion slices Season meat with garlic salt and pepper; spread top of meat with Dijon Sprinkle remainder of caraway Cover and bake for one hour.
