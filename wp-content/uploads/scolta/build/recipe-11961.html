Chipotle BBQ Sauce

This chipotle BBQ sauce is really smoky and hot, so if you can’t stand the heat, adjust it to your liking. Ingredients 2 cans chipotle in adobo including all of adobo sauce 1 bottle ketchup 1 cup molasses ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce ½ cup soy sauce 2 tbsp olive oil 2 tbsp all-purpose flour ¼ cup cider vinegar Procedure Whisk together oil and flour and cook on medium heat, stirring constantly until light blonde color is achieved. Add remaining ingredients and bring to a boil; reduce heat to low and cook 15 minutes. Cool for 15 minutes and purée in batches until smooth. Store in nonreactive container for up to 1 week.
