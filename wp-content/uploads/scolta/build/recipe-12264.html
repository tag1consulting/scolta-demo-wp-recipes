Deep Fried Urad and Rice Flour Dough Balls (Murukku)

Ingredients: 2 cups rice flour ½ cup urad dal 2 tsp jeera 4 tsp sesame seeds 1 tsp red chile powder Salt to taste 3 tbsp oil (hot) Oil for deep frying Procedure Dry roast the urad dal. Grind it into a fine powder, and keep it aside. In a wide bowl combine the rice flour, urad dal powder, chili powder, salt, sesame seeds, and jeera. Mix it well, then mix in the oil. Mix in enough water to make a dough of roti consistency. In a deep frying pan heat enough oil for deep frying. Once the oil is hot enough for frying, press dough into a murukku mould. Fry the murukku in batches until they are light golden brown in color. Drain the finished fritters on a paper towel. Notes, tips, and variations The frying oil should not be too hot, or the murukku will burn. These can keep up to 2 weeks in an airtight container.
