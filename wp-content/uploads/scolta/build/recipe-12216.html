Cucumber and Radish Salad

This salad is very easy to make. Ingredients 1 ea. (about 5 oz) pickling cucumber 3–4 ea. (about 5 oz) radishes 1 ea. (about 1 oz) green onion 1–2 tsp sour cream ½ tsp salt (to taste) Procedure Chop cucumbers and radishes into small cubes (about ¼ inch on the side). Chop green onions. Combine all of the above in an appropriately sized bowl. Add sour cream and mix well. Add salt to taste and mix again. Notes, tips, and variations For best taste, consume freshly prepared.
