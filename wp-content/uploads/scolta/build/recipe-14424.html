Spicy Chickpeas

Spicy chickpeas make a good fall-back meal using ingredients that many pantries will often have lying in a dusty corner, but that are also cheap to pick up. Ingredients 250 g canned chickpeas 2 tbsp oil 2 tsp Dijon mustard Pepper Salt Procedure Strain chickpeas and dry with a towel. Heat oil in large skillet. Add chickpeas, and sauté until slightly colored, about 6 minutes. Add Dijon mustard and stir. Season with pepper and salt to taste.
