Date Syrup

Date syrup is a syrup used in various foods and drinks, both alcoholic and nonalcoholic. This recipe originates from Unicorns in the Kitchen. Ingredients 3 cups (360 ml) water 1 cup (120 ml) Medjool dates, seeds removed Procedure Bring the water to a boil, and pour it over the dates in a heat-proof bowl. Let the dates soak for around half an hour. Use a blender to purée the date-water mixture. Transfer the mixture to a pot, and simmer over medium heat for 1 hour. Remove the mixture from the heat and allow to cool to room temperature. Strain the mixture through several layers of cheesecloth or a nut milk bag. Return the strained liquid to a pot, and simmer until it reaches your desired syrup consistency. Keep in mind that it will thicken after cooling—if you have a thermometer, you can use it to monitor the sugar concentration, aiming for somewhere between 104–113°C (219–235°F). Remove from the heat and allow to cool. Notes, tips, and variations Be careful while mixing the syrup; hot sugar can splash and cause serious burns. You can use this syrup to make date soda.
