Pasta with Vegetables

Ingredients ¼ tsp salt ⅓ pound uncooked dried spaghetti 2 medium tomatoes, chopped 1 medium cucumber, chopped ½ medium onion, chopped 1 tbsp butter or oil 3–5 tbsp pasta sauce or ketchup Procedure Bring 2 liters water to a boil with the salt. Add spaghetti to boiling water. Cook for 4 minutes. Add chopped tomatoes and cucumber to the boiling pasta. Boil for another 5 minutes. Drain the water, add the butter or oil, and mix in the chopped onion. Mix in sauce or ketchup, and serve.
