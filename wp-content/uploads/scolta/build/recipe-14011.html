Rhubarb Crumble III

Ingredients 500 g (1 lb) fresh trimmed rhubarb 75 g (3 oz) caster sugar 2 tbsp water 150 g (6 oz) self-raising flour 75 g (3 oz) butter 100 g (4 oz) brown sugar Procedure Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F / gas mark 5). Cut the rhubarb into pieces and stew for a short time with the caster sugar and water until tender. While the rhubarb is stewing, combine the flour and butter in a bowl until it resembles fine breadcrumbs. Stir in the brown sugar. Place the cooked rhubarb in a deep pie dish (1 L / 2 pt). Spoon over the crumble topping and spread out evenly Bake crumble for about 25 mins until the crumble is golden brown.
