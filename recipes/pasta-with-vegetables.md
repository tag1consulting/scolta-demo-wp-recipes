## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp salt
- ⅓ pound uncooked dried spaghetti
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 medium cucumber, chopped
- ½ medium onion, chopped
- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- 3–5 tbsp pasta sauce or ketchup

## Procedure

1. Bring 2 liters water to a boil with the salt.
2. Add spaghetti to boiling water. Cook for 4 minutes.
3. Add chopped tomatoes and cucumber to the boiling pasta. Boil for another 5 minutes.
4. Drain the water, add the butter or oil, and mix in the chopped onion.
5. Mix in sauce or ketchup, and serve.
