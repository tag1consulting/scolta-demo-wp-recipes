## Ingredients

- Mashed potatoes
- Egg yolk
- Egg, beaten
- Flour
- Breadcrumbs
- Drippings or other fat/oil

## Procedure

1. Combine the mashed potatoes and egg yolk.
2. Roll the potato mixture into balls.
3. Dip the balls in the beaten egg, then coat with flour or breadcrumbs.
4. Fry the balls in the drippings until golden.
