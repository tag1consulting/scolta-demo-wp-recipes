# Avocado Coconut Smoothie

**Avocado coconut smoothie** is a smoothie recipe made with avocado and coconut milk

## Ingredients

- Ripened avocado
- Spinach (optional)
- Honey
- Ice
- Coconut milk

## Equipment

- Blender

## Procedure

1. Blend coconut, avocado, honey, and spinach in a blender until smooth.
2. Add ice and serve chilled.

## Notes, tips and variations

- Do not use coconut water to make the smoothie as this will affect the overall outcome of the drink recipe.
- To get a thinner smoothie, add more milk; add more ice to get a thicker smoothie.
