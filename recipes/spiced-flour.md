## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup (90 g/3.2 oz) flour
- 1⅛ teaspoon paprika
- ⅔ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/6 teaspoon pepper
- ⅛ teaspoon (or less) cayenne pepper

## Procedure

1. Mix together ingredients thoroughly.

## Uses

- Use in preparation of Onion Blooms.
