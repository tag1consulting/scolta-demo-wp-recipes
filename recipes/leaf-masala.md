# Leaf Masala

**Leaf masala** is an aromatic blend of herbs and spices which create a wonderful, mild curry. It forms an integral part of Cape Malay Cuisine.

## Ingredients

- Coriander
- Turmeric
- Chile powder
- Fennel
- Cumin
- Cloves
- Cinnamon
- Curry leaves
- Cardamom
- Permento
- Star anise
- Bay leaves

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients together.
2. Use as directed in your given recipe.
