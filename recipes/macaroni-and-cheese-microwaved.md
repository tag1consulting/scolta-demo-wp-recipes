## Ingredients

- Uncooked macaroni pasta
- Cheese, grated
- ½ cup milk
- Salt
- Pepper
- Chives, chopped
- Water

## Procedure

1. Add macaroni in a bowl and add water for the macaroni to absorb. Cook in the microwave for 3 minutes.
2. Remove from the microwave. Add cheese and milk then stir. Insert into the microwave again for 30 seconds.
3. Add salt and pepper
4. Add chives for garnish
