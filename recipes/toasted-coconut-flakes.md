# Toasted Coconut Flakes

These **toasted coconut flakes** are a crunchy, caramelized snack made from fresh coconut. They are a popular snack in Nigeria.

## Ingredients

- Ripe fresh coconut
- Granulated sugar
- Salt

## Equipment

- Oven
- Grater
- Bowl
- Baking sheet/tray

## Procedure

1. Crack open the coconut, and separate out the coconut meat.
2. Grate the coconut meat into flakes.
3. Toss the coconut flakes with sugar and a pinch of salt.
4. Spread the coconut mixture on a baking sheet, and place on the lowest rack of the oven. Bake at 300˚F, stirring every few minutes until golden brown and crisp.
5. Remove the coconut flakes and cool completely.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Ensure you are fully concentrated while toasting the coconut, as it will burn quite fast.
- If you layer the coconut too thick on the pan, they will take very long to crisp up.
