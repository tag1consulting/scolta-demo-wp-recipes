# Tanzanian Sweet Fried Spaghetti

**Tambi za kukaanga na sukari** ("fried noodles with sugar") is a Tanzanian or Swahili recipe of sweet fried noodles. It is easy to cook and can be eaten with egg sauce, chicken, etc.

## Ingredients

- Oil
- 1 pound uncooked spaghetti
- 3 cups water
- ¾ cup sugar
- Ground cardamom to taste
- 1 pinch salt
- Dried raisins (optional)

## Special equipment

- Frying pan
- Stovetop

## Procedure

1. Heat a few tablespoons of oil in a frying pan.
2. Add the spaghetti and fry it until golden.
3. Add the water, sugar, cardamom, salt, and raisins.
4. Cover the pan and cook until the pasta has absorbed the water, stirring occasionally. If necessary, add more water.
5. Serve hot.
