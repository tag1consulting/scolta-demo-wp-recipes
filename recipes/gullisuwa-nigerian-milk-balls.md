# Gullisuwa (Nigerian Milk Balls)

**Gullisuwa** is a type of fritter commonly eaten in the northern part of Nigeria during festive periods and weddings, and as street food.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup powdered milk
- ¼ cup white granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- Vanilla powder
- Water
- Vegetable oil

## Equipment

- Bowl
- Wooden spoon
- Frying pan
- Sieve

## Procedure

1. Combine powdered milk, sugar, cornstarch, and vanilla powder in a bowl. Mix thoroughly.
2. Mix in enough water to form a dough.
3. Grease your hands with vegetable oil, and roll the dough into small balls.
4. Deep fry the dough in a pan of hot vegetable oil until browned all over.
5. Use spoon to remove the fritters from the oil and drain in a sieve.
6. Serve fresh.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Different variety of gullisuwa can be made by adding chilli pepper, cashew nuts, and coconut.
