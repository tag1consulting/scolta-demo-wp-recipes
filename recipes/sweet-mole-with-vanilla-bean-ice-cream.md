## Ingredients

- 1 whole dried ancho chile
- ¾ cups (4 ounces) piloncillo
- 1 clove
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 4 ounces semi-sweet dark chocolate bar, cut into pieces
- 2 cups high-quality vanilla bean ice cream

## Procedure

1. Wash, devein, and remove seeds from chile ancho.
2. Boil the chile, then blend with the smallest amount of water possible.
3. Strain off water.
4. In a saucepan, add pureed chile, piloncillo, clove, cinnamon and chocolate.
5. Boil for 3 minutes.
6. Remove clove.
7. To serve, allow vanilla ice cream to soften slightly in a bowl and stir in mole.
