# Mushroom Ketchup

**Mushroom ketchup** is a seasoning.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs edible mushrooms, chopped
- 3 tbsp salt
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp finely ground fresh horseradish
- 1 tbsp lemon zest
- ¼ tsp cloves
- ½ tsp allspice
- ¼ cup cider vinegar
- 1 pinch of ground cayenne

## Procedure

1. Combine the mushrooms and salt. Stir, smash well, and let rest 10 minutes.
2. Transfer mushrooms to a non-metal container, such as a ceramic crock. Cover and let sit overnight.
3. Combine the mushrooms, onion, horseradish, lemon zest, cloves, allspice, vinegar, and cayenne. Bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
4. Drape a clean cloth over a bowl. Pour the mixture over the cloth, and squeeze to extract all the liquid. The liquid is the mushroom ketchup.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- One may sprinkle leftover mushroom solids over food as a spice.
