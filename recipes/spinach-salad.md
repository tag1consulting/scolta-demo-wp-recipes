## Ingredients

### Dressing

- ¾ cup (180 ml) olive oil
- ¼ cup (60 ml) red wine vinegar
- ¼ tsp pepper
- ¼ tsp dry mustard
- ½ tsp salt (optional)
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
- 1½ tsp paprika
- 2 tsp sugar

### Salad

- 1 lb spinach, patted dry of any liquid
- 3 hard boiled eggs, chopped
- 6 strips vegetarian bacon (or bacon bits)
- 1 red onion, sliced

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients for the dressing. Mix well.
2. Combine the spinach, eggs, and sliced onion in a large bowl.
3. Pour the dressing on top of the salad, mix, and top with vegetarian bacon strips or bacon bits.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- When mixing the salad, the egg yolks will tend to crumble and disappear into the salad—this is normal.
- Add the dressing and bacon bits at the last minute.
- If you can't find Worcestershire sauce, you can use soy sauce in a pinch.
