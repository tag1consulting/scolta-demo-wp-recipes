## Ingredients

- 1 package (8 ounces) croissant roll dough
- 24 slices of pepperoni
- 4 slices of provolone cheese
- Pizza spices as desired (anise, basil, fennel, garlic, oregano, etc.)

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Unroll 2 croissant rolls and place together on ungreased cookie sheet, pointing in opposite directions, forming a rectangle by pinching sides together.
3. Lay 6 slices of pepperoni on top of each dough rectangle.
4. Lay a slice of provolone cheese on top of pepperoni slices.
5. Roll each into a tube.
6. Bake 12–15 minutes.
