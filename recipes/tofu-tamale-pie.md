## Ingredient

- 4 ounces tofu, washed, drained well, and broken up
- 1 ounce sharp cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 ounce finely-diced onion
- ½ cup finely-chopped celery
- ½ green pepper, finely chopped
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- 1 medium carrot, grated
- 1 teaspoon instant beef bouillon
- 8 ounces tomato juice
- ½ teaspoon garlic salt
- 2 teaspoons chili powder or to taste
- 1 ounce corn meal

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In non-stick skillet, add onion, celery, green pepper.
3. Add instant beef bouillon, tomato juice, and cook until vegetables are tender.
4. Add tofu, carrot, garlic salt, chili powder.
5. Simmer a few more minutes.
6. Add corn meal and mix thoroughly.
7. Pour into a one quart casserole and top with grated cheese.
8. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes or until browned.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Substitute vegetable broth in place of beef bouillon to make it vegetarian.
- May add black beans for added protein and flavor.
