## Ingredients

- Slices of bread (3 per person)
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 shakes garlic powder
- 3 shakes ground oregano leaves

## Procedure

1. Remove crusts from bread, and add other ingredients onto a microwave safe plate.
2. Make each slice of bread into ball, and lay on same plate.
3. Microwave plate for 25 seconds, wait 30 seconds, and serve.
