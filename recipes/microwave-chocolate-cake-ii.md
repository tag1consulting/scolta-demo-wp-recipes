# Microwave Chocolate Cake II

This recipe is for making an easy chocolate cake, with cheap ingredients that you're sure to have at home. Easy and quickly done in microwave.

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp (60 ml) all-purpose flour
- 4 tbsp (60 ml) white granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) whisked egg
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) milk
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp (15 ml) chocolate chips, pine nuts, or hazelnuts
- Vanilla and/or cinnamon to taste

## Procedure

1. Mix all the dry ingredients directly in a microwave-safe mug or bowl.
2. Add the egg and mix well.
3. Stir in the milk and oil.
4. Add the chocolate chips and vanilla. Stir well.
5. Microwave for 3 minutes at 1000w / 4 minutes at 700w / 3:30 minutes at 750w.
6. Cool slightly and enjoy.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- If you want a fudgy version, just omit the egg.
