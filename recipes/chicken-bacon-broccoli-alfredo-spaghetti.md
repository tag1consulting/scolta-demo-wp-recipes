# Chicken Bacon Broccoli Alfredo Spaghetti

This **chicken bacon broccoli alfredo spaghetti** recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty.

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) spaghetti
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 2 chicken breasts (400 g / 14 oz), chopped
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoons pepper
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 cups (200 g / 7 oz) broccoli florets
- 2 cups (500 ml / 1.1 pints) milk
- 1 cup (125 g / 4.5 oz) grated parmesan cheese
- ¼ cup (25 g / 1 oz) chopped parsley
- 4 strips of streaky bacon

## Procedure

1. Boil the spaghetti in water until *al dente*.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan over a medium heat.
3. Cook the chicken in oil for 4 minutes until cooked.
4. Add in salt, pepper, and garlic, and stir.
5. Stir in broccoli, then add milk and cook for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in cheese, parsley and bacon.
7. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the chicken sauce.
8. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve.
