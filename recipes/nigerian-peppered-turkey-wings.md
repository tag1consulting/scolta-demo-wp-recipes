# Nigerian Peppered Turkey Wings

**Nigerian peppered turkey wings** is a side dish that can be eaten with rice in Nigeria. It is also eaten at parties.

## Ingredients

- 6 turkey wings
- 2 small onions, peeled
- 4 cloves garlic (optional)
- ½ teaspoon ginger
- 2 teaspoon stock cube, crushed
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 medium red bell peppers
- ½ cup vegetable oil

- 1 teaspoon salt or to taste

## Equipment

- Pots
- Knife
- Bowl
- Oven

## Procedure

1. Wash the turkey wings and put in a pot with 1 onion, garlic, ginger, stock cube, and thyme. Cover with water and bring the mixture to a simmer.
2. Cook until the water dries up, then bake in the oven at 200°C.
3. Blend the bell pepper and remaining onion to get a smooth paste.
4. Heat a small amount of vegetable oil in a pot. Add the pepper paste, and simmer until thickened and losing its raw flavor. Season with salt.
5. Add the turkey wings to the sauce, and toss to coat.
