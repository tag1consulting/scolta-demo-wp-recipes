## Ingredients

- 1 medium-sized mango, peeled and fleshed removed
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1½ cups milk
- 2 drops vanilla extract (optional)
- 1 pinch of cinnamon powder
- 1 tablespoon maida (all-purpose flour)
- 2 bread slices

## Procedure

1. Blend together the mango flesh, sugar, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon powder until smooth.
2. Add a bit of maida to the batter and whisk it well.
3. Dip the bread slices in the batter and fry them in a oiled pan until brown on both sides.
4. Serve hot.
