# Palm Fruit with Syrup

**Palm fruit with syrup** is a Thai dessert. Thailand is a tropical country, so one must find desserts or foods that can help reduce the heat, such as palm fruit with syrup.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg palmyra palm fruit (*Borassus flabellifer*)
- 500 grams white sugar
- 6 cups water
- 2 pandan leaves
- Ice

## Procedure

1. Peel the palm fruit, and slice it.
2. Put the water in a pan, add the white sugar, and let it simmer over medium heat.
3. Add the palm fruit to the syrup, and heat it again.
4. Add the pandan leaves to the syrup.
5. Chill the fruit in syrup. Serve it cold with ice on top.
