## Ingredients

- ¾ cup butter, applesauce, equal parts butter and vegetable oil, or shortening
- 1 ¼ cup brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1 egg
- ⅓ cup water or milk
- 1 ½ tsp vanilla
- 3 cup raw oats (rolled or quick/old fashioned)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- 1 cup raisins
- ¾ cup walnuts, chopped

## Procedure

1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. Lightly grease cookie sheet with oil, shortening, or butter.
3. Combine your choice of fat (butter, apple sauce, butter/vegetable oil mix, or shortening), brown sugar, egg, milk, and vanilla; beat with mixer on high speed to blend well.
4. Combine oats, flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon; add to shortening mixture and combine until just blended.
5. Stir in raisins and nuts.
6. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls onto greased cookie sheet, 2 inches apart.
7. Bake for 10–13 minutes, or until lightly browned.
