# Tropical Squares

A delicious square recipe with a tropical flavor.

## Ingredients

### Base

- 1 cup (240 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon white granulated sugar
- ¼ cup (60 g) margarine, cold
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- 1 egg, beaten

### Top Layer

- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 cups (480 g) coconut
- 1 cup (240 g) white granulated sugar
- 1 tin crushed pineapple, drained
- 1 tablespoon margarine, melted
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice

## Procedure

### Base

1. Stir together flour, sugar and baking powder using a wooden spoon.
2. Cut in the margarine until the mixture looks like fine crumbs.
3. In a small bowl, beat the egg well. Mix into dry mixture using wooden spoon until combined.
4. Press into lined and lightly greased square tray.

### Top layer

1. Drain all of the juice out of the pineapple by wringing it in a cloth.
2. Add and mix remaining ingredients with the pineapple using a wooden spoon. Do not overwork it.
3. Spread evenly over base.
4. Bake at 350°F (180°C), or until topping is set and coconut is light brown.
5. Let cool before removing from pan.
