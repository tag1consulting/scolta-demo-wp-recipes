# Simple Chocolate Cheese Pie

**Chocolate cheese pie** is a quick and tasty dessert that's easy to make using simple ingredients.

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces (225 g) cream cheese
- ¼ cup (60 ml) unsweetened cocoa powder
- ¼ cup (60 g) powdered sugar
- 8 ounces (225 g) nondairy whipped topping
- 1 ready-made graham cracker crumb crust

## Procedure

1. Melt the cream cheese, and cool slightly. Transfer to a mixing bowl.
2. Mix in the cocoa powder and powdered sugar.
3. Fold in the whipped topping.
4. Pour mixture into the into ready-made crust
5. Cover and place in a refrigerator/freezer. Remove when solid.
6. Serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Fresh fruit, such as blueberries and cherries, make a nice addition.
- Melted caramel makes a nice touch when drizzled on top.
- Use fudge or melted chocolate bars in place of the cocoa.
