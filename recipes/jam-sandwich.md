# Jam Sandwich

The **jam sandwich** is, as its name suggests, a sandwich filled with jam. It can be filled with any kind of jam.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices of bread

- Some jam (any flavor)

## Procedure

1. Spread the jam onto the first slice of bread.
2. Put the second slice of bread on top of the side of bread you have put the jam on.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- If desired, the bread may be toasted before sandwich assembly.
