## Ingredients

- ¾ cup mango juice
- ¾ cup pineapple juice
- 1 banana, sliced
- 1 tbsp sugar (optional)
- 150 ml water
- Crushed ice, as required

## Procedure

1. Blend all except the crushed ice in a blender.
2. Serve with crushed ice.
