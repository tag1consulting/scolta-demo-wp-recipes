## Ingredients

- ½ cup reduced-fat sour cream
- 2 Tbsp cider vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- ¾ tsp garlic powder
- ¾ tsp dill weed
- ½ tsp salt
- 3 medium cucumber, sliced
- ½ cup sliced onion

## Procedure

1. Combine sour cream, vinegar, sugar, garlic powder, dill, and salt.
2. Add cucumbers and onion, and toss to coat.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
4. Serve with slotted spoon.
