## Ingredients

- ½ cup water
- 4 cups plain yogurt
- ⅔ cup honey
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup papaya pulp
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Procedure

1. Combine everything in a blender.
2. Blend until smooth.
