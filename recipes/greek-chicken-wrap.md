## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
- 2 ½ cups crumbled feta cheese
- ½ cup Greek Yogurt Sauce
- ½ cup fresh oregano, finely chopped
- 4 flour tortillas
- Olive oil

## Procedure

1. Combine feta, chicken, yogurt sauce, and oregano. Set aside.
2. Brush one side of each tortilla with olive oil. Add ¼ of chicken mixture and roll the longer side up.
3. Repeat until all tortillas have been rolled.
4. Sprinkle with additional chopped oregano, and serve cold as a snack.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- You could also do this with pitas for a more traditional version.
