## Ingredients

- 2.5 cups (600 g) white sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 stick (4 oz / 113 g) butter
- 9-inch (22 cm) pre-made pie crust

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Mix sugar, butter, and vinegar in saucepan. Bring to a boil.
3. Beat eggs in bowl.
4. Gradually whisk the saucepan contents into the eggs, then add vanilla.
5. Pour mixture into pie crust.
6. Bake pie in 375°F (190°C) oven for 35 minutes.
7. Let cool.
