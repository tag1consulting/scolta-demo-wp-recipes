## Ingredients

- 5 eggs
- 1 cup white granulated sugar
- 1 cup oil
- 1 cup matzah meal
- Orange extract
- 1 cup ground walnuts

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Beat eggs and add sugar, mix.
3. Mix in oil and add matzah meal.
4. Add walnuts and mix.
5. Add orange extract generously and mix.
6. Pour into 9-inch cake pan.
7. Bake for 30–40 minutes in the preheated oven.
