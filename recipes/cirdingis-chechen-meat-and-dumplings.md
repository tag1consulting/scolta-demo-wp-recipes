# Cirdingis (Chechen Meat and Dumplings)

**Cirdingis** (pronounced as "jirdingish") is a traditional Chechen meal consisting of dumplings and meat served with accompanying sauces. It may also be called "galnish".

## Ingredients

- 1 kg red meat
- ½ kg flour
- 3 cloves garlic
- Salt
- Tomato paste
- Oil
