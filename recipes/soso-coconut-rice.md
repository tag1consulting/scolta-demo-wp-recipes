# Soso (Coconut Rice)

**Soso**, also known as coconut rice, is a popular dish in various cuisines, particularly in Africa and the Caribbean. It is a flavorful rice dish that incorporates the rich and creamy taste of coconut milk.

## Ingredients

- Water
- 2 cups long-grain rice
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2–3 pandan leaves (optional)
- 1–2 tablespoons vegetable oil (optional)

## Equipment

- Large pot with lid
- Cooking spoon
- Stovetop

## Procedure

1. Rinse the rice thoroughly under cold water until the water runs clear. Drain any excess water.
2. In a large pot, combine the rinsed rice, coconut milk, water, salt, and pandan leaves (if using). Stir well to combine. If desired, you can add vegetable oil at this stage to prevent the rice from sticking together.
3. Place the pot over medium-high heat and bring the mixture to a boil, stirring occasionally.
4. Once it reaches a boil, reduce the heat to low, cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid, and simmer for about 15–20 minutes or until the rice is cooked and the liquid has been absorbed. Avoid lifting the lid while the rice is cooking to ensure even cooking.
5. After the cooking time, remove the pot from the heat but keep it covered. Let it rest for an additional 5–10 minutes to allow the steam to finish cooking the rice.
6. Fluff the rice with a fork to separate the grains and remove the pandan leaves if used.
7. Serve the coconut rice hot.
