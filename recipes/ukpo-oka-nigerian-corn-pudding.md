# Ukpo Oka (Nigerian Corn Pudding)

**Ukpo oka** is a delicacy made from corn, popular among the Ika-Ibo people of Delta State, Nigeria. It is also known as *nni oka*, *ekoki*, *sapala* and *igbankwu oka.*

## Ingredients

- 4 ears of fresh sweet corn
- 2 fresh peppers
- 1 onion
- ⅓ teaspoon ground crayfish
- ⅓ cup water
- ¼ cup palm oil
- ⅓ teaspoon salt

## Special equipment

- Blender
- Mold for steaming

## Procedure

1. Blend together the corn, pepper, onion, and crayfish with a small amount of water.
2. Transfer the mixture to a bowl, and stir in salt and palm oil to taste.
3. Pour the mixture into a mold. Place in boiling water, and steam for 45 minutes.
