## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp vegetable or peanut oil
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 200 g shrimp, peeled
- 300 g broccoli, cut into florets and rinsed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce

## Procedure

1. Heat a pan, add the oil and garlic, and fry until golden.
2. Add the shrimp and broccoli. Season with salt and oyster sauce, and add a little water. Stir together.
3. Put on a plate and serve.
