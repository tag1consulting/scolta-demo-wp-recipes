# Bubble Tea

**Bubble tea** is a tea beverage with black tapioca pearls mixed in.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (125 ml) cooked boba tapioca pearls
- 1 cup (250 ml) crushed ice
- 1 cup (250 ml) very strong chilled black tea
- 1 cup (250 ml) milk, to taste
- Honey or granulated sugar, to taste

## Procedure

1. Place pearls in a large glass.
2. Combine all remaining ingredients in a cocktail shaker. Shake vigorously until mixture is frothy.
3. Pour into glass and serve with large-diameter straws.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- If you don't want the drink to be frothy, simply stir the ingredients together instead of shaking.
