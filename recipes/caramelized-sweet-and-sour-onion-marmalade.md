## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons olive or vegetable oil
- 4 large yellow or white onions, sliced into thin strips
- ½ cups white granulated sugar
- ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon coarse ground black pepper

## Procedure

1. Heat oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add onions, and sauté, stirring, for about 15 minutes, or until they begin to brown. Do not cook over high heat, as the outside of the onions will char too quickly before the insides have softened and sweetened.
2. When onions have softened and turned a light golden brown, add sugar. Continue stirring until sugar melts and begins to bubble.
3. As the onions turn a richer brown, clear a space in the center of the pan. Pour balsamic vinegar directly onto the hot pan, then stir it into onions.
4. When vinegar has reduced, leaving a syrupy coating on the onions, turn off the heat.
5. Stir in salt and pepper, and allow onions to cool.

## Uses

- Used in Focaccia (Focaccia with Onion Marmalade, Blue Cheese, and Walnuts)
