# Scalloped Oysters

This recipe for **scalloped oysters** was adapted from the 1881 *Household Cyclopedia* and modernized.

## Ingredients

- 100 oysters, shucked
- 1 loaf bread, crumbed
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
- Salt, to taste
- Cayenne pepper, to taste
- Mace (optional)

## Procedure

1. Chop the eggs very fine, and mix with the crumbs. Season with cayenne, salt, and mace.
2. Cover the bottom of a deep pie dish with the eggs and crumbs.
3. Use a fork to place a layer of oysters over the egg mixture, along with 2–3 small pieces of butter. Continue until all ingredients are used up, reserving sufficient crumbs for a final layer.
4. Bake 45 minutes at 375–400°F. Serve hot.
