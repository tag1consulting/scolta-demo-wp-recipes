# Mascarpone Chocolate Sauce

Mascarpone cheese makes this sauce light and fluffy, while chocolate adds flavor and richness.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (300 g/11 oz) mascarpone cheese
- ½ cup (125 g/4.4 oz) chocolate syrup
- 2 tablespoons white granulated sugar
- 1–2 pinches of salt

## Procedure

1. Whisk together all ingredients in your serving bowl.
2. Serve as a dipping sauce with beignets or fruit; or, serve over ice cream.
