## Ingredients

- ½ cup oat milk
- 1 ½ cups cooked brown rice
- 4 dates, chopped
- 2 pinches of cinnamon
- ¼ cup unsalted pumpkin seeds

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients, and serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Instant brown rice will work fine.
