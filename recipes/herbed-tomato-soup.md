## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ½ cup diced onion
- 1 can (14 ounces) diced tomatoes
- 1½ cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1 can (16 ounces) tomato sauce
- 1 tablespoon fresh snipped basil (or 1 teaspoon dried)
- 1 teaspoon fresh snipped thyme (or ¼ teaspoon dried)
- 1 dash pepper
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar (optional)
- 4–6 fresh basil leaves (optional), sliced thinly into strips

## Procedure

1. In a 2-quart saucepan, sauté onion in olive oil until tender but not browned.
2. Add tomatoes, broth, tomato sauce, basil, thyme and pepper.
3. Bring to a boil.
4. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 20 minutes.
5. Stir in vinegar, if desired, just prior to serving.
6. Ladle into bowls and garnish with basil strips.
