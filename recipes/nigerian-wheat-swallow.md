# Nigerian Wheat Swallow

One variety of swallow in Nigeria is that made of wheat flour. It is served with various soups and stews, such as ẹgusi, okro, or ogbono soups.

## Ingredients

- Water
- Wheat flour

## Procedure

1. Put water in a pot, and bring it to a boil.
2. Gradually stir in the flour, mixing until the mixture is thick and lump-free.
3. Add a small amount of water, and cook for few minutes.
4. Serve with the soup of your choice.
