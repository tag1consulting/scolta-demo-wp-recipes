# Ghavoot (Iranian Nut and Seed Powder)

**Ghavoot** (قاووت) or **ghovvatoo** (قووتو) is a nutritionally-dense recipe made of powdered seeds, nuts, and sugar. There are several versions, depending on the components chosen, and their ratios. It is a traditional souvenir of Kerman, Iran.

## Ingredients

- Coffee beans
- Cinnamon
- Sesame seeds
- Cottonseeds
- Nigella seeds
- Poppy seeds
- Chickpeas
- Walnuts
- Almonds
- Pistachio nuts

## Procedure

1. Grind and roast each component.
2. Add an equal weight of sugar; grind everything together.
3. Serve as powder, or blend it with milk or hot water.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Ghavoot is a traditional energizer. You can consume it as a pre-workout snack.
