# Kid-Friendly Pasta

This easy recipe for **pasta** is designed for children to make and eat. Make sure to supervise children when cooking.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb whole-wheat spaghetti
- 2 carrots, cut into thin strips
- ½ lb green beans
- ¼ cup tomato pasta sauce

## Procedure

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil.
2. Add spaghetti and carrots to the boiling water, and cook for 4 minutes.
3. Add green beans, and cook for another 4 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, heat the tomato sauce.
5. Drain the spaghetti and the vegetables, then mix it with the heated sauce.
6. Add a pinch of salt if needed.
7. Serve.
