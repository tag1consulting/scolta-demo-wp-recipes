## Ingredients

- Rooibos teabags
- Fresh or powdered milk
- Water
- Brown sugar

## Equipment

- Cup
- Teaspoon
- Kettle

## Procedure

1. Pour the water in the kettle and let it boil.
2. Put the teabag in a cup, and pour the hot water in the cup.
3. Stir in 1 teaspoon of brown sugar, then 1 teaspoon milk.
4. Serve hot.
