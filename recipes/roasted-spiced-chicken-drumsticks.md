## Ingredients

- 1½ teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon chilli powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 6 chicken drumsticks

## Equipment

- Oven
- Paper towels
- Baking dish

## Procedure

1. Combine the paprika, onion powder, chili powder, and salt together in a bowl.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F.
3. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels.
4. Coat the chicken with oil and the prepared spice blend, ensuring all sides are well-coated. Transfer to a baking dish.
5. Bake the chicken drumsticks until the skin is brown and crispy (about 25 minutes).
6. Serve hot.
