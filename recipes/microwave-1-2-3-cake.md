# Microwave 1-2-3 Cake

**Microwave 1-2-3 cake** is a quick and easy sweet cake recipe made in the microwave. This recipe is quite forgiving in proportions: careful measurement is not required.

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 3 tablespoons bread crumbs

## Procedure

1. Beat the egg until smooth.
2. Add the honey and milk and mix well.
3. Add the breadcrumbs and mix until uniform.
4. Let the mixture sit for a few minutes, to allow the breadcrumbs to absorb the moisture.
5. Pour the mixture into a small silicone mold (e.g. Dollar store loaf pan).
6. Microwave on high for 4 minutes or a little after it stops rising.
7. Allow to cool a little; tip out onto a plate if desired.
8. Top with more maple syrup or honey, if desired.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Tip: coat the measuring spoon with oil before putting the honey in, so that it slides off easily and doesn't stick to the spoon.
