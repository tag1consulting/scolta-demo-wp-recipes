# Indian Rice

This makes an excellent side dish, and goes well with a large variety of things, from spicy eggplant to tofu to potatoes in coconut sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- ½ cup raisins
- ¼ cup cashews
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 4 cups water

## Procedure

1. Heat the olive oil in a pot.
2. Add the cumin, then the raisins and cashews. Sauté for two minutes.
3. Add the rice, and sauté for another minute.
4. Add water, and bring everything to a boil.
5. Lower the heat, cover, and cook for 12 minutes.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Basmati rice is recommended, as other types may turn out poorly. Do not use Italian risotto rice, or any sticky rice.
