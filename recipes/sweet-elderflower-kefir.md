# Sweet Elderflower Kefir

This is an absolutely delicious sweet drink.

## Ingredients

- 1 glass plain kefir
- 1 good dash of elderflower cordial
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 dash ground nutmeg

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Mix well and enjoy.
