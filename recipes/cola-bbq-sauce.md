# Cola BBQ Sauce

Great on ribs or chicken!

## Ingredients

- 1 bottle ketchup
- ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce
- 1 ½ cans good cola (Pepsi is recommended)
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup cider vinegar
- Hot sauce, to taste

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients and bring to a boil.
2. Cook over high heat for 10 minutes.
3. Store refrigerated in a nonreactive container for up to 1 week.
