# Nigerian Roasted Yam

**Roasted yam**, also known as **Ìṣu sisun** in the Yoruba language, is a typical Nigerian street food mostly eaten as a snack. It is prepared just like roasted corn and eaten with groundnuts or *boli* (roasted plantain).

## Ingredients

- Yam
- Ground pepper
- Water

## Procedure

1. Cut yam into suitably-sized pieces, then peel them.
2. Soak the yam in water overnight.
3. Rinse the yam and roast it, making sure all sides are deeply roasted.
4. Serve the roasted yam with groundnuts or roasted plantain.
