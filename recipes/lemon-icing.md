## Ingredients

- 1 egg yolk
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 pinch of salt
- 1½ cups confectioner's sugar

## Procedure

1. Break the egg yolk into a small bowl.
2. Add the lemon juice and salt; beat lightly.
3. Gradually beat in confectioner's sugar.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- If you want extra-stiff icing, add a little more powdered sugar.
