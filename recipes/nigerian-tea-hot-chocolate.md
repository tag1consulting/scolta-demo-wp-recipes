# Nigerian Tea (Hot Chocolate)

**Nigerian tea** refers to a hot chocolate beverage usually served at breakfast and with bread, meat pie, and many other snacks. Despite the name, there is no actual tea in the beverage.

## Ingredients

- Evaporated milk (preferably Peak Milk brand)
- Milo chocolate drink mix
- Hot water

## Procedure

1. Pour the evaporated milk into a mug, and stir in the chocolate drink mix.
2. Stir in hot water.
3. Serve with bread, Nigerian meat pie, or other Nigerian snacks.
