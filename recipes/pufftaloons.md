# Pufftaloons

In Australia these **pufftaloons** are usually made with a yeast-based bread dough, but this recipe uses baking powder instead.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 cup milk
- 1 dessert spoon butter

## Procedure

1. Combine flour and baking powder.
2. Rub in the butter.
3. Add enough milk to make a soft dough.
4. Deep fry in hot vegetable oil.
5. Serve hot with jam or golden syrup.
