## Ingredients

- Potatoes, peeled and sliced
- Salt
- Groundnut oil

## Equipment

- Frying pan
- Cooking spoon
- Bowl

## Procedure

1. Rinse the potatoes and season with salt.
2. Heat the oil in a pan, and add the potatoes when hot. Cook until golden all over, flipping as needed.
3. Serve hot.
