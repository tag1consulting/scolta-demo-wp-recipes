## Ingredients

- 3 cups tomato paste
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp dried basil
- 2 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 2–4 cloves of garlic, minced
