# Poultry Shake

This **poultry shake** is a blend of seasonings that can be used to flavor chicken. Everyone serious about frying chicken ought to have one of these.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup paprika
- ½ cup salt
- ⅓ cup garlic powder
- ¼ cup ground cayenne pepper
- ⅓ cup lemon pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients, then place in an airtight container with a shaker lid.
