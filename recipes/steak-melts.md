## Ingredients

- 2 ea. (12–16 ounces) boneless strip steaks, cooked to your liking
- 4 slices white bread
- Unsalted melted butter
- 4 slices pepper jack or Monterrey Jack cheese

## Procedure

1. Slice steaks thinly across the grain on the bias. Place ¼ of the strips onto each slice bread.
2. Place a slice of cheese on top of that, then press 2 slices together and then repeat with the other. Brush the sides facing out with melted butter.
3. Heat a griddle over high heat. Add sandwiches and cook until golden brown on each side. Serve immediately.
