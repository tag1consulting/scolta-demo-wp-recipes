# Eba (Cassava Flake Swallow)

**Ẹba** is a popular Nigerian swallow made from cassava flakes (garri) and is eaten with various soups, such as ẹgusi, ẹfọ riro, and ọgbọnọ soup. It is starchy and considered a heavy food.

## Ingredients

- 1 liter water
- 2 milk tins of processed cassava flakes (garri)

## Equipment

- Wooden spatula
- Pot

## Procedure

1. Boil water in a pot.
2. Gradually stir in the cassava flour, and cook until it forms a smooth dough.
3. Serve with your soup of choice.
