# Mustard Dip

This **mustard dip** can be used as a dip or spread in place of butter or plain mayonnaise.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup Dijon mustard
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon commercially-prepared horseradish

## Procedure

1. Mix all the ingredients together.
2. Store refrigerated for up to a few days.
