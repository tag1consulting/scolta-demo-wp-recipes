# Cold Potatoes Fried

*This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne.*

## Ingredients

- Meat drippings
- Boiled potatoes, cold and sliced
- Salt
- Pepper

## Procedure

1. Heat some drippings in a frying pan.
2. Add the sliced potatoes, and season with a little salt and pepper.
3. Cook the potatoes until quite hot.
4. Serve.
