# Butterbeer

**Butterbeer** is a drink from the Harry Potter book series.

## Ingredients

- 1 pint vanilla ice cream, softened
- ¼ cup butter, softened
- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon grated nutmeg
- 1 quart apple juice

## Procedure

1. Mix all ingredients thoroughly in a bowl.
2. Divide between mugs.
3. Heat as desired, and serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Add rum or brandy for an adult butterbeer.
