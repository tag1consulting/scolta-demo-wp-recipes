# Sweet Carrot Juice

**Carrot juice** is a refreshing beverage that is best enjoyed cold.

## Ingredients

- Carrots
- Sugar
- Water

## Equipment

- Grater
- Blender
- Strainer
- Knife

## Procedure

1. Chop or grate the carrots.
2. Place carrots and sugar in a blender, and add a small amount of water as needed.
3. Blend this mixture until well-puréed.
4. Strain the carrot purée to obtain a pure juice.
5. Chill and serve.
