## Ingredients

- ½ cup fresh red bell peppers, diced
- ½ cup raisins
- 1½ cups cubed tofu
- 2 cups water
- 2 cups instant couscous
- 1 tsp olive oil

## Procedure

1. Sauté tofu, red peppers, and raisins together until soft.
2. Bring the water to a boil, stir in the couscous, cover, remove from the heat, and let rest for 5 minutes until the water is absorbed. Fluff the couscous with a fork.
3. Combine the tofu mixture with couscous and olive oil. Flavor to taste with curry or other mixed spices.
