# Nigerian Palm Oil Boiled Yam (Ji Mmanu)

**Palm oil boiled yam**, also known as **ji mmanu**, is a typical Igbo food.

## Ingredient

- Yam, peeled and diced
- Fresh palm oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh chile pepper, blended to paste
- Utazi leaves, shredded
- Onion (optional)

## Procedure

1. Cook the yam pieces in salted boiling water for a few minutes.
2. Drain the yam pieces. Toss the yam with palm oil, salt, fresh pepper, and utazi leaves.
3. Serve.
