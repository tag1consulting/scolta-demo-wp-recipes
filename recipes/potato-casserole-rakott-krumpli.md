# Potato Casserole (Rakott Krumpli)

**Rakott krumpli** is a popular Hungarian potato casserole.

## Ingredients

- 6 medium potatoes
- 4 hard boiled eggs
- Salt
- 1 *kolbász* (Hungarian sausage) or 1 cup diced ham (optional)
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 350 grams sour cream around 40% fat (called *tejföl* or *smetana*)

## Procedure

1. Cook the potatoes for about 15–20 minutes.
2. Peel the potatoes and the eggs.
3. Slice the potatoes, eggs, and sausage.
4. Put a little olive oil in a baking dish. Arrange alternating layers of potatoes, eggs, and salt or sausage in the dish.
5. Drizzle olive oil over the filling, and top with sour cream.
6. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C for about 35 minutes.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Traditionally most Hungarian cooking used lard (preferably home-made) but olive oil works just as well as a substitution.
