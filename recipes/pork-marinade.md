## Ingredients

- 1 cup chicken broth
- ½ cup red wine
- ¼ cup freshly-squeezed lime juice
- ¼ cup dark honey
- 2 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp freshly-ground black pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in a large mason jar. Keep refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
