# Ginger Tea

**Ginger tea** is a drink brewed from fresh ginger. Some use it to soothe coughs and sore throats. It is also sometimes sipped to warm up the body.

## Ingredients

- Fresh ginger
- Water
- Sweetener (optional)

## Procedure

1. Cut ginger into small pieces, and rinse to remove dirt.
2. Add water to the sliced ginger and bring to a boil.
3. Allow to simmer for a few minutes, then strain the drink to remove ginger particles.
4. Add sweetener to taste, and serve.
