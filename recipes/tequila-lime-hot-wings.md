# Tequila Lime Hot Wings

Tangy with some heat.

## Ingredients

- 36 chicken wingettes
- Salt
- Black pepper
- ¾ cup tequila
- 6 tbsp freshly-squeezed lime juice
- 3 tbsp hot sauce
- Oil for deep frying

## Procedure

1. Sprinkle wingettes with salt and black pepper.
2. Heat oil to 350°F. Fry wings in batches for 12 minutes. Drain on a rack.
3. Combine remaining ingredients and boil over high heat until reduced by ⅓.
4. Toss wings with sauce mixture and serve warm.
