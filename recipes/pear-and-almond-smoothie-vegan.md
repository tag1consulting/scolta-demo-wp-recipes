# Pear and Almond Smoothie (Vegan)

This **pear and almond smoothie** is simple and vegan. It contains no added refined sugar.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups unsweetened vanilla-flavored almond milk
- 1 large, ripe D'Anjou pear
- 1 lemon wedge

## Procedure

1. Cut the pear into chunks, discarding the core. Place the chunks of pear in a blender.
2. Squeeze the juice of the lemon over the pear.
3. Add the almond milk. Blend. Serve immediately

## Notes and Variations

- If you add cinnamon and/or nutmeg to this, it can act as a vegan substitute for eggnog—however, it is not as thick as traditional eggnog.
