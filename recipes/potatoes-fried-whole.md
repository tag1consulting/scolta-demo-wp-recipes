## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Flour (optional)
- Egg yolk (optional), beaten
- Fine breadcrumbs (optional)
- Beef drippings

## Procedure

1. Boil the potatoes until almost done. Drain.
2. If desired, dip the potatoes in flour, then egg yolks, then breadcrumbs.
3. Heat the drippings in a pan. Add the potatoes, and fry until brown and crisp, shaking regularly to prevent burning.
4. Remove from pan, and serve hot.
