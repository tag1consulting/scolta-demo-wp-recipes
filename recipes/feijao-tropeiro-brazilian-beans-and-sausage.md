# Feijão Tropeiro (Brazilian Beans and Sausage)

**Feijao tropeiro** is a traditional side dish in many parts of Brazil.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs pinto beans
- 2 lbs sausage of your choice (mild Italian is good in this dish)
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 small yellow onions
- ½ pound slab bacon
- 4 hard boiled eggs
- 1 bunch green onions, chopped
- 1 bunch collard greens
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Procedure

1. Prepare beans according to their package. Drain and set aside to cool.
2. In large pan, cook bacon and sausage. Remove sausage and bacon.
3. Slice sausage into 1 inch-thick pieces.
4. In the pan with bacon grease, sauté white onion and garlic.
5. Slice collard green into small (⅛-inch) strips.
6. Mix all ingredients except hard-boiled eggs in large bowl using a wooden spoon.
7. Drizzle with olive oil and stir gently.
8. Garnish with sliced boiled eggs.
9. Serve hot or cold.
