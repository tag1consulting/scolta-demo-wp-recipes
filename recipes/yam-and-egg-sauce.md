# Yam and Egg Sauce

**Yam and egg sauce** is a dish eaten mostly as breakfast

## Ingredients

- Yam
- Tomatoes
- Peppers
- Egg
- Salt
- Groundnut oil

## Equipment

- Frying pan
- Pot
- Cooking spoon
- Bowl
- Knife
- Blender

## Procedure

1. Peel and slice the yam. Boil yam in water until soft, then drain.
2. Blend the tomatoes and peppers together. Whisk together with the egg and a pinch of salt.
3. Cook the egg mixture in a hot pan with a little oil.
4. Serve the eggs with the yam.
