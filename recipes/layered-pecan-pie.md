## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1 package (8 ounces) cream cheese
- ⅓ cup white granulated sugar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 ea. 9-inch unbaked pie shell, or single homemade pie crust
- 1¼ cups chopped pecans

### Topping

- 3 eggs
- 1 cup light corn syrup
- ¼ cup white granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Combine the cream cheese, sugar, and salt; beat until smooth.
3. Add egg and vanilla, beating well after each.
4. Spoon mixture into unbaked pie shell. Sprinkle with pecans.
5. Combine 3 eggs, corn syrup, remaining sugar, and vanilla; beat well.
6. Pour this mixture over pecans.
7. Bake for 35–40 minutes or until set.
