# Rice and Peas (Risi e Bisi)

**Risi e bisi** (or **rixi e bixi**) is a Northern Italian dish, a signature meal of the city of Venezia (Venice) in particular, in the Veneto region. Its name literally translates to "rice and peas".

## Ingredients

- Rice (*Vialone Nano* is prefered)
- Peas in their pods

## Procedure

1. Cook the rice per the cooking instructions on the packet.
2. Boil the peas with their pods. Mash them.
3. Add the mashed peas to the rice.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Bacon can be added to make it even more delicious.
