## Ingredients

- 3 ripe avocados
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 cup of salsa fresca

## Procedure

1. Peel and dice avocados.
2. Sprinkle the avocados with lime juice.
3. Add salsa fresca, and toss to mix.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- If you don't plan on serving immediately, place an avocado pit in the bowl to prevent browning, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and store in refrigerator.
