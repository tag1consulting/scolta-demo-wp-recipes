# Common Sauce (Hartshorne)

**Common Sauce** is a multi-purpose sauce that can be used for a number of meat-based dishes and side vegetables. This recipe is an interpreted method from the original recipe in Henry Hartshorne's the Household Cyclopedia.

## Ingredients

- Veal slices
- Ham slices
- 2 onions
- 2 parsnips
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 2 cloves
- 500 ml broth
- 175 ml white wine
- 2 slices of lemon

## Procedure

1. Combine veal, ham, onions, parsnips, garlic, and cloves in a large saucepan.
2. Add broth, wine, and lemon slices.
3. Simmer over low heat for 15 to 20 minutes, or until reduced.
4. Sieve the sauce and serve.
