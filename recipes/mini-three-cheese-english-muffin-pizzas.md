## Ingredients

- 6 large English muffins, split and buttered
- Grated Parmesan cheese
- 1½ cups (6 ounces) shredded cheddar cheese
- 6 frankfurters
- 1 can (10.5 ounces) pizza sauce
- 1 cup (4 ounces) shredded mozzarella cheese

## Equipment

- Oven
- Broiler

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Sprinkle buttered muffins with Parmesan cheese.
3. Toast under broiler until golden brown.
4. Place 1½ tablespoons of shredded cheddar cheese on each muffin.
5. Cut frankfurters crosswise in ¼-inch slices; arrange on muffins.
6. Top each with about 2 tablespoons of pizza sauce, and sprinkle with 1 tablespoon of mozzarella cheese.
7. Bake 12–15 minutes or until thoroughly heated.
