# Honey BBQ Hot Wings

Like BBQ and like hot wings? This combines both that in one dish!

## Ingredients

- 24 chicken wingettes
- Salt
- Black pepper
- ½ cup tomato paste
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 2 Tbsp cider vinegar
- 2 Tbsp hot sauce
- Oil for deep frying

## Procedure

1. Sprinkle wings with salt and black pepper.
2. Heat oil to 350°F (175°C).
3. Deep fry wings in batches for 11–12 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack.
4. Combine liquid ingredients except for oil.
5. Bring to a boil over medium high heat until reduced to 10 Tbsp.
6. Remove from heat and let rest for 5 minutes.
7. Toss wings with sauce; serve warm.
