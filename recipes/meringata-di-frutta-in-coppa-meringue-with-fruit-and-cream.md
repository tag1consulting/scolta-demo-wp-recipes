# Meringata di Frutta in Coppa (Meringue with Fruit and Cream)

A quick and easy *meringata* is made as follows:

## Ingredients

- Vanilla pastry cream, home-made or store-bought, as needed
- Any fruit of choice or a mixture, as needed
- Crème chantilly, as needed
- Crumbled meringue cookies, as needed
- Fresh mint or basil
- Raspberry coulis or other topping of choice

## Procedure

1. Spoon a few tablespoons of vanilla cream into the bottom of a martini or parfait glass.
2. Cover the custard with your fruit of choice, leaving enough room for the topping.
3. Cover the fruit with *crème chantilly*, and sprinkle crumbled meringue chips on top.
4. Serve with a sprig of mint or basil and drizzle with raspberry coulis.
