## Ingredients

- 8 ounces cubed processed cheese
- 1 can (4 ounces) mushrooms
- 1 can (15 ounces) sweet peas
- 3 cups cooked brown rice
- cooking spray
- ½ cup slivered almonds

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine cheese, mushrooms, peas, and rice in large bowl.
3. Coat 1½ quart baking pan with cooking spray.
4. Move mixed ingredients to baking dish.
5. Cover and bake for 20 to 25 minutes.
6. Sprinkle almonds on top
7. Bake 5 minutes more.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Instant brown rice will work fine.
- Mushroom "stems and pieces" are okay.
