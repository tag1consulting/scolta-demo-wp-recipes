## Ingredients

- 1½ cups ricotta or cottage cheese
- ¾ cup freshly-grated Parmigiano Reggiano
- 2 tsp finely-chopped onion
- 1 egg
- 1½ tsp salt

## Procedure

1. Stir all the ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Set aside while you roll the dough.
