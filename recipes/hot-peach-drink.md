## Ingredients

- 1 bottle (40 ounces) pure peach drink
- ¼ cup packed brown sugar
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- ½ cup peach schnapps (optional)
- Cinnamon sticks for garnish (optional)

## Procedure

1. Combine juice, brown sugar, cinnamon sticks, and margarine in a Dutch Oven.
2. Heat to boil.
3. Remove from heat.
4. Discard cinnamon sticks.
5. Add schnapps, if desired.
6. Garnish with additional cinnamon sticks, if desired.
