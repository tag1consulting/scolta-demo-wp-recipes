## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 hamburger patty
- ½–1 cup uncooked rice (per preference)
- Gravy

## Procedure

1. Cook the eggs sunny side up.
2. Cook the hamburger to your preference.
3. Steam the rice, and heat up the gravy.
4. Assemble the components in the following order:
   - Rice on bottom;
   - Hamburger on top of rice;
   - Eggs;
   - Gravy.
