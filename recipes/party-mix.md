# Party Mix

**Party mix** is a great recipe for when you have company over. It stores great in a sealed container, and if you're not too picky you can keep it out overnight. Adjust seasonings to your preference.

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons butter or margarine
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1½ teaspoons seasoned salt
- ¾ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- 3 cups (360g) corn cereal squares
- 3 cups (360g) rice cereal squares
- 3 cups (360g) wheat cereal squares
- 1 cup (100g) mixed nuts
- 1 cup (120g) bite-size pretzels
- 1 cup (120g) garlic-flavor bite-size bagel chips or regular-size bagel chips, broken into 1-inch pieces

## Procedure

### Oven method

1. Heat oven to 250°F (120°C).
2. In an ungreased large roasting pan, melt butter in oven.
3. Stir in seasonings.
4. Gradually stir in remaining ingredients until evenly coated.
5. Bake for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes.
6. Spread on paper towels to cool, about 15 minutes.
7. Store in airtight container.

### Microwave method

1. In large microwavable bowl, microwave butter uncovered on high about 40 seconds or until melted.
2. Stir in seasonings.
3. Gradually stir in remaining ingredients until evenly coated.
4. Microwave uncovered on high 5–6 minutes, thoroughly stirring every 2 minutes.
5. Spread on paper towels to cool.
6. Store in airtight container.
