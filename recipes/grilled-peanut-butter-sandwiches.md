# Grilled Peanut Butter Sandwiches

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Recipes | US Cuisine | Sandwiches

Here, "grilling" means frying, as it does for the Grilled Cheese Sandwich. Elvis Presley was famous for consuming these with sliced bananas and "burnt" bacon.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons peanut butter
- 2 slices white bread
- Butter or margarine

## Procedure

1. Spread peanut butter between bread slices.
2. Spread outsides of bread with softened or melted butter or margarine.
3. Cook in a non-stick skillet on medium heat, turning a few times, until bread is golden brown and peanut butter is melted.
4. Serve with plenty of milk.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Make a standard peanut butter and jelly sandwich and cook it in the same manner; these are very good.
- Make a standard peanut butter and marshmallow fluff sandwich and cook it in the same manner. These are very good, but can be a bit messy.
- Substitute almond or cashew butter for peanut butter.
- Other kinds of bread can be substituted for the white bread.
