## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (75 g/2.6 oz) salt
- 1 tbsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp ground paprika
- 1 tsp dry mustard
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dehydrated garlic (minced or powdered)
- 1 dehydrated onion (minced or powdered)
- 1 tsp celery seed
- 1 tsp parsley
- ½ tsp each of however many of the following you have on hand:
  - Thyme
  - Powdered turmeric
  - Cumin powder
  - Marjoram
  - Ground red cayenne pepper
  - Crushed or ground rosemary
- ¼ tsp each of however many of the following you have on hand:
  - Ground white pepper
  - Chili powder
  - Curry powder
  - Powdered coriander
  - Dill weed
  - Crushed or ground caraway
  - Asoefetida
  - Sugar
  - Powdered soup bouillon

## Procedure

1. Mix all ingredients and store in an airtight jar.
