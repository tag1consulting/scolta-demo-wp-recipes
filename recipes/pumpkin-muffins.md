## Ingredients

- 2 cups sifted all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup white granulated sugar
- 3 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 egg
- ½ cup milk
- 1 cup cooked and mashed pumpkin (canned pumpkin can be used as well)
- ¼ cup vegetable oil

## Procedure

1. Sift flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon into a bowl.
2. Beat egg; mix in milk, pumpkin, and oil.
3. Make a well in center of flour mixture; pour in pumpkin mixture all at once.
4. Stir quickly until flour is just moistened.
5. Fill greased muffin cups ⅔ full.
6. Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
7. Run spatula around each muffin to loosen; serve immediately.
