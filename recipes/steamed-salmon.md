## Ingredients

- Salmon
- 250 ml boiling water
- 2 tsp salt

## Procedure

1. Put your salmon in a pot.
2. Add the boiling water and sprinkle with salt.
3. Cover the pot, and steam over low heat until the flesh is just cooked.
