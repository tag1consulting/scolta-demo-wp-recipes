## Ingredients

- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 Tablespoons cider vinegar
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- 1 lb elbow macaroni pasta, cooked, drained, and chilled
- ⅓ cup chopped red onion
- ⅓ cup chopped celery

## Procedure

1. Whisk together mayonnaise, mustard, sugar, and vinegar in small bowl. Add salt and pepper to taste.
2. Toss the pasta, onion, and celery in a large bowl. Pour the dressing over top, and toss to combine.
3. Cool before serving.
