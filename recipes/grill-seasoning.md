## Ingredients

### Variation for chops

- 1 cup dark brown sugar
- ¼ cup salt
- ½ cup coarsely-ground black pepper
- 3 ⅔ tbsp red pepper flakes
- ½ cup paprika
- 2 tbsp cayenne pepper
- ¼ cup dried rosemary
- ¼ cup garlic powder
- ½ cup dried apples, finely minced in a food processor

### Variation for chicken

- ½ cup paprika
- ¼ cup smoked paprika
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup salt
- ¼ cup coarsely-ground black pepper
- ¼ cup lemon pepper
- ¼ cup cayenne pepper

### Extra spicy variation

- ½ cup cayenne pepper
- ¼ cup salt
- 1 cup dark brown sugar
- ½ cup smoked paprika

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in an airtight container with a shaker lid.
2. Use as desired or as directed in a given recipe.
