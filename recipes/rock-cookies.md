## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ¾ cup butter
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- ¼ pound dried currants
- ¼ tsp mace
- Zest of half a lemon
- 3 eggs, well beaten
- Candied cherries

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Sift flour, salt, and baking powder; rub in butter. Add sugar, currants, mace, and lemon zest. Stir in eggs.
3. Form into rough balls about 1 inch in diameter. If balls aren't holding together well, sprinkle with water. Garnish with ⅛ of a candied cherry.
4. Bake for 9 minutes at 350°F. Cool.
