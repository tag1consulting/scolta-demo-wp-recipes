# Tomato Basil Sausage Pasta

This **tomato basil sausage pasta** recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty.

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) uncooked spaghetti
- 450 g (1 lb) sausage meat
- ½ onion, chopped
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon pepper
- 2 cups (500 g / 1.1 lb) marinara (tomato) sauce
- 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 oz) milk
- ½ cup (50 g / 2 oz) fresh basil leaves, chopped

## Procedure

1. Boil the spaghetti in water until *al dente*.
2. Meanwhile, cook the sausage meat in a pan over a medium heat for 6 minutes until well browned.
3. Add the onion, salt, and pepper and cook for a further 2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in the marinara sauce, milk, and basil, and cook for 2 minutes.
5. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the sauce.
6. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve.
