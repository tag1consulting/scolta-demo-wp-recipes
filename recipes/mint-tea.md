# Mint Tea

There are two ways to make this mint infusion. You can either use dried up mint leaves or fresh mint leaves.

## Ingredients

- Dried or fresh mint leaves
- Water

## Equipment

- Kettle
- Teapot

## Procedure

1. Boil the water in a kettle.
2. Pour the boiling water into the teapot.
3. Put the mint leaves in the teapot. Let steep for several minutes.
4. Pour some hot tea in a cup and dilute with additional water. Even if it starts to look transparent, the taste is still there.
