# Kunun Tamba (Finger Millet Porridge)

**Kunun tamba** is a porridge made from finger millet. It is common in the northern part of Nigeria.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup tamba (finger millet)
- Ginger
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons sugar

## Equipment

- Sieve
- Bowl
- Pot

## Procedure

1. Wash the tamba and dry with a clean cloth.
2. Mix tamba with ginger. Grind the mixture until smooth.
3. Allow the ground mixture to cool down, then sieve the chaff to give a fine texture.
4. Boil water in a pot and add the tamba powder gradually, stirring gently to make a smooth mixture.
5. Add milk and sugar, then whisk until smooth and lump-free.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Finger millet porridge is usually prepared for babies as a weaning food. It is also eaten as breakfast in some regions.
- Dilute with water to get a thinner porridge
