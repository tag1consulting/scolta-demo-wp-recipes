# Burple Nurples

This recipe is loosely derived from the animated television show *Chowder*. It is unclear whether it is intended for consumption.

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 2 sticks butter, melted
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 pinch ground coriander
- 1 cup flour

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 320°F.
2. In a bowl, gently crack egg.
3. Mix in sugar, then butter, flour, and coriander to make a dough.
4. Shape dough into small balls.
5. Place dough balls on a cookie sheet.
6. Bake for 1 hour.
