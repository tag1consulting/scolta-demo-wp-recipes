# Chipotle BBQ Rub

This rub is great on pork, steak, fish, and chicken.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup paprika
- ¼ cup salt
- ½ cup chipotle powder
- 1 tbsp cayenne pepper
- ½ cup dark brown sugar
- ¼ cup dried rosemary
- ¼ cup dried thyme
- ½ cup dehydrated lemon peel

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients and keep in airtight container in cool, dark place.
