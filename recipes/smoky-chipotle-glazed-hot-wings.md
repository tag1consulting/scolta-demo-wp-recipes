## Ingredients

- 24 chicken wingettes
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 6 tbsp Smoky Chipotle Hot Sauce
- 6 tbsp butter, melted
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Oil for deep frying

## Procedure

1. Heat oil to 350°F. Fry wings in batches for 12 minutes each batch. Drain on a rack.
2. Combine remaining ingredients, add wings, and toss to coat. Serve.
