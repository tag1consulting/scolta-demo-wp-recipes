# Spiced Oysters

This recipe for **spiced oysters** has been adapted and modernized from the 1881 *Household Cyclopedia.*

## Ingredients

- 100 oysters
- 12 whole cloves
- 24 whole allspice
- Mace, to taste
- Cayenne pepper, to taste
- Salt to taste
- 1 cup cider vinegar

## Procedure

1. Strain the oyster liquor through a sieve, and put it in a saucepan.
2. Add the oysters, spices, salt, and cider vinegar.
3. Place them over a slow fire, and as soon as they boil remove them from the heat.
4. Pour the oysters into a large bowl, and let cool.
5. When cooled, cover and store the oysters.
