# Hogao (Colombian Scallion Tomato Sauce)

**Hogao** is a criollo sauce used in many Colombian dishes.

## Ingredients

- 2 large red tomatoes, finely diced
- 2 scallions, finely chopped
- ¼ cup oil
- Allspice
- Chicken bouillon cube
- Salt
- Pepper

## Procedure:

1. Heat oil in a medium frying pan.
2. Add scallions, and fry 5 minutes over medium heat.
3. Add tomato, allspice, bouillon, salt, and pepper. Let cook over low heat for 10 minutes.
