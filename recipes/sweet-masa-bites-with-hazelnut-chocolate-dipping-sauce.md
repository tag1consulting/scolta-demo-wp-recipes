## Ingredients

### Hazelnut chocolate dipping sauce

- ¼ cup hazelnut-flavored syrup
- ½ cup chocolate syrup
- 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract
- ¼ cup whipping cream, whipped to soft peaks

### Masa bites

- ½ cup whole milk
- 3 egg yolks
- 20 corn tortillas, torn into small pieces
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup unsalted butter
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 quarts vegetable oil, for frying

## Procedure

### Hazelnut chocolate dipping sauce

1. Combine syrups and vanilla.
2. Gently fold in whipped cream.
3. Refrigerate until needed.

### Masa bites

1. Place milk, eggs, salt, and sugar with the tortillas in a food processor, and pulse until chunky paste is achieved.
2. Move to a mixing bowl and stir in the butter. Work fast to prevent melting.
3. Pour oil into a 6-quart Dutch oven and heat to 375°F.
4. Roll tortilla mixture into 1 to 1½-inch balls. Fry, 6–8 at a time, until crisp and golden brown, about 2–3 minutes.
5. Drain on a cooling rack set above a sheet pan and let cool 1–2 minutes.
6. Serve warm with the hazelnut chocolate dipping sauce.
