# Mashed Potatoes

*This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne.*

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Butter
- Milk

## Procedure

1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then drain.
2. While hot, rub the potatoes through a colander into a clean pan.
3. For every pound of potatoes, mix in ½ ounce butter and 1 tablespoon milk; do not make them too moist.
4. Serve hot.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- **Onion mashed potatoes:** Prepare some boiled onions, rub them through a sieve, and mix them with potatoes.
