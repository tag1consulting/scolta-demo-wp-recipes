## Ingredients

### Topping

- 300 g prawn, fried with 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 8 crabsticks, cut into 6 parts and fried with 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 5 fishballs, cut in circles and fried with 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp parsley
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp black pepper

### Assembly

- 3 baguettes, cut into 1.5 cm slices

## Procedure

1. In a bowl, combine prawn, crabstick, fishball, parsley, sugar, salt and black pepper. Stir with spoon until well-mixed.
2. Top each slice of baguette with some of the the topping mixture.
3. Bake the bruschetta at 190°C for 15 minutes.
4. Serve hot.
