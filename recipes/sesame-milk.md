## Ingredients

- ½ cup tahini
- 1 tsp maple syrup or other vegan sweetener
- 1 tsp salt or soy sauce
- 2 cups water

## Procedure

1. Roughly combine ingredients.
2. Process with a blender until smooth.
3. Chill for several hours.
4. Stir before serving.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- This will keep in the refrigerator 4–5 days
