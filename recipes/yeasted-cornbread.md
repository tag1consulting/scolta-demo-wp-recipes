## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water
- 1 tablespoon active dry yeast
- 4 tablespoons white granulated sugar
- 1 cup yellow cornmeal
- ½ cup white flour
- ½ cup whole wheat flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Additional water, as necessary

## Procedure

1. Mix water, yeast, and sugar. Let this sit for a few minutes until it bubbles.
2. Stir in the rest of the ingredients, adding water until it is pourable.
3. Bake in a greased 9×9-inch pan at 350°F (175°C, gas mark 4) for 25 minutes or until the edges turn golden brown.
