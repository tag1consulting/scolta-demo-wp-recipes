## Ingredients

- Curd (thick plain "natural" yogurt)
- Sugar
- Cold water

## Procedure

1. Whip the curd in a bowl until loosened.
2. Mix in a quarter as much water as curd.
3. Sweeten with sugar to taste.
4. Serve cold.
