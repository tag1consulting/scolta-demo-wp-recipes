## Ingredients

- 1 pound chicken, cooked and diced
- 1 spoonful mayonnaise
- 1 handful freshly-cut lettuce
- Cooked bacon, diced
- 1 teaspoon dressing
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic

## Procedure

1. Toss together all ingredients until well-combined.
2. Serve cool or at room temperature.
