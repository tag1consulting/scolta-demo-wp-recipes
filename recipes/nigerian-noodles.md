# Nigerian Noodles

Nigeria has a variety of packaged noodle brands and numerous ways to prepare them. Brands include Indomie, Golden penny, Minimie, Mimee, Tummy Tummy, etc.

## Ingredients

### Base

- Noodles
- Seasoning cubes
- Pepper
- Water
- Vegetable

### Optional additions

- Egg
- Roast chicken
- Sauce
- Beef
- Onion bulb
- Green bell peppers
- Tomatoes
- Oil

## Equipment

- Pot
- Stove
- Knife (optional)
- Spoon
- Colander or strainer

## Procedure

### Method I

1. Toast the noodles in a dry pan.
2. Add some boiling water, along with seasoning and pepper.
3. Cook, stirring, until the water is fully absorbed and the noodles are tender.
4. Serve with the optional additions.
5. Use a timer to avoid overcooking
6. Don't overcrowd the pot

### Method II

1. Bring water to a boil in a pot with a dash of oil.
2. Add the noodles, and cook until the noodles are tender.
3. Drain the noodles, and serve with the optional additions.
