# Mexican Seasoning

**Mexican Seasoning** is sometimes just chili powder, though many variations on this theme exist. Make your own mixture ahead of time and store in a small shaker for easy use.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup chili powder
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1–2 teaspoons cumin (seed or powder)
- 1 teaspoon or more garlic
- 1 teaspoon or more of the following:
  - Mexican oregano
  - Basil
  - Coriander
  - Onion (powder or granulated)
  - Cinnamon
  - Crushed red pepper
  - Black pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Store in a cool, dry place.
