# Baked Potatoes

Potatoes are perfect for camping—they don't take up space in the cooler, and cooked in foil they are easy to prepare.

## Ingredients

- 1 large baking potato
- Cooking oil
- Salt
- Butter or sour cream (optional)

## Procedure

### Cleaning and Preparation

You must prepare your potatoes before using a method below, or the potatoes may cook incorrectly or explode itself.

1. Scrub potato on all sides to clean the surface.
2. Pierce skin of potato with a fork or small-tipped object to create holes for inside pressure.

### Camping variation

1. Clean and prepare potatoes as above.
2. Cook over medium-hot coals for 45 minutes or until soft.
3. Serve with salt and butter or sour cream.

### Microwave variation

1. Clean and prepare potatoes as above, but wrap them in plastic wrap, not foil. Be sure to poke the potato with a fork to make sure it is cooked evenly and does not explode in the microwave due to pressure.
2. Place a piece of paper towel in the microwave, and microwave potatoes on high for 8 minutes. Potatoes will be extremely hot when finished.
3. Cut potato into four and serve with toppings of your choice.

### Oven variation

1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F).
2. Clean and prepare potatoes as above.
3. Place the potatoes in the oven.
4. Cook for 1 hour.
5. Remove when cooked. Puncture potato and separate skin from flesh.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Other possible toppings include spicy beans, avocado, salsa, bacon bits, mushrooms grated cheese... or whatever is left in the fridge!

## Warnings

- When cooking is initially completed, handle the hot potato with care.
