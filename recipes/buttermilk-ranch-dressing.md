# Buttermilk Ranch Dressing

The all-American dressing, great for salads, sandwiches or as a dip with crudités. Tangy but not too sweet.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- ¾ cup sour cream
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Procedure

1. Mix ingredients until smooth.
