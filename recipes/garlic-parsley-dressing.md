## Ingredients

- ¼ cup vinegar
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Parsley

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients and pour the dressing on boiled broccoli or cauliflower salad.
