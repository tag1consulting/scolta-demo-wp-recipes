# Traditional Tuna Casserole

**Traditional tuna casserole** is a simple, quick-to-make meal, mentioned by Garrison Keillor on *A Prairie Home Companion.*

## Ingredients

- 1–2 cans (12 ounces / 300 g) tuna
- 1 cup (240 g) uncooked macaroni, or other short pasta
- 1 can of peas
- 1 can of mushrooms
- 2 tablespoons minced onion
- ⅛ cup (30 ml) milk
- 1 can (10.75 ounces / 305 g) cream of mushroom or cheddar cheese soup
- Several slices of cheese

## Procedure

### Variation I – Oven

1. Preheat oven to 350–400°F (180–200°C).
2. Mix all ingredients, except cheese slices, in a large casserole dish.
3. Bake for 1 hour, stirring 2 or 3 times.
4. When macaroni is tender, cover top with cheese slices, and return to oven for 5 minutes to melt.

### Variation II – Skillet

1. Assemble all ingredients, except sliced cheese, in large skillet with 1–1½ cups (240–360 ml) hot water.
2. Bring to boil, and simmer 15 minutes, frequently stirring.
3. Melt cheese on top before serving.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- This tastes **great** with a teaspoon or two of *Texas Pete* hot sauce.
- You may substitute chopped chicken or turkey for the tuna.
