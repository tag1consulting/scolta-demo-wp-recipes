## Ingredients

- 1 small can frozen orange juice
- 1 cup skim milk
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ cup sugar
- 14 ice cubes, crushed

## Procedure

1. Place all ingredients in blender, and blend until smooth.
2. Serve and enjoy.
