## Ingredients

### Salad

- 300 g bean sprouts, washed in hot water
- 6 sheets lettuce, washed and cut into 6 pieces
- 1 big onion, cut into rings
- 3 tomato, diced into 1 cm pieces
- 250 g cabbage, diced
- 6 cucumber, peeled and sliced 1 cm thick
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, each cut into 4 pieces

### Dressing

- 8 tbsp mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp mustard
- 3½ tbsp tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp black pepper

## Procedure

1. Mix all the vegetables ingredients on a bowl, and allow to rest for until 5 minutes.
2. Mix all the sauce ingredients with spoon.
3. Place the sauce, reserving 2 tbsp, into the vegetable mixture, and then mix again.
4. Top with egg quarters, then top eggs with reserved sauce.
