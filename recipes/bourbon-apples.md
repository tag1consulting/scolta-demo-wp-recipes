## Ingredients

- 1 Honeycrisp apple, cored, peeled, and sliced into 12ths
- ¾ cup bourbon
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice
- ¼ cup white granulated sugar
- ½ tsp freshly ground allspice
- 2 cloves, freshly ground
- ½ tsp freshly ground cinnamon

## Procedure

1. Coat apples with butter and lemon.
2. Combine sugar, allspice, cloves, and cinnamon. Sprinkle over apples and toss to coat.
3. Preheat a small stainless steel skillet over medium high heat. Add apples and sauté until lightly browned.
4. Add bourbon to pan and deglaze. Bring to a boil, and cook, stirring occasionally, until apples are fork tender. Serve immediately.
