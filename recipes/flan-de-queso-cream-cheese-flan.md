# Flan de Queso (Cream Cheese Flan)

This recipe for flan de queso was originally contributed by Brenda Ortiz.

## Ingredients

### Flan

- 8 oz cream cheese
- 14 oz *leche condensada* (sweetened condensed milk)
- 12 oz evaporated milk
- 4 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Caramel

- 1 cup of white granulated sugar

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
2. Add cream cheese, *leche condensada*, evaporated milk, eggs, and vanilla extract into a mixing bowl. Mix ingredients thoroughly at medium speed until smooth.
3. Place the sugar in the bottom of a small circular baking pan. Add the flan mixture on top without mixing the two layers.
4. Fill a large rimmed baking sheet with about 1.5 inch of water. This is called *baño de Maria* (bain-marie).
5. Carefully place baking sheet with flan mixture in the *baño de Maria*.
6. Bake flan for 45–50 minutes in the preheated oven. The flan is ready when you insert a toothpick in the center and it comes out clean.
7. Let flan cool until safe to handle. Tip the flan pan upside-down onto a serving plate, and refrigerate for 5 hours.
8. Serve with fresh mint leaves for garnish.
