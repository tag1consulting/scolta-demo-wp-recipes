## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 4 teaspoons coarsely-ground coriander seeds
- 2 tablespoons coarse salt (kosher or sea)
- 4 teaspoons dill weed
- 4 teaspoons paprika
- 4 teaspoons crushed red pepper flakes (or 3 teaspoons cayenne pepper)
- 4 teaspoons fresh coarsely-ground black pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine the ingredients, then use them to season steak. Can be stored in an airtight container for a number of months.
