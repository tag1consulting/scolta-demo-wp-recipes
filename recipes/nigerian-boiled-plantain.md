# Nigerian Boiled Plantain

**Boiled plantain** is a typical Nigerian food served with egg sauce, palm oil, or garden egg stew. The plantain can be unripe or ripe.

## Ingredients

- Unripe or ripe plantain
- Water

## Procedure

1. Boil some water in a pot, and add the plantain.
2. Cook for few minutes.
3. Serve with egg sauce, garden egg stew, or palm oil.
