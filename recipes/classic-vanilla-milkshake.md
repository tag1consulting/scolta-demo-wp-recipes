# Classic Vanilla Milkshake

**Milkshakes** are beverages made by blending cream and milk with other ingredients added for color and flavor.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (480 ml) vanilla ice cream
- 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk
- ¼ cup (60 ml) half & half
- 2 ½ tablespoons (35 g) sugar
- ⅛ teaspoon vanilla extract

## Procedure

1. Pour the milk, ice cream, and half & half in a blender and partially blend.
2. Add the sugar and flavoring, and blend until thoroughly mixed.
