# Tuwo Alkama (Wheat Swallow)

**Tuwo alkama** is a swallow made from wheat flour and water. It is commonly eaten in the northern part of Nigeria

## Ingredients

- Whole wheat flour

- Water

## Equipment

- Saucepan
- Sieve
- Turning stick

## Procedure

1. Sift the flour to remove any debris.
2. Bring a saucepan of water to a boil. Gradually and continuously stir in the flour until it forms a thick mixture.
3. Cook for 5 minutes, then serve.
