# Chew Bread

**Chew bread** is a type of bar cookie, typically containing nuts.

## Ingredients

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Combine all ingredients except nuts, and mix well with an electric mixer.
3. Add nuts, and stir in well.
4. Pour into greased and floured 9x13-inch (23x33 cm) cake pan.
5. Bake in preheated oven for 35–40 minutes.
