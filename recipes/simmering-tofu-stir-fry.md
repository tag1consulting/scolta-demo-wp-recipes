## Ingredients

- 1 block firm tofu
- Mixture of preferred vegetables (suggested combination: onion, green pepper, tomato)
- ½ ea. apple
- Soy sauce
- Olive oil
- Preferred seasonings (suggested seasonings: pepper, hot sauce, salt)

## Procedure

1. Slice tofu into manageable slices (no more than ¼ inch thick).
2. Slice all vegetables and apple.
3. Simmer tofu and vegetable in a light mixture of olive oil and soy sauce on medium high heat.
4. Cook until tofu is golden brown and vegetables have thoroughly soaked up juices.
5. Serve with a side of rice or couscous and season.
