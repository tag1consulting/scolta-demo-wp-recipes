# Silog

**Silog** is a heavy, easy-to-make breakfast on every Filipino's table.

## Ingredients

- 1 serving of fried rice
- 1 fried chicken egg (sunny side-up or beaten)
- Add-ons of your choice, such as:
  - 2 hot dogs
  - 1 can of corned beef
  - 1 longganisa sausage
  - Sliced cucumber and tomato

## Procedure

1. Place the fried rice and the egg on your plate.
2. Arrange your desired add-ons around the rice.
3. Serve with any desired condiments, such as spicy vinegar.
