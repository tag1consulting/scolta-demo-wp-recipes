## Ingredients

- ½ cup chopped Castilla walnuts
- ½ cup chopped Indian walnuts
- ½ cup of chopped almonds
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup wheat germ
- ¾ cup olive oil
- 1½ cup honey
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 4 cups rolled oats
- ½ cup raisins
- ½ cup blueberries

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients except the raisins and blueberries.
2. Spread the mixture on a tray and bake for 1 hour at 250°F, stirring every 10 minutes.
3. Let cool, then mix in the raisins and blueberries.
