# Calamansi Juice Drink

**Calamansi juice drink** is a Filipino version of lemonade because calamansi is readily available in the marketplace.

## Ingredients

- 3 or 4 calamansi
- 1 glass cold water
- ½ tablespoon granulated sugar
- Ice cubes (optional)

## Procedure

1. Halve the calamansi with a sharp knife.
2. Use your hands to squeeze the juice from the calamansi.
3. Combine the juice with the cold water in a glass.
4. Stir in sugar until dissolved.
5. Add ice cubes.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- This drink can also be served hot.
- It is a good source of Vitamin C.
