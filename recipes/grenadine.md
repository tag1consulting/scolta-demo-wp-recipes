# Grenadine

**Grenadine** is a pomegranate syrup used in various drinks, both alcoholic and nonalcoholic. This recipe originates from a Reddit thread.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 ml) pomegranate juice
- ½ cup (120 ml) white sugar

## Procedure

1. Mix the juice and sugar in a pot or saucepan.
2. Boil the juice and sugar, stirring constantly, until it becomes a thick and viscous syrup resembling dark honey.
3. Remove from the heat, and let it cool.
4. Let it cool.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Be careful while mixing the syrup; hot sugar can splash and cause serious burns.
- You can use grenadine to make a Shirley Temple or grenadine soda.
