## Ingredients

- 6 ounces mayonnaise
- 6 ounces sour cream
- 1½–2 ounces chili sauce
- 1/6 tsp cayenne pepper

## Procedure

1. Stir all ingredients together well.

## Uses

- Put 1 small cup into center of each Onion Bloom for dipping.
