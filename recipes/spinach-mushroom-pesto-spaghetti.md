# Spinach Mushroom Pesto Spaghetti

This **spinach mushroom pesto spaghetti** recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty.

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) spaghetti
- 2 tablespoon olive oil
- 140 g (5 oz) spinach
- 200 g (7 oz) mushrooms, sliced
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon pepper
- 1 cup (275 g / 9.5 oz) pesto
- ½ cup (60 g / 2 oz) grated parmesan cheese, plus extra for sprinkling

## Procedure

1. Boil the spaghetti in water until *al dente*.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan over a medium heat.
3. Cook the spinach for 5 minutes until it is wilted.
4. Add the mushrooms, salt, and pepper and cook for a further 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Stir in the pesto and cheese and cook for 2 minutes.
6. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the sauce.
7. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve.
8. To serve, sprinkle with some extra cheese.
