## Ingredients

- 4 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and sliced
- 4 Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and sliced
- ½ cup raisins
- 2 Tablespoons honey
- 3 Tablespoons apple juice or cider
- 3 Tablespoons melted butter

## Procedure

1. Arrange sliced sweet potatoes, apples, and raisins in a greased 2-quart baking dish.
2. Mix the liquids and butter together. Pour over the dish's contents.
3. Cover with foil and bake at 350°F for 30 to 45 minutes, until sweet potatoes are tender and glazed.
