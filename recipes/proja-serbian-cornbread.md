# Proja (Serbian Cornbread)

**Proja** (Serbian Cyrillic: *Пpoja*) is a Serbian national dish of cornbread.

## Ingredients

- 5 cups cornmeal
- 3 cups flour
- 3 eggs
- 3 cups oil
- 1 baking powder
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1 glass mineral water
- 1 big slice cheese, crushed
- Salt

## Procedure

1. Mix everything together to make a batter.
2. Bake the batter in a 5 cm deep greased pan until golden.
3. Serve with *kiseli kupus* and *pavlaka*.
