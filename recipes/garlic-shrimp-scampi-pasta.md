# Garlic Shrimp Scampi Pasta

This **garlic shrimp scampi** recipe is adapted from the YouTube channel Tasty.

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) spaghetti
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 garlic cloves
- 450 g (1 lb) shrimp
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 teaspoon red chile flakes
- ¼ cup (25 g / 1 oz) chopped parsley

## Procedure

1. Boil the spaghetti in water until *al dente*.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. Add the garlic and stir.
4. Add the shrimp and cook for 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they turn pink-red in color.
5. Season with salt and pepper, then stir in lemon juice, chili, and parsley.
6. Drain the pasta in a colander and stir into the pan of shrimp.
7. Divide the pasta between 4 plates and serve.
