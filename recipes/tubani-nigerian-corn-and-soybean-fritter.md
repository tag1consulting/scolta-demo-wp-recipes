# Tubani (Nigerian Corn and Soybean Fritter)

**Tubani** is a Nigerian dish eaten by the Hausa people. It is made from maize, soya beans, and baobab leaf powder.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups corn
- 2 cups soy beans
- 4 spoonfuls of kuka (baobab leaf powder)
- 1 small piece potash
- ½ cup chilli pepper
- 2 teaspoon stock cube
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil for frying

## Procedure

1. Blend all the ingredients together, adding water as needed to make a smooth dough.
2. Mold the dough into your desired shape.
3. Fry the dough in vegetable oil.
