## Ingredients

- 1 cup crumbled blue cheese
- ½ cup sour cream
- ½ tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 recipe Hot Wings or Buffalo Wings
- Celery sticks

## Procedure

1. Combine cheese, cayenne, sour cream, garlic, and salt in a serving bowl.
2. Serve with celery and hot wings/Buffalo Wings.
