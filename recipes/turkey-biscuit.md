# Turkey Biscuit

This recipe is designed for using the leftover turkey after a holiday meal, such as Thanksgiving or Christmas dinner.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cooked turkey meat, chopped
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 2 tablespoons ranch dressing
- ½ cup milk
- ¾ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 package frozen biscuit dough

## Procedure

1. Sauté the onion until tender.
2. Combine in a small bowl the all the other ingredients except the cheese and biscuits.
3. Stir this mixture, then place it into a baking dish, and bake for 15 minutes at 350°F until it is quite warm throughout.
4. Remove the baked mixture from the oven and sprinkle the shredded cheese on it.
5. Add a layer of frozen biscuits on top of this. Bake for about 20–30 minutes longer.
6. Serve.
