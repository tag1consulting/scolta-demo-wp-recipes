# Egbo (Nigerian Cornmeal Porridge)

**Egbo**, also known as corn porridge, is a dish common among the people from Ibadan, Nigeria. It is made from cooked dry corn. Egbo is referred to *ororo robo* when eaten with sauce, beans, and vegetables.

## Ingredients

- Dried corn
- Salt

## Equipment

- Mill

- Pressure cooker

## Procedure

1. Crack the corn using the mill, and blow to get rid of the husks.
2. Cook the corn with a pinch of salt using a pressure cooker.
3. Add more water, and cook until soft and pulpy.
