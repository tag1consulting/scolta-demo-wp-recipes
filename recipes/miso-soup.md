# Miso Soup

In traditional Japanese cuisine, miso soup is served for breakfast every day, and often served with meals as well.

## Ingredients

- ½ tsp sesame oil
- 3 cups water
- 2 tbsp miso
- 2 green onions (optional), finely minced
- ½ cake tofu (optional), cut into ½-inch cubes
- 2–3 fresh mushrooms (optional), thinly sliced
- 5–6 shrimp (optional), cubed or shredded
- Some pieces of seaweed (optional)

## Procedure

1. Heat oil in a small pot.
2. Add green onion. Cook for a few minutes while stirring frequently.
3. Add 2 ½ cups of water. Bring to a boil.
4. Dissolve miso in remaining ½ cup of water and add to pot.
5. Lower heat and add mushrooms, seaweed, and tofu, if desired.
