# Karak Chai I

**Karak chai** is a type of chai tea popular in Southwest and South Asia. This recipe originates from Unilever Food Solutions Arabia.

## Recipes

- 1 cup (240 ml) water
- 4 cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- ½ in (1.25 cm) thick ginger slice
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 black tea bags or 2 tsp (10 ml) black tea
- ½ cup (120 ml) milk or dairy-free milk
- 2 tsp (10 ml) white sugar

## Procedure

1. Boil the water in a saucepan.
2. Add the cardamom, cloves, ginger, cinnamon, and tea. Remove from the heat, and let steep for 5 minutes.
3. Mix in the milk and sugar.
4. Strain the mixture.
