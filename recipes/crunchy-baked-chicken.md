# Crunchy Baked Chicken

This is a surprisingly easy and filling dish.

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breasts
- ½ cup Italian breadcrumbs
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- ¼ cup butter, melted

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a shallow bowl, melt butter and place chicken breast in bowl to coat.
3. In another shallow bowl, mix Italian bread crumbs and Parmesan cheese.
4. Transfer chicken from butter bowl to breadcrumb bowl. Roll chicken in breadcrumb mixture until coated.
5. Place in greased pan.
6. Bake for 35–40 minutes.
