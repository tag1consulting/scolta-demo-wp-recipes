## Ingredients

- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 oranges, juiced
- 4 pounds rhubarb, cut into small pieces
- 1 pound raisins
- 3 pounds sugar

## Procedure

1. Combine the lemon juice, orange juice, and rhubarb.
2. Chop lemon and orange rind along with raisins; add to rhubarb mixture.
3. Mix all and let stand for 30 minutes.
4. Add sugar and bring to a boil, then simmer for 1 hour, stirring often.
5. When the mixture gets thick, pour into glasses or crock.
6. When cool, seal.
