# Tiramisu Mousse

This **tiramisu mousse** is so-called because its flavor profile matches tiramisu.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mascarpone cheese
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup espresso, cooled
- 2 egg yolks, beaten
- 1 cup whipping cream, whipped to soft peaks
- Cocoa powder

## Procedure

1. Combine mascarpone cheese, sugar, espresso, and eggs. Set aside.
2. Gently fold in the whipped cream.
3. Chill until set. Before serving, dust with cocoa powder.
