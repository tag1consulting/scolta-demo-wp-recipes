# Ove (Nigerian Yam Moi Moi)

**Ove** is a typical Nigerian pudding prepared from yam.

## Ingredients

- 2 tsp chile pepper paste
- 1 medium-sized yam, peeled and grated
- ¼ cup shredded fish
- 2 tbsp groundnut oil
- ¼ ea. onion, chopped
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 wrapping leaf (e.g. banana leaf)

## Procedure

1. Remove unwanted substances from the fish. Then, rinse and cook for few minutes.
2. Combine the groundnut oil, onion, pepper paste, and fish to make a sauce.
3. Stir the sauce into the grated yam along with the salt.
4. Wrap this mixture up in the leaf, then boil for a few minutes to cook it.
