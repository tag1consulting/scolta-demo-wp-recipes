# Kipspek (Bacon-wrapped Chicken)

**Kipspek**, which literally translates from Dutch into "chickenbacon", is a very easy snack consisting of cubed chicken wrapped in bacon, which is then breaded and fried.

## Ingredients

- 1–2 chicken breasts, cubed
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Bacon strips
- 1–2 eggs
- Fine powdery breadcrumbs
- Toothpicks
- Oil

## Procedure

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
2. Wrap each cube of chicken in a strip of bacon.
3. To make sure the bacon doesn't roll off the chicken, stick a toothpick from one end to the other.
4. Roll the kipspek in the breadcrumbs.
5. Whisk the eggs, and dip each kipspek in the eggs.
6. Roll the kipspek through the breadcrumbs once again. Leave them for about half an hour to dry out the surface.
7. Heat the oil in a deep pot.
8. Fry the kipspeks until they're golden brown all over.
9. Serve with ketchup or mustard—don't forget to remove the toothpick before taking a bite.
