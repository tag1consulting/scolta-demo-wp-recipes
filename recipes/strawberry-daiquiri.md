# Strawberry Daiquiri

The **strawberry daiquiri** is a cocktail composed mainly of rum, schnapps, and strawberries. It can be shaken or puréed in a blender for a smooth consistency.

## Ingredients

- Strawberry juice, for rim
- Sugar, for rim
- 1 oz light rum
- ½ oz strawberry schnapps
- 1 oz lime juice
- 2 tsp powdered sugar
- 2 oz fresh or frozen strawberries
- Crushed ice
- Whipped cream, for garnish
- Fresh strawberry, for garnish

## Procedure

1. Rim a glass in strawberry juice and sugar.
2. Shake rum, schnapps, lime juice, powdered sugar, strawberries, and crushed ice in a cocktail shaker.
3. Pour into the rimmed glass.
4. Garnish with whipped cream and a whole fresh strawberry.
5. Serve.
