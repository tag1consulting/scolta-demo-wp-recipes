## Ingredients

- ½ cup tomato paste
- 10–15 habaneros chiles, de-stemmed
- ¼ cup malt vinegar
- Salt to taste
- 5 drops red food coloring

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor and pulse until smooth.
2. Refrigerate in a non-reactive container for up to 2 months.
