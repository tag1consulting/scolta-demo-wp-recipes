# Rib Rub

Use this for all kinds of BBQ. It's a bit spicy, so use sparingly.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups smoked paprika
- ¼ cup salt
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 3 tbsp cayenne pepper (or adjust to taste)
- ¼ cup dry mustard
- ¼ cup garlic powder
- ¼ cup dried rosemary
- 1 tbsp cinnamon

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in a container with a shaker lid.
