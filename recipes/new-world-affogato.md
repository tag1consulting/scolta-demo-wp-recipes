## Ingredients

- 1 scoop vanilla ice cream
- 1 tsp coffee powder
- 1 tsp brandy
- 1½ tsp sugar
- 1½ tsp cinnamon powder, for garnishing (optional)

## Procedure

1. Place the ice cream in a coffee cup.
2. Boil water in a pan, and then add coffee and sugar to it, and then mix well.
3. Add brandy to coffee mixture.
4. Pour coffee mixture over the ice-cream.
5. Serve immediately, garnished with cinnamon powder.
