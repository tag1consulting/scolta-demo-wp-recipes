# Garlic and Tarragon Dressing

This is a simple salad dressing.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (60 ml) vinegar
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp garlic salt
- 2 tsp sugar (optional)
- ½ tsp dried tarragon

## Procedure

1. In a small bowl or jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine vinegar, oil, garlic salt, sugar and tarragon.
2. Pour it on lettuce or cabbage salads.
