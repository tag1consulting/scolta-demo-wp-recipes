# Sunglow Motel-Cafe Pickle Pie

The Sunglow Motel-Cafe in Bicknell, Utah serves the unique **pickle pie**, developed by Cula Ekker. This is their recipe, as it appeared in a *Salt Lake Tribune* article that is framed and hangs on the cafe's wall.

## Ingredients

- 5 eggs
- 2 cups white granulated sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp lemon extract
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 cup + 2 tbsp light cream
- ⅛ cup margarine, melted
- 12 oz sweet pickles, drained and ground
- 2 unbaked 9-inch pie shells

## Procedure

1. Beat eggs and sugar until lemon-colored and thick; beat in cinnamon, nutmeg, lemon extract, cornstarch, cream and margarine.
2. Stir ground sweet pickles into mixture.
3. Pour pickle mixture into unbaked pie shells.
4. Bake at 350°F for 1–1.25 hours, until a knife inserted in center comes out clean.
