## Ingredients

- 1 pound sharp cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 3 tablespoons diced pimentos
- 1 tablespoon white granulated sugar
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- ¼–2 teaspoons finely diced jalapeños, to taste

## Procedure

1. Mix ingredients thoroughly.
2. Store in well-sealed container in refrigerator.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Jalapeños are definitely optional.
