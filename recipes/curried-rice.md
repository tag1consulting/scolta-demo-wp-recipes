## Ingredients

- 1 cup (125 g/4.4 oz) uncooked brown rice or white rice
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- ½ onion, chopped
- ½ cup (60 g/2.1 oz) raisins
- 2 cups (500 ml/1.1 pint) water
- 3 vegetarian or chicken bouillon cubes

## Procedure

1. Find a large saucepan or frying pan which has a lid, and place over medium heat.
2. Add butter then chopped onion to pan. Cook the onion for a couple of minutes until it starts to brown slightly.
3. Add curry powder, and stir for a minute.
4. Mix in vegetarian bouillon cubes, raisins, rice, and water.
5. Put on pan lid and simmer until water is absorbed (about 15 minutes).
