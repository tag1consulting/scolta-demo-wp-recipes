## Ingredients

- 4 oz (110 g) smoked salmon
- 8 oz (225 g) low-fat cream cheese
- 2 oz (55 g) low-fat sour cream
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp green onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp basil, chopped
- 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
- 1 tbsp black pepper

## Procedure

1. In medium-sized bowl, crumble salmon; add cream cheese, sour cream, lemon juice, and pepper.
2. Mix until well incorporated or creamy.
3. Mix in basil and parsley.
4. Serve and enjoy.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Whipped cream cheese is much easier to mix.
- Add a pinch of paprika/cayenne for added heat.
