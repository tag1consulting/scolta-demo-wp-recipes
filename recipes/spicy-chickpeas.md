# Spicy Chickpeas

**Spicy chickpeas** make a good fall-back meal using ingredients that many pantries will often have lying in a dusty corner, but that are also cheap to pick up.

## Ingredients

- 250 g canned chickpeas
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- Pepper
- Salt

## Procedure

1. Strain chickpeas and dry with a towel.
2. Heat oil in large skillet. Add chickpeas, and sauté until slightly colored, about 6 minutes.
3. Add Dijon mustard and stir.
4. Season with pepper and salt to taste.
