## Ingredients

- 2 cups whole wheat flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar or jaggery
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon powder
- 2 cups soy milk
- 4 tablespoons oil

## Procedure

1. Mix together flour, baking powder, and sugar.
2. Mix soy milk into the dry mixture.
3. Heat a griddle until water sprinkled on it quickly evaporates. Spread a thin film of oil on the griddle.
4. Pour pancake batter on griddle to form 2–3-inch circles. Bubbles should start forming.
5. When the underside is light brown, flip the pancake.
6. When the other side is light brown, serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- For lighter pancakes use half white, and half whole-wheat flour
