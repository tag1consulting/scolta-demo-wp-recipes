# Drawn Butter

**Drawn butter** is a butter-based sauce typically served with lobster or crab.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (250 ml / 8.5 oz) water
- 2 teaspoons flour
- 2 ounces (57 g) unsalted butter

## Procedure

1. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan.
2. Mix the flour and butter together until they are perfectly smooth.
3. Stir this into the boiling water, and add salt to taste.
