## Ingredients

- 1 large or 2 small bananas
- Vanilla ice cream
- Milk
- Sugar
- Ground cinnamon (optional)

## Procedure

1. Blend the banana, ice cream, sugar, and milk as desired until smooth and creamy.
2. If desired, add a pinch of cinnamon.
