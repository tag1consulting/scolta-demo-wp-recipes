## Ingredients

### Crepes

- ½ cup (120 ml) flour
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp granulated sugar
- 3 tbsp melted butter
- ⅔ cup (160 ml) milk
- 1 pinch of salt

### Filling

- 2 cups (480 ml) chopped fruit or whole berries of your choice
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup (120 ml) granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 4 tbsp orange juice

### Garnish

- Powdered sugar
- Cinnamon

## Procedure

### Crepes

1. Mix all ingredients together in a large mixing bowl to make a batter.
2. Strain the batter to get rid of any lumps, then chill in the refrigerator for 2 hours.
3. Brush a 12 inch (30 cm) frying pan with butter. Ladle batter onto pan as if making crepes, leaving space for spreading.
4. Cook for 1 minute on each side.

### Filling

1. Combine orange juice, lemon juice, sugar, and vanilla extract in medium saucepan. Bring to boil and add fruit.
2. Put 2 tbsp fruit mixture on each pancake, and fold over to enclose the filling.
3. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and cinnamon.
