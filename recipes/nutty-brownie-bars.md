## Ingredients

- 1 regular package brownie mix
- 3 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
- ½ cup chopped almonds
- ¼ cup chopped walnuts
- 10 miniature chocolate coconut candy bars, chopped

## Procedure

1. Prepare brownie mix according to package directions, reducing oil to 3 tablespoons.
2. Pour into lightly greased 9-by-3-inch pan.
3. Spread condensed milk over the brownie batter.
4. Sprinkle with chopped nuts and candy bars.
5. Bake at 350°F for 35–38 minutes or until done.
6. Cool completely and cut into bars.
