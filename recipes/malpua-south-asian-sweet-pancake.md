## Ingredients

- 1 litre milk
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup semolina flour
- Ghee
- Water

## Procedure

1. Boil the milk until it reduces to half of its original volume.
2. Roast the semolina until light brown.
3. Mix the all-purpose flour and milk into the toasted semolina to make a batter. It should be smooth and lump-free.
4. Heat the ghee in a skillet.
5. Use a circular pouring motion to pour batter in the pan and make a small pancake.
6. Keep turning over. Take care that it does not become crisp or harsh.
7. Make a sugar syrup of good consistency. Dip the malpua in the syrup.
8. Serve.
