# Strawberry-Vanilla Lassi

**Lassi** with strawberry and vanilla.

## Ingredients

- 10 strawberries
- 4 tbsp superfine sugar
- 1 pack vanilla sugar
- 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 150 ml water
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 200 g yogurt

## Procedure

1. Put everything in a blender, and blend until smooth.
