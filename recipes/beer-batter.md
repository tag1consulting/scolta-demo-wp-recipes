# Beer Batter

**Beer batter** is a mixture used to coat various food items before deep-frying. Foods that can be beer-battered and fried include onion rings, fish, shrimp, chicken, and a variety of vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cornstarch
- ½ cup flour
- ⅓ teaspoon garlic salt
- ⅓ teaspoon paprika
- 1/6 teaspoon salt
- 1/6 teaspoon pepper
- 8 ounces (225 ml) beer

## Procedure

1. Stir together dry ingredients.
2. Stir in beer.
3. Mix together thoroughly.

## Use

- Use in preparation of onion blooms.
