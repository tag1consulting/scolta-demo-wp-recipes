# Prime Rib Seasoning

This seasoning will elevate your rib roast to steakhouse quality. It also works well on steaks.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup celery seed
- ¼ cup salt
- ½ cup coarsely ground black pepper
- ¼ cup coarsely ground coriander seed
- 2 tsp mesquite smoke powder
- ¼ cup chipotle powder

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in an airtight container with a shaker lid.
