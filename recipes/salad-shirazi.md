# Salad Shirazi

**Salad shirazi** is considered a fairly simple salad. It is popular, easy to make, and usually served in place of the green salad when there are health concerns.

## Ingredients

- Cucumber
- Onions
- Tomatoes
- Lemon juice or vinegar
- Salt
- Pepper
- Dried mint

## Procedure

1. Chop the cucumbers, tomatoes, and onions finely.
2. Marinate them in lemon juice or vinegar.
3. Add salt and pepper. Sprinkle with the dried mint.
