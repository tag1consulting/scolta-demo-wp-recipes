# Peanut Butter Banana Oatmeal

A version of **oatmeal** with peanut butter, banana, and honey.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats or instant oats
- ½ cup milk
- 1 banana, peeled and sliced
- 1–2 tbsp peanut butter
- ½ tbsp (or more) honey, to taste

## Procedure

1. Cook the oats in water. If using regular oats this should take around 10 minutes, while instant oats will cook more quickly.
2. Put the cooked oats in a bowl
3. Add milk, peanut butter, honey and sliced banana.
4. Mash until the banana and ingredients are all thoroughly combined.
5. Top with honey and serve warm.
