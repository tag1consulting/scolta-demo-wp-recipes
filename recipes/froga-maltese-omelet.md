## Ingredients

- 2 medium potatoes, peeled
- 1 bunch of spring onions, chopped
- 100 g cooked lean beef or 200 g corned beef (optional), chopped very small and fine
- Cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 handful of parsley, chopped fine
- Salt
- Pepper
- 6 eggs

## Procedure

1. Boil potatoes, then mash them.
2. Place the mashed potatoes in a bowl and add the spring onions, beef, cheese, the parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix together, then add the eggs and mix everything together.
3. Heat some oil in a frying pan. Add 3 large spoonfuls of the mixture to the frying pan, and flatten them out to around 1 cm thick. Cook until golden brown.
4. Serve hot with salad and fresh buttered bread.
