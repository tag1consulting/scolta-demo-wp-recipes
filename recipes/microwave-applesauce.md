# Microwave Applesauce

For all of you who would like to make your own applesauce, but without having to do much work, this recipe is for you.

## Ingredients

- 5 apples, peeled, cored, and diced
- 1½ cups apple jelly
- ¼ cup Grade B Amber maple syrup (Grade A Dark Amber would be okay too)
- ¼ cup freshly-squeezed orange juice
- 1 cinnamon stick

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in a microwave-safe container.
2. Cover container, leaving 1 corner open, and microwave on high 7–8 minutes or until apples are easy to mash.
3. Remove cinnamon stick and discard.
4. For a chunky sauce, mash the apples with a potato masher. For a slightly smoother sauce, use a hand mixer. For a smooth pureé, use an immersion blender.
