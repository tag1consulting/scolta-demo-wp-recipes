## Ingredients

- 2 large scoops of orange sherbet
- 1 large scoop of vanilla ice cream
- 6 ounces (170 g) Dr. Pepper (or equivalent) soda
- 1 large squeeze of chocolate syrup
- Dash of lime juice
- 1 pinch of salt (optional)

## Procedure

1. Combine all the ingredients in a blender.
2. Blend until mixture is thin enough to pour out of blender into a glass.
3. Enjoy with a spoon and/or soda straw.
