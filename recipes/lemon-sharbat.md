## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 pinch salt
- Black pepper to taste
- Black salt to taste

## Procedure

1. Mix lemon juice and water.
2. Add sugar and salt.
3. Add black pepper and black salt to taste.
4. Stir well and serve over ice.
