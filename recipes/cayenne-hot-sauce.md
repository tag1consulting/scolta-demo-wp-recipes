# Cayenne Hot Sauce

This **hot sauce** tastes very much like Tabasco and other vinegar-based cayenne hot sauces.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup malt vinegar
- ¼ cup cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp salt

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients. Store indefinitely at room temperature in a squirt bottle.
