# Chicken Curry (Mediterranean-inspired)

This is an Indian dish with Mediterranean influence.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 6 cloves of garlic, smashed
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 5 medium spicy long green chiles, slit in half length-wise
- 5 tbsp milk
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp dried basil
- Salt to taste
- 2 whole bone-in chicken thighs

## Procedure

1. Combine oil, garlic, lemon juice, chiles, milk, garam masala, dried basil, and salt to make a marinade.
2. Marinate the chicken in the marinade for about ½-1 hour.
3. Transfer chicken and marinade to a pot. Cover and cook over low heat until fully cooked and tender.
