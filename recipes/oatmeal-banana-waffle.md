## Ingredients

- 2 cups oatmeal
- 2 cups water
- ½ banana
- 2 tablespoons powdered milk
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Procedure

1. Turn waffle iron on high.
2. Put all ingredients in a blender and blend on high until the contents are smooth. Let the mixture sit for 10–15 minutes.
3. Spray the waffle iron with non-stick cooking spray.
4. Pour some of the batter onto the waffle iron. Cook for about 10 minutes. Repeat until you are out of batter.
