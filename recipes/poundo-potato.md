# Poundo Potato

**Potato poundo** is a Nigerian swallow made from potato flour.

## Ingredients

- Potato flour
- Water

## Procedure

1. Bring water to boil in a small pot.
2. Stir in potato flour until you get a thick mixture. Continue stirring to prevent lumps from forming.
3. Add some water, and cook for 5 minutes over low heat.
4. Stir again to ensure that it's smooth.
5. Remove from the fire and put in a wrapper.
6. Serve with any soup of your choice.
