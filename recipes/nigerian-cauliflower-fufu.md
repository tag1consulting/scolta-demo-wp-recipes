# Nigerian Cauliflower Fufu

**Nigerian cauliflower fufu** is swallow made with cauliflower, which is rich in fibre. Cauliflower fufu is similar to pounded yam, just like eggplant fufu is similar to amala.

## Ingredients

- 6 florets cauliflower
- 3 tablespoons palm oil (optional)
- ½ teaspoon psyllium husk

## Equipment

- Knife
- Sieve cloth/chiffon cloth
- Blender

## Procedure

1. Cut cauliflower into small pieces, and blend with a blender to give a purée.
2. Strain the cauliflower mixture through a cloth, retaining the solids.
3. Squeeze the sieve cloth to drain more water until relatively dry and a dough is formed.
4. Transfer the dough to a saucepan over medium heat, and stir in the psyllium husk.
5. Stir and mold to form a paste, and shape into portions.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Psyllium husk makes the cauliflower fufu moldable.
