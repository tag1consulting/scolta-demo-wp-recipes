# Sangah (Cameroonian Corn and Cassava Leaf)

**Sangah** is a Cameroonian dish made with corn, cassava leaf, and palm kernel extract.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg palm nut cream
- Water
- 500 g cassava leaves, well-pounded
- Sweet corn kernels
- Homi

## Equipment

- Pot with lid
- Stovetop
- Mortar and pestle

## Procedure

1. Combine the water and palm nut cream in a pot. Boil for a few minutes.
2. Stir in the pounded cassava leaves. Cover with the lid, and boil for 20 minutes.
3. Stir in as much corn as desired. Season to taste with homi.
4. Serve hot.
