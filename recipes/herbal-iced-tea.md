## Ingredients

- 0.75 L rosehip tea
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 oranges, juiced
- 5 drops orange flower extract
- 1 lime, cut into pieces
- 1 lemon, cut into pieces

## Procedure

1. Sweeten the rosehip tea with the honey and allow to chill.
2. Add orange juice and orange flower extract.
3. Divide the lime and lemon between 4 large glasses, and add the tea.
4. Serve ice-cold.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Note that mint tea, berry tea, or other herbal teas may replace the rosehip tea. However discard orange flower extract in these cases; add mint or fresh fruit instead.
- An artificial sweetener may be used instead of honey.
