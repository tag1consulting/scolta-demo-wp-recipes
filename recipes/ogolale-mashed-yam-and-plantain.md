# Ogolale (Mashed Yam and Plantain)

**Ogolale**, also known as **oritobor**, is a dish of mashed yam, unripe plantain, and palm oil. It is popular with the people of the Niger Delta.

## Ingredients

- Yam
- Unripe plantain
- Salt
- Palm oil

## Procedure

1. Boil the yam and plantain in a pot of salted water until cooked through.
2. Drain the water, and transfer the cooked yam and plantain to a clean container.
3. Use a spoon or spatula to mash the yam and plantain.
4. Mix in the palm oil.
5. Adjust seasoning to taste, and serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Additional ingredients may be added according to taste.
