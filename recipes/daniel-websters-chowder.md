# Daniel Webster's Chowder

From the 1881 *Household Cyclopedia*

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp chopped onion
- Fatty pork
- 1 quart mashed potatoes
- 1 ½ pounds sea biscuit, broken
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp summer savory
- ½ bottle mushroom catsup
- 1 bottle port or claret
- ¼ nutmeg pod, grated
- A few cloves
- Mace
- Allspice
- 6 pounds sea bass or cod, cut into slices
- 25 oysters
- Black pepper
- A few slices lemon

## Procedure

1. Fry the onion with the fatty pork. Transfer to a large pot.
2. Add all remaining ingredients to pot.
3. Add enough water to cover everything by 1 inch.
4. Bring to a boil. Simmer for 1 hour, stirring gently every so often.
5. Serve.
