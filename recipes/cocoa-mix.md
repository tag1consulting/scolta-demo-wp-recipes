## Ingredients

- 4 cups nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 cup cocoa powder
- 1 cup white sugar
- ½ tsp salt

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Mix thoroughly.
3. Store in an airtight container.

## Use

For each serving of hot cocoa, add 2 heaping teaspoons of cocoa mix to 1 cup of hot water, and stir until smooth.
