# Ploughman's Sandwich

The **ploughman's sandwich** is a popular traditional mouth-watering sandwich. The pickle provides some sourness, and the lettuce prevents dryness.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices of bread
- Sliced cheddar cheese
- Lettuce, preferably iceberg
- Sliced tomato
- Sweet pickle
- Optional extras such as sliced onions or mayonnaise.

## Procedure

1. Assemble a sandwich between the slices of bread, containing 1 layer of cheese, 2–3 layers of lettuce, a thin layer of sweet pickle, and a few tomato slices.
