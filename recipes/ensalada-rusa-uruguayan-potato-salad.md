# Ensalada Rusa (Uruguayan Potato Salad)

***Ensalada Rusa***, which translates into "Russian Salad" is a simple potato salad that is a staple in Uruguayan tables. It is served with "asado" in restaurants.

## Ingredients

- 2 large potatoes, diced
- 1 cup fresh peas
- 2 carrots, diced
- ½–¾ cup mayonnaise
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste

## Procedure

1. Cook all vegetables separately until tender, but not too soft.
2. Allow to cool completely.
3. Dress with mayonnaise, salt, and pepper.
4. Serve as a side dish.

## Notes, tips, and mayonnaise

- Please note that the mayonnaise used for this dish should not be sweet.
