## Ingredients

- ¼ pound (110 g) sweet chocolate
- 2 tablespoons cream
- sifted confectioner's sugar

## Procedure

1. Melt chocolate over hot water.
2. Add the cream.
3. Gradually beat in sugar until icing is creamy and thick enough to spread.
