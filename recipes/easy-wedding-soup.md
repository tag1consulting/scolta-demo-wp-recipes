# Easy Wedding Soup

**Easy wedding soup** is a simple variation on Italian wedding soup that requires only four ingredients and water.

## Ingredients

- 6 cups chicken broth (from bouillon cubes is fine)
- 2 cups water
- 1 packet (10 oz) frozen spinach
- 12 oz spicy chicken sausage (pre-cooked)
- 3 tbsp acini di pepe (*pastina*, little bead-like pasta)

## Procedure

1. Bring broth and water to boil in 4-quart saucepan.
2. Add frozen spinach.
3. Cut sausage into small pieces.
4. Add sausage and acini di pepe to soup.
5. Bring back to boil, and allow to boil for 10 minutes.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Calorie content may vary depending on the specific ingredients you use; 80 kcal is reasonable.
- The spicy sausage will impart more subtle flavor to the soup, making further spicing unnecessary. Using different types of sausage will vastly change the character of the soup.
- This recipe may require salt if you use homemade chicken stock. Bouillon cubes tend to be heavily salted already.
