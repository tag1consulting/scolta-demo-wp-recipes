## Ingredients

- ¼ cup tomato sauce
- Salt, pepper, and other seasonings to taste (see tips below)
- Cooking spray
- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons chopped green bell peppers
- 2 tablespoons chopped mushrooms
- ¼ cup shredded mozzarella cheese

## Procedure

1. Mix spices and tomato sauce and allow to meld flavors while preparing the rest of the recipe.
2. Beat eggs well.
3. Heat a small skillet over low heat, sprayed with cooking spray.
4. Pour beaten eggs into skillet, then cover skillet.
5. Cook for 3 minutes. Eggs should be almost set.
6. Carefully flip omelet over. Cook another minute.
7. Combine mushrooms and peppers and cook 30 seconds in microwave oven.
8. Spread tomato-spice mix over omelet. Sprinkle cheese over tomato sauce. Top cheese with hot mushrooms and peppers.
9. Cover skillet. Cook another 2 minutes. Cheese should be melted.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Basil, crushed red pepper, and oregano work well.
