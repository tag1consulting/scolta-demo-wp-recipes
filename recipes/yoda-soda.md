# Yoda Soda

**Yoda Soda** is a fun drink that looks like Master Yoda's head.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Mountain Dew soda (or any yellowish-green soda you like)

- 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 1 pinch of chocolate sprinkles

## Procedure

1. Fill glass with soda.
2. Add ice cream.
3. Drop a few chocolate sprinkles on the ice cream to look like hair.
