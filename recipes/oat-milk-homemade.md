## Ingredients

- ½ cup oats
- 2 liters water

## Procedure

1. Put oats in a blender. Blend the oats to the finest powder you can get
2. Add as much of the water as possible to the blender. Blend some more.
3. Strain the mixture into a jug, and add the rest of the water.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Stir the milk before consuming it.
- This will keep in the refrigerator at least a week.
- The blended oat solids can be used to make porridge.
