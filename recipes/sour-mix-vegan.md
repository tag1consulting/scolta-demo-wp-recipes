# Sour Mix (Vegan)

This homemade recipe for sour mix includes three different kinds of citrus and no egg.

## Ingredients

- 2 parts lime juice
- 2 parts simple syrup
- 1 part orange juice
- 1 part lemon juice

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients together.
2. Store in the fridge until use.
