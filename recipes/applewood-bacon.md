## Ingredients

- 1 pork belly from the loin end
- 2 cups salt
- 2 cups white granulated sugar
- 1 gallon apple cider
- 1 gallon iced water
- Applewood chunks

## Procedure

1. In a large pot, bring to apple cider to boil. Stir in salt and sugar until dissolved.
2. Transfer cider mixture to a very large container, and add iced water.
3. Once ice has melted and brine is cool enough, submerge the pork belly in the brine.
4. Refrigerate for 3 days.
5. Remove belly, and place on top of a running box fan for 30 minutes per side to dry out.
6. Place dry applewood chunks in the firebox of a cold smoker.
7. Insert pork belly and smoke for 3–4 hours.
8. Remove from smoker and place in the freezer for up to 2 hours.
9. Slice bacon as thick or as thin as you like, and cook or refrigerate for up to 3 weeks.
