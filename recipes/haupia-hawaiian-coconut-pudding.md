# Haupia (Hawaiian Coconut Pudding)

**Haupia** is a Hawaiian pudding made of coconut milk and cornstarch (originally Polynesian arrowroot starch).

## Ingredients

- 3 cups coconut milk
- ½ cup white granulated sugar
- 1 dash salt
- ½ cup cornstarch

## Procedure

1. Mix coconut milk with sugar and salt over a medium heat.
2. Slowly add cornstarch and stir until thickened.
3. Pour into a pan or square form.
4. Cool for 1 hour or until fully set.
5. Unmold and cut into squares.
