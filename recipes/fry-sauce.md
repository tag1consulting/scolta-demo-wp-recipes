# Fry Sauce

Fry sauce is a simple condiment, used often with fries. It uses one part of tomato ketchup with two parts of mayonnaise.

## Ingredients

- 2 tsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp tomato ketchup

## Procedure

1. Combine the mayonnaise and ketchup.
2. Mix until the sauce is a strong pink colour.
