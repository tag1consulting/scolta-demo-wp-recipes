## Ingredients

- 235 ml orange juice
- 130 g vanilla ice cream
- 235 ml lemon-lime soda

## Procedure

1. Place orange juice and ice cream in a blender.
2. Cover and process until smooth.
3. Stir in soda.
4. Pour into glasses.
