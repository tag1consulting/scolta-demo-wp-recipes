# Spinach Mushroom Omelet

This **spinach mushroom omelet** is an American diner-style breakfast dish that provides a creamy mix of fresh vegetable taste and sharp cheddar.

## Ingredients

- 3 standard eggs
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 pinch ground black pepper
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg (optional)
- 2 tbsp milk
- 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
- 1 heaping handful of fresh spinach
- ⅔ cup baby bella mushrooms, thinly sliced
- ½ ea. large red onion, diced
- ½ cup shredded aged cheddar cheese
- ¼ tsp paprika

## Equipment

- Stovetop
- Skillet (30 cm or 12 inch diameter)
- 2 spatulas
- Whisk
- Ramekin or other small bowl

## Procedure

1. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, salt, pepper, nutmeg and milk, until even and glossy.
2. Heat the skillet over medium heat. Add half the butter, tilting the skillet in a circular motion to coat evenly.
3. Add paprika, mushrooms, and onions, and sauté until lightly browned.
4. Add spinach and toss for 30 seconds, or until slightly wilted, but still crunchy. Remove the vegetables from the heat and set aside.
5. Add the remaining half of the oil or butter to the pan over medium heat, and coat evenly. Lower the heat.
6. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet, swirling the pan to ensure an even, creamy layer of eggs. Heat until the eggs have begun to set, with some fluff on top.
7. Top the egg layer with the vegetables, distributing the vegetables evenly. Sprinkle the cheese evenly on top.
8. Gently push the edges of the omelet half a centimeter toward the center of the skillet with two spatulas, making sure not to break it. Turn the heat off.
9. Use the spatulas to carefully lift/pull half the omelet off the skillet and fold it over the other half. Carefully move the entire omelet onto a plate and serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- If you want to prevent the mushrooms from browning after you slice them, submerge them in a bowl of cool water until ready to cook them. Drain before adding to the pan.
