## Ingredients

- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- 1½ tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon ground mustard
- ½ teaspoon dried sage leaves
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 pound (about 1.5 kg) pork butt roast
- 1 cup applesauce
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ⅓ cup honey
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 325°F (about 165°C).
2. Mix together pepper, flour, mustard, sage, and salt to get a rub.
3. Apply rub to pork roast and set aside.
4. Line a roasting pan with foil and spray with cooking spray.
5. Place roast in pan and bake for 30 minutes.
6. Mix together applesauce, cinnamon, honey, and nutmeg as a glaze.
7. Remove roast from oven and spread with glaze.
8. Return glazed roast to oven and bake until you get an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C), about 50–60 minutes.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Good with sweet potatoes and green beans.
