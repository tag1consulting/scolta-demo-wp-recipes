# Thousand Island Dressing

**Thousand Island** is a salad dressing commonly used with green salads. It can also be used as a sandwich spread, or many other uses.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (235 ml) mayonnaise
- ¼ cup (60 ml) ketchup
- 2 tablespoons (30 ml) white vinegar
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) sweet pickle relish
- 2 teaspoons (10 ml) finely-minced onions
- ¼ teaspoon (1 ml) salt
- 1 dash of pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in a blender, and process till smooth. The goal is to have a pink smooth sauce.
2. Pour the sauce into a bowl and chill covered in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes prior to use.

## Notes, tips and variations

- Instead of using the blender, whisk the ingredients in a bowl.

## Warnings

- Keep refrigerated.
