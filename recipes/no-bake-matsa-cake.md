## Ingredients

- 7 ounces brown sugar
- 2 ounces cocoa powder
- 2 ounces raisins, soaked in 1 tablespoon sweet red wine
- ½ lb butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 7 sheets of matzah, crumbled

## Procedure

1. Combine brown sugar, cocoa, soaked raisins, and melted butter.
2. Add this mixture to the crumbled matzah and mix well.
3. Divide the mixture into two 9-inch springform or other cake pans.
4. Knead the batter to create a flat and smooth surface.
5. Refrigerate for several hours.
