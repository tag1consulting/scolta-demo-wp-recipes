## Ingredients

- 1 banana
- 6 strawberries
- 1 cup milk
- 3 tbsp yogurt
- 5 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 1 cup cream
- 2 tsp apple juice
- 2 tsp vanilla extract

## Procedure

1. Purée all ingredients together in a blender.
