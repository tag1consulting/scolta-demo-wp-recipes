# Slinger

The "Slinger" is a breakfast item originating from St. Louis Missouri.

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 hamburger patty
- Hash browns
- Cheese (grated works best)
- Chili
- Onion

## Procedure

1. Cook the eggs in any manner you wish.
2. Cook the hamburger patty.
3. Cook the chili.
4. Dice the onion.
5. Place the hashbrowns and patty on a plate then put the eggs upon the patty and top it all with the chili, cheese, and onion
