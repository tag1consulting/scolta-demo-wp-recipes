# Oat Fufu

**Oat fufu** is a swallow gotten from oatmeal.

## Ingredients

- Oatmeal
- Water

## Equipment

- Blender
- Turning spoon
- Pot

## Procedures

1. Blend the oats to give a fine powder.
2. Gently add the oats gradually in a pot of boiling water until a dough is formed.
3. Stir over medium heat until it solidifies.
