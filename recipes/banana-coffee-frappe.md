## Ingredients

- 1 large scoop vanilla ice cream
- 1 tsp instant coffee powder
- ½ banana, chopped
- 6 ice cubes

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in a blender.
2. Blend until smooth and frothy.
3. Serve immediately.
