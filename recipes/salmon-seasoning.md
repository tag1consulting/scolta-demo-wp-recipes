## Ingredients

- ¾ cup paprika
- ¼ cup dehydrated lemon peel
- ¼ cup granulated garlic
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- ¼ cup black peppercorns, cracked
- ⅓ cup salt
- 3 tbsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp cayenne pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients, mixing well.
2. Transfer to an airtight container with a shaker lid, and store in a cool dark place.
