# Chocolate Mousse (Vegan)

This surprisingly delicious **chocolate mousse** dessert is easily made from three New World plants, namely avocado, cocoa and agave.

The avocados should be *very* ripe, to the point where the navel almost - or even just - starts to mold.

## Ingredients

- Avocado(s)
- Cocoa powder
- Agave syrup

## Procedure

1. Peel the avocados and remove the pit.
2. Break down the fleshy parts of the avocados using a fork or mixer; depending on the desired texture.
3. Add the cocoa powder and mix.
4. Add agave syrup to taste, and mix some more.
5. Serve.
