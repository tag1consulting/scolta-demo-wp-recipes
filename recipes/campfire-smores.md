# Campfire S'mores

**Campfire s'mores** are a classic snack and a favorite with children.

## Ingredients

- 2 graham crackers
- 1 marshmallow
- 4 squares milk chocolate

## Procedure

1. Toast marshmallow until golden brown over campfire on long green stick with bark peeled off.
2. Place on graham cracker, then top with chocolate and remaining cracker.

## Warnings

- Be careful; when the marshmallows are ready they are very hot and can catch fire. Don't burn yourself!
