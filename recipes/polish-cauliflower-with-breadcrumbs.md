# Polish Cauliflower with Breadcrumbs

**Cauliflower with breadcrumbs** is a popular dish and side-dish in Poland and many parts of Central Europe. It's really simple to make.

## Ingredients

- 1 head of cauliflower
- 50 ml butter
- 2–3 tablespoons breadcrumbs
- ½ teaspoon fresh dill (optional)

## Procedure

1. Boil the cauliflower for about 10 minutes.
2. Melt some butter in the oven or a microwave.
3. Mix the butter with the breadcrumbs, and add dill if using.
4. Drain the cauliflower and add the mixture on top.
5. Eat!
