## Ingredients

- 2 lb (about 1 kg) tender rhubarb, chopped
- 5 cups water
- ¼ cup lemon juice

## Procedure

1. Combine the rhubarb and water in a non-aluminum pot. Cook until mushy.
2. Strain the mixture well, reserving only the juice.
3. Mix in lemon juice.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- It's important to use a non-aluminum pot, because when combined with an acidic food like rhubarb, aluminum can impart an unpleasant flavor.
