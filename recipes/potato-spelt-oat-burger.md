## Ingredients

- Mashed potatoes
- Spelt
- Oat flakes
- Soy sauce
- Tomatoes (optional), chopped
- Onions (optional), chopped
- Salt
- Pepper
- Savory
- Chili powder
- Gyro spice

## Procedure

1. Combine the mashed potatoes, spelt flour, and oat flakes in about equal amounts (1:1:1).
2. Mix in soy sauce and water.
3. Fry the onions and tomatoes in a little oil, and add to the potato mixture.
4. Mix in salt, pepper, savory, chili powder, and gyro spice to taste.
5. Form the mixture into patties. Fry lightly in a pan with a thin layer of oil until browned on both sides.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Gyro spice is a spice blend consisting of paprika, chili pepper, onions, oregano, parsley, rosemary, thyme, marjoram, allspice, bay leaves, garlic, salt, and/or monosodium glutamate.
