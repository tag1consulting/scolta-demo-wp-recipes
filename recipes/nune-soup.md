# Nune Soup

**Nune soup**, also known as **locust bean soup**, is a typical Nigerian soup that is quick and easy to prepare. It is commonly consumed with ruam kanem (pounded yam) in the Tiv tribe.

## Ingredients

- Fresh pepper
- Fish
- Locust beans, rinsed
- Salt

## Procedure

1. Blend pepper the pepper to a paste. Boil it for few minutes.
2. Add the fish, locust beans, and salt to taste.
3. Stir and cook for few minutes.
4. Serve with pounded yam or akpu.
