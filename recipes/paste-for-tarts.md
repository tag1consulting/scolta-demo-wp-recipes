# Paste for Tarts

*This page incorporates text from the public domain cookbook Household Cyclopedia by Henry Hartshorne.*

## Ingredients

- 1 ounce white granulated sugar
- 1 pound fine flour
- 3 ounces butter
- 1 gill cream, boiling

## Procedure

1. Combine the sugar and flour.
2. Make it into a stiff paste with the boiling cream and butter.
3. Work the dough well, and roll it very thinly.
