# Egidi (Pounded Corn)

**Egidi**, also known as **pounded corn**, is a typical Yoruba dish common among the people of Ondo state. It is served with various soups, such as as okro, ogbono, and ẹgusi soups.

## Ingredients

- Fresh corn kernels
- Leaves, for wrapping
- Water

## Equipment

- Mortar and pestle
- Stove
- Pot
- Bowl/plate

## Procedure

1. Grind the corn kernels to make a paste.
2. Wrap the paste using wrapping leaves.
3. Cook for few minutes.
4. Pound using mortar and pestle until the mixture is lump-free.
5. Serve with soup.
