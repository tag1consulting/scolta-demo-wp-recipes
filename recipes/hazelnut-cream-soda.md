# Hazelnut Cream Soda

As this recipe shows, it's actually really easy to create your own sodas.

## Ingredients

- 2 volumes hazelnut-flavored syrup
- 1 volume heavy cream
- 2 volume seltzer water

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Serve cold over ice.
