## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups whole wheat flour
- 1 cup wheat germ
- 2 tablespoons white granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 tablespoon nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups milk
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 2 eggs

## Procedure

1. Heat waffle iron according to the manufacturer's directions.
2. Combine dry ingredients and mix well.
3. Add milk, vegetable oil, and eggs, and mix until smooth.
4. Pour 1 cup of batter onto the oiled waffle iron, and cook according to the manufacturer's directions.

## Notes, tips and variations

- Waffles are generally served with butter melted on top, then sweet syrup.
- Fruit may be spread on top.
