## Ingredients

- 1½ cups (125 g / 4.4 oz) strawberries
- 2 bananas
- ¾ cup (200 ml / 6.8 oz) apple juice
- ⅝ cup (150 g / 5.3 oz) natural (plain) yogurt
- ⅔ cup (50 g / 1.8 oz) ice cubes
- 1 tbsp honey

## Procedure

1. Cut off the tops of the strawberries.
2. Peel the bananas.
3. Blend all the ingredients in a blender until smooth.
4. Add honey to taste.
5. Pour the smoothie into 2 glasses.
6. Enjoy!
