# Peanut Sauce (Vegan)

This **peanut sauce** works very well when served with stir-fried tofu and greens or any steamed vegetables.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup peanut butter
- 1–2 cloves of garlic
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp vinegar, rice vinegar or cider vinegar
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar
- ¼ tsp salt
- ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- ½ cup hot water

## Procedure

1. Mix all ingredients together, adding the water last.
