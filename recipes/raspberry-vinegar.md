## Ingredients

- 4 pounds raspberries
- 2 cups vinegar
- Sugar

## Procedure

1. Mash the berries and add the vinegar and let stand for 4 days.
2. To each cup of liquid, add 1 cup of sugar.
3. Bring to a boil and cook for 20 minutes.
4. Strain and bottle.
