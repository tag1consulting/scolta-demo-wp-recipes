## Ingredients

- Pillsbury pie crust

- 1 cup sliced strawberries

- ¼ cup water
- ⅔ cup white granulated sugar

- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Whipped cream

## Procedure

1. Place your strawberries in a saucepan and add water, sugar, and lemon juice. Bring to a boil and stir occasionally until thick and syrupy.
2. Arrange the pie crust in a pie pan (unless the pie crust comes with a pan). Pour filling into the pie crust.
3. Bake in the oven at 350°F for 60 minutes or until golden brown. Remove pie, and let it cool.
4. Serve with whipped cream on top.
