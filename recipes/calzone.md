# Calzone

**Calzone** is a popular Italian form of pizza. The word means *sock* because the resulting pizza looks similar to a sock.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (240 ml) ricotta cheese
- 1 cup (240 ml) shredded mozzarella cheese
- 4 ounces (115 g) any topping (such as ham, bacon, salami, pepperoni, and/or sausage) (optional)
- 2 teaspoons (10 ml) oregano
- 1 package store-bought pizza dough

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Combine ricotta, mozzarella, toppings, and oregano.
3. Roll the pizza dough between 2 pieces of waxed paper.
4. Spoon the cheese mixture into the center of the dough.
5. Moisten edges of dough slightly. Fold the dough over, and seal edges by pressing with a fork.
6. Bake until golden brown, about 10 or 12 minutes.
7. Serve with marinara sauce.
