## Ingredients

- 2 packages instant ramen or udon noodles with flavoring mix
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 sausages
- ½ medium onion, chopped

## Procedure

1. Bring 1.5 liters of water to a boil.
2. Add noodles to to boiling water with one of flavoring packets.
3. Add chopped tomatoes and sausages. Boil for 5 minutes.
4. Add chopped onion and serve.
