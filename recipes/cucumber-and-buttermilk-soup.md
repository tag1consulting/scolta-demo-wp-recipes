# Cucumber and Buttermilk Soup

A cold tangy soup, refreshing and unique.

## Ingredients

- 5 hot house cucumbers
- 4 cups scallions
- 6 cups buttermilk
- 1 cup cream
- 2 tbsp tarragon
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste

## Procedure

1. Peel cucumbers.
2. Dice into ¼-inch dice.
3. Finely chop scallions.
4. Mix everything together.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Dill can be substituted for tarragon.
