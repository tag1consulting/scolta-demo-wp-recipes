## Ingredients

- 4 English muffins
- 1 cup pizza sauce (store-bought)
- 1 package pepperoni
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese

## Procedure

1. Slice English muffins in half.
2. Spoon 1 tbsp pizza sauce on each English muffin half and spread evenly.
3. Place 4 pepperoni slices on each English muffin half.
4. Cover each mini pizza with ¼ cup mozzarella cheese.
5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10 minutes/
6. Let cool then eat.
