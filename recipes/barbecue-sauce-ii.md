## Ingredients

- 1 cup ketchup (catsup)
- ½ cup molasses
- ½ teaspoon "concentrated" liquid smoke flavoring

## Procedure

1. Mix everything in a pot.
2. To use, heat the sauce with some pre-cooked meat in it.
