# South Side Fizz

A **south side fizz** is the same as a traditional south side, but with soda water.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ oz gin
- ½ oz lime juice
- ½ oz simple syrup
- 2 sprigs mint
- Club soda

## Procedure

1. In a shaker, muddle the mint, lime juice and simple syrup.
2. Add the gin and fill with ice.
3. Shake, and strain into a highball glass filled with crushed ice.
4. Stir until frost appears on the outside of the glass.
5. Fill with club soda and garnish with another mint sprig.
