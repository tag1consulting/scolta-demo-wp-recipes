# Denver Crunch

**Denver Crunch** is a layered dessert that includes a crunchy base, a cream cheese middle, and a fruit top.

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup chopped pecans
- 2 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup butter

### Filling

- 11 oz cream cheese
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Fruit filling of choice

## Procedure

### Crust

1. Mix crust ingredients like pie crust until crumbly.
2. Press in pan. Bake at 350°F until brown. Cool.
3. Take fork and crumble into small pieces. Some may be reserved for topping if you choose.

### Filling

1. Beat cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla until fluffy.
2. Spoon the filling on the crust until it is covered.
3. Top with your favorite fruit filling.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Thickened blackberries and boysenberries make excellent toppings.
