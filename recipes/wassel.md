## Ingredients

- 1½ gallons apple juice
- 128 ounces cranberry juice
- 128 ounces white grape juice
- 2 cups orange juice
- 6 ounces mulling spices
- Sliced oranges

## Procedure

1. Mix together liquid ingredients, then stir in mulling spices and orange slices.
2. Warm, but do not boil.
3. Serve hot.
