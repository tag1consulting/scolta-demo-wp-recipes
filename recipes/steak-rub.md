# Steak Rub

Great spices like coriander, garlic, and smoked paprika. This also packs a punch with cayenne, so this is not for people who don't like a lot of heat.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (80 g/2.8 oz) chili powder
- ¼ cup (40 g/1.4 oz) coarsely ground black pepper
- 7 tbsp coarsely ground coriander seed
- 3 tbsp garlic powder
- ¼ cup (40 g/1.4 oz) smoked paprika
- 3 tbsp cayenne pepper

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients in an airtight container with a shaker lid.
