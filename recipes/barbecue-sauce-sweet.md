## Ingredients

- 1 cup tomato catsup (ketchup)
- ¾ cup cider vinegar
- 2–3 tablespoons mustard
- 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1–2 teaspoons celery seed
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon onion flakes
- Garlic powder or minced garlic to taste (optional)

## Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients together and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, until it thickens slightly.
2. Arrange chicken pieces or pork chops in a greased baking dish.
3. Pour half of sauce over and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 to 40 minutes.
4. Pour remaining sauce over before serving.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- You can also keep sauce in refrigerator and use it like bottled BBQ sauce.
