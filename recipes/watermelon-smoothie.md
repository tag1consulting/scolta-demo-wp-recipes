# Watermelon Smoothie

**Watermelon smoothie** is a hydrating and healthful beverage.

## Ingredients

- Watermelon
- Milk

## Equipment

- Blender
- Knife
- Sieve

## Procedures

1. Cut the watermelon into small pieces after removing the seeds.
2. Combine all of the ingredients in a blender until smooth. Drain through a sieve.
3. Serve.
