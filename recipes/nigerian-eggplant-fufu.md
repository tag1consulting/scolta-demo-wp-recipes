# Nigerian Eggplant Fufu

**Nigerian eggplant fufu** is a swallow made from eggplants Despite the dark purple colour of eggplant, it turns a dark greyish colour as in amala when cooked.

## Ingredients

- 2 eggplants

- 2 tablespoons psyllium husk powder

## Equipment

- Pot
- Food processor
- Paper towels

## Procedure

1. Cut the eggplant into small pieces and soften in a pot of boiling water. Drain well.
2. Absorb excess water from the eggplant with paper towels, then blend with the food processor.
3. Transfer the eggplant purée to a pot, and stir over medium heat for 2 minutes.
4. Add the psyllium husk, and stir for additional 3 minutes.
5. Shape into desired portions.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Choose small or medium-sized eggplant for your fufu, as they contain fewer seeds and are sweeter. It is also best to use eggplant with indentations since they are sweeter and have fewer seeds.
