## Ingredients

- 4 lemons
- 4 tablespoons sugar
- 1 liter carbonated water
- A few leaves of fresh mint and/or lemon balm (*Melissa officinalis*)

## Procedure

1. Juice the lemons, and dissolve the sugar in the juice.
2. Add carbonated water and the fresh herb leaves.
3. Serve cold.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- Lemon juice may be substituted by orange juice or other citrus juice, if desired.
