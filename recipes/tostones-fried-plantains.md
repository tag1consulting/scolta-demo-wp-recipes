## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large ripe plantain

## Procedure

1. Heat oil in a frying pan over low heat.
2. Peel plantain, and cut it horizontally or in circular slices.
3. Add plantains to frying pan and fry on both sides for about 1 minute or until golden brown.
4. Drain, cool, and enjoy!
