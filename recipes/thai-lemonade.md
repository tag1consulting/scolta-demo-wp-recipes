# Thai Lemonade

This is the Thai version of **lemonade**.

## Ingredients

- 125 ml water
- 100 g sugar
- 2 stalks lemongrass
- 2 cm piece of ginger, peeled and cut into pieces
- 1 liter carbonated water
- Lime, sliced, for garnish

## Procedure

1. Bring the water to a boil with the sugar, lemon grass, and ginger. Boil for 20 minutes.
2. Allow the syrup to cool, then remove lemongrass and ginger.
3. Pour the syrup in 4 large glasses, and add the carbonated water.
4. Garnish glasses with lime discs.
