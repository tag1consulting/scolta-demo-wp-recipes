# Eton Mess Easter Sharing Eggs

**Eton mess Easter sharing eggs** is a recipe adapted from a recipe on page 60 of the March 2023 edition of Tesco's magazine.

## Ingredients

- 1 medium-sized hollow dark chocolate Easter egg
- ⅓ cup (80 ml/2.7 fl oz) heavy cream
- 4 tablespoons raspberry jam
- ¾ cup (200 g/7.1 oz) reduced-fat soft cheese
- 1 meringue nest, crumbled
- 1 tablespoon strawberry-infused apple pieces or freeze-dried strawberries

## Procedure

1. Halve the Easter egg carefully by running a sharp knife under hot water, wiping dry, and pressing into the joint to separate.
2. Whip the cream in a mixing bowl to soft peaks.
3. Fold in 3 tablespoons of jam and the soft cheese.
4. Spoon the mixture into the egg halves.
5. Scatter the crumbled meringue nest and apple pieces over top.
6. Drizzle the egg halves with remaining jam and serve.
