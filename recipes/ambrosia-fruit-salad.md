# Ambrosia Fruit Salad

**Ambrosia Fruit Salad** is a sweet mixture of mandarin oranges, fruit cocktail, coconut, and marshmallows coated in whipped cream. It originated in the 1900s and had a period of popularity in the United States. It is widely considered to be a traditional dish, suitable for family gatherings.

Below is one interpretation.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups of mandarin oranges, drained
- 2 cans of pineapple, drained
- 2 cups of grapes
- 3 cups whipped cream
- 1 cup sour cream
- 6–8 cups miniature marshmallows

## Procedure

1. Mix the mandarin oranges, pineapple, and grapes in a large bowl.
2. Fold in the whipped cream, sour cream, and marshmallows.
3. Chill for at least an hour.
4. Stir again before serving.
