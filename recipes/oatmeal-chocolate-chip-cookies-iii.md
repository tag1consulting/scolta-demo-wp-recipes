# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies III

These are traditional chewy chocolate chip cookies with oatmeal added to provide a bit of extra body to the cookie.

## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter, softened
- ⅓ cup white granulated sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 cup plus 2 tbsp flour
- ¼ cup rolled oats (oatmeal)
- ½ cup chocolate chips

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Cream together butter and sugars.
3. Add egg and vanilla and mix.
4. Add salt, baking soda, and flour; mix.
5. Mix in oatmeal.
6. Mix in chocolate chips.
7. Scoop cookies onto a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet.
8. Bake 9–11 minutes at 375°F.
