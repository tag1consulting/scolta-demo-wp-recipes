# Garlic Lemon Marinade

This **garlic lemon marinade** works well with fish and chicken. It can also be used as a sauce to accompany lobster.

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup freshly-squeezed lemon juice
- 8 cloves garlic, smashed and minced
- 1 ½ tsp salt
- 1 ½ tsp freshly-ground black pepper
- 1 lemon, finely zested
- ¼ cup vegetable oil

## Procedure

1. Process all ingredients in a blender until smooth.
2. Keep refrigerated for up to 1 ½ weeks. Shake well before using.
