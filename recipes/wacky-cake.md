# Wacky Cake

A common recipe for **wacky cake** follows.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups sifted flour
- 1½ cups white granulated sugar
- 1 cup cocoa powder
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups water
- ¾ cups vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp vinegar
- 2 tsp vanilla

## Instructions

1. Combine flour, sugar, cocoa, and baking soda in a large bowl.
2. Add water, oil, vinegar and vanilla. Blend thoroughly.
3. Pour into a greased square cake pan.
4. Bake at 180°C or 350°F for 35–40 minutes.
5. Cool completely.
