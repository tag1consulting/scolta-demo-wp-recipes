# Pad Thai Sauce

**Pad thai sauce** is commonly used in a Thai dish that shares the same name. This is a simplified version that is versatile enough to use in many other dishes. This recipe works best with rice noodles and is a great addition to any cook's stir-fry repertoire.

## Ingredients

- 1 garlic clove
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil (preferably peanut oil)
- 3 tablespoons tamarind or pineapple juice
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Thai fish sauce
- ¼ cup (60 ml) sugar
- 1 tablespoon sambal oelek chili paste
- ½ tablespoon cornstarch

## Procedure

1. Mince the garlic. Lightly brown it in a frying pan over high heat with the oil.
2. Combine the juice, soy sauce, fish sauce, and sugar and mix thoroughly
3. After mixture starts bubbling, add the chili paste and cornstarch. Mix well and cook for another minute or two.
