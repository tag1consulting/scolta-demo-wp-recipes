# Creamy Aioli Salsa

This is a cross between an aioli (a mayonnaise-like sauce which uses garlic instead of egg yolk as an emulsifier) and a pico de gallo-style salsa.

## Ingredients

- 1 lime
- Several tomatoes, chopped
- Olive oil
- 1 pinch salt
- 5 cloves garlic
- 1 or more jalapeño chiles or serrano chiles, chopped
- 1 bunch cilantro (optional), chopped

## Procedure

1. Squeeze the lime juice into a blender-safe bowl.
2. Add 2 teaspoons of olive oil and all other ingredients.
3. Use an immersion blender to blend everything to a purée.
4. Gradually blend in more olive oil, tasting, until the mixture reaches your desired creaminess.
