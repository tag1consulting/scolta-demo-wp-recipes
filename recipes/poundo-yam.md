# Poundo Yam

**Poundo yam** is a swallow made from yam flour. It is similar to pounded yam; however, this version is made with yam flour and does not require a mortar and pestle. It can be served with ẹfọ riro, ogbono soup, or ẹgusi soup.

## Ingredients

- Water
- Yam flour

## Procedure

1. Boil water using a pot.
2. While stirring slowly, pour yam flour until the mixture is lump-free and smooth.
3. Add a small quantity of water and cook for few minutes.
4. Serve with the soup of your choice.
