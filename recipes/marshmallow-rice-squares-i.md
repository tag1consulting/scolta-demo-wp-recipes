# Marshmallow Rice Squares I

This recipe for **quick marshmallow rice squares** is super-simple and requires little knowledge of cooking!

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mini marshmallows
- 1 cup puffed cereal (e.g. Rice Krispies)

## Procedure

1. Combine marshmallows and cereal in microwaveable bowl.
2. Microwave for 1–2 minutes, depending on your machine's power.
3. Stir to coat the cereal with marshmallow. Press into a buttered baking pan or dish.
4. Cut it into squares and allow it to cool and set.
