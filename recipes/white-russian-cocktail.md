# White Russian Cocktail

A **White Russian** is a creamy, coffee-flavored cocktail made by combining vodka, coffee liqueur, and cream or milk.

## Ingredients

- 2 oz vodka
- 1 oz coffee liqueur
- 1 oz heavy cream or milk

## Procedure

1. Fill a glass with ice.
2. Pour in the vodka, followed by the coffee liqueur.
3. Top with heavy cream or milk. Gently stir to mix.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- For a **blind Russian**, replace the cream with Irish cream liqueur.
- For a **dairy-free option**, replace the milk with either almond or oat milk.
