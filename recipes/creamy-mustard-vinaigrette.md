# Creamy Mustard Vinaigrette

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Recipes | Salad Recipes

This variation on the basic vinaigrette is much thicker and goes great with contrasting flavors in salad such as dried cranberries and bell peppers.

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 5 tablespoons olive oil (light or virgin)
- 2 teaspoons white wine vinegar or cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- Salt to taste
- Freshly-ground pepper to taste

## Procedure

1. Mix all ingredients except oil until well blended.
2. Slowly mix in oil until fully incorporated.
3. Add salt and pepper to taste.
