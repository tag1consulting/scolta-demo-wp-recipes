# Queens Soup

**Queen's Soup**, or *Zuppa Regina di Riso*, is a rice soup. One recipe is as follows.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon ground rice
- ½ pint (285 ml) milk
- 1 oz (30 g) butter
- Fowl (poultry) stock

## Equipment

- Saucepan
- Bain-marie
- Sieve

## Procedure

1. Put the ground rice into a saucepan. Gradually add the milk.
2. Cook gently for 12 minutes in a bain-marie, stirring the whole time, until very smooth.
3. Add the butter, and pass through a sieve.
4. Mix the fowl stock, and serve immediately.
