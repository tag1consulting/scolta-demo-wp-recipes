# Herrencreme (German Custard Dessert)

**Herrencreme** is a German dessert based on custard and cream.

## Ingredients

- 1 L milk
- 6 tbsp granulated sugar
- 2 packets (88 g) German custard powder (e.g. Dr. Oetker)
- 1 pinch salt
- ¼ L whipped cream
- Grated chocolate or chocolate sprinkles
- Rum

## Procedure

1. Combine milk, sugar, salt, and custard powder in a saucepan. Cook, stirring, over medium heat until it has thickened.
2. Place a sheet of cling film on the surface of the pudding to prevent a skin from forming, and let cool completely.
3. Transfer the pudding to a bowl. Stir in rum and chocolate to taste.
4. Fold in the whipped cream.
5. Chill in fridge for about 2 hours.
6. Serve.
