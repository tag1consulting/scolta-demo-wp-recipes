# Brown Sauce (Hartshorne)

**Brown Sauce** is a tangy, slightly sweet sauce accompanying cooked breakfasts and chips (fries).

This recipe is an interpreted method from the original recipe in Henry Hartshorne's the Household Cyclopedia.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp French vinegar
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp pepper

## Procedure

1. Mix together the sugar, vinegar, olive oil, mustard, salt, and pepper.
2. Serve or store in a bottle.
