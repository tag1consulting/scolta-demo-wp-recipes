# Nigerian Fruit Skewer

**Fruit skewer**, also known as **fruit kebab** is a typical Nigerian snack prepared from varieties of fruit.

## Ingredients

- Pineapple, peeled
- Pear, peeled
- Apple, peeled
- Pawpaw, peeled
- Watermelon
- Banana, peeled

## Procedure

1. Cut all the fruit into small chunks.
2. Thread the fruit chunks onto a skewer.
