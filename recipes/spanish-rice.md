## Ingredients

- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped (optional)
- 1 green bell pepper, finely minced
- 2 serrano chiles, finely minced
- ½ cup shredded cooked chicken or pork
- 1 onion, finely minced
- 3 ½ cups chicken broth
- 3 bay leaves
- ¼ cup minced chorizo, casing removed
- 1 Tbsp minced garlic

## Procedure

1. Melt butter in a large pot over medium high heat. Add onion, chiles, garlic, meats, and rice, and cook until chorizo and rice are golden brown.
2. Add broth and bay leaves. Bring to a boil and cook until liquid has evaporated and rice is tender.
3. Sprinkle with cilantro before serving, if desired.
