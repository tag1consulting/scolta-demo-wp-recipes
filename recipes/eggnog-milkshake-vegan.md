# Eggnog Milkshake (Vegan)

Originally posted to Foodista by user Teresa Lindsey. Foodista recipes are released under a CC-BY license.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups of Silk Nog (vegan eggnog substitute)
- 1 pint of Soy Dream vanilla ice cream
- Nutmeg

## Preparation

1. Blend nog and ice cream together in blender.
2. Pour into a glass, and sprinkle with nutmeg.
