# Pulse Chutney

**Pulse chutney** is an vegan Indian recipe. Use fewer chili peppers if you do not want it so hot.

## Ingredients

- 2 oz (50 g) green mung beans
- 1 ½ inch (3 ½ cm) piece of ginger root
- 4 green chile peppers
- Salt
- Lemon juice

## Procedure

1. Soak the green mung beans for 3–4 hours. Allow to sprout.
2. Mince the green chilies, fresh ginger, and salt.
3. Grind to a paste in a mortar and pestle or food processor.
4. Season with lemon juice to taste.
