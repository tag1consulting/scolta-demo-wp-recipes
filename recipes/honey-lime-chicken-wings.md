## Ingredients

- 32 chicken wingettes
- ½ cup freshly-squeezed lime juice
- 3 Tbsp honey
- Cornmeal
- Poultry shake
- 3 eggs, beaten
- ¼ cup chipotle hot sauce
- 5 cloves garlic, smashed and minced
- 4–5 drops red food coloring
- Oil for deep frying

## Procedure

1. Dip chicken in beaten eggs. Season liberally with poultry shake.
2. Dredge chicken in cornmeal. Let rest 2–3 minutes.
3. Deep fry in batches at 350°F (175°C) for 11–12 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack.
4. Meanwhile, combine remaining ingredients in a large nonreactive bowl. Add wings and toss to coat. Serve warm.
