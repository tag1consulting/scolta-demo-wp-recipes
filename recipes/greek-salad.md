## Ingredients

- Red tomatoes, chopped
- Cucumber, sliced into rounds
- Red or white onions, sliced into half-moons
- Green bell peppers, cut into small pieces
- Greek olives
- Extra-virgin or virgin olive oil
- Vinegar or lemon juice
- Salt
- Feta cheese
- Oregano

## Procedure

1. Combine tomatoes, cucumbers, onions, peppers, and olives in a deep bowl.
2. Add enough olive oil, vinegar or lemon juice, and salt to taste. Toss the ingredients for half a minute.
3. Place a feta cheese slice on top and season with a splash of olive oil.
4. Top with a sprig of oregano if desired.
