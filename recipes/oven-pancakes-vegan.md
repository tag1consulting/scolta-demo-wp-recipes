## Ingredients

- 1 cup (236 ml) flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoon white granulated sugar
- 1 cup (236 ml) soy milk
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 230 °C (446 °F) and oil an 8-inch oven-safe skillet.
2. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar.
3. Add the soy milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla; stir well.
4. Stir in the apple cider vinegar, mix well, and pour immediately into the skillet.
5. Bake at 230 °C (446 °F) for 18 to 20 minutes, and serve.

## Notes, tips, and variations

- When a toothpick is inserted into the completed product, it should come out clean.
