## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp salt
- ⅓ pound uncooked dried spaghetti
- 2–4 sausages
- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- 3–5 tbsp pasta sauce or ketchup

## Procedure

1. Bring 2 liters water to a boil, and add salt.
2. Add the spaghetti to the boiling water, and cook for 3 minutes.
3. Add sausages to pasta, and boil for 5 minutes.
4. Drain water.
5. Add butter to the pasta, then mix in sauce or ketchup.
6. Serve.
