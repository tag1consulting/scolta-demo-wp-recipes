## Ingredients

### Filling

- ½ cup (about 120 ml) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) cornstarch
- 4 cups (950 ml) sliced peeled peaches
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) lemon juice

### Topping

- 1 cup (about 240 ml) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons shortening
- ½ cup (about 120 ml) milk

## Procedure

### Filling

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Combine granulated sugar and cornstarch in a mixing bowl.
3. Stir in peaches and lemon juice to make a sugary-brown syrup.
4. Bring the peach mixture to a boil in a saucepan, and simmer until it thickens. Transfer to a 2-quart casserole dish, and put into the oven.

### Topping

1. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a mixing bowl. Stir, until well mixed.
2. Add shortening, and cut with a pastry blender until well mixed.
3. Mix in milk to make a ball of dough.

### Assembly

1. Remove casserole dish from the oven. Add dough in spoon-sized dollops on top of fruit.
2. Return to 400°F oven. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until topping turns brown.
3. Serve.
