## Ingredients

- 5 large sweet potatoes
- 2¼ cups brown sugar
- 5 cloves
- Zest of 1 orange
- 2 cups water
- ½ cup brandy
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 1 stick cinnamon

## Procedure

1. Slice the sweet potatoes and lay in a deep baking pan with the cinnamon stick.
2. Mix the liquids, zest, cloves, brown sugar and nutmeg together in a pot; bring to a boil and cook for 5 minutes.
3. Pour liquid mixture over the dish's contents.
4. Cover with foil and bake at 350°F for 30 minutes or until sweet potatoes appear to be tender and glazed.
5. Chill for 3 hours, and serve cold.
