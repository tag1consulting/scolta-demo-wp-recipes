## Ingredients

- 2 slabs St. Louis style/cut pork spareribs
- 6 tbsp Barbecue Rub
- 2 tbsp minced fresh ginger
- 1 cup peach preserves
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp cider vinegar
- Dry applewood chunks

## Procedure

1. Season ribs with the Barbecue Rub. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
2. Place applewood in the firebox of a 250°F smoker or in the coal area of a 250°F charcoal grill set for indirect heat.
3. Combine remaining ingredients and bring to a boil over high heat until reduced by ⅓. Cover tightly and set aside.
4. Place ribs in smoker/on top of grill grate and cook, brushing often with preserve mixture, for 5–6 hours.
5. Brush ribs generously with preserve mixture and place on a medium high grill and cook, repeatedly brushing side facing you, until well browned. Repeat one more time.
6. Remove and let rest 10 minutes; slice into 1-rib portions and serve.
