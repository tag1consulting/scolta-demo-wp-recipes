## Ingredients

- 1 cup (250 ml) powdered orange flavor drink mix (e.g. Tang brand)
- 1 cup (200 g) white granulated sugar
- ½ cup (125 ml) instant tea
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground cloves
- 1 tsp mixed spices
- 1 pinch nutmeg

## Procedure

1. Mix together thoroughly all ingredients.
2. Store in an airtight container.
3. Add 2–3 teaspoons to 1 cup of hot water.
