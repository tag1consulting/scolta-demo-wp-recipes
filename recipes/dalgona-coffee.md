# Dalgona Coffee

**Dalgona coffee** (달고나 커피) is a refreshing, cold drink originating from South Korea.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp instant coffee
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp warm water
- Milk
- Ice cubes (optional—you can omit if you're using hot milk if you prefer hot coffee)

## Procedure

1. Put instant coffee and sugar in a bowl.
2. Gradually add water while whipping with a hand mixer (highly recommended; if not available, manually whisk the mixture up to 400 times).
3. Pour milk in a cup, add ice cubes, and add the cream on top.
