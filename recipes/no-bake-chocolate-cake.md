# No-Bake Chocolate Cake

This is a simple-to-make cake and delicious too!

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 3 sticks (1½ cups) margarine
- 2 cups (450 g) fine sugar
- 5 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup cocoa powder
- 2½ packs graham crackers, plain digestive biscuits, or whole wheat biscuits; crumbled

## Procedure

1. Melt the margarine.
2. Mix the sugar and the egg.
3. Add the melted butter to the egg/sugar mixture.
4. Add the cocoa and the vanilla extract.
5. Add crumbled graham crackers. Mix well until all crackers are covered.
6. Line a cake pan with aluminum foil and pour in mixture.
7. Leave in refrigerator for 1 hour (preferably longer).
8. Unmold and serve.
