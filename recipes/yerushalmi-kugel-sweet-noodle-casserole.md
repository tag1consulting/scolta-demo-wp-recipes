# Yerushalmi Kugel (Sweet Noodle Casserole)

**Yerushalmi kugel** is named after the city of Jerusalem. Legend has it that it originated in Jerusalem with the followers of the Vilna Gaon in the 18th century.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (200 g) noodles (egg noodles are preferred, but others work fine as well)
- ⅔ cup (160 g) sugar (white sugar is helpful because it's more obvious when it begins to brown)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 handful of raisins
- 1–2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 3 eggs

## Procedure

1. Boil noodles according to the package instructions and drain.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan, and add ⅓ cup (80 g) sugar.
3. Stir continuously until the sugar begins to brown. Remove from heat.
4. The sugar will continue to brown for a few more minutes, creating a thick caramel.
5. Add the caramel to the noodles.
6. Add the sugar, eggs, cinnamon, pepper, and raisins. Stir.
7. Bake in a pan at 350°F (180°C) until the top begins to brown (about 40 minutes).
