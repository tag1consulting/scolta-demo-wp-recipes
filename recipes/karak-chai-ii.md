# Karak Chai II

**Karak chai** is a type of chai tea popular in Southwest and South Asia. This recipe originates from the Youtube channel MATBAKH UK.

## Recipes

- 2 cups (480 ml) milk or non-dairy milk
- 7 cardamom pods
- 2 black tea bags or 2 teaspoons (10 ml) black tea
- 2½ tbsp (37.5 ml) sweetened condensed milk or cream of coconut

## Procedure

1. Combine the milk, cardamom, and tea in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the condensed milk.
4. Strain the mixture, discarding the solids.
