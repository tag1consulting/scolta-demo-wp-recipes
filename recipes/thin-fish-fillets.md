## Ingredients

- 4 ea. (24 ounces) flounder or other flat fish fillets
- Olive oil
- ½ cup dry vermouth
- 2 tablespoon (¼ stick) unsalted butter, softened
- Salt
- Freshly-ground black pepper
- 4 lemon wedges

## Procedure

1. At least 20 minutes before you plan to cook, preheat broiler.
2. Lightly oil bottom of a 9x13-inch ovenproof dish.
3. Arrange fillets in a single layer in the dish.
4. Pour in vermouth, using only enough to be about ½ inch deep.
5. Put pan under broiler and broil the fish as close to the heat as possible for about 2 minutes.
6. Remove and quickly spread softened butter over the fillets.
7. Return the pan to the broiler and cook for about 5 minutes more, until fish is just cooked through.
8. Remove fish from the broiler.
9. Salt and pepper each fillet and serve it with lemon wedges.
