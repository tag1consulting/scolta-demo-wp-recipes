## Ingredients

- 36 chicken wingettes
- 1 cup balsamic vinegar
- 3 sprigs rosemary, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 1 ½ tbsp cayenne pepper
- ¼ cup paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- Cornmeal, as needed
- Vegetable oil for deep frying

## Procedure

1. Place chicken in a zip-top plastic bag. Combine remaining ingredients except for oil and cornmeal and refrigerate overnight.
2. Heat oil to 350°F. Meanwhile, dredge chicken in cornmeal. Fry in batches for 12 minutes. Drain on a cooling rack.
3. Rest 5 minutes and serve.
