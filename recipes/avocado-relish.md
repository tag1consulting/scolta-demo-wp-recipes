## Ingredients

- 2–3 avocados
- Paprika, to taste
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- Salt, to taste

## Procedure

1. Remove the avocado flesh from the peel and pit.
2. Mash the avocado flesh with the remaining ingredients.
3. Let rest 5–10 minutes before serving.
